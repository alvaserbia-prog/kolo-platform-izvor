# Privacy Policy of the KOLO Platform

This Privacy Policy is adopted on the basis of Art. 66 paragraph 2 and Art. 81 paragraph 4 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System and in accordance with the Law on Personal Data Protection ("Official Gazette of the RS", no. 87/2018, hereinafter: LPDP).

**KOLO Foundation**

Registered address: Šetalište 16, 25000 Sombor, Republic of Serbia

Registration number: 28836627

Tax identification number (PIB): 115840443

Email: privatnost@ekolo.rs

The KOLO Foundation determines the purposes and means of personal data processing in the KOLO system. The Foundation is the controller even when it does not physically store users' data in its own databases — the legally relevant criterion is the determination of the purpose and means of processing, not the physical storage of data (Art. 2 para. 1 item 8 LPDP). The KOLO Protocol is the technical means of processing.

**Data Protection Officer (DPO).** The Foundation has not, at this time, appointed a data protection officer.

The obligation to appoint one exists where the controller carries out processing as a public authority, where the core activities of the controller consist of processing operations which, by virtue of their nature, scope or purposes, require regular and systematic monitoring of a large number of individuals, or where the core activities consist of processing special categories of data on a large scale (Art. 56 paragraph 2 of the LPDP). The Foundation is not a public authority, and the number of individuals whose data is processed and the scale of processing of special categories of data do not at this time reach the threshold set by that provision.

The Foundation reviews this assessment at least once a year, as well as upon activation of any new module within the meaning of Chapter VIII of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. The criteria under which the obligation is deemed to have arisen are established and published by a decision of the Managing Board; once the obligation arises, the Foundation appoints a data protection officer, publishes their contact details and submits them to the Commissioner in accordance with Art. 56 paragraph 8 of the LPDP.

The earlier arrangement, in which a data protection officer was appointed while at the same time deciding on the purposes and means of processing, did not ensure the independence required by Art. 56 paragraph 6 of the LPDP. The Foundation therefore abolished that arrangement rather than retaining it in a form in which that independence cannot be achieved.

**Data protection contact.** All questions regarding the processing of personal data and all requests for the exercise of rights under Article 13 of this Policy are to be addressed to **privatnost@ekolo.rs**. The Foundation responds to them within the time limits set out in Article 13. This address does not cease to apply upon the appointment of a data protection officer.

For all questions relating to the processing of personal data, you may contact us at the above addresses.

### Article 2 — Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all persons who use the KOLO Platform (hereinafter: the Platform), including natural persons registered on the Platform and contact persons of legal entities that are in contact with the Foundation.

A natural person with an account on the Platform is referred to in this policy as a user, regardless of status, and that is a user of the service within the meaning of the regulations. An adult user has the status of user while not in the Kolo, is a member without confirmation once introduced into the Kolo, a Kolo member once their reality has been confirmed by a confirmation in the chain of confirmations, and a Kolo keeper once ZRNO has been entered for them as a Kolo member (Article 2 and Articles 27–30 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). A participant is a member without confirmation, a Kolo member or a Kolo keeper.

The Platform is intended for adults, and for minors who have reached seven years of age under a special regime governed by the **Rulebook on the Participation of Children**, upon activation of Module 4 (Children) in accordance with Art. 58 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System and with the application of enhanced requirements under Art. 16 LPDP. The processing of a minor user's data is described in more detail in subsection 4.7.

The Platform is intended for persons living in the Republic of Serbia. The place is selected from the code list of settlements in Serbia, donations are received in dinars, and the Foundation is registered in Serbia. Translations of the Platform and of the acts into other languages exist for members of national minorities and foreigners living in Serbia, and not for offering services to persons in other states. Processing is therefore carried out under the Law on Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Serbia.

This Privacy Policy supplements the provisions of Chapter IX of the Rulebook on the KOLO System and applies together with the Records of Processing Activities. In the event of inconsistency, the provisions of the Rulebook on the KOLO System shall prevail.

### Article 3 — Principles of Processing

Data protection in the KOLO system is based on data protection by design and by default (Art. 50 LPDP) and rests on three design decisions.

The first is pseudonymity of the ledger. Records in the contribution ledger are tied to pseudonyms, not to users' personal names. The user's first and last name is kept only in the identity register under section 4.14, separately from the contribution ledger: it is entered only in the cases enumerated in that section, is not displayed to other users, except to the Kolo member who calls the user from the call list (section 4.17), and is not included in POEN records. Pseudonymity is not anonymity — pseudonymized data remain personal data within the meaning of the LPDP because they can, with additional information, be linked to an identified person.

The second is data separation. The Foundation does not store personal data of users in its own databases — all users' data are stored on the Protocol's infrastructure. The Foundation directly stores only the banking documentation of donations and the record of the link between the legal entity sponsor and the user in whose record the contribution is entered.

The third is data minimization. The Platform collects only the data necessary for the functioning of the system. Data minimization is a structural principle of the KOLO system that cannot be abolished by any governance decision.

### Article 4 — Categories of Data, Purpose, and Legal Basis for Processing

The KOLO system processes the following categories of personal data.

**4.1 User Data**

Upon registration, we collect the following data: a pseudonym (account name) chosen by the user independently, an email address, a password stored exclusively in hashed form, and the date of joining the system.

Purpose of processing: functioning of the system, identification of the user within the system, communication and account verification, access security.

Legal basis: execution of a contractual relationship (Art. 12 para. 1 item 2 LPDP) — by joining the system, the user accepts the terms of use that constitute the contractual relationship with the Foundation as controller.

The user is responsible for ensuring that the pseudonym does not contain personal data that could lead to their identification.

**4.2 Proof-of-Reality Data**

Proof of reality is a model of user verification based on personal acquaintance or on a conversation following a call, in the chain of confirmations — participants establish the reality of users on the basis of direct acquaintance, by a confirmation or by an introduction into the Kolo. Reality is established only in the chain of confirmations; a payment, a sponsorship, a farm registration or an entry in the identity register does not confirm it. Proof of reality does not require the collection of personal documents, identity card numbers, or the unique citizen identification number.

Within proof of reality we collect: the verification graph (a record of who verified whom, in pseudonymous form), the datum of whether you are in the Kolo, or confirmed, in the chain of confirmations, and verification records (verifier's pseudonym, verification sequence number, verified person's pseudonym, timestamp, type of verification and supervisor's pseudonym).

A verification is a confirmation or an introduction into the Kolo (Article 5a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality): a person who is confirmed is a Kolo member, and a person who is introduced into the Kolo is a member without confirmation and belongs to the Kolo. When a member without confirmation is confirmed by a Kolo member who knows them personally, by a Kolo keeper after a telephone conversation (Article 5c of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality), or by the Foundation, the verification record also records who confirmed them, when and in what manner; that confirmation is not a new verification, and an exchange is not its condition. That confirmation is subject to supervision in the same way as a verification, so data on supervision are also kept alongside it. When a user becomes a Kolo member, the platform shows them the list of linked users — those whom they introduced into the Kolo while they were a member without confirmation and the one who introduced them, if these are still members without confirmation — from which they personally confirm those whom they know (Article 5d of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality); the list is compiled from the verification graph and does not collect new data. A confirmation never occurs automatically. The type of verification and the datum on the confirmation form part of the verification record and of the verification graph. The difference between a confirmation and an introduction into the Kolo is not a degree of belonging to the Kolo — it determines only the actions that a member without confirmation may not take (Article 5a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality): in the chain of confirmations and in supervision, in confirming the conditions for a support programme and in member recommendations, towards children's accounts, in a farm, and with regard to the activation of ZRNO.

The data of the call list, on which a user who does not know any Kolo member waits for a Kolo member to call them in order to get acquainted, are described in section 4.17.

Purpose of processing: ensuring the principle of one person, one account and the integrity of the common-good ledger.

Legal basis: execution of a contractual relationship (Art. 12 para. 1 item 2 LPDP).

The verification graph, even in pseudonymous form, constitutes the processing of personal data within the meaning of the LPDP.

**4.3 Voluntarily Entered Data**

The user may, on their own initiative, enter additional data for easier use of the Platform: name and surname, telephone number, address or other contact data, a profile picture (avatar), and a description. Entering these data is not a condition for proof of reality or for access to system functions.

Profile pictures (avatars) and images that the user attaches to listings are stored with the image storage processor (Article 8); only the internet address (URL) of the image is recorded in the database, not the image itself. Images are deleted upon deletion of the account, that is, upon removal of the listing.

Purpose of processing: facilitating the use of the Platform and communication among users, at the user's discretion.

Legal basis: user's consent (Art. 12 para. 1 item 1 LPDP).

Consent is voluntary and may be withdrawn at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. The user may delete voluntarily entered data at any time.

**4.4 Activity Data**

During the use of the Platform, data on exchanges and contributions are automatically recorded in pseudonymous form: the amount of the POEN ledger update, the time of the update, and the pseudonyms of the parties.

Purpose of processing: maintaining the common-good ledger and the functioning of the accounting framework of the system.

Legal basis: execution of a contractual relationship (Art. 12 para. 1 item 2 LPDP) while the user participates in the system. After the termination of the user's status, identification data are deleted, and records remaining in the ledger are not personal data within the meaning of the LPDP because they can no longer be linked to an identified or identifiable person.

**4.5 Donation and Sponsorship Data**

For natural person donations we collect: the donation amount, the donation date, and the identity of the donor. Identification of the donor is provided through the banking system — the Foundation receives donations from verified bank accounts. These data are stored by the Foundation. For public donations (within the meaning of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations) we publicly publish the donor's first and last name together with the amount and date of the donation, in the donation list available to adult users; the donor's pseudonym is not displayed in the list and is visible only in the overview of POEN transactions, which is available to participants. The legal basis is the donor's consent (Art. 12 LPDP), given by choosing a public donation for the purpose of recording POEN. Consent may be withdrawn at any time, separately for each public donation, on the donations screen; upon withdrawal the first and last name are removed from the list and deleted from the donation record, while the donation remains in the list with its amount and date. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of publication before withdrawal nor POEN already recorded. For anonymous donations the donor's name is not published and no POEN is recorded; such a donation enters the list solely by its amount and date, without a name, without a pseudonym and without a link to a profile, so the donor cannot be established from it. The name of a donor who makes a public donation is published in the list of donations and is not entered into the description of the record of recorded POEN; that record states only the amount and the basis. The donor is aware that publicly linking their name to a donation makes it possible to connect their pseudonymous record with their identity; the disclosure is voluntary. No POEN is recorded for an anonymous donation because a record that cannot be attributed to any person cannot be verified (Art. 5a of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations), and not because the Foundation sets publication as a condition. The first and last name of a donor who makes a public donation is also entered in the identity register (section 4.14), where it matches the name of the payer from the bank statement; withdrawal of consent to publication does not delete that entry. A public donation of at least 5,000 dinars, made in a single payment, brings a donor who is a user onto the call list (section 4.17); the donation does not confirm reality, and neither the donation nor the POEN recorded for it depends on the call.

The payer's details from the bank statement are also kept with the donation record. A contribution is recorded exclusively for the user with whose funds the payment was made, so that datum serves to verify that the payer and the donor coincide and to prevent abuse of the system. A statement on the origin of funds is not collected: the identification of the payer and the verification of the origin of funds are carried out by the bank through which the donation was made. The legal basis is the legitimate interest of the Foundation (Art. 12(1)(6) LPDP) and the statutory obligation of financial reporting. The measures the Foundation applies for that purpose are laid down by Chapter IV of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations and are prescribed voluntarily — the Foundation is not an obliged entity within the meaning of the regulations on the prevention of money laundering and the financing of terrorism.

Purpose of processing: fulfilment of the statutory obligation of financial reporting.

Legal basis: statutory obligation (Art. 12 para. 1 item 3 LPDP).

For legal entity sponsorship we collect: the name, registered office address, registration number and PIB of the legal entity or entrepreneur, the first and last name and function of the representative submitting the application, data on the contribution, and the link between the legal entity sponsor and the user in whose system record the contribution is entered. The representative's first and last name is also entered in the identity register (section 4.14). A sponsorship in money brings an applicant who is a user onto the call list (section 4.17); the sponsorship does not confirm reality, and neither the sponsorship nor the POEN recorded for it depends on the call. Before the first application, the sponsor is entered once in the sponsor register, and the logo, the place and the address for display on the map are submitted upon entry, with a statement that it consents to the address being displayed; the display on the map lasts as long as the sponsor is entered and ceases upon deletion of the entry, which is possible when the sponsor has no agreement on sponsorship in goods and services with unredeemed vouchers; contributions made up to the deletion remain on the ranking list, unless you request that the sponsor also be removed from it; the entry also records its status (under review, approved, on the ban list) and the fact that the Foundation compared the name, registration number and representative with the register. The sponsor's name and logo are published on the sponsor ranking list; the address, registration number, PIB and representative are not published. The name and logo are also displayed on the vouchers and alongside the offer of vouchers. The donation agreement, which contains the representative's name, is kept in accordance with accounting regulations.

For sponsorship in goods and services we collect, in addition to the above data on the sponsor: an email address and a telephone number for contact with the giver, the applicant's relationship to the giver (representative, entrepreneur, owner, one of the owners, beneficial owner, holder of the holding), a description of the good or service, the number of vouchers, the unit price the giver applies in trade, a photograph of the price list, the place and address of handover or provision, the giver's declaration of compliance with regulations, the declaration of authorisation and the applicant's confirmation that they are aware of the manner in which the contribution is recorded, as well as data on the conclusion of the agreement (time and checksum of the text). For a holder of a registered agricultural holding we collect the first and last name and the name and number of the holding. The photograph of the price list is visible only to the administrator team. The sponsor's name and the value of sponsorship in goods and services are published on the sponsor ranking list.

Purpose of processing: recording of sponsorship and fulfilment of the financial reporting obligation.

Legal basis: legitimate interest of the Foundation (Art. 12 para. 1 item 6 LPDP) and statutory obligation of maintaining financial records.

**4.6 Special Categories of Data**

Upon activation of Module 3 (Social Programs) in accordance with Art. 57 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, the system may process special categories of data: parental status, age, disability, student status, or belonging to another qualifying group.

Under support to mothers, data concerning a person who is not themselves a user are processed — the date of birth of your child. The legal basis is the consent of the parent or legal guardian (Art. 16 of the LPDP), which you give as the applicant; only the date of birth is processed, without the child's name and without any other data concerning them, and it is erased when the application ceases to be valid.

The Foundation does not retain copies of submitted documentation. Only the minimum required for calculating entitlements is recorded in the system: for support to mothers, the dates of birth of children (without the children's names); for support to the elderly, the support recipient's date of birth; for disability, the date of the competent authority's decision (without the decision number, diagnosis, or medical documentation); for education, the educational institution and field of study. Access to these data is restricted to the person at the Foundation who processes the application — verifiers and other users do not have access to them, and the data are maintained pseudonymously, linked to the applicant's pseudonym, not to their name and surname.

Application to a social programme and requesting confirmation from verifiers is carried out exclusively with your explicit consent and if you are in the Kolo. Before approval, the fulfilment of conditions is confirmed by a Kolo member who knows you personally — your verifier, if they confirmed you, or another Kolo member who knows you personally — under full responsibility and without access to the data you entered, or by the Foundation; a Kolo member who only introduced you into the Kolo after a telephone conversation and a member without confirmation do not confirm the fulfilment of conditions. If you have more than one verifier on the basis of verifications existing before the introduction of the one-confirmation rule, confirmation by one of them is sufficient (Article 4 of the Rulebook on Support Programmes). The application is not approved without that confirmation, and a refusal requires a reason. Consent is requested before confirmation is requested from anyone, and it states how many persons will be asked. Those persons learn which program is concerned, which may reveal to them your membership in a special category; they are notified exclusively within the Platform — the message sent by electronic mail or to your device states neither the name of the program nor your pseudonym. This is the only disclosure of membership in a program that the application entails; if you do not consent to it either, the application cannot be submitted, because the fulfilment of the conditions is not established without the confirmation of those persons.

The description of the record of POEN recorded does not contain the name of the program. The name of the program is displayed to participants, together with the support recipient's pseudonym and the amount, and is derived from the application for the program — not from the description of the record, which is permanent and forms part of the data export. Unregistered persons and users who are not in the Kolo are shown exclusively a daily total per program — the number of support recipients and the total number of POEN recorded — from which neither the identity of a support recipient nor their individual amount can be established; a day on which, within one program, a contribution was recorded for only one support recipient is not published, because the total would then equal the individual amount. The ground on which the entitlement within the program was established is not displayed to any user.

The verifiability of the ledger thereby remains complete: the total number of POEN is public, the sum of the records in the Protocol is zero, and the sum of the published daily amounts, together with the other recording channels, yields the change in that total. What disappears from the public overview is the link between a particular user, the basis on which their contribution was recorded, and the amount.

This approach does not rest on the name of the program alone. The amount of the daily recording is derived from the data on the basis of which the entitlement was established and would itself disclose them: in the case of support for the elderly the amount is uniquely determined by the support recipient's year of birth, and in the case of support for mothers by the number of children and their ages. A published individual amount would disclose those data without a single word beside it, so removing the name without removing the amount would not suffice.

A breakdown by program is available to you, in your account, alongside the program itself, and to the Foundation to the extent necessary for administering the program.

You may withdraw your consent at any time, without stating reasons, by an action available to you alongside the program itself. Withdrawal terminates the procedure, ends the recording of POEN, and deletes the data you entered. The entered data are also deleted when the application ceases to be valid in another way — by refusal, by suspension at the review of status, or by termination of your status as a user. POEN already recorded is not annulled, as that would alter a ledger which must remain verifiable.

Purpose of processing: automatic recording of contributions in POENs for users belonging to qualifying groups, while protecting the integrity of the programs from false applications.

Legal basis: explicit consent of the user (Art. 17 para. 2 item 1 LPDP). Consent may be withdrawn at any time, with the consequence of ceasing the automatic recording of POENs. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Activation of this module requires a prior update of the data protection impact assessment.

**4.7 Data of Minors**

Upon activation of Module 4 (Children) in accordance with Art. 58 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, the system processes data of minor users. The conditions and scope of processing are governed by the **Rulebook on the Participation of Children**.

**Two entry paths.** A minor user's account is opened by a parent from their own account, or by the minor themselves. In the latter case, until it is taken over by a parent (waiting child), the account has a profile and may form friendships; the Chat Room, listings, communication with adults, and the recording of POEN are closed. Until then the pseudonym, the data necessary for access to the account, the parent's email address, and the records of friendships formed, with their dates, are processed about the minor. An account that nobody takes over within fourteen days is deleted, together with those records. The minor user may delete such an account themselves at any time.

**Three states.** A minor user is in one of three states (Rulebook on the Participation of Children): **waiting child** — until a parent takes over the account; **child with a parent** — when the parent has opened or taken over the account but is not a Kolo member or a founder (a member without confirmation does not meet that condition), or the existence of the child has not yet been confirmed; and **Kolo child** — when the parent is a Kolo member or a founder and the existence of the child has been confirmed. The state is determined from data that are already processed: the link with the parent, the parent's confirmation and the type of their verification (section 4.2), and the confirmation that the child exists. A child with a parent sees listings without the advertiser's pseudonym, except for the listings of their friends, and uses the Chat Room and messages only with friends; until they are a Kolo child, they do not publish listings, do not re-register POEN in either direction, not even with the parent, and no POEN is entered for them. When the parent's confirmation ceases, the child returns to the state of child with a parent; that is not a measure against the child.

**Legal basis in the period before takeover.** Until the account is taken over the parent's consent does not yet exist, so in that period the minor user's data are processed on the basis of legitimate interest (Art. 12 para. 1 item 6 LPDP), for the purpose of establishing contact with the parent in order to obtain the consent required by law. The scope is reduced to what is necessary for that purpose, the period is limited to fourteen days, the functions that would expose the minor user to adults are closed, and the minor user may delete the account themselves at any time. Upon takeover of the account the basis becomes the parent's consent under Art. 16 LPDP.

**The parent's email address** entered by the minor is data about a third party. It is processed on the basis of legitimate interest (Art. 12(1)(6) LPDP), for the purpose of obtaining the consent required by law, and solely in order to send one message inviting the takeover of the account. That message states only the minor user's pseudonym — never their name — because the accuracy of the address has not been verified. The address is not recorded as the address of the minor user's account and opens no path to login or password recovery.

**The minor user's email address.** A minor user may state their own email address. Doing so is voluntary and is not a condition for using the account. The address is processed exclusively for the purpose of regaining access to the account — setting a new password when the minor user has forgotten it; no notifications are sent to it. The address is recorded only after the minor user has confirmed it by opening a link sent to that very address, and until confirmation it has no effect; this prevents an incorrectly stated address from opening a path to the minor user's account. The address is removed at any time. If a minor user has no address of their own, the parent sets the new password from their child's profile; the previous password is not required for this, and the minor user is notified of the change.

**Categories of data:** pseudonym; the data necessary for access to the account (password in encrypted form); the parent's email address; the minor user's own email address, if they state one; the date of birth entered by the parent; the link to one or two parents; the parent's statement on the existence of the child and the confirmation that the child exists; the friendship graph with dates; messages in the children's Chat Room and in conversations; POEN records; listings; the school stated by the minor user themselves.

**Purpose of processing:** enabling minor users to participate in the system under a special limitation regime and recording their contribution (Art. 15, item 9 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

**Legal basis:** consent of a parent or legal guardian (Art. 16 LPDP), with additional restrictions for persons under fifteen years of age; for the parent's email address and for the minor user's data in the period before the account is taken over — legitimate interest (Art. 12 para. 1 item 6 LPDP), as described above.

**Parental insight is deliberately limited.** The parent does **not** read the conversations their child has with other minor users — they see the list of friendships with dates and the list of conversations without content. The reason is that insight into such a conversation also touches the other child, whose parent has not granted that insight. When a Kolo child becomes friends with a minor user who is not a Kolo child, the parent of the Kolo child receives a notification that the friendship has been formed; even then they do not read the conversations. The parent does read a minor user's conversation with an adult user, and the adult party is shown a notice in that conversation that it is read by a parent.

**Why consent here is stronger than usual.** The parent does not confirm consent merely by pressing a button in a message: a minor user's account gains full access only once the child is a Kolo child — when the parent is a Kolo member confirmed by someone who knows them personally, and when the existence of the child has been confirmed by a Kolo member who knows the parent personally, that is, when both the parent and the existence of the child have been confirmed by a third party in the real world. This exceeds the reasonable-effort standard of Art. 16(2) LPDP.

**The school and the overview by school.** A minor user may themselves state the school they attend, by choosing from a list; a waiting child does not state a school. The Platform publishes an aggregate overview by school — the number of Kolo children and their share in the number of enrolled pupils — which contains no personal data and is available to everyone. An overview of the Kolo children of a single school, with their pseudonym and record balance, is available exclusively to a Kolo child of that same school; it is not shown to adult users, to persons who are not signed in, to a waiting child, to a child with a parent, or to a minor user of another school. A child with a parent who has stated a school is not included in the number per school or in the school overview until they become a Kolo child. The stated school may be changed at most once every thirty days, and the datum is deleted upon attaining majority and upon termination of status; no history of earlier choices is kept.

**The profile of a minor user is not available to adult users.** Instead of the profile, a notice is displayed with the pseudonym of the minor user and the pseudonym of their parent. This is a narrowing compared to the earlier scope: until this amendment the profile was available to every confirmed user. Parental consent does not open the profile — it governs communication and exchange. Information on who the parents of a minor user are, and on who the children of an adult user are, is available to signed-in users.

Activation of this module requires a prior update of the data protection impact assessment and the adoption of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children.

**Confirmation that the child exists.** A parent who opens or takes over an account gives a statement under full responsibility that they have a child of the stated age; the text of the statement is kept and is not altered after it is given. A parent who is a Kolo member or a founder may request that the existence of the child be confirmed by another Kolo member. The request is seen by Kolo members and founders — not by members without confirmation — and only as the parent's pseudonym with a note that they are requesting confirmation that they have a child; the pseudonym, age and other data about the child are not shown to them. The confirmation is given only by a Kolo member or founder who knows the parent personally; the other parent of the same child does not give it. Who gave the confirmation and when is recorded alongside it, and where the confirmation is subject to supervision (Article 10 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality), also the data on supervision under section 4.2. The confirmation is not tied to a time limit: if it is not given, nothing is taken away from the parent, and the child remains in the state of child with a parent. Anyone who falsely confirms the existence of a child is liable as for a false verification.

**Report of false parenthood.** A minor user receives a notification each time someone takes over their account, with the pseudonym of the person who took over the account. The minor user, the other parent or any other user may report to the Foundation that the person who opened or took over the account is not the parent of that child (Article 4e of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children). Upon a report, the Foundation immediately suspends that person's link with the child's account: while the check is ongoing, the person has no insight into the child's profile and no parental powers. The report is decided upon by an authorized person of the Foundation; where it establishes that the person is not the parent, it terminates the link, and the parent's place becomes free. The person to whom the report relates is not told who submitted it. The report (the person submitting it, the person to whom it relates, the minor user, the time and the allegations), the suspension and the decision are processed; the purpose is to protect the child from a person who is not their parent, and the legal basis is legitimate interest (Art. 12 para. 1 item 6 LPDP). The report and the decision are kept for as long as the minor user's account exists. A false statement of parenthood entails exclusion (Article 28 of the Terms of Use); an appeal may be lodged against the decision (Article 37a of the Terms of Use).

**4.8 Data in a Listing by a User**

A user who is not in the Kolo (an adult) may post a listing offering or seeking a good or a service (Article 16 of the Terms of Use). By posting a listing they present themselves to participants and thereby establish the contact necessary for the conduct of verification under Article 5 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality.

The listing contains: the advertiser's pseudonym, the title and description of the good or service, the category, the price, the place (a settlement from the register), photographs the advertiser attaches themselves, and, at their own choice, a telephone number.

The listing is publicly visible to all visitors of the Platform, including non-registered persons, and is indexed by search engines. The telephone number is not public — it is accessible exclusively to participants. The listing carries a visible mark indicating that the advertiser is not in the Kolo.

The advertiser chooses what to publish. A photograph of their face, name, and surname are neither requested nor required; an advertiser who includes them in a listing does so on their own initiative and thereby publishes them publicly.

A user may, starting from a published listing, initiate a direct conversation (messages) with the advertiser; a user at most three new conversations per day, for which purpose who initiated the conversation is recorded. An advertiser who is a user may respond in the conversation even before being in the Kolo; outside a listing, they do not contact users who have not contacted them. The content of the conversation is processed for the purpose of arranging an exchange and establishing contact for verification. The legal basis for processing the conversation content is the legitimate interest of establishing contact for the purpose of exchange and verification (Art. 12 para. 1 item 6 LPDP); messages are deleted in accordance with Article 10 of this Policy.

Purpose of processing: publishing an offer for the exchange of goods and services and establishing contact between a user and the participants who may verify them.

Legal basis: advertiser's consent (Art. 12 para. 1 item 1 LPDP), given by posting the listing, with a warning that the listing is publicly visible. **4.9 Protection of System Integrity**

To prevent misuse of the proof-of-reality system — false or "farmed" verifications, account multiplication, and other patterns that would devalue the ledger and governance — the Foundation analyses patterns in the verification graph and contribution ledger. This processing does not collect new data: it uses exclusively existing pseudonymous data (verification graph, POEN ledger metadata, account activity indicators) and a derived risk record (labels of violated rules and a numerical score), accessible exclusively to authorised persons.

Purpose of processing: protection of the integrity of proof of reality, the common-good ledger, and governance.

Legal basis: legitimate interest of the Foundation and honest users (Art. 12 para. 1 item 6 LPDP).

The system does not make automated decisions within the meaning of Art. 38 LPDP — it only flags an account or group of accounts for review, and every measure is examined and taken by an authorised person. Flagging prioritises the absence of genuine activity rather than the density of connections, so that a close-knit real-world community is not wrongly flagged. You have the right to object to this processing in accordance with Article 13 of this Policy.

One of the checks is the "closed group" check (Article 11 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality): from data that are already processed (the verification graph and the POEN ledger), the Foundation identifies groups of members without confirmation connected by introducing one another into the Kolo, who exchange only within the group, without any exchange with a Kolo member. The check produces no effect whatsoever on the user — it does not change their status, verification or records — but only shows the group to the Foundation, which may invite the members of the group to a conversation.

**4.11 Procurement proposal and participation in collective procurement**

Collective procurement is a project by which the Foundation acquires goods using dinar funds, and participants collect them in equal shares with the annulment of POEN records (Articles 14a and 51a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). Two groups of data are processed for this purpose.

First, the procurement proposal: the name of the good you state and the time of entry. The proposal contains no quantity, price, or reasoning. Each participant has at most one active proposal. The register of proposals is published **in aggregate — only the name of the good and the number of users who proposed it, without pseudonyms**.

Second, participation in a published procurement: the fact that you applied, your position in the order, the day of collection you entered, the collection code, and the time of collection. The number of POEN by which the order is established is not collected anew — it is read from the record of contributions (subsection 4.4).

The proposal and the application are open to every participant with an active account (Article 4 of the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement). No other eligibility criteria are set, and **no evidence whatsoever concerning your occupation or property is collected**.

Purpose of processing: establishing the needs of the community in order to decide on a procurement, and carrying out the procurement decided upon.

Legal basis: performance of the contractual relationship between you and the Foundation (Art. 12(1)(2) of the LPDP). Submitting a proposal and applying are voluntary, and their absence has no consequences for your status.

Visibility: **the supplier is provided solely with a list of collection codes — not with pseudonyms or any other data about you**. Participants see the list of those who collected a share, with the pseudonym and position in the order; **the number of POEN in your record is not published alongside that list**. Your individual proposal is not published.

The establishment of the order is the application of publicly published rules and does not constitute automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 38 of the LPDP: it produces no legal consequences for your status. Against the established order and the outcome of a procurement you have the right of complaint under Article 33 of the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement and Article 37a of the Terms of Use, decided upon by a human being; the rights under Article 13 of this Policy remain unaffected thereby.

The account of a minor user cannot be a member of a farm.

The Foundation does not check the data in the register.

Extract from the Register of Agricultural Holdings: it is not attached to the registration and the Foundation does not collect it (Article 4b of the Rulebook on Farms).

The registration of a farm entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings brings a holder who is a user onto the call list (section 4.17); the registration does not confirm reality. The name of the farm is chosen by the applicant; if the name contains a surname, the surname becomes public together with the name.

Purpose of processing: displaying the farm on the public map of local producers and on the farm's profile, linking listings on behalf of the farm with the farm, and establishing the conditions for the farm contribution.

Display on the map is an integral part of the farm's participation in the system and a condition of the farm contribution; a user who does not wish the farm to be displayed does not register it, or withdraws from the farm.

The data under this section are included in the data export under Article 13.

For a farm approved earlier, the address is written out only once you give that statement. So that the farm's point is found on the map automatically, the entered address is sent from your browser to the address search service of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (Nominatim, United Kingdom); you can move the point that is found.

**4.14 Identity register**

The Foundation maintains an identity register — a register in which your first and last name is kept alongside the pseudonym of your account (Article 31a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). One first and last name is kept per account.

The first and last name is entered only in the following cases: when you register a farm or join one — you enter the first and last name yourself (section 4.13); when the Foundation establishes the receipt of your public donation — the donor's first and last name, where it matches the name of the payer from the bank statement (section 4.5); when you become a cooperative member — the first and last name is submitted by the cooperative (section 4.12); when the Foundation confirms the receipt of a sponsorship under an application you submitted as the representative of a legal entity or as an entrepreneur — your first and last name from the application, after comparing it with the Register of Business Entities (section 4.5); and when you get onto the call list — you enter the first and last name yourself (section 4.17). The following are not processed: the unique citizen identification number, the identity card number, or data from other identity documents; copies of identity documents are not kept. Where the first and last name is compared with an identity document or a public register, only the fact that the comparison was carried out is recorded.

Purpose of processing: giving effect to the principle of one person, one account (Article 31 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, Article 8 of the Terms of Use) and verifying the ground on which a contribution was recorded.

Legal basis: the entry is made for the execution of a contractual relationship (Art. 12 para. 1 item 2 LPDP) — for the participation you yourself requested, in a farm, in a cooperative, under a sponsorship application, under a public donation or on the call list. The name remains entered even when that participation ends (leaving a farm, termination of the status of cooperative member, withdrawal of consent to publication of the name with a public donation); the basis for this is the legitimate interest of the Foundation (Art. 12 para. 1 item 6 LPDP) in ensuring that the same person does not run more than one account and that the ground of a previously recorded contribution can be verified. The name is not used for any other purpose, is not shown to anyone outside the administrator team, except to the Kolo member who calls you from the call list (section 4.17), and is deleted upon termination of status, so that interest outweighs the interference with your rights; you may object to that processing (Article 13). The entered first and last name is compared with the names kept alongside other accounts in order to prevent multiple accounts (section 4.17); the comparison discloses to another user neither your name nor the pseudonym of the other account. The entry is a condition only for participation in a farm, for the status of cooperative member, for a sponsorship application and for the call list; it is not a condition for proof of reality or for access to the other functions of the system. The entry is not a verification, never confirms reality and does not change your status; reality is confirmed only in the chain of confirmations.

Visibility: the first and last name from the identity register is not displayed to other users, does not appear alongside a farm, a cooperative or a listing, and is not entered in POEN records. It is accessible only to the Foundation's administrator team (Article 8) and to you, as well as to the Kolo member who calls you from the call list — by a click and only while the call is open (section 4.17, Article 31a paragraph 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). An inaccurate entry is corrected by an appeal of the type “Data correction” (Article 13). The data under this section are included in the data export under Article 13.

**4.17 Call list and conversation request**

2) when the Foundation establishes the receipt of your public donation of at least 5,000 dinars, made in a single payment, where the payer's name from the bank statement matches your name and the payer is not associated with another account (section 4.5);

4) when, as the holder, you submit the registration of a farm, entered or not entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings (section 4.13).

Before you get onto the list, you enter your first and last name, place and telephone number, and we inform you that a Kolo member will call you and that that Kolo member will see your first and last name and telephone number, as well as whether the same number or the same first and last name is kept alongside another account, together with the place of that account. You leave the data yourself, when you consent to the call. A minor user does not get onto the call list.

For that purpose we process: your pseudonym, the ground on which you are on the list, first and last name (in the identity register, section 4.14), place and telephone number, the text of the consent you gave and the time of consent; in the case of a conversation request also a short description of what you do and when you are available for a conversation. The first and last name and the telephone number are compared with the names and numbers kept alongside other accounts; where they match, the Kolo member who opens the call sees an indication that a match exists and the place of the other account, but not its pseudonym, while both accounts are seen only by the administrator team. While the call is open, we also process the data on with whom it is open and until when. The Foundation also records which Kolo member opened the call and when.

Purpose of processing: so that a Kolo member may call you in order to get acquainted, after which they may verify you in the chain of confirmations, or so that a Kolo keeper may call you for confirmation; the prevention of multiple accounts of the same person (Article 8 of the Terms of Use); the record of who opened the call serves to protect against misuse of your name and telephone number.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 12 para. 1 item 1 LPDP), given when you leave your telephone number, or when submitting the conversation request. You withdraw consent by withdrawing from the list, or by withdrawing the request, without consequences for your status, the other functions of the account, the donation, the sponsorship and the farm, and without consequences for the contribution recorded on account of them. The record of who opened the call is kept on the basis of legitimate interest (Art. 12 para. 1 item 6 LPDP) — the protection of persons on the list against misuse of their data — and you may object to that processing (Article 13). Keeping the first and last name, place and telephone number after the call as well, comparing them with other accounts, and the procedure for establishing a multiple account are based on the legitimate interest of the Foundation (Art. 12 para. 1 item 6 LPDP) in ensuring that the same person does not run more than one account, because verification cannot confirm uniqueness. The interest outweighs the interference with your rights: the comparison blocks nothing and takes no decision, neither the name nor the pseudonym of the other account is disclosed to another user, and the decision is taken by a human, with a statement of reasons and the right to a complaint; you may object to that processing (Article 13).

Visibility: the list, without first and last names and without telephone numbers, is seen by Kolo members; members without confirmation do not see the list (Article 5a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality). Kolo members receive a notification of a new conversation request. The first and last name and the telephone number are opened by a click, only to the Kolo member who opened the call and only while the call is open: at any one time a call is open with only one Kolo member, for at most ten minutes, and one Kolo member has only one call open at any one time. The record of who opened the call is seen only by the Foundation's administrator team. The first and last name and the telephone number do not enter notifications, email or the audit log. If someone harasses you by misusing the number, report it to the Foundation.

Getting onto the call list does not confirm reality. Neither the donation, nor the sponsorship, nor the contribution recorded on account of them depends on it. Your reality is established only by verification in the chain of confirmations; after the conversation the Kolo member may introduce you into the Kolo, and you then become a member without confirmation (section 4.2).

The data from the list are deleted when you enter the Kolo, or when you are confirmed, when you withdraw from the list or withdraw the request, or when your user status terminates. The first and last name, place and telephone number remain alongside the account as long as it exists: you may change the place and the number, but not delete them, and they are deleted upon closure of the account. The datum on the current opening of a call is deleted when the opening expires or when the Kolo member closes it. The record of which Kolo member opened the call is kept for as long as your account exists and is deleted upon closure of the account, or by the pseudonymization under Article 11. The data under this section, except the record of who opened the call, are included in the data export under Article 13. The record of who opened the call is not exported through the Platform; you obtain it on request sent by email to privatnost@ekolo.rs (right of access, Article 13). The Kolo member who calls you and verifies you is at the same time your verifier, so they are known to you anyway.

Regardless of the user's status and regardless of voluntary disclosures, the following data are never publicly accessible: email address, technical logs and access log, verification graph, including the type of verification and the datum on the confirmation of a member without confirmation, the first and last name from the identity register, the telephone number from the call list, the record of who opened the call, voluntarily entered data that have not been explicitly disclosed, the first and last name of a cooperative member, the farm's BPG, the extract from the Register of Agricultural Holdings, the telephone number of the farm holder, the datum of who recommended whom, the datum of who confirmed the existence of a child, and special categories of data, including the fact that a user is in a particular social programme.

The public nature of the pseudonymous contribution ledger is a structural characteristic of the system, not an option the user may disable. A user who does not accept this condition may not use the Platform. Unlike the pseudonymous ledger, the public display of a donor's first and last name is optional — the donor chooses it by selecting between a public and an anonymous donation.

Even with pseudonymity, the combination of amounts, times, and frequency of transactions may in certain cases enable the indirect identification of the user. The user is aware of this limitation and accepts it upon registration.

### Article 11 — Termination of Status and Pseudonymization of Data

Upon termination of the user's status — whether by withdrawal, exclusion, or death — the pseudonymization procedure is initiated in accordance with Art. 34 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

The email address, all voluntarily entered data, and data in published listings are deleted, including the title, description, place, and photographs; the listings are removed from the advertising space and the photographs are deleted from storage. The free text entered alongside a re-registration of POEN is also deleted, both the text entered by the user and the text entered by their counterparty. The user's connections in the graph of confirmations are anonymized. The first and last name from the identity register (section 4.14) is deleted. The data from the call list and the record of who opened the call (section 4.17) are deleted. The first and last name of a cooperative member (section 4.12) are deleted; the link between the account and the cooperative remains under the pseudonym, for the purpose of verifying the growth thresholds of the cooperative's record that have already been recorded.

The user's POEN records are annulled, and the numerical history is retained under a pseudonym that does not point to a name. That procedure is pseudonymization, not anonymization: the Foundation retains the account's internal identifier, so the retained records remain personal data within the meaning of the LPDP and enjoy the full protection of this policy. They are retained because erasure is not an unlimited right — under Art. 30 para. 3 LPDP it does not apply where the processing is necessary for compliance with a statutory retention obligation and for the establishment, exercise, or defence of a legal claim.

After the termination of status, the numerical records of the ledger alongside a pseudonym that does not point to a name and the basis on which the POEN was recorded remain public. The first and last name alongside a public donation are removed from the list of donations and deleted from the donation record (Art. 5a of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations); the donation remains in the list with its amount and date, without a pseudonym. Payment details, including the payer's name from the bank statement, and the donation agreement are not published and are kept in accordance with accounting regulations.

This separation of identification and accounting data resolves the tension between the user's right to erasure of personal data (Art. 30 LPDP) and the integrity of the common-good ledger. The contribution ledger is by design consistent — deleting one user's records would undermine the consistency of the entire ledger, which is the common good of all users. By separating data, both the user's rights and the integrity of the ledger are preserved.

The deactivation of an account does not automatically entail the deletion of all data. Certain data are retained even after the termination of status for the periods prescribed by law:

Donation data — in accordance with the periods prescribed by tax regulations and the Law on Accounting.

Data subject to a statutory retention obligation — for the periods prescribed by law.

Upon expiry of the statutory periods, data are permanently deleted or anonymized. The user does not have the right to request deletion of data before the expiry of the statutory retention periods.

### Article 14 — Data Security

The Foundation applies technical and organizational data protection measures appropriate to the risk, in accordance with Art. 51 LPDP.

Technical measures include: encryption of data in transit using TLS encryption (minimum version 1.2), encryption of data at rest at the hosting infrastructure level, pseudonymization of the ledger (records are linked to a pseudonym, not to a name and surname), and separation of identification data from accounting data in separate records; the identity register (section 4.14) is kept separately from the contribution ledger and is accessible only to the administrator team.

Organizational measures include: access control based on the principle of minimum necessity — every user, administrator, and process has access only to the data necessary for the performance of their function; multi-factor authentication for administrative access to the infrastructure; a confidentiality obligation for all persons with access to users' data; regular training of employees and associates on data protection; regular security checks and penetration testing of the system.

The integrity of the contribution ledger in the Protocol is protected by the zero-sum invariant — the sum of all balances in the system always equals zero and is automatically verified — along with atomic (all-or-nothing) recording of each change and timestamping of records; administrative actions are logged in the audit trail. Any deviation from the zero-sum invariant is visible and subject to review.

In the event of a personal data breach that may endanger users' rights and freedoms, we will notify the Commissioner within 72 hours of becoming aware of the incident (Art. 52 LPDP), and notify you without undue delay if the incident may result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms (Art. 53 LPDP).
