# Rulebook on Farms

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1 — Subject matter

(1) This Rulebook governs, in accordance with Article 39 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, the registration and approval of a farm, farm members, the farm contribution, listings on behalf of a farm, the map of local producers, the false declaration and the termination of a farm.

(2) Terms from the Rulebook on the KOLO System have the same meaning in this Rulebook.

### Article 2 — Definitions

In this Rulebook:

1) **farm**: a household under Article 39 paragraph 1 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System that offers food or agricultural products in the platform's advertising space;

2) **farm member**: an adult user who works on the farm permanently or occasionally and whose account is linked to the farm in accordance with this Rulebook;

3) **applicant**: the farm member who submitted the registration;

4) **holder**: the person in whose name the farm is entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings; for a farm that is not entered in that register, as well as for a registered farm that the holder has not joined, the powers and obligations of the holder under this Rulebook belong to the farm member who, under Article 12, is responsible for the declaration of accuracy of data;

5) **BPG**: the agricultural holding number from the Register of Agricultural Holdings.

### Article 3 — The farm and the Register of Agricultural Holdings

(1) A farm has no account and does not participate in exchange. The farm members participate in exchange, each on their own behalf.

(2) Entry in the Register of Agricultural Holdings is not a condition for registration. A farm entered in that register and a farm not entered in it participate under the same conditions.

(3) No extract from the Register of Agricultural Holdings is attached to the registration. The Foundation does not check data in that register and relies on the applicant's declaration; the BPG, where stated, serves only as an identifier of the farm in the Foundation's records.

## II — Registration and Approval

### Article 4 — Who registers a farm

(1) A farm may be registered by any adult user. Whether the farm may have members depends on the status of the applicant:

1) a Kolo member, or a founder, registers a farm as holder or as member, and the farm may have members;

2) a member without confirmation registers a farm as holder, without members; the farm may have members from the time the applicant becomes a Kolo member;

3) a user registers a farm as holder, without members, and by submitting the registration receives an offer of the call list (Article 8 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality); once they have been introduced into the Kolo, point 2 applies to them, and once they become a Kolo member, point 1;

4) a minor user may not register a farm.

(2) A farm that was once able to have members does not lose that right when the applicant's confirmed status ceases (Article 14).

(3) Being placed on the call list and approval of the farm do not confirm the applicant's reality; reality is confirmed only in the chain of confirmations, under the Rulebook on Proof of Reality.

### Article 5 — Registration

(1) The registration is submitted by an adult user from their own account. The registration contains:

1) the BPG, if the farm is entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings, or an indication that it is not entered in it;

2) the name of the farm;

3) the place, the address and the exact location of the farm, with the applicant's statement that they consent to the address being displayed alongside the point on the map;

4) the role of the applicant: holder or farm member;

5) a description of the farm: what is produced and in what manner;

6) the applicant's declaration that the data are accurate, that the farm exists and produces what has been stated, and that they are personally responsible for what they offer, under the rules that apply to it;

7) at least three listings offering food or an agricultural product, posted on behalf of the farm;

8) the first and last name of the applicant;

9) the telephone number of the holder, or of the applicant where the holder has no account on the platform, for the purpose of the conversation under Article 8.

(2) The first and last name of the applicant is entered in the identity register (Article 31 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), alongside the applicant's account, and not alongside the farm. If a first and last name has already been entered alongside the account, it is not entered again. The first and last name is required also where the farm is not entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings.

(3) If, under Article 4, the farm may have members, the applicant may, with the registration, invite other adult users whom they know personally to join the farm (Articles 9 and 10).

(4) Registration is free of charge.

### Article 6 — Listings accompanying the registration

(1) The listings accompanying the registration must meet the content minimum established by the Terms of Use. A listing may be seasonal or for advance reservation.

(2) The listings are published immediately, as listings of the applicant, and Article 38 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System applies in respect of them. They receive the DOMAĆE mark when the farm is approved.

(3) The amount stated in the listing is determined by the person who posts the listing.

(4) The condition of three listings is met for the farm as a whole.

### Article 7 — Review and approval

(1) The Foundation reviews the registration together with the members who have joined by that time and with the listings, and checks that the registration is complete, that the declaration has been given, that the listings represent a genuine offer of food or agricultural products and that they do not offer a prohibited good (Article 23 of the Terms of Use). The Foundation removes a listing offering a prohibited good, stating the reason, and does not count it among the listings under Article 5 paragraph 1 point 7.

(2) If the registration is not complete, the Foundation returns it to the applicant, stating what needs to be supplemented.

(3) The farm is not approved until every member who has joined by that time has a first and last name entered in the identity register and until the conversation under Article 8 has been held with the holder.

(4) When the conditions are met, the Foundation approves the farm. Upon approval, the farm receives a public profile and a point on the map of local producers, listings on behalf of the farm receive the DOMAĆE mark, and the farm contribution is noted for the members (Article 15).

### Article 8 — Conversation with the holder

(1) Before approving the farm, the Foundation speaks with the holder, by telephone or in person, for the purpose of approving the farm: in order to satisfy itself that the farm exists and produces what has been stated in the registration. The conversation does not serve to confirm the holder's reality and is not a check of documents; no documents are requested in it. The Foundation does not speak with the other farm members under this Article.

(2) Where the holder is a user who came onto the call list after the registration, the conversation under this Article and the conversation after which a Kolo member introduces them into the Kolo (Article 12 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality) are held as a single conversation. The Kolo member who calls them from the call list then also records that the conversation for the purpose of approving the farm has been held, and whether after it there are grounds for suspicion under paragraph 4. That Kolo member is responsible for the introduction into the Kolo, under the Rulebook on Proof of Reality; the Foundation decides on the approval of the farm.

(3) The telephone number from the registration (Article 5 paragraph 1 point 9) is accessible only to the Foundation, is used exclusively for this conversation and is deleted when the Foundation decides on the registration. The Foundation records only that the conversation took place. A user leaves a telephone number for the call list separately, when they consent to the call (Article 9 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality).

(4) If the conversation cannot be held or if after it there are grounds to suspect that the declaration in the registration is untrue, the Foundation returns the registration for supplementation, stating the reasons.

## III — Farm Members

### Article 9 — Joining

(1) Only an adult user may be a farm member. A minor user may not be invited to join or join a farm.

(2) The farm admits members when, under Article 4, it may have members. Every farm member who is a Kolo member may invite another adult user whom they know personally to join the farm, upon registration or later; the member who issued the invitation is responsible for each person they invite.

(3) The invited person joins by accepting the invitation, with a declaration that they work on the farm permanently or occasionally and with their own first and last name, which is entered in the identity register in the manner under Article 5 paragraph 2. Joining is not approved until the first and last name has been entered and until the condition under Article 10 has been met.

(4) The Foundation reviews joining after the approval of the farm in the manner under Article 7, without the conversation under Article 8.

(5) A person may be a member of only one farm.

### Article 10 — Condition for joining

(1) Joining is approved only when the invited person is a Kolo member, or a founder.

(2) An invited person who is a user or a member without confirmation may be confirmed by the member who invited them or by another Kolo member, or founder, who knows them personally (Article 4 and Article 5 paragraph 1 point 1 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality); that person is responsible for the confirmation as for any confirmation.

(3) Joining does not require that the invited person be placed on the call list.

### Article 11 — Management of the farm

All farm members who are Kolo members manage the farm on the platform equally: each of them posts listings on behalf of the farm, edits the farm's profile and, where under Article 4 the farm may have members, invites users whom they know personally to join the farm (Article 9). An applicant who is a user, or a member without confirmation, manages the farm in the same manner.

### Article 12 — Responsibility for the declaration of accuracy of data

(1) One person is responsible for the declaration of accuracy of the data from the registration (Article 5 paragraph 1 point 6): for a farm entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings, the holder, and for a farm not entered in that register, the applicant.

(2) If a farm entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings was registered by a member, the applicant is responsible for the declaration until the holder joins the farm; upon joining, the holder gives the declaration under Article 5 paragraph 1 point 6 and from then on is responsible for it.

(3) The data under Article 5 paragraph 1 points 1 to 3 are changed only by the person responsible for the declaration. The order of joining and a contribution already recorded are not changed thereby.

### Article 13 — Withdrawal and taking over the declaration

(1) A member may withdraw from the farm at any time. The POEN in their record remains theirs, and the contribution that had been noted for them is recorded under the conditions of Article 16. The contribution of a member who has withdrawn is not annulled or written off even when the farm later terminates, except when the farm terminates because of a false declaration (Article 20).

(2) When the person responsible for the declaration of accuracy of data withdraws from the farm, requests the deletion of a farm that also has other members (Article 21 paragraph 3), or their status as a user terminates, the next member in the order of joining automatically receives a request to take over the declaration under Article 5 paragraph 1 point 6. If they take it over, they are responsible for it from then on.

(3) If the member refuses it or does not respond within 30 days of the request, the request is addressed to the next member in the order of joining, under the same conditions. The request contains a notice that the farm terminates if no member takes over the declaration, and that the farm contribution is then annulled, or written off, for the members (Article 23).

(4) If no member takes over the declaration, or if the farm no longer has any members, the farm terminates.

### Article 14 — Cessation of the confirmed status of a farm member

(1) When the confirmed status of the holder or of another farm member ceases (Article 31 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality), they lose the rights under this Rulebook that belong to a Kolo member: to manage the farm (Article 11) and to invite users to join the farm (Article 9). They acquire the rights again once they have been confirmed.

(2) The farm continues to operate. The cessation of the confirmed status of one member does not change their status as a farm member, the order of joining, the responsibility for the declaration under Article 5 paragraph 1 point 6, or the contribution noted, or recorded, for them in respect of the farm; the other farm members who are Kolo members retain all rights under Article 11.

(3) When a farm member also ceases to belong to the Kolo (Article 31 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality), the farm contribution recorded for them remains, and the noted contribution is recorded when the member is in the Kolo again (Article 16 paragraph 1 point 2). The annulment of a confirmation or of an introduction into the Kolo does not annul the farm contribution.

## IV — Farm Contribution

### Article 15 — Noting the contribution

(1) When the Foundation approves the farm, the contribution is noted for the farm members in the order of joining: 5,000 POEN for the first member, and 2,000 POEN each for the second to the sixth member. The contribution is noted for each member separately.

(2) The order of joining is determined by the moment of accepting the invitation; the applicant is the first member.

(3) For a member who joined before approval, the contribution is noted at the moment of the farm's approval, and for a member who joins after approval, when the Foundation approves their joining.

(4) For one farm, the contribution is noted for at most six members. A place is not freed by the withdrawal of a member nor by the annulment, or write-off, of their contribution; for the seventh member in order and any further members, no contribution is noted.

### Article 16 — Recording the contribution

(1) The noted contribution is recorded in the member's record, equally for all members, when both conditions are met:

1) the member has published a listing in which they offer a good or a service and which meets the content minimum, on behalf of the farm or a personal one; the condition is also met by a listing published before joining, and also when it is no longer published, except for a listing removed because of a breach of the Terms of Use;

2) the member is in the Kolo.

(2) If a contribution for the first listing under Article 38 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System has already been recorded for the member, the farm contribution is recorded as soon as the condition under paragraph 1 point 2 is met.

### Article 17 — Contribution for one farm

(1) A person has the farm contribution for at most one farm.

(2) A person who withdraws from a farm that continues to exist retains the contribution noted, or recorded, for them in respect of that farm, and does not acquire it again by joining another farm.

(3) A person whose contribution was annulled, or written off, upon the termination of a farm may acquire it again, under the conditions of Article 24.

## V — Listings on Behalf of a Farm and the Map of Local Producers

### Article 18 — Listings on behalf of a farm

(1) When posting a listing, a farm member chooses whether to post it on behalf of the farm or as a personal listing.

(2) A listing posted on behalf of an approved farm carries the DOMAĆE mark (Article 20 of the Terms of Use) and leads to the farm's profile. It carries the mark as long as the farm is approved and as long as the person who posted the listing is a farm member.

(3) The update of the POEN ledger on account of an exchange under a listing on behalf of a farm is made in the record of the member who posted the listing.

### Article 19 — Map of local producers

(1) An approved farm is displayed on the public map, at its exact location, with its name, description, address and listings on behalf of the farm. The address is written out alongside the point on the basis of the statement under Article 5 paragraph 1 point 3; the display of the address is part of the participation requested by the applicant and lasts as long as the farm exists on the platform. Display on the map is a condition of the farm contribution.

(2) Sponsors under the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations are also displayed on the same map.

(3) Farm members are displayed under their pseudonyms. The BPG is not displayed to other users. The first and last name of members is not displayed to other users, nor alongside the farm.

(4) The point is removed from the map upon the termination of the farm, on any ground under Article 22.

## VI — False Declaration and Termination of the Farm

### Article 20 — False declaration

(1) A false declaration includes in particular the registration of a farm that does not exist or does not produce what has been stated, the registration of another person's farm without the knowledge of its members, and joining a farm by a person who does not work on it.

(2) When the Foundation establishes that a declaration is false, it annuls the farm's approval. Upon annulment of the approval the farm terminates, and the farm contribution that had been noted, or recorded, for all persons who were farm members in the period to which the false declaration relates, including those who have since withdrawn from the farm, is annulled, or written off, under Article 23.

(3) Exclusion, under the procedure set out in Article 32 of the Terms of Use, is imposed only on those persons under paragraph 2 whom the Foundation establishes to have made the false declaration or to have known of it: the person who registered the farm or is responsible for the declaration of accuracy of data where that declaration is false, a member whose declaration upon joining is false, and a person who knew of the false declaration. For the other persons under paragraph 2, the contribution is annulled, or written off, under Article 23, without exclusion.

### Article 21 — Deletion of the farm

(1) The person who, under Article 12, is responsible for the declaration of accuracy of data may delete the farm from the platform if the farm has no other members and has no agreement on sponsorship in goods and services with unused vouchers.

(2) Before deletion, the person is informed of the consequences under Article 22 paragraph 2 and Article 23, including the number of POEN that will be written off from them. Upon deletion, listings on behalf of the farm also cease to be displayed.

(3) The person under paragraph 1 may not delete a farm that also has other members. When such a person requests deletion, the request has the effect of their withdrawal from the farm: the other members, in the order of joining, receive a request to take over the declaration, in the manner and within the time limit under Article 13. The farm terminates only if no member takes over the declaration.

### Article 22 — Grounds for termination of the farm

(1) An approved farm terminates:

1) when no member takes over the declaration of accuracy of data, or when the farm no longer has any members (Article 13 paragraph 4 and Article 21 paragraph 3);

2) upon deletion from the platform (Article 21 paragraph 1);

3) upon annulment of the approval because of a false declaration (Article 20).

(2) Upon the termination of the farm, the point is removed from the map, and the name, description, address, location and BPG are deleted.

### Article 23 — Annulment and write-off of the contribution upon termination

(1) Upon the termination of the farm, on any ground under Article 22 paragraph 1, the farm contribution that had been noted for the members is annulled, and the farm contribution that had been recorded for them is written off, on the ground under Article 17 paragraph 2 point 6 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. The write-off is executed even where the record thereby becomes negative; each person returns solely what was recorded to them, and the burden is not transferred to another person.

(2) In the case of termination under Article 22 paragraph 1 points 1 and 2, a farm member within the meaning of paragraph 1 is the person who is a farm member at the moment of termination, and the contribution of a member who withdrew earlier is not affected (Article 13 paragraph 1). In the case of termination because of a false declaration, a farm member also includes a person who was a farm member in the period to which the false declaration relates, including where they have since withdrawn (Article 20 paragraph 2).

(3) A contribution recorded under Article 38 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System is not written off upon the termination of the farm.

(4) The annulment and write-off under this Article are not a measure against a farm member, but the cessation of recognition of a contribution that was tied to the existence of the farm. The only measure against a person is exclusion under Article 20 paragraph 3.

(5) Every affected person is notified of the termination of the farm, of the annulment of the noted contribution and of the number of POEN written off from them.

### Article 24 — Acquiring the contribution again

A person whose contribution was annulled, or written off, upon the termination of a farm may register a farm or join a farm and acquire the farm contribution again, under the conditions of Articles 4, 9, 10, 15 and 16. This does not apply to a person on whom exclusion has been imposed (Article 20 paragraph 3).

### Article 25 — Complaint

A complaint against a decision on the registration of a farm, on joining a farm and on the annulment of the farm's approval, as well as against the termination of the farm and against the annulment and write-off of the farm contribution, may be lodged under Article 40 of the Terms of Use.

## VII — Final Provisions

### Article 26 — Entry into force

This Rulebook enters into force on the date of its adoption.
