# Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1 — Subject matter

This Rulebook governs the parameters for recording financial contributions in the KOLO system, specifically: donation tiers for natural persons and the donation recording coefficient, the initiation and confirmation of sponsorship recording for legal entities, entrepreneurs and holders of registered agricultural holdings, the recording of sponsors' contributions under the same table, the sponsor ranking list, as well as sponsorship in goods and services.

### Article 2 — Definitions

Terms used in this Rulebook have the meaning established in the Rulebook on the KOLO System. In addition, the following terms are used in this Rulebook:

**Donation recording coefficient** — the number by which the recorded contribution of a donor, whether a natural person or a sponsor, is increased when determining the number of POEN. It is determined by the donation tier. It is not a price, not an exchange rate, and not an accounting coefficient within the meaning of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

**Donation tier** — the level of a donor's cumulative contribution that determines the donation recording coefficient.

**Sponsor's cumulative contribution** — the sum of all confirmed monetary contributions by the sponsor up to a given moment.

**Sponsorship application** — a request submitted by an adult user on the platform to initiate the recording of a sponsorship contribution.

**Sponsorship in goods and services** — the obligation of a legal entity, an entrepreneur or a holder of a registered agricultural holding to hand over goods, or to provide services, free of charge to persons designated by the Foundation, governed by Chapter IVa of this Rulebook.

**Giver** — a legal entity, entrepreneur or holder of a registered agricultural holding that has assumed an obligation under sponsorship in goods and services.

**Voucher** — a document in electronic form by which the giver promises the holder the handover of a precisely specified good, or the provision of a precisely specified service, within a set period and at a set place. A voucher is not issued for a monetary amount.

## II — Donations by Natural Persons

### Article 3 — Right to donate

Any system user may donate money to the Foundation in dinars or another currency. Verification in the chain of confirmations is not a condition for donating, nor for recording a contribution on that basis. A donation does not confirm reality. A public donation of at least 5,000 dinars, made in a single payment, places the user on the call list under the conditions of Article 4a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality; after a conversation, a Kolo member may introduce them into the Kolo, by verification in the chain of confirmations. The donation and the contribution recorded on its basis do not depend on the call.

A donation is made by payment to the Foundation's account. The Foundation issues a donation receipt upon the donor's request, in accordance with the law. The tax treatment of a donation depends on the donor's status, the Foundation's registered status, and the applicable tax regulations at the time of the donation.

A donation is executed exclusively by a transfer of funds from account to account of the Foundation, or by a payment instrument issued in the donor's name. The Foundation does not accept cash.

The contribution is recorded exclusively in the record of the user with whose funds the payment was made. A payment made by another person is not recorded as the contribution of the user to whose reference number it was made. A payment by a person who is not a user of the system carries no recording of POEN — the funds are accepted as a donation and used to pursue the aims of the Foundation.

The payer's details from the bank statement are kept with the donation record and with the record of the POEN recorded. In card payments the donor declares that the payment instrument is in their name.

Where a donation has been made in a foreign currency, the dinar amount credited to the Foundation's account is authoritative. The contribution is recorded according to that amount, not the amount the donor sent; the difference arising from conversion and payment transaction costs is not recorded.

Receipt of a donation is established by the Foundation, by comparing the payer's details with the user's account. The payment intermediary's report of a completed card charge is not an establishment of receipt but a ground for entering the payment into that procedure; the contribution is not recorded at the moment of the charge.

With each donation, the donor chooses whether the donation is public or anonymous. By making a public donation, the donor agrees that their first and last name be publicly displayed in the donation list on the platform, and POEN is recorded under Article 4 only for a public donation. An anonymous donation does not display the donor's name, and no POEN is recorded for it. The choice is made for each donation separately and applies to donations made after the entry into force of these provisions.

### Article 4 — Donation tiers and recording coefficient

The larger the user's cumulative contribution to the common good, the higher the donation recording coefficient — the next contribution is recorded in a larger number of POEN.

The tier is determined on the basis of the sum of all of the user's previous donations and the new donation. The recording coefficient for the newly reached tier applies to the entire new donation.

| **Tier** | **Cumulative donation (RSD)** | **Recording coefficient** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 | 1.00 |
| 2 | 5,000 | 1.10 |
| 3 | 10,000 | 1.20 |
| 4 | 20,000 | 1.30 |
| 5 | 50,000 | 1.40 |
| 6 | 100,000 | 1.50 |
| 7 | 200,000 | 1.60 |
| 8 | 500,000 | 1.70 |
| 9 | 1,000,000 | 1.80 |
| 10 | 2,000,000 | 1.90 |
| 11 | 5,000,000 | 2.00 |

The number of POEN recorded equals the donation amount in dinars multiplied by the recording coefficient.

The table continues without limit. Each subsequent tier begins at the next threshold in the 1–2–5 sequence (10,000,000, 20,000,000, 50,000,000 dinars and so on), and the recording coefficient increases by 0.10 with each subsequent tier.

The tier is cumulative and permanent — it does not decrease through the use of POEN or through any other user activity.

The tier attained is a permanent recognition of a deed performed, not an acquired status. It cannot be lost, cannot be transferred to another person and confers no right against the Foundation.

The recording coefficient expresses the regard in which a contribution to the common good is held. A larger single contribution has greater significance for the community than the same amount spread out over time, because a rounded sum makes it possible to meet a concrete need of the community at once — to pay for infrastructure, to launch a programme, or to carry out a project. The community therefore holds such a contribution in higher regard.

The recording coefficient is not a price of POEN and is not a discount on a donation. Nothing is acquired by a donation: the donor acquires no good, no claim against the Foundation, no right to a refund, no right to redemption of POEN, and no influence in decision-making on the rules of the system. Since there is no object of acquisition, the difference in the coefficient does not constitute more favourable terms of acquisition but a greater measure of recognition of the contribution.

A donation confers no right to goods from a collective procurement, secures no position in the collection order, and creates no claim against the Foundation on that basis. The right to participate in a procurement arises from the decision initiating it, while the recorded contribution is the criterion by which precedence is determined (Article 19 of the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement).

### Article 5 — Legal qualification of donation recording

POEN is recorded exclusively for public donations within the meaning of Article 3. For anonymous donations, no POEN is recorded and they do not count toward the cumulative donation tier under Article 4.

The recording of POEN on account of a donation is an automatic act of the Protocol within the meaning of Art. 39 and Art. 73 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. It does not count towards the daily issuance limit of the Protocol Programs.

The automatism concerns the amount, not the initiation. The Protocol determines the number of POENs without discretion, pursuant to Article 4 of this Rulebook; the recording itself is initiated only after the Foundation has established receipt of the donation within the meaning of Article 3. A donation is therefore not a way to acquire POEN at the moment it is needed.

Recorded POEN have the same status as all other POEN in the system.

### Article 5a — Donation list and public recognition of donors

The platform maintains a list of donations by natural persons, available to adult users. A donor who has made a public donation is identified in the list by first and last name, together with the amount and date of the donation. The donor's pseudonym is not displayed in the list; it is visible only in the overview of POEN transactions, which is available to participants. The first and last name are recorded with the donation at the moment it is registered and are not altered by subsequent profile changes. Anonymous donors are not identified in the list.

Publication of the donor's name serves the transparency of the Foundation's handling of funds and the verification of the basis on which POEN was recorded. The total number of recorded POEN is public and the sum of all records in the Protocol equals zero; a donation that carried POEN but could not be attributed to any person would be a recording that cannot be verified. For that reason POEN is not recorded for an anonymous donation.

The donor is aware that publicly linking their name to a donation makes it possible to connect their pseudonymous record in the system with their identity. The donor chooses whether to donate publicly or anonymously; the Foundation accepts both forms of donation equally and uses them for the same aims. The absence of POEN recording for an anonymous donation follows from the reason set out in paragraph 2 of this Article, and not from the withholding of consent.

The donor may at any time withdraw consent to the publication of their first and last name, separately for each public donation. Upon withdrawal, the first and last name are removed from the list and deleted from the donation record, while the donation remains in the list with its amount and date. In the same way, the first and last name are removed from the list and deleted upon the cessation of the status of user. Withdrawal does not delete the first and last name from the identity register. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of publication up to the withdrawal nor POEN already recorded, because the basis of the recording was verified at the moment of recording, and the donation remains attributed to the donor's account after withdrawal as well. Payment details and the donation agreement under Article 5b are not published, and the Foundation keeps them in accordance with accounting regulations. Withdrawal does not cover what third parties may already have recorded outside the Platform.

The first and last name of a donor who makes a public donation is also entered in the identity register under Article 31a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, if it has not already been entered alongside the account and if it matches the name of the payer from the bank statement. That entry is not displayed to other users, except to the Kolo member who calls the donor from the call list in order to get acquainted, under the conditions of Article 4a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality; it does not depend on publication in the list of donations, and is deleted upon the termination of the user's status. An anonymous donation does not enter a name in the identity register.

### Article 5b — Donation agreement

For every donation by a natural person the Foundation draws up a donation agreement and delivers it to the donor through the Platform. The agreement is concluded by payment of the donation; the donor's signature is not required. The agreement is drawn up in electronic form at the moment the Foundation establishes receipt of the payment and accepts the donation.

The agreement contains the amount of the donation, a statement that the donation is voluntary and without consideration, a statement that through the donation the donor acquires no good or service, no claim against the Foundation, no right to a refund, no right to redeem POEN and no influence over decisions on the rules of the system, the Foundation's obligation to use the donation solely for the aims for which it was established, and the number of POEN recorded on account of the donation together with a note that this recording is not a counter-performance.

The agreement does not contain a statement on the origin of funds. The identification of the payer and the verification of the origin of funds are carried out by the bank through which the donation was made (Article 13b paragraph 1 point 3).

The text of the agreement is kept with the donation record as it stood at the moment the donation was accepted and is not altered by subsequent amendments to this Rulebook.

The donor may access the agreement at any time through the Platform and may print it.

## III — Sponsorship

### Article 6 — Sponsorship

Sponsorship is a donation of money by a legal entity, an entrepreneur or a holder of a registered agricultural holding to the Foundation, or sponsorship in goods and services under Chapter IVa of this Rulebook. The sponsor is not a system user and receives neither POEN nor ZRNO.

The relationship between the Foundation and the sponsor is governed by a donation agreement.

### Article 6a — Entry of the sponsor in the sponsor register

Before the first sponsorship application, in money or in goods and services, an adult system user enters the legal entity, entrepreneur or registered agricultural holding in the sponsor register, from their own account. Verification is not a condition for the entry nor for an application for sponsorship in money. A minor user does not make an entry.

The entry of a legal entity or entrepreneur contains: the name, the legal form (company, entrepreneur, association, cooperative, institution or other legal entity), registered office address, registration number and tax identification number; an email address and a telephone number for contact; the place and address at which the sponsor is displayed on the map, with a statement that it consents to the address being displayed; the first and last name of the user making the entry and their relationship to the sponsor — legal representative, entrepreneur, owner, one of the owners, beneficial owner or a person authorised by the competent body of the sponsor; the logo. The legal form does not affect the calculation of the contribution. A registered agricultural holding is entered exclusively by its holder, stating their first and last name, the name and number of the holding, contact details and the logo; the Foundation may request a certificate of entry of the holding in the register where there is a reason to do so. The logo is not mandatory; a sponsor who does not submit one is shown by name only.

A user who is not the legal representative, the entrepreneur or the holder of the holding declares that they are authorised to conclude donation agreements on behalf of the sponsor. Where the sponsor is an association or a cooperative, the user, including the legal representative, declares that the competent body of the sponsor has authorised them to make the entry and to conclude donation agreements on behalf of the sponsor. The user confirms that they are aware that the contribution under the applications they submit is recorded in their record, that the Foundation does not distribute it among the owners, members or bodies of the sponsor, and that they are obliged to inform the owners, if they are not the sole owner, and, in the case of an association, a cooperative, an institution or another legal entity, the competent bodies and the members of the sponsor, of the agreements and of the recorded contribution. The platform records the declaration and the confirmation.

The Foundation compares the name, the legal form, the registration number and the representative with the data of the register in which the sponsor is entered, records that the comparison was carried out, and approves or rejects the entry with a statement of reasons.

The same legal entity, entrepreneur or holding is entered once, and the entry is maintained by the user who made it. A change of the name, the registration number or the logo is subject to a renewed check by the Foundation; contact details are changed without a check.

The display of the sponsor on the map lasts as long as the sponsor is entered. The user who maintains the entry may delete it if the sponsor has no agreement on sponsorship in goods and services with unredeemed vouchers. Upon deletion, the display on the map and the offer of vouchers cease, while the contributions made up to the deletion remain on the ranking list, with the name and logo. At the request of the user who maintained the entry, the sponsor is also removed from the ranking list. The agreements and the sponsor register under Article 9 are kept in accordance with accounting regulations.

From an approved entry, the user submits the sponsorship applications under Article 7 and Article 13e of this Rulebook. The ban list under Article 13j is tied to the entry.

### Article 7 — Initiation of sponsorship

Sponsorship in money is initiated by the user who entered the sponsor in the sponsor register (Article 6a), by submitting a sponsorship application from the approved entry.

The application contains the amount of the donation in dinars. The minimum amount of a sponsorship application is 10,000 dinars.

The first and last name of the user who submitted the application is entered in the identity register under Article 31a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, alongside their account, upon confirmation of receipt of the donation, once the Foundation has carried out the comparison under Article 6a, paragraph 4 upon entry of the sponsor, and for a registered agricultural holding once the Foundation has approved the entry. If a different first and last name is already entered for the account, the application is not submitted until the discrepancy has been resolved. The first and last name of the applicant are not displayed to other users, except to the Kolo member who calls the applicant from the call list in order to get acquainted, under the conditions of Article 4a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality.

Upon submission of the application, the platform automatically generates a donation agreement between the entered sponsor, represented by the user who submitted the application, and the Foundation. For a registered agricultural holding, the agreement is concluded by the holder of the holding, stating the name and number of the holding. The donation agreement does not contain provisions on POEN or on the recording of POEN.

### Article 8 — Signing and performance

The applicant signs the generated donation agreement on behalf of the legal entity or entrepreneur, or as the holder of a registered agricultural holding. The agreement is concluded electronically on the platform, in the manner established by Article 13g, paragraph 4 of this Rulebook: the applicant confirms that they have read the agreement, that they are authorised to conclude it and that they consent to its conclusion on the platform, and enters a one-time code delivered to their email address. The platform records the time of conclusion and the checksum of the text and delivers a copy of the agreement to the giver and to the applicant. No POEN is recorded upon signing. The donation is performed by payment into the Foundation account. The holder of a registered agricultural holding pays the donation from the account of the holding or from their own account.

Upon receipt of the donation, the Foundation confirms receipt on the platform. Following confirmation, the Protocol records POEN in the applicant's record in accordance with Article 10 of this Rulebook. Confirmation of receipt does not confirm the applicant's reality. Where the applicant is a user, upon confirmation of receipt of sponsorship in money they are placed on the call list under the conditions of Article 4a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality; after a conversation, a Kolo member may introduce them into the Kolo, by verification in the chain of confirmations. The sponsorship and the contribution recorded on its basis do not depend on the call.

If the donation has not been received or the value does not correspond, the Foundation rejects the confirmation with reasons. The applicant may submit a new application.

### Article 9 — Sponsor register

The Foundation maintains a sponsor register in which it records all confirmed applications, including data on the sponsor (legal entity, entrepreneur or registered agricultural holding), the type and value of the donation, the date of confirmation, and the pseudonym of the user in whose record the entry was made.

On the basis of the register, the Foundation determines each sponsor's cumulative contribution by summing the values of all confirmed applications.

### Article 10 — Sponsorship recording coefficient

A sponsor's contribution is recorded under the table in Article 4 of this Rulebook, with the same tiers and the same recording coefficients as a donation by a natural person. The legal form of the giver does not affect the recording coefficient.

The tier is determined on the basis of the sponsor's cumulative contribution and the new donation. The recording coefficient of the newly reached tier applies to the entire new donation. The number of POEN recorded equals the donation amount in dinars multiplied by that coefficient. The tier is cumulative and permanent.

The sponsor's cumulative contribution and the user's cumulative donation under Article 4 are kept separately and are not added together, including where the sponsor's contribution is recorded in the record of a user who also donates personally. The cumulative contribution of a registered agricultural holding is kept for the holding, separately from the personal donations of its holder and from the cumulative contribution of a legal entity or entrepreneur with which the holder is connected.

POEN recorded under previously applicable sponsorship coefficients is not annulled. The cumulative contribution of existing sponsors is carried over and continues to serve as the basis for determining the tier.

### Article 11 — Legal qualification of sponsorship recording

The recording of POEN on account of sponsorship is an automatic act of the Protocol within the meaning of Art. 40 and Art. 73 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. It does not count against the daily emission limit of the Protocol Programme.

The contribution is recorded in the record of the user who submitted the sponsorship application. The contribution from a single application is recorded in the record of a single user and is not distributed among other owners or other persons connected with the sponsor, including where the sponsor has several owners. On account of the contribution the sponsor acquires no POEN, no ZRNO, no claim against the Foundation, no right to a refund of the donation and no other right.

The recording of POEN is not a payment from the legal entity, entrepreneur or holding to the user, is not a distribution of profit or any other form of transfer of value from the sponsor's assets, and creates no property right. POEN has no value outside the system (Art. 12 and Art. 13 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

### Article 12 — Tax obligations of the sponsor

Tax obligations arising from the donation — including value added tax, corporate income tax, and other public levies — are borne by the sponsor in accordance with the applicable regulations.

The Foundation does not provide tax advice to sponsors. The sponsor is responsible for the compliance of their donations with tax and other regulations.

### Article 13 — Sponsor ranking list

The platform maintains a public sponsor ranking list based on the cumulative contribution value from the sponsor register. The ranking list is kept in two separate parts: according to monetary contributions and according to the value of sponsorship in goods and services. In both parts, the sponsor's name and the amount in dinars are published; the number of POEN is not published.

A sponsor is identified on the ranking list by their full registered name, abbreviated name, or trade name under which they operate, according to the data entered by the user in the sponsorship application. The registered office address, the registration number, the tax identification number, the number of the holding and the representative, or the holder of the holding, are not published on the ranking list.

Publication of the sponsor ranking list constitutes public recognition of contributions and an element of transparency in the Foundation's work. The Foundation may publicly name a sponsor and publicly thank them.

The record of sponsors contains the sponsor's name and its logo, where one was submitted upon entry (Article 6a). The name and logo are displayed on the ranking list and, in the case of sponsorship in goods and services, also on the vouchers and alongside the offer of vouchers. On the map, the sponsor is displayed with its name, logo, place and address, alongside the offer of vouchers. Nothing else is displayed besides this.

On account of the contribution the sponsor acquires no right to a link to their website, to advertising space on the Platform, to being mentioned in the Foundation's campaigns or to any other promotional performance, and cannot contract for or demand such performance. Public recognition is an act of the Foundation, not a counter-performance for the contribution.

Neither a donation nor a sponsor's contribution gives any advantage in the selection of an offer in a collective procurement or in any other procedure of the Foundation. A sponsor who is also a bidder participates on an equal footing with the other bidders, under the conditions of the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement.

## IV — Prevention of money laundering and the financing of terrorism

### Article 13a — Purpose and voluntariness

The Foundation is not an obliged entity within the meaning of the regulations on the prevention of money laundering and the financing of terrorism. POEN is neither digital assets nor a virtual currency, the Foundation provides no payment services and performs no activity on account of which it would acquire the status of an obliged entity.

The Foundation lays down the measures in this Chapter voluntarily, out of caution and in order to protect the integrity of the system. Laying them down constitutes neither an acknowledgement of the status of an obliged entity nor a basis for the obligations that the regulations prescribe for obliged entities.

The reason for laying them down voluntarily is structural. The Foundation receives dinar funds, keeps a record of contributions which is transferred between users within the system, and with those funds acquires goods which it makes available to users and support recipients; in addition, under sponsorship in goods and services the contribution is recorded to the applicant, while the good or service is given to the voucher holder directly by a third party — the giver (Chapter IVa). The Foundation pays no money to a user on any basis whatsoever, a donation is not returned, and POEN is neither redeemed nor converted, so there is no exit in money; but a structure of that kind may be abused, and the measures in this Chapter make abuse harder and make it visible.

### Article 13b — Measures

The Foundation applies the following measures:

1) a donation is executed exclusively cashlessly, in the manner laid down by Article 3 of this Rulebook;

2) the contribution is recorded exclusively in the record of the user with whose funds the payment was made, and the payer's details are kept with the donation record;

3) the identification of the payer and the verification of the origin of funds are carried out by the bank through which the donation was made, which is an obliged entity under the regulations on the prevention of money laundering and the financing of terrorism; the Foundation does not carry out its own verification of the origin of funds and does not require a statement on origin;

4) a donation from an account opened abroad is recorded once the Management Board has established the identity of the donor and the basis of the payment; the record of such a donation is marked separately;

5) the application of this Chapter is monitored by the director of the Foundation;

6) documentation on donations is kept for ten years from its creation, in accordance with the periods laid down by the accounting regulations.


### Article 13c — Action upon suspicion

Where there are grounds to suspect that a payment was made for the purpose of money laundering or the financing of terrorism, the Foundation does not record the contribution and records no POEN until the suspicion has been dispelled.

The Foundation notifies the competent authority of such a payment where the regulations so provide, and acts upon its order.

The measures under Articles 27 and 28 of the Terms of Use apply to a user who took part in the abuse.

Where POEN has already been recorded, its annulment is carried out exclusively on the grounds laid down by the Rulebook on the KOLO System. Exclusion of a user under Article 28 of the Terms of Use entails annulment of the record under Article 34 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. This Rulebook establishes no new ground for annulment.

## IVa — Sponsorship in goods and services

### Article 13d — Nature

Sponsorship in goods and services is established by a donation agreement between the giver and the Foundation, by which the giver undertakes to hand over goods, or to provide services, free of charge to persons who take them in the allocation under the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement.

The person to whom a voucher is issued acquires their own right to demand from the giver the handover of the good or the provision of the service.

The Foundation acquires neither ownership nor possession of the goods, does not provide the services, and is not liable for the performance of the giver's obligation. The giver is directly liable to the voucher holder for the quality, defects and safety of the good and the service.

### Article 13e — Application

The application for sponsorship in goods and services is submitted by the participant who entered the giver in the sponsor register (Article 6a), from the approved entry. For a registered agricultural holding, that is exclusively its holder.

The application contains: a precise description of the good or service; the number of vouchers; the unit price the giver applies in trade; a photograph of the giver's price list showing that price; the place and address of handover or provision.

In the application, the giver declares that it complies with the regulations applicable to trade in that good or to the provision of that service.

The first and last name of the applicant is entered in the identity register under the conditions of Article 7, paragraph 3 of this Rulebook, upon confirmation of receipt.

### Article 13f — Value and scope

The value of sponsorship in goods and services equals the number of vouchers multiplied by the unit price from the application.

The Foundation checks that the photograph of the price list has been attached. The Foundation does not appraise, compare or determine the price.

The subject of a voucher may not be goods and services referred to in Article 21 of the Terms of Use, goods whose advertising is prohibited or restricted by law — tobacco and related products, spirits and medicines — nor money and coupons or cards that can be exchanged for money. The Foundation checks this when reviewing the application. Vouchers are published and distributed by the Foundation, so these restrictions do not apply to listings that users themselves publish in the exchange space.

The value of a single agreement is at least 10,000 and at most 50,000 dinars.

The giver may conclude a subsequent agreement when at least half of the vouchers issued under the previous agreement have been redeemed. The expiry of the vouchers' validity period does not open the possibility of a subsequent agreement. The Foundation may, by its own decision and with a statement of reasons, permit a subsequent agreement even before half of the vouchers have been redeemed.

A voucher is valid for one year from issuance.

### Article 13g — Agreement

Upon acceptance of the application, the platform generates a donation agreement between the giver, represented by the person who submitted the application, and the Foundation. The text of the agreement is kept as it was at the moment of conclusion and is not altered by subsequent amendments to this Rulebook.

The agreement contains: the data on the giver and the contact details; the first and last name of the applicant and their relationship to the giver; a description of the good or service; the number of vouchers; the value under Article 13f; the validity period, the place and the address; a statement that the donation is voluntary and free of charge and that through it the giver acquires no claim against the Foundation and no advantage in any procedure of the Foundation; the giver's obligation to keep the goods available, or to secure appointments, until the expiry of the period, and to provide the good and the service in the same quality as to other persons; the right of the voucher holder under Article 13d, paragraph 2; the giver's obligation to issue, upon handover or provision, the invoice prescribed for that transaction, where one is prescribed.

The agreement does not contain provisions on POEN or on the recording of POEN.

The agreement is concluded electronically on the platform. The applicant signs it by confirmation on the platform, after confirming that they have read the agreement and that they are authorised to conclude it, and entering a one-time code delivered to their email address. An agreement so concluded is valid without a seal or handwritten signature. The platform records the time of conclusion and the checksum of the text and delivers to the giver a copy of the agreement for printing.

Upon signing of the agreement, the platform issues the vouchers.

The giver may designate on the platform participants who confirm, on the giver's behalf, the handover of the good or the provision of the service under a voucher, and may revoke that authorisation at any time. Such a user confirms vouchers through their own account and acquires no other authority in connection with the agreement; the giver is responsible for their confirmations.

### Article 13h — Recording

Sponsorship in goods and services is received within the meaning of Article 15 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System when the agreement has been signed and the vouchers issued. The Foundation then confirms receipt, and the Protocol records POEN in the record of the person who submitted the application.

The number of POEN recorded equals the value under Article 13f multiplied by the recording coefficient corresponding to the tier the sponsor has reached through its cumulative monetary contribution (Article 10). A sponsor without a monetary contribution is recorded with a coefficient of 1.00.

The value of sponsorship in goods and services does not enter the sponsor's cumulative contribution and does not affect the tier.

The recording is an automatic act of the Protocol and does not count against the daily emission limit of the Protocol Programme.

### Article 13i — Legal qualification

Article 11 of this Rulebook applies mutatis mutandis to recording under this Chapter.

The recording of POEN is not consideration for goods or services. The Foundation does not receive the goods and services, and the voucher holder receives them from the giver without any performance towards the giver or the Foundation. The recording coefficient is not a price, not an exchange rate, and does not express the value of a good or service in POEN.

### Article 13j — Write-off upon non-performance

Where the Foundation, upon a complaint by the voucher holder (Article 37a of the Terms of Use), establishes that the giver did not hand over the good or did not provide the service, and did not remedy this within fifteen days of the Foundation's request, the agreement is terminated. The person who submitted the application has written off the number of POEN recorded under the agreement divided by the number of vouchers, for each voucher under that agreement that has not been redeemed by the termination, regardless of whether it has been issued to a user. The amount written off also includes the increase by the recording coefficient. Vouchers under a terminated agreement lapse, and for the holders of issued unredeemed vouchers the annulment is corrected in accordance with the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement.

A giver whose agreement has been terminated is entered on the ban list and may not conclude a new agreement on sponsorship in goods and services until the Foundation removes it from that list.

The write-off is executed even where the record thereby becomes negative (Article 14, paragraph 3, item 7 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). The burden is not transferred to another person. The person who submitted the application receives their own notification of the write-off.

Without termination, no write-off is made: for a voucher that has not been issued to a user; for a voucher that the holder did not redeem before the expiry of the period; nor where the good was handed over or the service provided but the holder is not satisfied with the quality — in that case the holder exercises their rights against the giver under Article 13d.

### Article 13k — Exclusion of connected persons

The vouchers of a single giver may not be taken by the person who submitted the application, the legal representative and the owners of the giver, or by persons connected with them by kinship, marriage, cohabitation or a common household. When taking a voucher, a user declares that they are not such a person.

### Article 13l — Tax obligations of the giver

Tax obligations arising from sponsorship in goods and services are borne by the giver in accordance with the regulations. The Foundation does not provide tax advice.

## V — Allocation of Surplus

### Article 14 — Allocation rule

When dinar inflows from donations and sponsorship exceed the Foundation's operating costs, the surplus is directed to system programmes.

The rules for allocating the surplus are established by the founder of the Foundation and the Foundation in Phase 1, and by the Upper Kolo in Phase 2, in accordance with the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

## VI — Amendments and Final Provisions

### Article 15 — Authority to amend

The table in Art. 4 of this Rulebook, including the addition of new tiers, are amended by the Management Board (UO) of the Foundation in Phase 1, and by the Upper Kolo in Phase 2, in accordance with the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

Amendments to parameters take effect upon publication on the platform and apply to donations and sponsor contributions received after the entry into force.

### Article 16 — Entry into force

This Rulebook enters into force on the date of its adoption and is published on the Foundation's website and on the KOLO platform.
