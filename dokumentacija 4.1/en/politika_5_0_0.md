# Privacy Policy of the KOLO Platform

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1 — Subject Matter

(1) By this Policy the KOLO Foundation informs the persons whose data it processes about which personal data are processed on the KOLO Platform (hereinafter: the Platform), for which purposes, on which legal basis, who sees them, how long they are kept and which rights the person has, in accordance with the Law on Personal Data Protection ("Official Gazette of the RS", no. 87/2018; hereinafter: LPDP).

(2) Terms from the Rulebook on the KOLO System have the same meaning in this Policy.

(3) This Policy applies together with the Records of Processing Activities, in which the particulars required by Article 47 of the LPDP are kept for each processing activity.

### Article 2 — Controller

(1) The controller of personal data is the KOLO Foundation (hereinafter: the Foundation), Šetalište 16, 25000 Sombor, Republic of Serbia, registration number 28836627, tax identification number (PIB) 115840443.

(2) The Foundation determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data in the KOLO system. The KOLO Protocol is the technical means of processing. The data are stored on infrastructure provided by the Foundation, with processors with which the Foundation concludes a data processing agreement (Article 39).

(3) All questions about the processing of personal data and all requests for the exercise of rights under Article 43 are to be addressed to privatnost@ekolo.rs. This address also applies when the Foundation appoints a data protection officer.

(4) In the event of the dissolution of the Foundation, the infrastructure and the ledger are transferred only to a person that accepts the principles and limits of the common good (Article 48 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). Users are informed of the transfer and of the new controller before the transfer is carried out.

### Article 3 — Data Protection Officer

(1) The Foundation assesses whether it is obliged to appoint a data protection officer. The obligation exists where the controller carries out processing as a public authority, where its core activities consist of processing operations which, by virtue of their nature, scope or purposes, require regular and systematic monitoring of a large number of persons, or where its core activities consist of processing special categories of data on a large scale (Article 56 paragraph 2 of the LPDP).

(2) The Foundation has not appointed a data protection officer. The Foundation is not a public authority, and the number of persons whose data are processed and the scale of processing of special categories of data do not reach the threshold under Article 56 paragraph 2 of the LPDP. Until a data protection officer is appointed, the Director of the Foundation is responsible for data protection.

(3) The Foundation reviews the assessment at least once a year, upon every significant change in the number of users or in the scale of processing of special categories of data, and before introducing any new processing of personal data. When the obligation arises, the Foundation appoints a data protection officer, publishes their contact details and submits them to the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (hereinafter: the Commissioner).

(4) The data protection officer may not be a person who decides on the purposes and means of processing, because such an arrangement would not ensure the independence required by Article 56 paragraph 6 of the LPDP.

### Article 4 — To Whom the Policy Applies

(1) This Policy applies to:

1) users: all persons with an account on the Platform, regardless of status, including minor users;

2) visitors of the Platform who are not signed in;

3) persons who enter a sponsor in the register and submit a sponsorship application;

4) persons whose data are entered by a user and who are not themselves users: a parent whose email address is entered by a minor, a child whose date of birth is entered by an applicant for a support programme, and a person in the applicant's care.

(2) The statuses of users (user who is not in the Kolo, member without confirmation, Kolo member and Kolo keeper) and the position of a minor user are established by Articles 2, 28 and 52 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. What a user sees about others depends on their status (Chapter III).

(3) The Platform is intended for persons living in the Republic of Serbia: the place is selected from the register of settlements in Serbia, donations are received in dinars, and the Foundation is registered in Serbia. Translations of the Platform and of the acts exist for members of national minorities and foreigners living in Serbia, and not for offering services to persons in other states. Processing is therefore carried out under the LPDP.

### Article 5 — Principles of Processing

(1) Data protection in the KOLO system is based on data protection by design and data protection by default, in accordance with the LPDP.

(2) Records in the contribution ledger are tied to pseudonyms, not to personal names. The first and last name is kept only in the identity register (Article 24), separately from the contribution ledger. Pseudonymity is not anonymity: pseudonymised data remain personal data, because with additional information they can be linked to an identified person.

(3) The Platform collects only the data necessary for the functioning of the system. Data minimisation is a limit of the common good (Articles 10 and 15 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System) and cannot be abolished by any governance decision.

(4) The application is executed and the database is located in the European Union (Article 40).

## II — Account, Consent and Technical Data

### Article 6 — Account

(1) When an account is opened we process: the pseudonym you choose yourself, your email address, your password, which is kept exclusively in a form from which it cannot be read, the date the account was opened and the declaration of legal age. The following are also kept with the account: the selected language, the fact that you have confirmed your email address, your email notification settings, the notifications the Platform sends you and your earlier pseudonyms.

(2) An earlier pseudonym is kept so that links to your profile shared earlier lead to your account and so that another user does not take it over; it is deleted when you return to that pseudonym and upon closure of the account.

(3) If you sign in with a Google account, Google provides the Foundation with the email address, name and profile picture of that account; the name and picture are entered as voluntarily entered data (Article 7), which you may change or delete. The Foundation does not provide Google with data from the Platform.

(4) If you turn on notifications on your device, we process the subscription address provided by your browser and the browser designation; the subscription is deleted when you turn notifications off and upon closure of the account.

(5) Purpose of processing: maintaining the account, signing in to the Platform, communication with you and access security.

(6) Legal basis: execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP): by opening an account and accepting the Terms of Use you establish a contractual relationship with the Foundation.

(7) The pseudonym should not contain data by which you can be recognised.

(8) The data under this Article are kept for as long as the account exists; the consequences of termination of status are established by Article 42.

### Article 7 — Voluntarily Entered Data

(1) You may, at your own choice, enter your first and last name, telephone number, place, description and profile picture, and select the categories of listings you follow. Entering these data is not a condition for proof of reality or for access to the functions of the system.

(2) Your first and last name and telephone number are visible to participants only if you yourself choose this on your profile; you may withdraw that choice at any time, after which those data are no longer displayed to other users. Withdrawal does not affect anything third parties may already have recorded outside the Platform.

(3) Profile pictures and listing images are stored with the image storage processor (Article 39); only the internet address of the image is entered in the database.

(4) Purpose of processing: easier use of the Platform and communication among users, according to your choice.

(5) Legal basis: consent (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 1 of the LPDP). You may change or delete the data at any time, thereby withdrawing consent; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

(6) The place and telephone number you left for the call list may be changed, but not deleted, as long as the account exists (Article 16).

### Article 8 — Consent and Proof of Consent

(1) For processing whose legal basis is consent, as well as for the acceptance of the Terms of Use by which the contractual relationship is established, the Foundation records that consent has been given. The record contains what was accepted, when, the designation of the version of the act to which the acceptance relates and the text that was displayed to the user, in the language in which it was displayed.

(2) A published version of an act is not altered: an amended act receives a new designation, and the earlier one remains available in the form in which it was accepted. The record of consent therefore permanently refers to the text that was in force at that moment.

(3) The IP address, device data and other data by which the user is additionally identified are not recorded alongside the record of consent. Collecting such data for the purpose of proving consent would be an extension of processing contrary to the principle of minimisation (Article 5 paragraph 3).

(4) Acceptance of the Terms of Use and acceptance of this Policy are recorded separately, as two statements, because they relate to different matters.

(5) Consent may be withdrawn at any time, without giving reasons and as easily as it was given. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, so the record of the consent given is not deleted; instead, the moment of withdrawal is also recorded in it.

(6) You see the consents you have given and the text with which you gave them in your account and in the data export (Article 43).

(7) For consent to cookies that are not necessary (Article 10), the decision is recorded in the cookie itself, together with the version of the notice under which it was made; for a signed-in user it is also recorded with the account. For a visitor who is not signed in, no identifier whatsoever is established for the purpose of keeping that record, for the reason under paragraph 3.

(8) Purpose of processing: proving that consent has been given and that the contractual relationship has been established. Legal basis: the controller's legal obligation to prove consent (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 3 and Article 15 paragraph 1 of the LPDP).

(9) The record is kept for as long as the processing to which the consent relates lasts, and thereafter for as long as a legal claim may be asserted or contested on that basis.

### Article 9 — Technical Data and Logs

(1) For the security of the Platform, the IP address, device and browser data and the time of access are logged at infrastructure level.

(2) For a signed-in user, the Platform records in the activity log which pages they opened and when. No activity log is kept for a visitor who is not signed in. The activity log is accessible only to the Foundation's superadministrator.

(3) Actions of the administrator team on users' data are recorded in the audit log: who performed the action, when and which action. The audit log is accessible only to the Foundation's administrator team.

(4) Purpose of processing: security of the Platform, prevention of misuse, detection of unauthorised access and technical support.

(5) Legal basis: legitimate interest of the Foundation (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP). You have the right to object to this processing (Article 43).

(6) The data under paragraphs 1 to 3 are kept for at most 12 months; the activity log is also deleted upon closure of the account.

### Article 10 — Cookies and Traffic Measurement

(1) The Platform uses cookies necessary for signing in, maintaining the session and security. They are not subject to consent, because they are necessary for the provision of the service.

(2) The Platform also uses analytical cookies of the Google Analytics service (Google Ireland Limited or Google LLC), exclusively with the visitor's consent (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 1 of the LPDP), which is given before those cookies are set and may be withdrawn at any time through the cookie settings. Consent may be refused as easily as it is given; refusal or withdrawal does not affect the ability to use the Platform.

(3) The Google Analytics service is sent the addresses of the pages opened, without the pseudonym and other account designations, and usage events (registration, publication of a listing, contacting an advertiser, re-registration of POEN, confirmation, started donation), without the pseudonym, account identifier, amount of POEN or dinars and name of a support programme. Google determines the approximate location of the visitor from the IP address. The pages of the administrator team, of supervision, of the children's space and of the confirmation of the conditions of a support programme are not measured. Google Analytics is not loaded for a minor user. Google keeps these data for 14 months. The data may be transferred to the United States of America (Article 40).

(4) The Platform also measures traffic without cookies, with the Vercel Analytics service (Vercel Inc.), which sets no cookies, reads no data from the device and does not track the visitor across websites; only the aggregate number of visits to individual pages is processed. The legal basis is the legitimate interest of the Foundation in knowing how much the Platform is used (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP), and you have the right to object to this processing (Article 43).

## III — Contribution Ledger, Pijaca and Communication

### Article 11 — Contribution Ledger

(1) The Protocol keeps the contribution ledger: the POEN and ZRNO records, the amount and time of each update, the ground of the update and the pseudonyms of the parties. Free text that the parties themselves enter is also recorded with a re-registration of POEN. A noted contribution that has not yet been recorded is kept with your account.

(2) Purpose of processing: keeping the ledger of the common good and of the accounting framework of the system.

(3) Legal basis: execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP). The availability of the pseudonymous ledger to participants is a structural characteristic of the system (Article 56 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, Article 16 of the Terms of Use), and not an option that the user may disable; a user who does not accept it cannot use the Platform.

(4) Availability of the ledger:

1) a visitor who is not signed in sees the general indicators of the system;

2) a user who is not in the Kolo sees the amounts and timestamps of transactions, without the pseudonyms of the parties and without the state of the records;

3) a participant sees the pseudonyms of the parties in each transaction and the state of the records;

4) you always see your own records and your own noted contribution; a noted contribution is seen by no one else and is not included in public indicators until it is recorded.

(5) The exceptions to the display of individual transactions are:

1) a transaction in which either party is a minor user is not displayed to anyone other than the parties and the parent of the minor user, because the children's space is not public;

2) recording under a support programme is displayed in the manner under Article 27.

(6) Aggregate indicators of the state of the system and of concentration (Article 56 paragraph 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System) are calculated from the same data, without collecting new data about you. An indicator does not contain a pseudonym, does not relate to re-registrations between users and is not stated when it covers fewer than five persons. The daily snapshot of the indicators does not contain personal data.

(7) Even with pseudonymity, the combination of the amounts, time and frequency of transactions may in certain cases enable indirect identification. We inform you of this limitation before registration.

(8) The contribution ledger is kept for as long as the account exists. After termination of status, the numerical history is kept under a pseudonym for 10 years from termination of status (Article 42), after which the pseudonym is replaced by a designation that does not point to a person and the records remain permanently in the ledger as anonymous. The free text accompanying a re-registration is deleted upon termination of the status of either party, and a record description containing personal data is deleted when the basis for its processing ceases, while the record remains (Article 55 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

### Article 12 — Listings

(1) A listing contains the advertiser's pseudonym, the title and description of the good or service, the category, the amount, the place (a settlement from the register), photographs the advertiser attaches themselves and, at the advertiser's choice, a telephone number. A listing by a user who is not in the Kolo carries a mark that the advertiser is not in the Kolo.

(2) A listing is public: it is seen by all visitors of the Platform, including persons who are not signed in, and it is indexed by search engines. The advertiser's telephone number is accessible only to participants.

(3) For persons who are not signed in, the advertiser's pseudonym is not linked to the contribution ledger or to the profile, and for users who are not in the Kolo it is linked only to the restricted profile under Article 16 of the Terms of Use.

(4) A photograph of the person and the first and last name are neither requested nor needed; an advertiser who includes them in a listing publishes them on their own initiative.

(5) Purpose of processing: publishing an offer or a request for the purpose of exchange and establishing contact between users.

(6) Legal basis: the advertiser's consent (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 1 of the LPDP), given by publishing the listing, with a warning that the listing is public. Consent is withdrawn by removing the listing, without consequences for the advertiser's status; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

(7) A listing is kept until the advertiser removes it or until it is removed in accordance with the Terms of Use; upon removal it ceases to be publicly accessible, and the photographs are deleted from storage.

### Article 13 — Chat Room and Conversations

(1) Messages in the Chat Room are seen by all signed-in users of that Chat Room, together with the pseudonym, profile picture and status of the sender. Minor users have a separate Chat Room (Article 27 of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children). Messages in the Chat Room are deleted after 30 days.

(2) In a conversation between two users, the pseudonyms of the parties, the content of the messages, the time of sending, whether a message has been read and the listing in relation to which the conversation was started are processed. A user who is not in the Kolo starts a conversation themselves only with an advertiser in relation to a listing, at most three new conversations per day, so who started the conversation is recorded.

(3) A conversation is seen only by its parties. A parent reads their child's conversation with an adult user (Article 35). The Foundation does not read conversations; it has insight only for the purpose of removing disputed content under the Terms of Use. A notification of a new message sent by email contains an excerpt of the message.

(4) Purpose of processing: communication among users, above all for the purpose of arranging an exchange.

(5) Legal basis: execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP).

(6) Messages in conversations between users are deleted when at least one party closes their account, or when 24 months have passed since the last message in the conversation.

### Article 14 — Automated Processing

(1) The Protocol automatically:

1) records POEN on the basis of recorded contributions, according to the rules of the system;

2) calculates the accounting coefficient at the end of each accounting period, according to a public formula;

3) records POEN for support recipients under support programmes.

(2) This processing may affect your position in the system, so you have the right to request an explanation of the logic of the processing and human review of a specific outcome and to express your point of view (Article 38 of the LPDP). The request is addressed to the address under Article 2 paragraph 3.

(3) The flagging of an account in integrity monitoring (Article 19) and the establishment of the order in collective procurement (Article 28) are not decisions within the meaning of Article 38 of the LPDP: they produce no legal consequences, and every measure is taken by a human.

## IV — Proof of Reality

### Article 15 — Proof of Reality and the Record of the Chain of Confirmations

(1) The reality of a user is established exclusively in the chain of confirmations, by a confirmation or by an introduction into the Kolo (Article 4 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality). A payment, a sponsorship, a farm registration and an entry in the identity register do not confirm it. Proof of reality does not require personal identity documents, the identity card number or the unique citizen identification number.

(2) We process verification records (Article 18 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality): the pseudonym of the verifier and of the verified person, the sequence number of the verification, the time, the type of verification and, with an introduction into the Kolo, also who confirmed the introduced person, when and in what manner, as well as the supervisor's pseudonym and the data on supervision. The verification records make up the record of the chain of confirmations.

(3) After the verification, the verified person receives a notification with the verifier's pseudonym and may report that they do not know the verifier (Article 15 paragraph 5 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality).

(4) When you become a Kolo member, the Platform shows you the list of linked users from the record of the chain of confirmations (Article 17 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality); the list does not collect new data.

(5) Purpose of processing: establishing the reality and continuity of users and the integrity of the ledger of the common good.

(6) Legal basis: execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP).

(7) The record of the chain of confirmations, including the type of verification and the data on supervision, is not accessible to other users; the data on supervision are displayed neither to the verifier nor to the verified person.

(8) The record of the chain of confirmations is kept for as long as the account exists; upon termination of status, the user's links in it are pseudonymised (Article 42).

### Article 16 — Call List

(1) You get onto the call list under the conditions under Article 8 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality: as a user who does not know any Kolo member, when you yourself submit a conversation request or when you accept the Foundation's offer after a public donation, a sponsorship in money or a farm registration, or as a member without confirmation, by a request for a conversation for confirmation. A minor user does not get onto the list.

(2) Before you get onto the list, you yourself enter your first and last name, place and telephone number, and we inform you that a Kolo member, or a Kolo keeper, will call you and that they will see your first and last name and telephone number, as well as whether the same number or the same first and last name is kept with another account, together with the place of that account.

(3) We process: the pseudonym, the ground on which you are on the list, the first and last name (in the identity register, Article 24), the place, the telephone number, the text of the consent and the time of consent, and in the case of a conversation request also a short description of what you do and when you are available. While a call is open, with whom it is open and until when are processed. The Foundation records which Kolo member opened the call and when, as well as the moment at which the Kolo member, before the first opening, gave the statement of confidentiality (Article 10 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality).

(4) Purpose of processing: so that a Kolo member may call you in order to get acquainted, after which they may introduce you into the Kolo, or so that a Kolo keeper may call you for confirmation; the record of who opened the call serves to protect against misuse of your name and telephone number.

(5) Legal basis: explicit consent (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 1 of the LPDP), given when you yourself leave the data. You withdraw consent by withdrawing the request, without consequences for your status, the other functions of the account, the donation, the sponsorship, the farm and the contribution recorded on account of them. The record of who opened the call is kept on the basis of legitimate interest (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP), for the purpose of protecting persons on the list against misuse of their data, and you have the right to object to that processing (Article 43).

(6) The list, without first and last names and telephone numbers, is seen by Kolo members; a member without confirmation does not see it. A request by a member without confirmation is seen only by Kolo keepers. The first and last name and the telephone number are opened by a click, only to the Kolo member who opened the call and only while the call is open: for at most 10 minutes, with one Kolo member at a time; the Kolo member processes them under the authority of the Foundation, only for the purpose of that conversation. The record of who opened the call is seen only by the Foundation's administrator team. The first and last name and the telephone number do not enter notifications, email or the audit log. The telephone number from the list is not displayed in any other part of the Platform, except when you yourself turn on its display on your profile. If someone harasses you by misusing the number, report it to the Foundation.

(7) Getting onto the list does not confirm reality, and neither the donation, nor the sponsorship, nor the contribution recorded on account of them depends on it.

(8) The data from the list are deleted when you enter the Kolo, or when you are confirmed, when you withdraw the request and when your user status terminates. The first and last name, place and telephone number remain with the account for as long as it exists and are deleted upon its closure. The datum on the current opening is deleted when the call is closed. The record of which Kolo member opened the call is kept for as long as your account exists and is deleted upon its closure; it is not exported through the Platform, and you obtain it on request sent to privatnost@ekolo.rs (Article 43).

### Article 17 — Prevention of Multiple Accounts

(1) The first and last name and the telephone number from the call list are compared with the names and numbers kept with other accounts (Article 13 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality). Where they match, the Kolo member who opened the call sees an indication that a match exists and the place of the other account, but not its pseudonym; both accounts are seen only by the Foundation's administrator team. A match blocks nothing and takes no decision.

(2) When the Foundation establishes whether two accounts are accounts of the same person (Article 14 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality), it processes the data kept with both accounts (telephone number, first and last name, payer from the bank statement, place and chain of confirmations) and the decision with the statement of reasons and the amount of the write-off. Together with the decision, one supervision case is opened concerning the recipients of re-registrations from the account that ceases; the case contains only the pseudonyms of the recipients and the sum of the re-registrations.

(3) Purpose of processing: preventing the same person from running more than one account (Article 8 of the Terms of Use).

(4) Legal basis: legitimate interest of the Foundation (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP), because verification cannot confirm uniqueness. The interest outweighs the interference with your rights: the comparison blocks nothing and takes no decision, neither the name nor the pseudonym of the other account is disclosed to another user, and the decision is taken by a human, with a statement of reasons and the right to a complaint under Article 40 of the Terms of Use. You have the right to object to this processing (Article 43).

(5) The decision establishing a multiple account and the supervision case accompanying it are kept for as long as the account the person retained exists.

### Article 18 — Recommendations

(1) A Kolo member, or a founder, may recommend another adult user (Article 20 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality). We process who recommended whom and when; a recommendation contains no text, rating or reasoning.

(2) Only the number of recommendations received is displayed on the profile. Who recommended you is not displayed to anyone, not even to you. Whom you have recommended is seen only by you, on that user's profile, so that you can withdraw the recommendation.

(3) A recommendation does not affect your status, access to functions or the display of listings, and no decision whatsoever about you is taken on its basis.

(4) Purpose of processing: displaying the number of recommendations and preventing the same user from recommending the same user more than once. Legal basis: execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP).

(5) A recommendation is kept until the person who gave it withdraws it and is deleted upon termination of the status of the person who gave it or of the person who received it.

### Article 19 — Supervision of Verifications and Integrity of the System

(1) Kolo keepers supervise verifications under Articles 22 to 27 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality. The supervision outcome "needs review" or "disputed" forms a supervision case with the designation of the verification record, the outcomes, the subject of suspicion and the reason code. The supervision case is kept by the Foundation's administrator team; it is not accessible to other supervisors, the verifier, the verified person or the public.

(2) In order to prevent false verifications, the multiplication of accounts and other misuse that would devalue the ledger and voting, the Foundation analyses patterns in the record of the chain of confirmations and in the contribution ledger. This processing does not collect new data: it uses existing pseudonymous data and derives a risk record (labels of violated rules and a numerical score), accessible only to the Foundation's superadministrator. Flagging gives priority to the absence of genuine activity, and not to the density of connections, so that a close-knit real community is not wrongly treated.

(3) The closed group check (Article 26 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality), from data that are already processed, identifies groups of members without confirmation connected by introducing one another into the Kolo who exchange only within the group. The check produces no effect whatsoever on the user (it does not change their status, verification or records), but only shows the group to the Foundation, which may invite the members of the group to a conversation.

(4) Signals accompanying supervision are derived from data that are already kept and are not stored as a separate record.

(5) Purpose of processing: protecting the integrity of proof of reality, of the ledger of the common good and of voting.

(6) Legal basis: for the supervision of verifications: execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP), because supervision is an integral part of the verification mechanism; for the analysis of patterns: legitimate interest of the Foundation and of honest users (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP), to which you have the right to object (Article 43).

(7) A supervision case closed with a finding that there are no grounds for suspicion is deleted upon the expiry of 90 days from closure. An open risk record is kept until a human reviews it, and a reviewed one for at most 12 months. The supervision outcome shares the fate of the verification record.

## V — Donations and Sponsorship

### Article 20 — Donations

(1) For a donation we process the amount, the date, the choice between a public and an anonymous donation, the payer's details from the bank statement and the donation agreement. When paying by payment card, your statement that the payment instrument is in your name is also processed; the Foundation neither receives nor stores payment card data.

(2) The payer's details serve to verify that the payer and the donor coincide, because a contribution is recorded exclusively in the record of the user with whose funds the payment was made (Article 6 of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations). A statement on the origin of funds is not collected: the identification of the payer and the verification of the origin of funds are carried out by the bank through which the donation was made. The Foundation applies the measures under Chapter V of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations voluntarily; the Foundation is not an obliged entity under the regulations on the prevention of money laundering and the financing of terrorism.

(3) Purpose of processing: keeping the financial records of the Foundation and verifying the ground on which a contribution was recorded.

(4) Legal basis: the legal obligation to keep financial records (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 3 of the LPDP); for the payer's details and their comparison with the account: legitimate interest of the Foundation (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP), for the purpose of preventing misuse of the system.

(5) A public donation of at least 5,000 dinars, made in a single payment, may, if you are a user who is not in the Kolo, bring you onto the call list, under the conditions under Article 16. A donation does not confirm reality.

(6) Donation data and the donation agreement are kept for 10 years from their creation, in accordance with accounting regulations.

### Article 21 — List of Donations and the Donor's Name

(1) For a public donation, the donor's first and last name is published in the list of donations accessible to adult users, together with the amount and date, without the pseudonym and without a link to the profile (Article 11 of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations). The donor's pseudonym is visible only in the contribution ledger, which is accessible to participants. The name is not entered in the description of the record of recorded POEN; that record states only the amount and the ground.

(2) An anonymous donation enters the list only with its amount and date, without a name, pseudonym or link to a profile, so the donor cannot be established from it. No POEN is recorded for an anonymous donation because an entry that cannot be attributed to any person cannot be verified (Article 7 of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations), and not because the Foundation sets publication as a condition.

(3) Publicly linking a name to a donation makes it possible to link your pseudonymous record with your identity; the disclosure is voluntary, and we inform you of this before you choose.

(4) Legal basis for publication: consent (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 1 of the LPDP), which you give by choosing a public donation.

(5) You may withdraw consent at any time, separately for each public donation, on the donations screen. Upon withdrawal, the first and last name are removed from the list and deleted from the donation record, while the donation remains in the list with its amount and date. The same happens upon termination of status. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of publication before withdrawal or POEN already recorded, and does not delete the name from the identity register (Article 24).

### Article 22 — Sponsors

(1) When a sponsor is entered in the sponsor register (Article 16 of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations) we process: the name, legal form, registered office address, registration number and PIB of the legal entity or entrepreneur; the email address and telephone number for contact; the place and address for display on the map, with the statement of consent to the display of the address; the logo, if attached; the first and last name of the user making the entry and their relationship to the sponsor, and their statements; the status of the entry and the fact that the Foundation compared the name, legal form, registration number and representative with the register. For a registered agricultural holding we process the first and last name of the holder, the name and number of the holding and the contact details.

(2) With an application for sponsorship in money, the data on the contribution and the link between the sponsor and the user in whose record the contribution is recorded are processed. The applicant's first and last name is entered in the identity register (Article 24). A sponsorship in money may bring an applicant who is a user who is not in the Kolo onto the call list (Article 16); a sponsorship does not confirm reality.

(3) The name, logo and amount of the contribution in dinars are published on the sponsor ranking list; the registered office address, registration number, PIB, holding number and representative, or holder, are not published. On the map, the sponsor is displayed with its name, logo, place and address, together with the offer of vouchers.

(4) Purpose of processing: the register of sponsors and of sponsorship and keeping the financial records of the Foundation.

(5) Legal basis: execution of the contract with the sponsor (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP), including the display on the map, with the address, as part of the participation requested by the entry, for which a statement is given upon entry; for the data on the user making the entry, or on the representative: legitimate interest of the Foundation (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP); for accounting documentation: legal obligation (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 3 of the LPDP).

(6) The display on the map lasts for as long as the sponsor is entered and ceases upon deletion of the entry, which is possible when the sponsor has no agreement on sponsorship in goods and services with unused vouchers. Contributions made up to the deletion remain on the ranking list, unless the user who kept the entry requests that the sponsor also be removed from it. Agreements and the sponsor register are kept for 10 years from their creation, in accordance with accounting regulations.

### Article 23 — Sponsorship in Goods and Services

(1) With an application for sponsorship in goods and services (Article 28 of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations) we process: the description of the good or service, the number of vouchers, the unit price the giver applies in trade, a photograph of the price list, the place and address of handover or provision, the giver's declaration of compliance with regulations and the data on the conclusion of the agreement (time and checksum of the text). The applicant's first and last name is entered in the identity register (Article 24).

(2) The photograph of the price list is seen only by the Foundation's administrator team. The sponsor's name and the value of the sponsorship in goods and services are published on the ranking list, and the name and logo also on the vouchers and alongside the offer of vouchers.

(3) The purpose and the legal basis are the same as under Article 22.

(4) The data under this Article, including the photograph of the price list, are kept for 10 years from the expiry of the vouchers' validity period, together with the accounting documentation.

## VI — Identity Register

### Article 24 — Identity Register

(1) The Foundation keeps the identity register, in which your first and last name is kept alongside the pseudonym of your account (Article 31 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). One first and last name is kept per account.

(2) The first and last name is entered only:

1) when you register a farm or join one: you enter it yourself (Article 25);

2) when the Foundation establishes the receipt of your public donation: where it matches the name of the payer from the bank statement (Article 20);

3) when you become a cooperative member: it is submitted by the cooperative (Article 26);

4) when the Foundation establishes the receipt of a sponsorship under an application you submitted: from the application, after comparing it with the register (Articles 22 and 23);

5) when you get onto the call list: you enter it yourself (Article 16).

(3) If a first and last name has already been entered, a later case does not change it. No identity register is kept for a minor user.

(4) The unique citizen identification number, the identity card number and data from other documents are not processed, and copies of documents are not kept. Where the first and last name is compared with a document or a public register, only the fact that the comparison was carried out is recorded.

(5) Purpose of processing: giving effect to the principle of one person, one account (Article 8 of the Terms of Use) and verifying the ground on which a contribution was recorded.

(6) Legal basis: the entry is made for the execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP): for the participation you yourself requested. The name remains entered even when that participation ends (withdrawal from a farm, termination of the status of cooperative member, withdrawal of consent to the publication of the name with a public donation); the basis for this is the legitimate interest of the Foundation (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP) in ensuring that the same person does not run more than one account and that the ground of a previously recorded contribution can be verified. That interest outweighs the interference with your rights, because the name is not used for any other purpose, is seen only by the circle of persons under paragraph 8 and is deleted upon termination of status; you have the right to object to that processing (Article 43).

(7) The entered first and last name is compared with the names kept with other accounts in order to prevent multiple accounts (Article 17). The entry is a condition only for the participation under paragraph 2; it is not a condition for proof of reality or for access to the other functions. The entry is not a verification, never confirms reality and does not change your status.

(8) The first and last name from the identity register is not displayed to other users, does not appear alongside a farm, a cooperative or a listing, and is not entered in POEN records. It is accessible only to the Foundation's administrator team, to you, to the person acting on behalf of a cooperative for the cooperative members of that cooperative (Article 26) and to the Kolo member who calls you from the call list, by a click and only while the call is open (Article 16).

(9) An inaccurate entry is corrected by a complaint concerning inaccurate personal data (Article 40 of the Terms of Use).

(10) The first and last name from the identity register is kept for as long as the account exists and is deleted upon termination of status (Article 42).

## VII — Parts of the System

### Article 25 — Farms and the Map of Local Producers

(1) A farm is registered and managed by an adult user, under the conditions under Articles 4 and 11 of the Rulebook on Farms. A minor user cannot be a farm member.

(2) We process: the name of the farm, the settlement, the address and exact location (geographic coordinates), the description of production, the role of the applicant, the agricultural holding number (BPG) or an indication that the farm is not entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings, the pseudonyms of the members and the order and time of joining, who invited whom to join, the declaration of accuracy of data, the statement of consent to the display of the address and a member's declaration that they work on the farm (the time and the text displayed), the moment from which at least one farm member has continuously had an active listing offering something (for the aggregate indicator of continuous supply), the request to a member to take over the declaration of accuracy and their reply, the Foundation's decisions on the registration, on joining and on the annulment of approval, and the datum on the termination of the farm and its ground.

(3) The extract from the Register of Agricultural Holdings is not attached and the Foundation does not collect it, and it does not check the data in that register (Article 3 of the Rulebook on Farms).

(4) The first and last name of the applicant and of each member is entered in the identity register (Article 24), not alongside the farm. The name of the farm is chosen by the applicant; if the name contains a surname, the surname becomes public together with the name.

(5) With the registration, the telephone number of the holder is left, or of the applicant where the holder has no account, for the purpose of the conversation before approval (Article 8 of the Rulebook on Farms). The number is seen only by the Foundation's administrator team, is used only for that conversation and is deleted upon the decision on the registration; only the fact that the conversation took place is recorded. Where the holder is a user from the call list, the conversation is held by the Kolo member who called them, who records that the conversation took place and whether, after it, there are grounds for suspicion. No telephone number is collected from the other members.

(6) A farm registration may bring a holder who is a user who is not in the Kolo onto the call list (Article 16); the registration does not confirm reality.

(7) The name, description, address and exact location on the map, listings on behalf of the farm and the pseudonyms of members are public and accessible to all visitors, including persons who are not signed in. The address is written out alongside the point on the map on the basis of the statement under paragraph 2. The BPG is not displayed to other users.

(8) So that the farm's point can be found on the map, the entered address is sent from your browser to the address search service of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (Nominatim, United Kingdom); you may move the point that is found.

(9) Purpose of processing: displaying the farm on the map and on the farm's profile, linking listings on behalf of the farm with the farm and establishing the conditions for the farm contribution.

(10) Legal basis: execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP). The display on the map, including the address, is an integral part of the farm's participation and a condition of the farm contribution (Article 19 of the Rulebook on Farms); a person who does not wish the farm to be displayed does not register it, or withdraws from it.

(11) Farm data are kept for as long as the farm exists. Upon termination of the farm, on any ground (Article 22 of the Rulebook on Farms), the point is removed from the map, and the name, description, address, location and BPG are deleted. Upon withdrawal and upon closure of the account, the member ceases to be displayed with the farm. The member's link with the farm, also after withdrawal, the order of joining, the declaration of accuracy and the datum on the termination of the farm are retained in pseudonymous form, for the purpose of verifying that a person has a farm contribution for at most one farm, for the write-off upon termination of the farm and for the procedure in the event of a false declaration.

### Article 26 — Cooperative Members

(1) A cooperative member is a participant whose account is linked to a cooperative participating in the system (Article 6 of the Rulebook on Cooperatives). A person may be a cooperative member in only one cooperative. The account of a minor user is not linked to a cooperative.

(2) We process: the first and last name of the cooperative member, the pseudonym, the name of the cooperative and the time of linking or of the termination of the status of cooperative member. The first and last name and the pseudonym are submitted to the Foundation by the cooperative, only for persons who have consented to this; the Foundation does not collect them from you directly and does not request any other data about your membership in the cooperative. The first and last name is entered in the identity register (Article 24).

(3) The person acting on behalf of the cooperative (Article 15 of the Rulebook on Cooperatives) sees the first and last name, the pseudonym and the telephone number you have entered in your account, only for the cooperative members of that cooperative; they have no access to other data, including special categories of data. That person acts under the authority of the Foundation and in accordance with the acts of the KOLO system and, before being authorised, signs a statement of confidentiality, outside the Platform. Regarding that person, the granting and revocation of the authorisation are processed, and their actions are recorded in the audit log (Article 9); the basis is the execution of a contract, on the basis of the authorisation they accepted, and the data on the authorisation are kept for as long as their account exists.

(4) The records of a cooperative are visible to participants under the name of the cooperative, and cooperative members are displayed in them under their pseudonyms. The first and last name and the telephone number of a cooperative member are not displayed to other users.

(5) Purpose of processing: linking the account with the cooperative and establishing the number of cooperative members for the growth thresholds of the cooperative's record (Article 9 of the Rulebook on Cooperatives).

(6) Legal basis: consent (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 1 of the LPDP). Upon withdrawal of consent, the status of cooperative member terminates (Article 8 of the Rulebook on Cooperatives); withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

(7) Termination of the status of cooperative member does not delete the name from the identity register. The link between the account and the cooperative and the times of linking and termination are retained in pseudonymous form, for the purpose of verifying the growth thresholds that have already been recorded.

(8) When a cooperative helps in the amicable settlement of a dispute between users (Article 18 of the Rulebook on Cooperatives), the data needed for the dispute are submitted to the cooperative by the parties themselves, and the cooperative processes them as controller. The Foundation does not submit data about users to the cooperative.

### Article 27 — Support Programmes

(1) Support programmes require the processing of special categories of personal data (Article 17 of the LPDP). Only what the Rulebook on Support Programmes determines is entered in the application: for Mothers Support, the date of birth of each child, without the name and without any other data about the child; for Elderly Support, the date of birth; for Special Support on the ground of reduced capacity, the date of the decision and, if it contains one, the expiry date, without the degree of disability, the assessment of working capacity, the diagnosis and medical documentation, or a statement on illness, and about a person in the applicant's care only the date of coming of age when that person is a minor; for Special Support on the ground of loss of home, a statement and the date of the event; for Student Support, a statement with the higher education institution and the year of study. Documents are not attached and copies of them are not kept.

(2) A child's date of birth is a datum about a person who is not themselves a user. The legal basis for it is the consent of the parent or legal guardian (Article 16 of the LPDP), which you give as the applicant.

(3) Applying to a programme and requesting confirmation that the conditions are met are carried out exclusively with your explicit consent and if you are a participant. Consent is requested before confirmation is requested from anyone, and it states everything under Article 6 of the Rulebook on Support Programmes, including how many persons will be asked to confirm.

(4) That the conditions are met is confirmed by a Kolo member, or a founder, who knows you personally, within the circle under Article 5 of the Rulebook on Support Programmes, and where there is no such person: by the Foundation. A Kolo member who introduced you into the Kolo, or confirmed you, after a telephone conversation and a member without confirmation do not confirm that the conditions are met. Those persons do not see the data you entered, but they learn which programme is concerned, which may reveal to them your belonging to a special category of data; they are notified within the Platform, and a message sent by email or to a device states neither the name of the programme nor your pseudonym. This is the only disclosure of belonging to a programme that the application entails; if you do not consent even to it, the application cannot be submitted, because it is not established that the conditions are met without confirmation.

(5) The entered data are seen, and the application is decided on, only by the person at the Foundation who processes the application. The data are kept tied to the applicant's pseudonym, not to their first and last name.

(6) The description of the record of recorded POEN does not contain the name of the programme. The name of the programme is displayed to participants, together with the pseudonym of the support recipient and the amount, and is derived from the application to the programme, not from the description of the record. Persons who are not signed in and users who are not in the Kolo are shown only a daily total per programme: the number of support recipients and the total number of recorded POEN; a day on which, within one programme, a contribution was recorded for only one support recipient is not published. The ground on which the entitlement was established is not displayed to any user; the breakdown by ground is seen only by you, alongside the programme, and by the person at the Foundation who processes the application.

(7) The amount of the daily recording is derived from the data on the basis of which the entitlement was established: for Elderly Support the amount is uniquely determined by the support recipient's year of birth, and for Mothers Support by the number of children and their ages. We inform you of this in the consent.

(8) Purpose of processing: recording contributions in POEN for support recipients, while protecting the programmes from untruthful applications.

(9) Legal basis: explicit consent (Article 17 paragraph 2 point 1 of the LPDP). You may withdraw consent at any time, without giving reasons, by an action available alongside the programme itself. Upon withdrawal the procedure is terminated, the recording of POEN ceases, and the data you entered are deleted. POEN already recorded is not annulled. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

(10) The entered data are also deleted when the application ceases to be valid in another way: by refusal, by suspension (Article 8 of the Rulebook on Support Programmes) or by termination of your user status.

### Article 28 — Collective Procurement

(1) For a procurement proposal we process the name of the good and the time of entry. The proposal contains no quantity, price or reasoning. The register of proposals is published in aggregate: the name of the good and the number of users who proposed it, without pseudonyms (Article 10 of the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement).

(2) For participation in a procurement we process: the application, the position in the order, the day of collection entered, the collection code and the time of collection. The number of POEN by which the order is established is not collected anew, but is read from the contribution ledger (Article 11).

(3) The proposal and the application are open to a participant under the conditions under Article 4 of the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement; no evidence concerning occupation or property is collected.

(4) The supplier is provided only with a list of collection codes, and not with pseudonyms or other data about you. Participants see the list of those who collected a share, with the pseudonym and the position in the order, without the number of POEN in the record. Your individual proposal is not published.

(5) The establishment of the order is the application of publicly published rules and produces no legal consequences for your status; you may lodge a complaint against the order and the outcome of a procurement under Article 40 of the Terms of Use.

(6) Purpose of processing: establishing the needs of the community and carrying out the procurement. Legal basis: execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP). Submitting a proposal and applying are voluntary.

(7) A proposal is deleted when you choose another name or remove it, when a procurement for the selected name of the good is carried out, upon termination of status and at the latest 12 months from entry. Data on participation and collection are kept for 10 years, together with the accounting documentation of the procurement; upon termination of status they are pseudonymised.

### Article 29 — Vouchers

(1) In the allocation of vouchers from sponsorship in goods and services (Chapter VII of the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement) we process: the declaration that you are not a person connected with the giver, the consent under paragraph 3, the voucher and its code, the time of taking, the number of POEN annulled upon taking, the time of use and who confirmed it, as well as any complaint concerning the voucher.

(2) The giver and the participants it has designated to confirm vouchers on its behalf see, when checking, the description of the voucher, the validity period and your pseudonym; a used voucher is recorded with the giver together with your pseudonym. If the giver designates you to confirm vouchers on its behalf, the datum on that authorisation (who designated you, when, and when the authorisation was revoked) and on the vouchers you have confirmed is processed.

(3) The record of the annulment, with the description of the good or service and the name of the giver, is displayed alongside your pseudonym in the contribution ledger accessible to participants, with the explicit consent you give when taking the voucher, because such a display may reveal data about you, including data concerning health. Without consent the voucher cannot be taken; taking a voucher is voluntary. Upon withdrawal of consent, the description of the good or service and the name of the giver are deleted from the description of the record of the annulment, while the record remains in the contribution ledger (Article 33 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement); withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of the display before withdrawal.

(4) Purpose of processing: carrying out the allocation and protecting the rights of the voucher holder towards the giver.

(5) Legal basis: execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP); for the display under paragraph 3 and for the giver's and its designated persons' insight into your pseudonym (paragraph 2): the consent you give when taking the voucher (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 1 of the LPDP), or explicit consent where the display may reveal data concerning health (Article 17 paragraph 2 point 1 of the LPDP).

(6) The data under this Article are kept for 10 years from the expiry of the voucher's validity period, together with the accounting documentation; the record of the annulment remains in the contribution ledger.

### Article 30 — Operational Contribution

(1) For an application for an operational contribution task we process the pseudonym, the execution plan and the saved text of the statement you give with the application (Article 10 of the Rulebook on Operational Contribution); for execution: the proof of execution you attach (a photograph, a report, a link or another attachment), the number of units executed and the decisions of the person confirming execution, with the statement of reasons; as well as objections by participants and the decisions on them. For a cooperative contribution, the cooperative that assigned the unit is also recorded (Article 23 of the Rulebook on Operational Contribution). Photographs of proof are stored with the image storage processor (Article 39).

(2) Applications, plans, the pseudonyms of the contributor and of the confirming person, decisions, recorded POEN and objections are accessible only to participants (Article 25 of the Rulebook on Operational Contribution). The contributor and the confirming person are displayed only under their pseudonyms.

(3) The proof of execution is seen by the persons confirming execution and by the Foundation's administrator team. An alert to the Foundation about execution (Article 39 paragraph 3 point 5) contains the name of the task, the pseudonym and the proposed POEN.

(4) Purpose of processing: carrying out the task and confirming execution before the contribution is recorded. Legal basis: execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP); the saved text of the statement is also kept on the basis of the legitimate interest of the Foundation (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP): proof of the legal nature of the contribution and the defence of legal claims.

(5) The data under this Article are kept for as long as the account exists. Upon termination of status, the execution plan, the description and the proof of execution, including the photograph in storage, are deleted; the saved statement and the numerical data on execution are retained under the pseudonym, as part of the contribution ledger (Article 11).

### Article 31 — Voting in the Upper Kolo

(1) From Phase 2 we process: for a participant who submits a proposal, the pseudonym and the content of the proposal; for a Kolo keeper who has activated ZRNO, support for a proposal, the vote and the voting power per accounting period and the delegation of voting power (the link between delegator and delegate).

(2) Proposals, the number of supports, the totals of voting power and the register of decisions are accessible to users; the pseudonyms of proposers and of the Kolo keepers who supported a proposal are seen only by participants. An individual vote is not published, except in the case under Article 46 paragraph 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System (Article 19 of the Rulebook on the Upper Kolo).

(3) The act by which the Management Board implements a decision states how the members of the Management Board voted, by name, without pseudonyms.

(4) Purpose of processing: establishing the composition and the voting power and decision-making in the Upper Kolo. Legal basis: execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP); for stating the vote of a member of the Management Board: legitimate interest of the Foundation (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP), for the purpose of the verifiability of the conduct of the Foundation's organs.

(5) The register of decisions is kept for the duration of the system and does not contain individual votes. Individual votes are kept for as long as the account exists, and upon termination of status they are pseudonymised. Delegation ceases upon revocation and upon termination of status.

### Article 32 — Complaints and Reports

(1) For a complaint to the Foundation (Article 40 of the Terms of Use) we process: the person submitting it, the type and subject of the complaint, the allegations, the reply with the statement of reasons, and who replied and when. When the complaint relates to a re-registration in connection with an exchange, the other party is invited to state its position, and its statement, the decision and the amount of the correction are also recorded; the statement of reasons for the decision is also communicated to the other party.

(2) For a listing report we process the person reporting, the listing, the reason for and description of the report and the decision. The person reporting is not disclosed to the advertiser.

(3) For a decision on the removal of content, suspension and exclusion (Terms of Use) we process the decision and its reason.

(4) For a report of an error on the Platform we process the title, the description, the pseudonym of the person reporting, the status and the reply. The list of error reports, with the title, the pseudonym of the person reporting and the status, is seen by signed-in adult users. An alert to the Foundation (Article 39 paragraph 3 point 5) contains the title of the report and the pseudonym.

(5) Complaints, reports and cases are seen by the Foundation's administrator team; every decision is taken by a human, with a statement of reasons.

(6) Purpose of processing: deciding on complaints and reports, enforcing the rules of the system and remedying errors. Legal basis: execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP); for the data on the person to whom a report or decision relates: legitimate interest of the Foundation (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP) in enforcing the rules of the system and protecting other users, to which you have the right to object (Article 43).

(7) The data under this Article are kept for as long as the account of the person submitting exists. Upon termination of status, the description and other free text are deleted, while the decision and its effect are retained under the pseudonym, for the purpose of the defence of legal claims; an error report is deleted upon termination of status. A correction of a POEN record remains in the contribution ledger (Article 11).

## VIII — Minor Users

### Article 33 — Account of a Minor User and Legal Basis

(1) A minor user is a person from the age of seven until eighteen years of age, and their participation is governed by the Rulebook on the Participation of Children.

(2) About a minor user we process: the pseudonym; the data necessary for access to the account; the parent's email address entered by the minor; their own email address, if they state one; the date of birth entered by the parent, or entered by the decision on the conversion of an adult account into a minor's account; the link with one or two parents; the parent's declaration on the existence of the child and the confirmation of the existence of the child; friendships with dates; messages in the children's Chat Room and in conversations; POEN records and requests to the parent for approval of a re-registration above the threshold; listings; the school they state themselves. The state of the account (waiting child, child with a parent, Kolo child; Article 5 of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children) is determined from data that are already processed.

(3) Purpose of processing: the participation of the minor user in the system under a special regime of restrictions and the recording of their contribution (Article 19 paragraph 1 point 9 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(4) Legal basis: for a minor user under 15 years of age: the consent of the parent, in accordance with Article 16 of the LPDP; for a minor user who has reached 15 years of age: execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP), to which the minor user is a party with the consent of the parent (Article 3 of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children); for the data before the take-over of the account and for the parent's email address: the legitimate interest under Article 34.

(5) The consent of the parent is stronger here than usual: the account of a minor user gains full access only once the child is a Kolo child: when the parent is a Kolo member, confirmed by someone who knows them personally, and when the existence of the child has been confirmed by a Kolo member who knows the parent personally. This exceeds the reasonable-effort standard under Article 16 paragraph 2 of the LPDP.

(6) For a minor user no identity register is kept, Google Analytics is not loaded, and the minor user does not get onto the call list and does not donate.

(7) The data of a minor user are kept for as long as the account exists. Upon coming of age, friendships and the school datum are deleted, while their own email address is retained.

### Article 34 — Legal Basis in the Period until Take-over

(1) An account that a minor has opened themselves, until it is taken over by a parent (waiting child), has a profile and may form friendships; the Chat Room, listings, communication with adults and the recording of POEN are closed. Until then, the pseudonym, the data necessary for access to the account, the parent's email address and the records of friendships formed, with dates, are processed.

(2) The consent of the parent does not yet exist in that period, so the data are processed on the basis of legitimate interest (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP), for the purpose of establishing contact with the parent in order to obtain the consent required by law. The scope is reduced to what is necessary for that purpose, the period is limited to 14 days, the functions that would expose the minor user to adults are closed, and the minor user may delete the account themselves at any time. Upon take-over of the account, the basis becomes the one under Article 33 paragraph 4.

(3) The parent's email address is a datum about a third party. It is processed on the basis of legitimate interest, exclusively in order to send one message inviting the take-over of the account, in which only the minor user's pseudonym is stated, never their name, because the accuracy of the address has not been verified. The address does not become the address of the minor user's account and opens no path to signing in or to password recovery.

(4) An account that nobody takes over within 14 days of its opening is deleted, together with the parent's address, the invitation and the records of friendships.

### Article 35 — Parental Insight and Visibility of a Minor User

(1) A parent does not read the conversations their child has with other minor users: they see the list of friendships with dates and the list of conversations, without messages. When a Kolo child becomes friends with a minor user who is not a Kolo child, the parent of the Kolo child receives a notification that the friendship has been formed; even then they do not read the conversations. A parent reads a minor user's conversation with an adult user, and the adult party is shown in that conversation that it is read by the parent (Article 19 of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children).

(2) The profile of a minor user is not accessible to adult users: instead of the profile, a notice is displayed with the pseudonym of the minor user and the pseudonym of the parent. The consent of the parent to communication and exchange does not open the profile. The datum on who the parents of a minor user are, or who the children of an adult user are, is accessible to participants (Article 25 of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children).

(3) The email address of a minor user is stated voluntarily and is processed exclusively for the purpose of setting a new password; no notifications are sent to it. It is recorded only once the minor user confirms it by opening a link sent to that very address, which is valid for 24 hours; until confirmation it has no effect. The address may be removed at any time. While a minor user has no confirmed address of their own, the new password is set by the parent from the child's profile, without the previous password, and the minor user is notified of the change (Article 21 of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children).

### Article 36 — School

(1) A minor user in the state of child with a parent or Kolo child may themselves state their school, by choosing from a list; a waiting child does not state a school.

(2) The Platform publishes an aggregate overview by school (the number of Kolo children and their share in the number of enrolled pupils), which contains no personal data and is available to everyone. An overview of the Kolo children of a single school, with the pseudonym and the state of the record, is available exclusively to a Kolo child of that same school; it is not displayed to adult users, persons who are not signed in, a waiting child, a child with a parent or a minor user of another school. A child with a parent who has stated a school is not included in the number per school or in the overview until they become a Kolo child.

(3) The school may be changed at most once every 30 days. The datum is deleted upon coming of age and upon termination of status; no history of earlier choices is kept.

### Article 37 — Wish Box

(1) In the Wish Box (Article 28 of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children) we process: the text of the proposal the minor user submitted, the support they gave (which proposal they supported), the state of the proposal, the reason for refusal and the reply of the Foundation.

(2) The author of a proposal is not displayed to anyone, not even to the Foundation's administrator team, which reviews the proposal before it is displayed without the datum on the author. Other children see the text of the proposal without the author, and the parent only the text of their own child's proposal.

(3) Notifications about a proposal do not contain its text. The data are not sent outside the Platform, and Google Analytics is not loaded (Article 33 paragraph 6).

(4) Purpose of processing: the child's participation in shaping the children's space. Legal basis: as for the other data of a minor user (Article 33 paragraph 4).

(5) The data are kept for as long as the account of the minor user exists. Upon deletion of the account and upon coming of age, the proposals, with the supports and the reply, and the supports the minor user gave are deleted. The data export covers proposals and supports.

### Article 38 — Confirmation of the Existence of a Child and Report of False Parenthood

(1) A parent who opens or takes over an account gives a declaration under full responsibility that they have a child of the stated age and states the child's date of birth; the text of the declaration is kept and is not altered. A parent who is a Kolo member, or a founder, requests confirmation of the existence of the child (Article 13 of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children). The request is seen by Kolo members and founders, not by members without confirmation, and only as the parent's pseudonym with a note that they are requesting confirmation that they have a child; the pseudonym, age, sex and school of the child are not displayed to them. Who gave the confirmation and when is recorded alongside it, and where the confirmation is subject to supervision, also the data on supervision (Article 15).

(2) A minor user receives a notification each time someone takes over their account, with the pseudonym of the person who took it over. The minor user, the other parent or any other user may report to the Foundation that that person is not the child's parent (Article 17 of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children). The report (the person submitting it, the person to whom it relates, the minor user, the time and the allegations), the suspension of the link and the decision are processed. The person to whom the report relates is not told who submitted it.

(3) Purpose of processing: protecting the child from a person who is not their parent. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP).

(4) The request and the confirmation of the existence of the child are kept for as long as the link between the parent and the child exists. The report of false parenthood, the suspension and the decision are kept for as long as the minor user's account exists.

## X — Retention and Termination of Status

### Article 41 — Retention Periods

(1) The retention period for each type of data is established in the Article in which the processing is described. Where a period is not specifically established, the data are kept for as long as the account exists.

(2) Upon expiry of the period, the data are deleted or permanently anonymised, so that the person can no longer be identified. The Foundation verifies at least once a year whether the period has expired for any type of data and deletes or anonymises the data for which it has.

### Article 42 — Termination of Status and Pseudonymisation

(1) Upon termination of user status (by withdrawal, exclusion or death), pseudonymisation is carried out under Article 33 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. When a death is reported by a family member or another close person (Article 34 of the Terms of Use), we process that person's email address and allegations and, where requested, proof of death, only for the purpose of that procedure; those data are deleted when the procedure is concluded, and only the record that the status terminated by death is retained.

(2) The following are deleted: the email address; voluntarily entered data; the data in listings, including the title, description, place and photographs, and the listings are removed from the Pijaca and the photographs are deleted from storage; the free text entered alongside a re-registration of POEN, both the text entered by the user and the text entered by their counterparty; a record description containing personal data (Article 29 paragraph 3); the first and last name from the identity register; the data from the call list and the record of who opened the call; the activity log; earlier pseudonyms; the recommendations the user gave and received; the subscription to device notifications; the plan, description and proof of execution of operational contribution (Article 30); the description and other free text of complaints and reports and error reports (Article 32).

(3) The user's links in the record of the chain of confirmations are pseudonymised, while the verifications the user gave remain. The link between the account and a cooperative and a farm remains under the pseudonym, for the reasons under Articles 25 and 26.

(4) The first and last name alongside a public donation is removed from the list of donations and deleted from the donation record; the donation remains in the list with its amount and date (Article 12 of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations). Payment details, including the payer's name from the bank statement, and the donation agreement are not published and are kept in accordance with accounting regulations.

(5) The user's POEN records are annulled, and the numerical history is retained under a pseudonym that does not point to a name. That procedure is pseudonymisation, not anonymisation: the Foundation retains the internal identifier of the account, so the retained records still constitute personal data and enjoy full protection under this Policy. The numerical records alongside a pseudonym that does not point to a name and the ground on which the POEN was entered remain in the ledger accessible to participants.

(6) The numerical history is retained under the pseudonym for 10 years from termination of status, and is then anonymised and remains permanently in the ledger, for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims and for the integrity of the ledger on which other users rely, because deleting the records of one user would undermine the consistency of the entire ledger; the legal basis is the legitimate interest of the Foundation and of users (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP). Dinar records of donations and sponsorship are also retained for compliance with the legal retention obligation (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 3 of the LPDP). To that extent the right to erasure does not apply, under Article 30 paragraph 3 of the LPDP.

## XI — Security and Impact Assessment

### Article 44 — Data Security

(1) The Foundation applies technical and organisational protection measures appropriate to the risk, in accordance with the LPDP.

(2) The technical measures are: encryption of data in transit (TLS, at least version 1.2), encryption of data at rest at infrastructure level, pseudonymisation of the ledger and separation of the identity register from the contribution ledger.

(3) The organisational measures are: access according to the principle of minimum necessity: every user, member of the administrator team and process has access only to the data necessary for their function; multi-factor authentication for administrative access to the infrastructure; a confidentiality obligation for all persons with access to users' data; training of persons with such access; security checks and penetration testing of the system.

(4) The integrity of the contribution ledger is protected by the zero-sum rule (Article 17 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), which is verified automatically, by recording every change on an "all or nothing" basis and by timestamping records. Every deviation from the zero-sum rule is visible and subject to review.

### Article 45 — Personal Data Breach

The Foundation notifies the Commissioner of a personal data breach that may result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of persons within 72 hours of becoming aware of it (Article 52 of the LPDP), and notifies the data subject without undue delay where the breach may result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms (Article 53 of the LPDP).

### Article 46 — Data Protection Impact Assessment

(1) The Foundation carries out a data protection impact assessment before commencing processing (Article 54 of the LPDP) and updates it before the introduction of any new processing, in particular the processing of special categories of data and of data of minors (Article 49 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(2) The results of the impact assessment are available to the Director of the Foundation and serve as the basis for applying protection measures.
