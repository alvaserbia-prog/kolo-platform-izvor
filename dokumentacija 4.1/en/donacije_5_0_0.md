# Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1 — Subject matter

(1) This Rulebook governs, in accordance with Articles 36 and 37 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, the recording of financial contribution and sponsorship: donations by natural persons, donation tiers and the recording coefficient, the entry of sponsors and sponsorship in money, the sponsor ranking list, sponsorship in goods and services, and measures against money laundering and the financing of terrorism.

(2) Terms from the Rulebook on the KOLO System have the same meaning in this Rulebook.

### Article 2 — Definitions

The following terms are also used in this Rulebook:

1) **donation recording coefficient**: the number by which the recorded contribution of a donor, whether a natural person or a sponsor, is increased when determining the number of POEN; it is determined by the donation tier; it is not a price, not an exchange rate, and not an accounting coefficient within the meaning of the Rulebook on the KOLO System;

2) **donation tier**: the level of the cumulative donation, or of the sponsor's cumulative contribution, that determines the donation recording coefficient;

3) **cumulative donation**: the sum of all public donations of a user whose receipt the Foundation has established;

4) **sponsorship in money**: a donation of money to the Foundation made by a sponsor;

5) **sponsor's cumulative contribution**: the sum of all sponsorships in money of one sponsor whose receipt the Foundation has established;

6) **sponsorship application**: a request that an adult user submits on the Platform to initiate the recording of a sponsorship contribution;

7) **sponsorship in goods and services**: the obligation of a legal entity, an entrepreneur or a holder of a registered agricultural holding to hand over goods, or to provide services, free of charge to persons who take a voucher in the allocation under the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement;

8) **giver**: a legal entity, entrepreneur or holder of a registered agricultural holding that has assumed an obligation under sponsorship in goods and services;

9) **voucher**: a document in electronic form by which the giver promises the holder the handover of a precisely specified good, or the provision of a precisely specified service, within a set period and at a set place. A voucher is not issued for a monetary amount.

## II — Donations by Natural Persons

### Article 3 — Right to donate

(1) Any adult user may donate money to the Foundation in dinars or in another currency. Belonging to the Kolo is not a condition for donating, nor for recording a contribution on that basis. A minor user does not donate.

(2) A donation does not confirm reality. A public donation of at least 5,000 dinars, made in a single payment, may bring the user onto the call list, under the conditions of Article 8 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality. The donation and the contribution recorded on its basis do not depend on the call.

### Article 4 — Method of payment

(1) A donation is made exclusively by a transfer of funds from account to account of the Foundation, or by a payment instrument issued in the donor's name. The Foundation does not accept cash.

(2) Where a donation has been made in a foreign currency, the dinar amount credited to the Foundation's account is authoritative. The contribution is recorded according to that amount, not the amount the donor sent; the difference arising from conversion and payment transaction costs is not recorded.

### Article 5 — Donation receipt and tax treatment

(1) The Foundation issues a donation receipt upon the donor's request, in accordance with the law.

(2) The tax treatment of a donation depends on the donor's status, the Foundation's registered status, and the applicable tax regulations at the time of the donation.

### Article 6 — Payer and establishment of receipt

(1) The contribution is recorded exclusively in the record of the user with whose funds the payment was made. A payment made by another person is not recorded as the contribution of the user to whose reference number it was made. A payment by a person who is not a user carries no recording of POEN; the funds are accepted as a donation and used to pursue the aims of the Foundation.

(2) The payer's details from the bank statement are noted with the donation record and with the record of the POEN recorded. In card payments the donor declares that the payment instrument is in their name.

(3) Receipt of a donation is established by the Foundation, by comparing the payer's details with the user's account. The payment intermediary's report of a completed card charge is not an establishment of receipt but a ground for entering the payment into that procedure; the contribution is not recorded at the moment of the charge.

### Article 7 — Public and anonymous donation

(1) With each donation, the donor chooses whether the donation is public or anonymous. By making a public donation, the donor agrees that their first and last name be displayed in the list of donations (Article 11).

(2) POEN is recorded only for a public donation, under Article 8. For an anonymous donation no POEN is recorded, and it does not count towards the cumulative donation.

(3) The total number of recorded POEN is public and the sum of all records in the Protocol equals zero (zero-sum rule); a donation that carried POEN but could not be attributed to any person would be an entry that cannot be verified. That is why no POEN is recorded for an anonymous donation, and not because consent is withheld.

(4) The Foundation accepts both forms of donation equally and uses them for the same aims.

### Article 8 — Tiers and recording coefficient

(1) The tier is determined on the basis of the sum of the cumulative donation and the new donation. The recording coefficient for the newly reached tier applies to the entire new donation.

(2) The tiers and recording coefficients are:

| Tier | Cumulative donation (RSD) | Recording coefficient |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 | 1.00 |
| 2 | 5,000 | 1.10 |
| 3 | 10,000 | 1.20 |
| 4 | 20,000 | 1.30 |
| 5 | 50,000 | 1.40 |
| 6 | 100,000 | 1.50 |
| 7 | 200,000 | 1.60 |
| 8 | 500,000 | 1.70 |
| 9 | 1,000,000 | 1.80 |
| 10 | 2,000,000 | 1.90 |
| 11 | 5,000,000 | 2.00 |

(3) Tier 11 is the highest tier. Any larger sum remains at tier 11.

(4) The number of POEN recorded equals the donation amount in dinars multiplied by the recording coefficient. The number of POEN is rounded to the nearest whole number.

(5) The tier is cumulative and permanent: it does not decrease through the use of POEN or through any other activity of the user. The tier attained is a permanent recognition of a deed performed, not an acquired status. It cannot be lost, cannot be transferred to another person and confers no right against the Foundation.

### Article 9 — Nature of the recording coefficient

(1) The recording coefficient expresses the regard in which a contribution to the common good is held. A larger single contribution has greater significance for the community.

(2) The recording coefficient is not a price of POEN and is not a discount on a donation. Nothing is acquired by a donation: the donor acquires no good, no claim against the Foundation, no right to a refund, no right to redemption of POEN, and no direct influence in decision-making on the rules of the system; the contribution recorded on account of a donation leads to a vote only as any other contribution does, through the inscription and activation of ZRNO. Since there is no object of acquisition, the difference in the coefficient does not constitute more favourable terms of acquisition but a greater measure of recognition of the contribution.

(3) A donation confers no right to goods from a collective procurement, secures no place in the collection order, and creates no claim against the Foundation on that basis. The right to participate in a procurement arises from the decision initiating it, while the recorded contribution is the criterion by which precedence is determined (Article 17 paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement).

### Article 10 — Legal qualification of donation recording

(1) The recording of POEN on account of a donation is an automatic act of the Protocol within the meaning of Articles 14 and 19 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. It does not count towards the common daily limit.

(2) The automatism concerns the amount, not the initiation. The Protocol determines the number of POEN without discretion, under Article 8; the entry itself is initiated only after the Foundation has established receipt of the donation (Article 6 paragraph 3). A donation is therefore not a way to obtain POEN at the moment it is needed.

(3) Recorded POEN has the same status as all other POEN in the system.

### Article 11 — List of donations

(1) The Platform maintains a list of donations by natural persons, available to adult users. A public donation is listed with the donor's first and last name, together with the amount and date, without the pseudonym and without a link to the donor's profile; the donor's pseudonym is visible only in the contribution ledger, which is available to participants (Article 56 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). An anonymous donation is listed only with its amount and date.

(2) The first and last name are noted with the donation at the moment it is recorded and are not altered by subsequent profile changes.

(3) Publication of the donor's name serves the transparency of the Foundation's handling of funds and the verification of the ground on which POEN was entered. Publicly linking the name to the donation makes it possible to link the donor's pseudonymous record with their identity, of which the donor is informed before the choice under Article 7.

### Article 12 — Withdrawal of consent to publication of the name

(1) The donor may at any time withdraw consent to the publication of their first and last name, separately for each public donation. Upon withdrawal, the first and last name are removed from the list and deleted from the donation record, while the donation remains in the list with its amount and date. In the same way, the first and last name are removed from the list and deleted upon termination of the user's status.

(2) Withdrawal does not delete the first and last name from the identity register. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of publication up to the withdrawal, nor POEN already recorded, because the ground for the entry was verified at the moment of recording, and the donation remains attributed to the donor's account after withdrawal as well. Withdrawal does not cover what third parties may already have noted outside the Platform.

(3) Payment details and the donation agreement (Article 14) are not published.

### Article 13 — Entry in the identity register

(1) The first and last name of a donor who makes a public donation is entered in the identity register (Article 31 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), if it has not already been entered alongside the account and if it matches the name of the payer from the bank statement of the payment. An anonymous donation does not enter a name in the identity register.

(2) The entry is not displayed to other users, except to the Kolo member who calls the donor from the call list in order to get acquainted, under the conditions of Article 10 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality. The entry does not depend on publication in the list of donations and is deleted upon termination of the user's status.

### Article 14 — Donation agreement

(1) For every donation by a natural person, the Foundation draws up a donation agreement and delivers it to the donor through the Platform. The agreement is concluded by payment of the donation; the donor's signature is not required. The agreement is drawn up in electronic form at the moment the Foundation establishes receipt of the payment and accepts the donation.

(2) The agreement contains the amount of the donation, a statement that the donation is voluntary and without consideration, a statement that through the donation the donor obtains no good or service and acquires no claim against the Foundation, no right to a refund of the donation, no right to redemption of POEN and no direct influence in decision-making on the rules of the system, the Foundation's obligation to use the donation solely for the aims for which it was established, as well as a statement that the contribution recorded in connection with the donation under the rules of the KOLO system is not determined by that agreement, that this entry is not a counter-performance for the donation, and that the donor has no claim to it. The agreement does not contain the number of POEN, the recording coefficient or the tier; the donor sees them in the ledger on the Platform.

(3) The agreement does not contain a statement on the origin of funds. The identification of the payer and the verification of the origin of funds are carried out by the bank through which the donation was made (Article 38 point 3).

(4) The text of the agreement is kept with the donation record as it stood at the moment the donation was accepted and is not altered by subsequent amendments to this Rulebook. The donor may access the agreement at any time through the Platform and may print it.

## III — Sponsorship

### Article 15 — Forms of sponsorship

(1) Sponsorship is a donation of money to the Foundation by a legal entity, an entrepreneur or a holder of a registered agricultural holding (sponsorship in money), or sponsorship in goods and services for the benefit of users (Chapter IV).

(2) A donation by a legal entity for which no sponsorship application has been submitted is not recorded in anyone's POEN record (Article 36 paragraph 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

### Article 16 — Entry of the sponsor in the sponsor register

(1) Before the first sponsorship application, in money or in goods and services, an adult user enters the legal entity, entrepreneur or registered agricultural holding in the sponsor register, from their own account. Belonging to the Kolo is not a condition for the entry nor for an application for sponsorship in money.

(2) The entry of a legal entity or entrepreneur contains:

1) the name, the legal form (company, entrepreneur, association, cooperative, institution or other legal entity), the registered office address, the registration number and the tax identification number;

2) an email address and a telephone number for contact;

3) the place and address at which the sponsor is displayed on the map, with a statement of consent to the address being displayed;

4) the first and last name of the user making the entry and their relationship to the sponsor: legal representative, entrepreneur, owner, one of the owners, beneficial owner, or a person authorised by the competent body of the sponsor;

5) the logo, if submitted.

(3) The legal form does not affect the calculation of the contribution.

(4) A registered agricultural holding is entered exclusively by its holder, stating their first and last name, the name and number of the holding, contact details and the logo, if submitted. The Foundation may request a certificate of entry of the holding in the register where there is a reason to do so.

(5) The logo is not mandatory; a sponsor who does not submit one is displayed by name only.

### Article 17 — Statements of the user making the entry

(1) A user who is not the legal representative, the entrepreneur or the holder of the holding declares that they are authorised to conclude donation agreements on behalf of the sponsor. Where the sponsor is an association or a cooperative, the user, including the legal representative, declares that the competent body of the sponsor has authorised them to make the entry and to conclude donation agreements on behalf of the sponsor.

(2) The user confirms that they are aware that the contribution under the applications they submit is recorded in their record, that the Foundation does not distribute it among the owners, members or bodies of the sponsor, and that they are obliged to inform of the agreements and of the recorded contribution the owners, if they are not the sole owner, and, in the case of an association, a cooperative, an institution or another legal entity, the competent bodies and the members of the sponsor.

(3) The Platform records the statement and the confirmation.

### Article 18 — Check, amendment and deletion of the entry

(1) The Foundation compares the name, the legal form, the registration number and the representative with the data of the register in which the sponsor is entered, records that the comparison was carried out, and approves or rejects the entry with a statement of reasons.

(2) The same legal entity, entrepreneur or holding is entered once, and the entry is maintained by the user who made it. A change of the name, the registration number or the logo is subject to a renewed check by the Foundation; contact details are changed without a check.

(3) From an approved entry, the user submits applications for sponsorship in money (Article 19) and for sponsorship in goods and services (Article 28). The ban list under Article 35 is tied to the entry.

(4) The display of the sponsor on the map lasts as long as the sponsor is entered. The user who maintains the entry may delete it if the sponsor has no agreement on sponsorship in goods and services with unused vouchers. Upon deletion, the display on the map and the offer of vouchers cease, while the contributions made up to the deletion remain on the ranking list, with the name and logo. At the request of the user who maintained the entry, the sponsor is also removed from the ranking list.

### Article 19 — Application for sponsorship in money

(1) Sponsorship in money is initiated by the user who entered the sponsor in the sponsor register, by submitting a sponsorship application from the approved entry.

(2) The application contains the amount of the donation in dinars. The minimum amount of a sponsorship application is 10,000 dinars.

(3) The first and last name of the user who submitted the application is entered in the identity register (Article 31 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), alongside their account, upon confirmation of receipt of the donation, once the Foundation has carried out the comparison under Article 18 paragraph 1, and for a registered agricultural holding once the Foundation has approved the entry. Article 13 paragraph 2 applies mutatis mutandis to the entry.

(4) Upon submission of the application, the Platform generates a donation agreement between the entered sponsor, represented by the user who submitted the application, and the Foundation. For a registered agricultural holding, the agreement is concluded by the holder of the holding, stating the name and number of the holding. Article 14 paragraph 2 applies mutatis mutandis to the content of the agreement, and the agreement also contains a statement that the sponsor does not receive the contribution.

### Article 20 — Conclusion of the agreement and confirmation of receipt

(1) The applicant concludes the donation agreement on behalf of the legal entity or entrepreneur, or as the holder of a registered agricultural holding, electronically on the Platform, in the manner under Article 32 paragraph 1. The Platform records the time of conclusion and the checksum of the text and delivers a copy of the agreement to the sponsor and to the applicant. No POEN is recorded upon conclusion of the agreement.

(2) The donation is made by payment into the Foundation's account. The holder of a registered agricultural holding pays the donation from the account of the holding or from their own account.

(3) When the Foundation establishes receipt of the donation, it confirms it on the Platform. Following confirmation, the Protocol records POEN in the applicant's record under Article 22.

(4) Confirmation of receipt does not confirm the applicant's reality. An applicant who is a user may, through confirmation of receipt, come onto the call list, under the conditions of Article 8 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality. The sponsorship and the contribution recorded on its basis do not depend on the call.

(5) If the donation has not been received or the amount received does not correspond to the agreement, the Foundation rejects the confirmation with a statement of reasons. The applicant may submit a new application.

### Article 21 — Sponsor register

(1) The Foundation maintains a sponsor register in which it records all confirmed applications with data on the sponsor (legal entity, entrepreneur or registered agricultural holding), the type and value of the donation, the date of confirmation, and the pseudonym of the user in whose record the recording was made.

(2) On the basis of the register, the Foundation determines each sponsor's cumulative contribution by summing the values of all confirmed sponsorships in money.

### Article 22 — Sponsorship recording coefficient

(1) A sponsor's contribution is recorded under the table in Article 8, with the same tiers and the same recording coefficients as a donation by a natural person. The legal form of the giver does not affect the recording coefficient.

(2) The tier is determined on the basis of the sponsor's cumulative contribution and the new donation; Article 8 paragraphs 1, 4 and 5 apply mutatis mutandis to the coefficient, the number of POEN and the permanence of the tier.

(3) The sponsor's cumulative contribution and the user's cumulative donation are kept separately and are not added together, including where the sponsor's contribution is recorded in the record of a user who also donates personally. The cumulative contribution of a registered agricultural holding is kept for the holding, separately from the personal donation of its holder and from the cumulative contribution of a legal entity or entrepreneur with which the holder is connected.

### Article 23 — Legal qualification of sponsorship recording

(1) The recording of POEN on account of sponsorship is an automatic act of the Protocol within the meaning of Articles 14 and 37 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. It does not count towards the common daily limit.

(2) The contribution is recorded in the record of the user who submitted the sponsorship application. The contribution from a single application is recorded in the record of a single user and is not distributed among other owners or other persons connected with the sponsor, including where the sponsor has several owners. On account of the contribution the sponsor acquires no POEN, no ZRNO, no claim against the Foundation, no right to a refund of the donation and no other right.

(3) The recording of POEN is not a grant from the legal entity, entrepreneur or holding to the user, is not a distribution of profit or any other form of transfer of value from the sponsor's assets, and creates no property right. POEN has no value outside the system (Article 16 paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

### Article 24 — Tax obligations of the sponsor

(1) Tax obligations arising from sponsorship in money and from sponsorship in goods and services, including value added tax, corporate income tax and other public levies, are borne by the sponsor, or by the giver, in accordance with the applicable regulations.

(2) The Foundation does not provide tax advice to sponsors. The sponsor is responsible for the compliance of its donations with tax and other regulations.

### Article 25 — Sponsor ranking list

(1) The Platform maintains a public sponsor ranking list based on the cumulative value of contributions from the sponsor register. The ranking list is kept in two separate parts: according to the contribution in money and according to the value of sponsorship in goods and services. In both parts, the sponsor's name and the amount in dinars are published; the number of POEN is not published.

(2) A sponsor is identified on the ranking list by its full registered name, abbreviated name, or the trade name under which it operates, according to the data entered by the user upon entry. The registered office address, the registration number, the tax identification number, the number of the holding and the representative, or the holder of the holding, are not published on the ranking list.

### Article 26 — Display of the sponsor and public recognition

(1) Publication of the sponsor ranking list constitutes public recognition of contributions and an element of transparency in the Foundation's work. The Foundation may publicly name a sponsor and publicly thank it.

(2) The record of sponsors contains the sponsor's name and its logo, where one was submitted upon entry (Article 16). The name and logo are displayed on the ranking list and, in the case of sponsorship in goods and services, also on the vouchers and alongside the offer of vouchers. On the map, the sponsor is displayed with its name, logo, place and address, alongside the offer of vouchers. Nothing else is displayed besides this.

(3) On account of the contribution the sponsor acquires no right to a link to its website, to advertising space on the Platform, to being mentioned in the Foundation's campaigns or to any other promotional performance, and can neither contract for nor demand such performance. Public recognition is an act of the Foundation, not a counter-performance for the contribution.

(4) Neither a donation nor a sponsor's contribution gives any advantage in the selection of an offer in a collective procurement or in any other procedure of the Foundation. A sponsor who is also a bidder participates on an equal footing with the other bidders, under the conditions of the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement.

## IV — Sponsorship in Goods and Services

### Article 27 — Nature

(1) Sponsorship in goods and services is established by a donation agreement between the giver and the Foundation, by which the giver undertakes to hand over goods, or to provide services, free of charge to persons who take a voucher in the allocation under the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement.

(2) The person to whom a voucher is issued acquires their own right to demand from the giver the handover of the good or the provision of the service.

(3) The Foundation acquires neither ownership nor possession of the goods, does not provide the services, and is not liable for the performance of the giver's obligation. The giver is directly liable to the voucher holder for the quality, defects and safety of the good and the service.

### Article 28 — Application

(1) The application for sponsorship in goods and services is submitted by the participant who entered the giver in the sponsor register (Article 16), from the approved entry. For a registered agricultural holding, that is exclusively its holder.

(2) The application contains:

1) a precise description of the good or service;

2) the number of vouchers;

3) the unit price the giver applies in trade;

4) a photograph of the giver's price list showing that price;

5) the place and address of handover or provision.

(3) In the application, the giver declares that it complies with the regulations applicable to trade in that good or to the provision of that service.

(4) The first and last name of the applicant is entered in the identity register under the conditions of Article 19 paragraph 3, when the Foundation establishes that the agreement has been concluded (Article 33).

### Article 29 — Value and scope

(1) The value of sponsorship in goods and services equals the number of vouchers multiplied by the unit price from the application.

(2) The Foundation checks that the photograph of the price list has been attached. The Foundation does not appraise, compare or determine the price.

(3) The value of a single agreement is at least 10,000 and at most 50,000 dinars.

(4) The giver may conclude a subsequent agreement when at least half of the vouchers issued under the previous agreement have been used. The expiry of the vouchers' validity period does not open the possibility of a subsequent agreement. The Foundation may, by its own decision and with a statement of reasons, permit a subsequent agreement even before half of the vouchers have been used.

(5) A voucher is valid for 12 months from issuance.

### Article 30 — Subject of a voucher

(1) The subject of a voucher may not be goods and services referred to in Article 23 of the Terms of Use, goods whose advertising is prohibited or restricted by law (tobacco and related products, spirits and medicines), nor money and coupons or cards that can be exchanged for money. The Foundation checks this when reviewing the application.

(2) Vouchers are published and distributed by the Foundation, so the restrictions under paragraph 1 do not apply to listings that users themselves publish on the Pijaca.

### Article 31 — Agreement

(1) Upon acceptance of the application, the Platform generates a donation agreement between the giver, represented by the applicant, and the Foundation. The text of the agreement is kept as it was at the moment of conclusion and is not altered by subsequent amendments to this Rulebook.

(2) The agreement contains:

1) the data on the giver and the contact details;

2) the first and last name of the applicant and their relationship to the giver;

3) a description of the good or service, the number of vouchers and the value under Article 29 paragraph 1;

4) the validity period of the vouchers, and the place and address of handover or provision;

5) a statement that the donation is voluntary and free of charge and that through it the giver acquires no claim against the Foundation and no advantage in any procedure of the Foundation;

6) the giver's obligation to keep the goods available, or to secure appointments, until the expiry of the period, and to provide the good and the service in the same quality as to other persons;

7) the right of the voucher holder under Article 27 paragraph 2;

8) the giver's obligation to issue, upon handover or provision, the invoice prescribed for that transaction, where one is prescribed.

(3) The agreement does not contain provisions on POEN or on the recording of POEN.

### Article 32 — Conclusion of the agreement and issuance of vouchers

(1) The agreement is concluded electronically on the Platform. The applicant signs it by confirmation on the Platform, after confirming that they have read the agreement and that they are authorised to conclude it, and entering a one-time code delivered to their email address. An agreement so concluded is valid without a seal or handwritten signature. The Platform records the time of conclusion and the checksum of the text and delivers to the giver a copy of the agreement for printing.

(2) Upon signing of the agreement, the Platform issues the vouchers.

(3) The giver may designate on the Platform participants who confirm, on the giver's behalf, the handover of the good or the provision of the service under a voucher, and may revoke that authorisation at any time. Such a participant confirms vouchers through their own account and acquires no other authority in connection with the agreement; the giver is responsible for their confirmations.

### Article 33 — Recording

(1) When the agreement has been signed and the vouchers issued, the Foundation establishes that the sponsorship in goods and services has been realised (Article 37 paragraph 1 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), and the Protocol records POEN in the applicant's record. If the giver does not perform the obligation, the ledger is corrected under Article 35.

(2) The number of POEN recorded equals the value under Article 29 paragraph 1 multiplied by the recording coefficient corresponding to the tier the sponsor has reached through its cumulative contribution in money (Article 22), rounded to the nearest whole number. A sponsor without a contribution in money is recorded with a coefficient of 1.00.

(3) The value of sponsorship in goods and services does not enter the sponsor's cumulative contribution and does not affect the tier.

(4) The recording is an automatic act of the Protocol and does not count towards the common daily limit.

### Article 34 — Legal qualification

(1) Article 23 applies mutatis mutandis to recording under this Chapter.

(2) The recording of POEN is not consideration for goods or services. The Foundation does not receive the goods and services, and the voucher holder receives them from the giver without any performance towards the giver or the Foundation. The recording coefficient is not a price, not an exchange rate, and does not express the value of a good or service in POEN.

### Article 35 — Write-off upon non-performance and ban list

(1) Where the Foundation, upon a complaint by the voucher holder (Article 40 of the Terms of Use), establishes that the giver did not hand over the good or did not provide the service, and did not remedy this within 15 days of the Foundation's request, the agreement is terminated.

(2) The applicant has written off the number of POEN recorded under the agreement divided by the number of vouchers, for each voucher under that agreement that has not been used by the termination, regardless of whether it has been issued to a user. The amount written off also includes the increase by the recording coefficient. Vouchers under a terminated agreement lapse, and for the holders of issued unused vouchers the annulment is corrected under the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement.

(3) The write-off is executed even where the record thereby becomes negative (Article 17 paragraph 2 point 7 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). The burden is not transferred to another person. The applicant receives their own notification of the write-off.

(4) Without termination, no write-off is made: for a voucher that has not been issued to a user; for a voucher that the holder did not use before the expiry of the period; nor where the good was handed over or the service provided but the holder is not satisfied with the quality; in that case the holder exercises their rights against the giver under Article 27.

(5) A giver whose agreement has been terminated is entered on the ban list and may not conclude a new agreement on sponsorship in goods and services until the Foundation removes it from that list.

### Article 36 — Exclusion of connected persons

The vouchers of a single giver may not be taken by the applicant, the legal representative and the owners of the giver, or by persons connected with them by kinship, marriage, cohabitation or a common household. When taking a voucher, a user declares that they are not such a person.

## V — Prevention of Money Laundering and the Financing of Terrorism

### Article 37 — Purpose and voluntariness

(1) The Foundation is not an obliged entity within the meaning of the regulations on the prevention of money laundering and the financing of terrorism: POEN does not meet the elements of digital assets or of a virtual currency (Article 16 paragraph 4 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), the Foundation provides no payment services (Article 16 paragraph 7 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), and it performs no other activity on account of which it would acquire the status of an obliged entity. The Foundation pays no money to a user on any basis, a donation is not returned, and POEN is neither redeemed nor converted.

(2) The Foundation lays down the measures in this Chapter voluntarily, out of caution and in order to protect the integrity of the system, so as to make abuse harder and make it visible. Laying them down constitutes neither an acknowledgement of the status of an obliged entity nor a basis for the obligations that the regulations prescribe for obliged entities.

### Article 38 — Measures

The Foundation applies the following measures:

1) a donation is made exclusively cashlessly, in the manner established by Article 4;

2) the contribution is recorded exclusively in the record of the user with whose funds the payment was made, and the payer's details are noted with the donation record;

3) the identification of the payer and the verification of the origin of funds are carried out by the bank through which the donation was made, which is an obliged entity under the regulations on the prevention of money laundering and the financing of terrorism; the Foundation does not carry out its own verification of the origin of funds and does not require a statement on origin;

4) a donation from an account opened abroad is recorded once the Foundation has established that the payer from the bank statement matches the user, as with a payment from a domestic account; the identification of the payer is carried out by the bank, and the record of such a donation is marked separately;

5) the application of this Chapter is monitored by the Director of the Foundation;

6) documentation on donations and sponsorship, including the agreements and the sponsor register (Article 21), is kept for 10 years from its creation, in accordance with the periods laid down by the accounting regulations.

### Article 39 — Action upon suspicion

(1) Where there are grounds to suspect that a payment was made for the purpose of money laundering or the financing of terrorism, the Foundation does not record the contribution and does not enter POEN until the suspicion has been dispelled.

(2) The Foundation notifies the competent authority of such a payment where the regulations so provide, and acts upon its order.

(3) The measures under Articles 31 and 32 of the Terms of Use apply to a user who took part in the abuse.

(4) Where POEN has already been recorded, annulment is carried out exclusively on the grounds established by the Rulebook on the KOLO System. Exclusion of a user under Article 32 of the Terms of Use entails annulment of the record under Article 33 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. This Rulebook establishes no new ground for annulment.

## VI — Final Provisions

### Article 40 — Amendment of parameters

(1) The table in Article 8 is amended by the Management Board in Phase 1, and by the Upper Kolo in Phase 2, in accordance with the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

(2) Amendments to parameters take effect upon publication on the Platform and apply to donations and sponsor contributions whose receipt is established after the amendment enters into force.

### Article 41 — Entry into force

This Rulebook enters into force on the date of its adoption.
