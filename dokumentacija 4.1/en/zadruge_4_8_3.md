# Rulebook on Cooperatives

*This Rulebook governs the participation of cooperatives in the KOLO system: the conditions for opening a cooperative's record, cooperative members, the growth of a cooperative's record, the grounds for entries in the record, the distribution of goods, cooperative contribution, and the termination of participation. It is adopted on the basis of Article 56 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.*

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1

*Subject matter*

This Rulebook governs: the conditions under which a cooperative participates in the KOLO system, the cooperative's record in the Protocol, the status of cooperative member, the recording of the growth of a cooperative's record, the grounds for entries in a cooperative's record, the distribution of goods, cooperative contribution, support from the Foundation, and the termination of a cooperative's participation.

Terms not defined in this Rulebook shall have the meaning established in the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

### Article 2

*Relationship with the Rulebook on the KOLO System*

This Rulebook elaborates Article 15, item 5 and Article 56 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. In the event of any inconsistency, the provisions of the Rulebook on the KOLO System shall prevail.

### Article 3

*Definitions*

**Cooperative** — a legal entity entered in the register under the Law on Cooperatives that has concluded a cooperation agreement with the Foundation within the KOLO system.

**Cooperative's record** — the record in the Protocol kept for a cooperative as a collective form.

**Cooperative member** — a participant whom the cooperative has admitted to membership under its own rules and whose account is linked to the cooperative in accordance with Article 7.

**Locality** — the settlement in which the cooperative operates within the system.

**Distribution** — the receipt of goods from users and their issuing to other users through the cooperative, in accordance with Chapter V.

**Cooperative contribution** — a task for the community in the locality published by the cooperative, in accordance with Chapter VI.

### Article 4

*Autonomy of the cooperative*

A cooperative is managed by its cooperative members, through the bodies established by law and by the cooperative's rules. The Foundation has no right to vote, to propose, or to give consent in the bodies of the cooperative.

A cooperative does not become the owner of any part of the common good. The Foundation acquires no share, stake, or right to the property of the cooperative.

## II — Participation of a Cooperative in the System

### Article 5

*Conditions*

The Foundation opens a cooperative's record when the following conditions are met:

1) the cooperative is entered in the register under the Law on Cooperatives;

2) at least five cooperative members are participants (Kolo members and members without confirmation);

3) a cooperation agreement has been concluded;

4) the cooperative has designated its locality;

5) the condition under Article 6 is met in the locality.

The Foundation rejects a request only if one of the conditions under paragraph 1 is not met, stating the reasons.

### Article 6

*Locality*

A locality is one settlement from the register of settlements maintained by the platform. More than one cooperative may operate in the same locality.

The first cooperative in a locality may be entered in the system when that locality has at least fifty participants (Kolo members and members without confirmation). The Foundation participates in the establishment of the first cooperative in a locality through the support referred to in Article 18.

Where a cooperative already operates in a locality, each subsequent cooperative in the same locality may be entered in the system on the initiative of users, under the conditions of Article 5; the condition under paragraph 2 of this Article does not apply to it.

A participant belongs to the locality stated in their account.

## III — Cooperative Members

### Article 7

*Linking of accounts*

Only a participant — a Kolo member or a member without confirmation — may be a cooperative member.

The cooperative submits to the Foundation the first and last name of the cooperative member and the pseudonym of their account, for persons who have consented to this. Upon receipt, the Foundation links the account to the cooperative and enters the first and last name in the identity register under Article 31a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

A person may be a cooperative member within the system in only one cooperative.

If a different first and last name has already been entered alongside the account in the identity register, the Foundation does not link the account to the cooperative until the discrepancy has been resolved.

### Article 8

*Rights of a cooperative member*

A cooperative member has all the rights that belong to them as a Kolo member, or as a member without confirmation. The status of cooperative member adds to those rights only rights within the cooperative: participation in distribution, in cooperative contribution, and in the use of the cooperative's equipment and land, under the cooperative's rules.

The status of cooperative member confers no additional authority in the KOLO system.

### Article 9

*Termination of the status of cooperative member*

The status of cooperative member terminates upon withdrawal or exclusion from the cooperative, of which the cooperative notifies the Foundation, upon the closure of the account, and upon the termination of the cooperative's participation.

The POEN in the cooperative member's record remains theirs. Termination of the status of cooperative member does not delete the first and last name from the identity register; it is deleted upon the termination of the user's status.

## IV — Growth of a Cooperative's Record

### Article 10

*Thresholds*

The Protocol records new POEN records in the cooperative's record when the number of cooperative members reaches the following thresholds:

| Number of cooperative members | POEN |
|---|---|
| 5 (opening of the record) | 50.000 |
| 10 | 100.000 |
| 20 | 200.000 |
| 50 | 500.000 |
| 100 | 1.000.000 |
| 200 | 2.000.000 |
| 500 | 5.000.000 |

Every cooperative member within the meaning of Article 7 is counted towards a threshold.

### Article 11

*Each threshold once*

Each threshold is recorded once and can never be recorded twice.

When the number of cooperative members falls, the POEN recorded for a threshold reached is not written off, and reaching the same threshold again is not recorded again.

A Circle and a cooperative are the same collective form under different names. A Circle whose members register as a cooperative continues as a cooperative with the same record, and the thresholds it reached as a Circle are not recorded again.

Recording is an automatic act of the Protocol and does not count towards the daily limit of Protocol Programs. The POEN recorded enters the total number of recorded POENs. Recording is neither a remuneration nor a grant from the Foundation and does not give rise to any obligation of the cooperative towards the Foundation.

## V — Cooperative's Record and Distribution

### Article 12

*The record cannot be negative*

A cooperative's record cannot be negative. The cooperative disposes only of the POEN recorded in its record.

### Article 13

*Grounds for entries in the record*

A POEN ledger update from or to a cooperative's record is made only on a ground entered in the Protocol:

1) receipt of a good for distribution;

2) issuing of a good from distribution;

3) cooperative contribution;

4) use of equipment, machinery, or land at the cooperative's disposal;

5) a service that the cooperative provides or receives.

A ledger update without a ground under paragraph 1 is not permitted, either towards a user or towards another cooperative.

The actions under this Rulebook are not carried out with the account of a minor user.

### Article 14

*Distribution*

The cooperative receives a good for distribution in the name and on behalf of its cooperative members. Upon receipt, the cooperative updates the ledger in favour of the user who brought the good, from its own record.

The cooperative issues a good to a cooperative member or to another cooperative; upon issuing, the ledger is updated in favour of the cooperative's record.

A good that is not issued or that perishes gives rise to no obligation of the user who brought it.

### Article 15

*Amount*

The number of POENs for the receipt and issuing of a good, for a task, and for the use of equipment is determined by the cooperative, through its bodies and under its own rules.

The Foundation does not determine, recommend, approve, or publish the amount.

### Article 16

*Who acts on behalf of the cooperative*

The cooperative proposes a person whom the Foundation includes in the administrator team. That person carries out the actions in the cooperative's record: entering receipts and issues, publishing tasks, and confirming execution.

The person under paragraph 1 acts in accordance with the acts of the KOLO system and, before being included, signs a statement of confidentiality regarding the data they access.

## VI — Cooperative Contribution

### Article 17

*Task*

A cooperative may publish a task for the community in its locality, with a proposed number of POENs.

Execution is confirmed by the cooperative; the person performing the task does not confirm their own execution.

Upon confirmation, the ledger is updated from the cooperative's record in favour of the person who performed the task. The number of POENs does not express the value of working time.

## VII — Support from the Foundation

### Article 18

The Foundation provides the cooperative with organizational and expert support in its establishment and operation.

The Foundation may, from donation funds, finance the operating costs of the cooperative in dinars, for a specific purpose and by an annex to the cooperation agreement. The financing is without consideration and confers on the Foundation none of the rights under Article 4.

The cooperative participates in the collective procurements of the Foundation as a bidder, under the same conditions as other bidders.

## VIII — Transparency and Personal Data

### Article 19

The records of a cooperative are visible to all participants, under the name of the cooperative; cooperative members are shown in them under their pseudonyms.

The first and last name of a cooperative member is not shown to other users. The Foundation processes it for the purpose of linking accounts and counting cooperative members under Article 10 and keeps it in the identity register under Article 31a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, in accordance with the Privacy Policy and the Records of Processing Activities.

## IX — Termination of Participation

### Article 20

The participation of a cooperative terminates upon termination of the cooperation agreement or upon the deletion of the cooperative from the register.

Upon termination of participation, the cooperative's record is closed, and the POEN recorded in it is annulled by a counter-entry in favour of the Protocol, on the ground under Article 56 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. POEN is not the property of the cooperative and is not transferred to anyone. Users' records created earlier remain intact.

## X — Final Provisions

### Article 21

This Rulebook is adopted and amended by the Management Board (UO) of the KOLO Foundation, under the procedure established in the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

The Cooperatives Module is activated by the Foundation (Rulebook on the KOLO System, Article 54), following a prior update of the data protection impact assessment.

### Article 22

This Rulebook enters into force on the date of its adoption by the Management Board (UO) of the KOLO Foundation.
