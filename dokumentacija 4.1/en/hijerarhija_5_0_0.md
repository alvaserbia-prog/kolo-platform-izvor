# Rulebook on the Hierarchy of Acts of the KOLO System

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1 — Subject matter

(1) This Rulebook governs the system of acts of the KOLO system, their mutual rank, the rules for resolving inconsistencies between acts, and the competence and limits for their adoption and amendment.

(2) Terms from the Rulebook on the KOLO System have the same meaning in this Rulebook.

### Article 2 — Position of this Rulebook

(1) This Rulebook is adopted on the basis of the Statute of the KOLO Foundation (hereinafter: the Statute) and is subordinate to the Statute.

(2) The rank and content of this Rulebook may not be altered by an act of lower rank.

### Article 3 — Whitepaper and informational content

(1) The KOLO System Whitepaper (hereinafter: the Whitepaper) is a conceptual and public document describing the purpose, architecture and principles of the system.

(2) The Whitepaper is not a binding act, does not create rights or obligations and does not enter into the hierarchy set out in this Rulebook.

(3) In the event of a discrepancy between the Whitepaper and any binding act, the binding act prevails.

(4) Paragraphs 2 and 3 apply mutatis mutandis to answers to frequently asked questions, texts on the screens of the platform, videos and other informational content of the Foundation and the platform, without prejudice to Article 10 paragraph 4.

## II — System and Rank of Acts

### Article 4 — Binding acts of the KOLO system

The binding acts of the KOLO system are:

1) the Statute;

2) this Rulebook;

3) the Rulebook on the KOLO System;

4) other rulebooks;

5) personal data protection acts;

6) platform acts;

7) forms and internal acts.

### Article 5 — Hierarchy of acts

(1) The acts referred to in Article 4 points 1–4 form a hierarchical order, in the sequence in which they are listed there. The Statute is the supreme internal act.

(2) An act of higher rank is superior to every act of lower rank.

(3) Personal data protection acts, platform acts, and forms and internal acts do not enter into the hierarchical order referred to in paragraph 1; they apply in parallel, in the manner governed by Articles 9–11.

### Article 6 — Rulebook on the KOLO System

The Rulebook on the KOLO System is the overarching operational act of the KOLO system; it governs the organisation, functioning and internal rules of the system.

### Article 7 — Other rulebooks

(1) Other rulebooks elaborate specific matters within the framework established by the Rulebook on the KOLO System; each bears its own name according to its subject matter.

(2) The other rulebooks of the KOLO system are:

1) the Rulebook on Proof of Reality;

2) the Rulebook on Founding Contribution;

3) the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations;

4) the Rulebook on Operational Contribution;

5) the Rulebook on the Upper Kolo;

6) the Rulebook on Cooperatives;

7) the Rulebook on Farms;

8) the Rulebook on Support Programmes;

9) the Rulebook on the Participation of Children;

10) the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement.

(3) Other rulebooks are mutually equal in rank and are delineated by subject matter.

(4) In addition to the rulebooks listed in paragraph 2, other rulebooks may be adopted when the Rulebook on the KOLO System expressly refers the regulation of a specific matter to a separate rulebook; such a rulebook has the rank of other rulebooks.

### Article 8 — Rule on conflict of acts

(1) In the event of inconsistency between provisions of acts of different rank, the act of higher rank applies.

(2) An act of lower rank may not restrict, amend or repeal what is governed by an act of higher rank, nor govern a specific matter in a manner contrary to it.

(3) A provision of an act of lower rank that conflicts with an act of higher rank does not apply to the extent of the conflict; the remainder of the act remains in force.

(4) An inconsistency between two rulebooks of the same rank is resolved by applying the rulebook whose subject matter is more narrowly and directly related to the matter being regulated.

### Article 9 — Personal data protection acts

(1) Personal data protection acts are the Privacy Policy, the Records of Processing Activities and the Data Protection Impact Assessment.

(2) These acts govern the processing of personal data in the KOLO system and apply in parallel with other acts, supplementing them within their domain.

(3) In matters of personal data processing, the acts referred to in paragraph 1 and the personal data protection regulations prevail; no other act of the KOLO system may govern the processing of data in a manner contrary to them.

### Article 10 — Platform acts

(1) Platform acts are the Terms of Use and the Risk Acceptance Statement; they govern the relationship with the user.

(2) Platform acts must be consistent with the acts in the hierarchical order and may not restrict or deny rights recognised to the user by those acts.

(3) In the event of inconsistency between a platform act and the Rulebook on the KOLO System or any of the other rulebooks, the solution more favourable to the user applies.

(4) Mandatory consumer protection provisions and provisions protecting the weaker contracting party apply directly and remain unaffected by the provisions of platform acts.

### Article 11 — Forms and internal acts

(1) Forms and internal acts are the templates of donation agreements and sponsorship agreements and other acts of an individual or executive nature by which the acts of the KOLO system are implemented.

(2) Forms and internal acts implement the acts in the hierarchical order and must be consistent with them.

(3) Forms and internal acts may not introduce general rules or govern matters reserved for the Rulebook on the KOLO System or other rulebooks.

## III — Adoption and Amendment of Acts

### Article 12 — Adoption and amendment of acts

(1) The Statute is adopted and amended under the procedure prescribed by the Statute and the Law on Endowments and Foundations.

(2) This Rulebook, in all phases of the system, is adopted and amended by the Management Board.

(3) In Phase 1, the Rulebook on the KOLO System and other rulebooks are adopted and amended by the Management Board; an amendment may also be proposed by the founder of the Foundation.

(4) Upon the system's transition to Phase 2, the content of an amendment to the Rulebook on the KOLO System and other rulebooks in the matters referred to in Article 43 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System is determined by a decision of the Upper Kolo, adopted under the procedure set out in the Rulebook on the KOLO System and the Rulebook on the Upper Kolo. The amendment is adopted by the Foundation's Management Board, which is obliged to adopt it and does not assess the expediency of the decision; refusal is possible only on the grounds referred to in Article 46 paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. A decision of the Upper Kolo, to the extent that it requires the spending of the Foundation's dinar funds, has the effect of a recommendation (Article 46 paragraph 6 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). In other matters, the Rulebook on the KOLO System and other rulebooks are amended by the Management Board within its statutory powers.

(5) Personal data protection acts, platform acts, and forms and internal acts are adopted and amended within the competence of the Foundation, in accordance with the Statute, regulations and acts of higher rank.

(6) General acts of the Foundation, within the meaning of the Statute, are adopted in all phases by the Management Board. A decision of a body established by a rulebook is not a general act of the Foundation; it is implemented by an act of the Management Board, in the manner established by the act by which the body was established.

### Article 13 — Limits on amendments

No amendment to the acts of the KOLO system, including a decision adopted in Phase 2, may amend, restrict or repeal the four principles or the two limits of the common good referred to in Article 10 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

## IV — Final Provision

### Article 14 — Entry into force

This Rulebook enters into force on the date of its adoption.
