# Rulebook on Proof of Reality

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1 — Subject matter

(1) This Rulebook governs belonging to the Kolo and the confirmed status of users, the call list, establishing a multiple account, the chain of confirmations, the member recommendation, the daily pace of confirming, supervision, the chain without a loop, the position of founders, the consequences of termination of status on verifications and the procedure upon a false verification.

(2) Terms from the Rulebook on the KOLO System have the same meaning in this Rulebook, in particular verification, confirmation and introduction into the Kolo (Article 2 paragraph 4 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(3) In this Rulebook, the person who gives a verification is called the verifier, and the person to whom it is given the verified person. Those terms denote only roles in the procedure, not a status.

## II — Belonging to the Kolo and Confirmed Status

### Article 2 — One verification

(1) A user is in the Kolo when one valid verification has been recorded for them in the chain of confirmations: a confirmation or an introduction into the Kolo (Article 4). A user's reality in the chain of confirmations is established by one verifier.

(2) A participant cannot be verified again while their verification remains valid. A second verification of the same user is not recorded; the confirmation of a member without confirmation is not a new verification (Article 5).

(3) Belonging to the Kolo has no degrees: no number, percentage or other indicator of a degree of belonging is kept. The difference between a confirmation and an introduction into the Kolo is not a degree of belonging: the reality of a member without confirmation is established by verification in the same way as the reality of a Kolo member, and the difference determines only the actions under Article 6 and other actions that the acts of the Foundation attach to a Kolo member.

(4) The defence against fake accounts does not rest on the number of confirmations, but on the verifier's responsibility for every verification (Chapter IX), on the daily pace of confirming (Article 21), on supervision (Articles 22 to 27) and on the fact that the POEN record for a verification is entered only once the verified person makes a first confirmed contribution (Article 19).

### Article 3 — Effect of verification and confirmed status

(1) Verification conditions access to the system functions that belong to a participant. Confirmed status conditions confirming the reality of other users and other actions that the acts of the Foundation attach to a Kolo member.

(2) An adult user who has been neither confirmed nor introduced into the Kolo has the status of user, except for founders (Article 29). The position of a minor user is governed by the Rulebook on the Participation of Children.

### Article 4 — Confirmation and introduction into the Kolo

(1) The confirmed person becomes a Kolo member, and the person introduced into the Kolo a member without confirmation. A Kolo member introduces into the Kolo a user from the call list (Article 8) after a telephone conversation (Article 12). The type of verification is entered in the verification record.

(2) A verification carried out by a member without confirmation is always an introduction into the Kolo, so the person they introduce is also a member without confirmation. A founder has the position of a Kolo member in the chain of confirmations.

(3) An introduction into the Kolo is an ordinary verification: POEN are recorded on account of it under Article 19, and the daily pace, supervision and responsibility for a false verification apply as they do to a confirmation.

### Article 5 — Confirmation of a member without confirmation

(1) A member without confirmation is confirmed by:

1) a Kolo member, or a founder, who knows them personally; an exchange of goods and services may be the way in which they met, but is not a condition for confirmation;

2) a Kolo keeper after a telephone conversation, under the conditions of Article 16;

3) a Kolo member from the list of linked users (Article 17);

4) the Foundation by its own decision, with a statement of reasons.

(2) The confirmed person thereby becomes a Kolo member. A member without confirmation never becomes a Kolo member automatically.

(3) The confirmation of a member without confirmation is not a new verification: no new verification record is recorded and no POEN is entered on account of it. Who confirmed the member without confirmation, when and in what manner is noted alongside the verification record.

(4) Whoever confirms a member without confirmation is responsible for it as for a confirmation, and Chapter IX applies to that confirmation; the prohibition under Article 28 applies to it as well, except in the case under Article 17 paragraph 3. If the confirmation is established to be false, it is annulled, and the user remains a member without confirmation for as long as their introduction into the Kolo remains valid.

### Article 6 — Position of a member without confirmation

(1) A member without confirmation has the rights and obligations that the acts of the Foundation attach to a participant; they do not have the rights attached to a Kolo member or to a confirmed user, unless an act expressly provides otherwise.

(2) A member without confirmation may not:

1) see the call list or introduce users from that list into the Kolo;

2) confirm anyone (neither another member without confirmation, nor the existence of a child, nor the fulfilment of the conditions for a support programme) nor see a request to confirm the existence of a child;

3) give a member recommendation (Article 20);

4) activate ZRNO;

5) perform supervision (Article 23).

(3) A member without confirmation enters ZRNO under the conditions of Article 22 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, but does not activate it: they have no vote, are not part of the composition of the Upper Kolo and do not delegate a vote; they may write off free ZRNO. By entering ZRNO they do not become a Kolo keeper; they become a Kolo keeper when they are confirmed.

(4) A member without confirmation may register a farm as holder, without members, under the conditions of the Rulebook on Farms. Their child does not become a Kolo child; the position of the child is governed by the Rulebook on the Participation of Children.

### Article 7 — Verification upon coming of age

(1) When a minor user comes of age, the verification is given to them automatically by the first parent, in the order of linking to the child's account, who is a Kolo member, or a founder; that verification is a confirmation.

(2) If no parent is a Kolo member or a founder, but a parent is a member without confirmation, the verification is given automatically by the first such parent in the order of linking; that verification is an introduction into the Kolo.

(3) If no parent is in the Kolo, the adult user has the status of user.

(4) The transition of a minor user to an adult account is further governed by the Rulebook on the Participation of Children.

## III — Call List and Multiple Account

### Article 8 — Who comes onto the call list

(1) A user who does not know any Kolo member may come onto the list of users awaiting a call (call list). A user comes onto the call list:

1) when they themselves ask for a conversation, in a request in which they state their place, briefly what they do and when they are available for a conversation;

2) when the Foundation establishes the receipt of their public donation in an amount of at least 5,000 dinars, made in one payment, where the name of the payer from the bank statement matches the user's name and the payer is not attached to another account;

3) when the Foundation establishes the receipt of a sponsorship in money under an application the user submitted, under the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations;

4) when, as the holder, they submit a registration of a farm, regardless of whether the farm is entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings.

(2) In the cases under points 2 to 4 of paragraph 1, the Foundation, after comparing the data on the payer, the applicant or the holder respectively with the account, offers the list to the user, and the user is placed on the call list when they themselves leave the data under Article 9. A payment, a sponsorship and a registration of a farm do not confirm reality.

(3) An anonymous donation, the sum of several smaller payments, a payment for which it has been established that the payer is attached to another account, and sponsorship in goods and services do not place a user on the call list.

(4) A member without confirmation who seeks confirmation after a telephone conversation also comes onto the call list, once they meet the conditions under Article 16 paragraph 1. A member without confirmation who has entered ZRNO comes onto the list in first place.

(5) A minor user does not come onto the call list. An account for which the Foundation has established under Article 14 that it is another account of the same person does not come onto the list.

### Article 9 — Entry onto the list and consent

(1) Before coming onto the list, the user, or the member without confirmation, enters their first and last name, place and telephone number and is informed that a Kolo member, or a Kolo keeper, will call them and that they will see their first and last name and telephone number, as well as the indication under Article 13 paragraph 2. They are also informed that a Kolo member who calls never asks for money, a password or a code from the telephone, and that whoever asks for it is not calling on behalf of the system. They leave the data themselves, with explicit consent that the data be accessible to Kolo members in the manner under this Rulebook.

(2) The first and last name is entered in the identity register (Article 31 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), and the place and telephone number alongside the account. Those data remain alongside the account as long as it exists and are deleted upon its closure; the user may change the place and telephone number, but may not delete them.

(3) The telephone number from the list is not used for any other purpose and is not displayed in any other part of the platform, except when the user themselves switches on its display on their profile.

### Article 10 — Who sees the list and opening the data

(1) The call list is seen only by Kolo members. The call list is not seen by members without confirmation. The request of a member without confirmation for confirmation is seen and opened only by Kolo keepers, because only they confirm after a telephone conversation (Article 16).

(2) The first and last name and the telephone number are not visible until a Kolo member opens them by a click. By opening the data, the Kolo member opens the call: the data are accessible only to them and only while the call is open. A call is open to only one Kolo member at a time, for at most 10 minutes, and one Kolo member may have only one call open at a time.

(3) The conversation is conducted by the Kolo member of their own free will: no one obliges them to do so, and by the conversation they do not perform any work for the Foundation. Before the name and number are opened for the first time, the Kolo member declares that they will use them only for the conversation for the purpose of getting acquainted, confirmation or introduction into the Kolo, will not record them and will not pass them on to anyone; only the time of the declaration is recorded. They process the name and number under the authority of the Foundation and solely for that purpose.

(4) The Foundation records which Kolo member opened the data. That record is accessible only to the Foundation's administrator team, is kept as long as the account of the called user exists and is not exported through the platform, and the called user receives it on a request sent to privatnost@ekolo.rs.

(5) Any other use of the name and number is an abuse and a serious breach of the Terms of Use (Articles 28 and 32).

### Article 11 — Time limit for the call

(1) The Foundation is obliged to ensure that a user who has come onto the call list is called by a Kolo member at the latest within 7 days of the day on which they came onto the list. The same time limit applies to a member without confirmation, who is called by a Kolo keeper.

(2) A member without confirmation who has entered ZRNO is called by a Kolo keeper as soon as possible, and at the latest within 3 days of the day on which they came onto the list.

(3) The Foundation ensures the call by displaying the request to Kolo members, or to Kolo keepers respectively, together with the time limit, and by separately marking a request whose time limit has passed. The platform shows the user by which day they will be called. To a member without confirmation who does not meet the conditions under Article 16 paragraph 1, it shows which condition is not met and from which day it will be met.

### Article 12 — After the conversation and removal from the list

(1) Coming onto the call list does not confirm reality. Neither the donation or sponsorship, nor the contribution recorded on account of them, depends on it; that contribution is recorded under the general rules of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations.

(2) A Kolo member who calls a user and, after the conversation, assesses that they know them well enough to answer for the verification may introduce them into the Kolo; a verification after such a conversation is an introduction into the Kolo (Article 15 paragraph 3). The conversation is not in itself a verification and does not oblige the Kolo member to introduce the user into the Kolo. A member without confirmation from the list may be confirmed by a Kolo keeper after a conversation under the conditions of Article 16.

(3) The request and the data on the list are deleted when the user enters the Kolo, or when the member without confirmation is confirmed, when they withdraw the request or when their status as a user terminates. The record of which Kolo member opened the data is kept under Article 10 paragraph 4, and the first and last name, place and telephone number under Article 9 paragraph 2.

### Article 13 — Comparison of data

(1) The first and last name and the telephone number are compared with the names and numbers kept alongside other accounts, in order to prevent multiple accounts.

(2) Where the same telephone number, or the same first and last name, is kept alongside another account, the Kolo member who opened the call sees, next to the name and number, an indication that a match exists and the place of the other account, but not its pseudonym; both accounts are seen only by the Foundation's administrator team.

(3) A match is not a prohibition and prevents neither entry onto the list nor introduction into the Kolo: the same number may be shared by members of a household, and the same name by namesakes. Only the Foundation decides on a multiple account, under Article 14.

### Article 14 — Establishing a multiple account

(1) Each natural person may have one account (Article 8 of the Terms of Use). The verifier does not guarantee that the verified person has no other account, because they know the person, not their accounts; that two accounts are accounts of the same person is established by the Foundation under this Article.

(2) Only the Foundation establishes this, never the platform by itself. The procedure is initiated on the basis of the indication under Article 13 paragraph 2, on a note by a Kolo member after a conversation, on a report, or on knowledge obtained in another way.

(3) Before establishing this, the Foundation compares the data kept alongside both accounts (telephone number, first and last name, the payer from the bank statement, place and the chain of confirmations) and contacts the person on both accounts. The decision is taken with a statement of reasons communicated to the person. A complaint against the decision is permitted within 15 days (Article 40 of the Terms of Use).

(4) Where it establishes that two accounts are accounts of the same person, the Foundation determines the account the person retains, and the other account ceases. The contribution recorded to the ceasing account is annulled (Article 33 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), and the verifications that account received and gave fall under the rules of Chapter IX on accounts that are not unique. From the account the person retains, the net amount of the re-registrations from the ceasing account in favour of the retained account, reduced by the re-registrations in the opposite direction, is written off; the write-off is executed even where the record thereby becomes negative (Article 17 paragraph 2 point 8 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), and the person is notified of the write-off.

(5) Where the ceasing account was used to acquire POEN or a vote, the Foundation may also exclude the person (Article 32 of the Terms of Use).

(6) The records of other persons to whom POEN was re-registered from the ceasing account are not affected. Together with the decision under paragraph 4, the Foundation opens a single supervision case concerning the recipients of those re-registrations, with the sole question of whether the recipient is the same person or whether the re-registrations were fictitious (Article 28 of the Terms of Use). While the case is pending, the recipients' accounts are not restricted, and the recipient is not notified until a measure is imposed on them; a measure is imposed only under the procedure under this Article, or under the Terms of Use. The case is opened only upon establishing a multiple account and is not monitoring of ledger updates.

## IV — Chain of Confirmations

### Article 15 — Verification mechanism

(1) The verifier (a Kolo member, a founder or a member without confirmation) establishes the reality of a user on the basis of direct acquaintance, by a confirmation or by an introduction into the Kolo. The verifier confirms two things: reality (the verified person exists as a natural person) and continuity (the same person accesses the system), and declares that they are not aware that the verified person has another account in the system. The verifier does not guarantee uniqueness (Article 14).

(2) Verification is an act of personal acquaintance, not of document verification. The verifier does not collect or submit the personal documents of the verified person.

(3) Verification is based on direct personal acquaintance sufficient for the verifier to confirm, on their own responsibility, the reality and continuity of the verified person. This Rulebook does not prescribe the manner of acquiring such acquaintance nor require physical presence at the moment of verification; the verifier alone assesses whether they know the verified person well enough.

(4) The user seeking verification generates a one-time code by which they consent to the verification and bind their account to that act, and a verifier who knows them carries out the verification using that code. That mechanism does not collect the personal data of the verified person and does not constitute proof of presence, but rather confirmation of the verified person's consent and of the identity of the account.

(5) The verified person is notified of the completed verification and may report it if they do not know the verifier.

(6) The verifier is responsible for the truthfulness of the verification, not for monitoring the verified person after it. A false verification (Article 32) entails the consequences under Chapter IX.

(7) A user may also establish the contact needed for verification by publishing a listing in which they present themselves to Kolo members, as well as by a request for a conversation (Article 8).

### Article 16 — Confirmation after a telephone conversation

(1) A member without confirmation whom no Kolo member knows personally may be confirmed by a Kolo keeper after a telephone conversation, if all of the following conditions are met:

1) the member without confirmation has been in the Kolo for at least 30 days from the introduction;

2) no exchange report or listing report against them has been upheld;

3) no supervision case (Article 27) against them has been concluded with a finding of false verification.

(2) A member without confirmation who meets the conditions under paragraph 1 may request a conversation for confirmation and then comes onto the call list (Article 8 paragraph 4). The platform notifies them of this as soon as the conditions are met.

(3) A Kolo keeper confirms only if, after the conversation, they assess that they can answer for the reality and continuity of the member without confirmation (Article 15 paragraph 1); the conversation in itself is not a confirmation. They are responsible for the confirmation under Chapter IX. A confirmation after a telephone conversation is always subject to supervision (Article 22) and is counted in the daily pace of confirming (Article 21). No POEN is entered on account of it.

### Article 17 — List of linked users

(1) When a user becomes a Kolo member, the platform shows them the list of linked users: those whom they introduced into the Kolo while they were a member without confirmation and who are still members without confirmation (one level down), and the one who introduced them into the Kolo, if that person is also still a member without confirmation (one level up).

(2) A Kolo member confirms from the list of linked users those whom they know personally, by marking them, without a new conversation. Each marked person is a separate confirmation of a member without confirmation (Article 5): the one who marked them is responsible for it, under Chapter IX, it is subject to supervision under Article 22 and is counted in the daily pace of confirming (Article 21). No POEN is entered on account of a confirmation from the list of linked users.

(3) A Kolo member may also confirm the member without confirmation who introduced them into the Kolo (one level up), by way of the exception under Article 28 paragraph 2. Such a confirmation is always subject to supervision.

(4) The list of linked users covers only one level down and one level up. Everyone who is confirmed from the list of linked users becomes a Kolo member and receives their own list of linked users.

### Article 18 — Verification record

(1) Every verification is recorded in a verification record containing:

1) the identifier of the verifier (pseudonym);

2) the ordinal number of the verifier's verification;

3) the identifier of the verified person (pseudonym);

4) the timestamp of the verification;

5) the type of verification (confirmation or introduction into the Kolo), and, alongside an introduction into the Kolo, the information on who confirmed the introduced person and when, and in what manner (personally, from the list of linked users or after a telephone conversation), where this occurs, and the identifier of the supervisor of that confirmation or an empty field if it is not subject to supervision;

6) the identifier of the supervisor (pseudonym) or an empty field if the verification is not subject to supervision.

(2) A verification and a confirmation that are subject to supervision are supplemented, once the supervision outcome is recorded, with supervision data: the supervision outcome and, alongside the outcomes "needs review" and "disputed", the subject of suspicion and the reason code (Article 24). Supervision data are kept for each supervisor who recorded an outcome.

(3) Supervision data form part of the record of the chain of confirmations and are not accessible to other users (Article 30 paragraph 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System); they are shown neither to the verifier nor to the verified person.

(4) Verification records constitute the record of the chain of confirmations (Article 30 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

### Article 19 — POEN for verification

(1) Upon the recording of a verification record, the Protocol notes a contribution: 1,000 POEN to the verifier and 1,000 POEN to the verified person. The amount is the same for both parties and for everyone, and for the same verified person it is recorded once. No one else in the chain of confirmations receives anything on account of that verification: neither the person who verified the verifier, nor the supervisor.

(2) The noted contribution is entered in the Protocol once the verified person makes their first confirmed contribution to the common good, namely:

1) the first listing by which they offer a good or a service, approved within the meaning of Article 38 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System;

2) a public donation to the Foundation upon receipt of which the contribution has been recorded under the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations;

3) a sponsorship under which the contribution has been recorded in the record of the user who submitted the application;

4) an operational contribution whose completion has been confirmed under the Rulebook on Operational Contribution.

(3) Each of the forms under paragraph 2 has been confirmed by the Foundation, or by a Kolo keeper; none of them is achieved by the user's own decision. Re-registration of POEN between users is not a contribution within the meaning of this Article.

(4) Where the condition under paragraph 2 is met before the verification record is recorded, as well as in the case of verification upon coming of age (Article 7), to which the condition does not apply, the entry occurs at the moment the verification record is recorded.

(5) Until entered, a noted contribution does not constitute a POEN record: it forms no part of any user's record, nor of the total number of recorded POEN in the system. The verification record, the verification and the rights arising from them do not depend on the POEN entry under this Article.

(6) If the verification record ceases to be valid before the entry, the noted contribution lapses; the annulment of POEN records and the restitution under Article 36 do not apply, since no entry was ever made.

(7) The Foundation may enter a noted contribution even where the condition under paragraph 2 is not met, with a statement of reasons that is recorded, for a user who contributes to the common good in a manner not achieved through any of the forms under paragraph 2.

(8) Supervision of a verification is not recorded as a contribution and no POEN is entered to the supervisor for it. The total recording per verification amounts to 2,000 POEN.

(9) The recording of POEN for verification is an automatic act of the Protocol (Article 14 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), through the channel under Article 19 paragraph 1 point 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

### Article 20 — Member recommendation

(1) An adult Kolo member, as well as a founder, may recommend another adult user. A member without confirmation and a minor user do not give recommendations. A recommendation is given on the user's profile, when that profile is accessible to the person recommending. No one may recommend themselves.

(2) One user may recommend another once. A recommendation may be withdrawn at any time.

(3) Only the number of recommendations received is displayed on the user's profile. Who recommended the user is not displayed to anyone: neither to the user who received the recommendation, nor to other users.

(4) A recommendation is the personal opinion of the person who gives it. A recommendation is not a verification and does not replace it. It is not recorded as a contribution, does not produce a POEN record and does not affect the user's status, access to platform functions, verification or the display of listings. The Foundation does not check recommendations and does not vouch for a user whom someone has recommended.

(5) Upon termination of a user's status, the recommendations they gave and the recommendations they received are deleted.

## V — Daily Pace of Confirming and Supervision

### Article 21 — Daily pace of confirming

(1) The number of verifications a verifier may perform is not limited. What is limited is the pace: the verifications the verifier has performed in the course of one calendar day according to Serbian time are counted, including confirmations of members without confirmation, confirmations from the list of linked users and confirmations after a telephone conversation.

(2) Before the third and before the fourth verification on the same day, the verifier is warned that rapidly confirming a larger number of persons is regarded as a sign of possible abuse of the chain of confirmations and that, if they continue, their confirming will be stopped.

(3) The fifth verification on the same day is not recorded. The verifier's ability to verify is stopped until the end of that day, and the Foundation is notified thereof. The stop applies exclusively to verifying other users; other platform functions remain available.

(4) Stopping verifying is neither a suspension nor a measure against the verifier. On the occasion of the notification, the Foundation may initiate the procedure under Chapter IX or the suspension procedure under the Terms of Use.

(5) The rule in this Article applies to all verifiers, including founders, Kolo keepers and members without confirmation. It does not apply to verification upon coming of age (Article 7).

### Article 22 — What is subject to supervision

(1) The following are subject to supervision:

1) verifications performed by members without confirmation and by Kolo members who are neither founders nor Kolo keepers;

2) the confirmation of a member without confirmation given by a Kolo member who is neither a founder nor a Kolo keeper, including a confirmation from the list of linked users;

3) the confirmation of the existence of a child under the Rulebook on the Participation of Children given by a Kolo member who is neither a founder nor a Kolo keeper;

4) always, regardless of who gives them: a confirmation after a telephone conversation (Article 16) and the confirmation of the introducer from the list of linked users (Article 17 paragraph 3);

5) always, an introduction into the Kolo after a telephone conversation with a user from the call list, even when carried out by a Kolo keeper; only a founder is exempt.

(2) Other verifications and confirmations performed by founders and Kolo keepers, as well as the confirmation of a member without confirmation by decision of the Foundation, are not subject to supervision.

### Article 23 — Supervisor

(1) A supervisor is every Kolo keeper. The supervisory function follows automatically from status, without appointment.

(2) The supervisor examines the legitimacy of the verification performed and records the supervision outcome (Article 24). Supervision carries no POEN (Article 19 paragraph 8).

(3) Supervision may not be performed by a Kolo keeper who took part in the supervised verification or confirmation, neither as verifier nor as the verified person. The same supervisor may not record an outcome twice on the same verification record.

(4) The provisions of this Chapter on the verification, the verification record, the verifier and the verified person apply also to the confirmation of a member without confirmation, to the person giving the confirmation and to the confirmed person.

### Article 24 — Supervision outcome

(1) Supervision is performed after verification. A verification takes effect upon the recording of the verification record; the supervisor subsequently examines it and records the outcome in the verification record.

(2) The supervision outcome is one of three:

1) sound: the supervisor finds nothing objectionable;

2) needs review: something is unclear to the supervisor and they ask that another supervisor look at the record; the record remains available to the other supervisors. This outcome is not an assertion that the verification is false, but an invitation for someone else to look;

3) disputed: the supervisor considers that the verification is untrue.

(3) Alongside the outcomes "needs review" and "disputed", the supervisor must enter the subject of suspicion (the verifier, the verified person, both users or a part of the network) and a reason code. The outcome "sound" requires no statement of reasons.

(4) The reason codes are:

1) they do not know each other: there are grounds to suspect that the verifier and the verified person are not directly acquainted;

2) account shows no signs of reality: the verified person's account shows no signs that a real person stands behind it;

3) duplicate account: there are grounds to suspect that the verified person already has an account in the system;

4) pattern of verifications: the distribution or frequency of verifications indicates coordinated conduct;

5) report by the verified person: the verified person has reported that they do not know the verifier (Article 15 paragraph 5);

6) other: a reason not covered by the preceding points, with a brief description.

(5) No time limit is prescribed for recording a supervision outcome. The supervision outcome does not affect the verifier's ability to perform further verifications and does not alter the effect of the verification; a verification is annulled solely under the procedure under Chapter IX.

### Article 25 — Order and signals

(1) Verifications performed by a member without confirmation are shown to supervisors first in the supervision queue. The order does not alter the effect of the verification.

(2) With each verification record awaiting supervision, the platform shows the supervisor signals:

1) the verification is the second or a later one performed by the verifier on the same day;

2) the verifier was a member without confirmation at the time of the verification;

3) it is an introduction into the Kolo from the call list, a confirmation after a telephone conversation or the confirmation of the introducer from the list of linked users;

4) the verified person has not carried out any exchange 7, 14 or 30 days after the verification;

5) the verifier and the verified person are not from the same place and have not exchanged with each other.

(3) Signals are derived from data already kept in the system and are not stored as a separate record. A signal is not a supervision outcome or a claim that the verification is disputed, does not alter the effect of the verification and does not release a verification without signals from supervision.

### Article 26 — Closed group check

From data already kept in the system (the record of the chain of confirmations and the POEN ledger), the Foundation identifies groups of members without confirmation who have introduced one another into the Kolo and who exchange only within the group, without exchange with a Kolo member. The check only shows the group to the Foundation, which may invite the members of the group to a conversation. It produces no effect whatsoever towards a user: it does not change their status, verification or records, is not a supervision outcome or a claim that the verification is false, and does not in itself initiate the procedure under Chapter IX.

### Article 27 — Supervision case

(1) Recording the outcome "needs review" or "disputed" automatically forms a supervision case. The case contains the designation of the verification record, the recorded outcomes, the subject of suspicion and the reason code.

(2) The supervision case is kept by the Foundation's administrator team and is available to the Management Board. It is not available to other supervisors, to the verifier, to the verified person or to the public.

(3) The supervision case is a record, not a body. Forming a case does not in itself produce legal effect towards a user and does not mean that the verification is false.

(4) A case is closed by the establishment of a false verification or by a finding that there are no grounds for the suspicion. A case closed by a finding of no grounds is deleted upon the expiry of 90 days from closure; a suspicion that was not confirmed does not remain as a permanent record about a user. If no other supervisor responds following the outcome "needs review", the case remains open, without effect towards a user.

## VI — Chain Without a Loop

### Article 28 — Chain without a loop

(1) A verifier may not verify anyone from their own ascending line: the person who verified them, nor the person who verified that verifier, and so on upward, to a founder.

(2) The only exception to the prohibition under paragraph 1 is the confirmation of the introducer from the list of linked users (Article 17 paragraph 3): a Kolo member may confirm the member without confirmation who directly introduced them into the Kolo. The exception does not extend further upward, nor to verification.

(3) The prohibition under paragraph 1 is determined from the verifications in force; when a verification is annulled, the prohibition that arose from it ceases as well.

## VII — Founders

### Article 29 — Founders

(1) Founders are the initial Kolo keepers, determined at the establishment of the system: the person entered in the register of the Serbian Business Registers Agency as the founder of the Foundation and the persons whom the Management Board designated at that time by a decision, with their identity publicly disclosed on the platform.

(2) The circle of founders is closed (Article 58 paragraph 4 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(3) Founders are confirmed from the establishment of the account, and that confirmed status does not derive from the chain of confirmations. The reality of the person from the register derives from the public record; the reality of persons designated by a decision of the Management Board is confirmed directly by the Management Board, with their identity publicly disclosed.

(4) Founders cannot be verified in the chain of confirmations.

(5) With regard to verification, founders have the rights of Kolo keepers; their verifications are not subject to supervision, not even in the case of an introduction into the Kolo after a telephone conversation with a user from the call list, and the daily pace of confirming applies to them as well. A confirmation after a telephone conversation and the confirmation of the introducer are subject to supervision even when given by a founder (Article 22 paragraph 1 point 4).

## VIII — Consequences of Termination of Status on Verifications

### Article 30 — Termination of the verifier's status

(1) The termination of the status of a user who was the verifier of other users (withdrawal, exclusion, death) does not affect the verifications they performed. The users whom they verified retain their verification, status and the POEN records arising from those verifications.

(2) The termination of the verifier's status is not a ground for annulling a verification. A verification record whose verifier has ceased to be a user is processed in accordance with Article 33 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, but remains valid and continues to form part of the record of the chain of confirmations.

(3) A noted contribution under Article 19 that has not been entered by the time the verifier's status terminates is entered, once the conditions are met, only to the verified person.

### Article 31 — Termination of verification and of confirmed status

(1) A verification terminates solely by annulment under the procedure under Chapter IX.

(2) A user whose verification has terminated becomes a user. They lose access to the functions that belong to a participant, but retain their account and may be verified again through the chain of confirmations. The consequences for entered ZRNO are governed by Article 29 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

(3) When only the confirmation of a member without confirmation is annulled, confirmed status terminates, and the user remains a member without confirmation for as long as their introduction into the Kolo remains valid (Article 29 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(4) This Article does not concern a founder, because a founder's confirmed status does not derive from verification (Article 29).

## IX — False Verification and Restitution

### Article 32 — Establishing a false verification

(1) A false verification is a verification by which the verifier confirmed the reality of a person who does not exist as a natural person or whose continuity is not ensured, as well as a verification of a person whom the verifier knew to have another account in the system. A verification of a person who is subsequently established to have another account (Article 14), where the verifier did not know this, is not a false verification.

(2) A false verification is established by the Foundation, with a statement of reasons, in every phase of the system.

(3) The procedure is initiated on a supervision case (Article 27), on a report by the verified person (Article 15 paragraph 5) or on knowledge obtained in another way. Establishment is made separately for each verification.

(4) The procedure under this Chapter applies to every verification, regardless of whether it is a confirmation or an introduction into the Kolo, as well as to the confirmation of a member without confirmation and to the confirmation of the existence of a child under the Rulebook on the Participation of Children.

### Article 33 — Consequences of an established false verification

(1) Upon the establishment of a false verification, that verification is annulled, and it terminates for the verified person (Article 31). Upon the establishment of a false confirmation of a member without confirmation, that confirmation is annulled.

(2) The establishment of one false verification triggers a review of the other verifications of the same verifier, but does not in itself annul them. Each of them is annulled solely if it is itself established as false under Article 32.

(3) The verification of a user who exists as a natural person, who is unique and whose continuity is ensured remains in force even where the same verifier performed a false verification in another case. Truthfulness is assessed by reference to the user to whom the verification relates, not by reference to the verifier.

### Article 34 — Cascade of annulment

(1) The annulment of a verification is carried further solely through accounts for which it has been established that no real person stands behind them or that they are not unique.

(2) The establishment for such an account annuls all verifications that the account touches, both those it performed and those it received, without separate establishment under Article 32 for each of them.

(3) The cascade stops at the first user for whom it has not been established that they are non-existent or non-unique: for that user, only the verification received from such an account is annulled, while the verifications they themselves performed remain in force.

(4) A network of fabricated accounts is brought down by an establishment being made for each of its accounts; the order of establishment does not affect the outcome.

(5) The termination of a verification does not in itself trigger a cascade. A user whose verification has terminated has the position under Article 31, and the verifications they performed remain in force.

### Article 35 — Scope of the annulment of POEN records

(1) The annulment of a verification annuls solely the POEN records that arose from that verification through the channel under Article 19: 1,000 POEN to the verifier and 1,000 POEN to the verified person.

(2) POEN records arising through other channels (through exchange, operational contribution, financial contribution, sponsorship, growth of cooperatives, founding contribution, contribution for a first listing or farm contribution) are not annulled. Article 33 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System does not apply to the annulment under this Article; a financial contribution is non-refundable (Article 13 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System) and cannot be annulled indirectly.

(3) Every annulment of a POEN record is accompanied by a counter-entry of the Protocol in the same amount, so the zero-sum rule remains preserved (Article 17 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

### Article 36 — Restitution

(1) If the user whose POEN records are being annulled does not hold enough recorded POEN to cover the annulment, the uncovered part is restitution. Everyone whose record is reduced by restitution is notified, together with the ground and the amount.

(2) The uncovered part of an annulment affecting the verified person passes to the verifier from that verification, and their record is reduced by the same amount. The uncovered part of an annulment affecting the verifier themselves remains on their record. A negative POEN record may arise solely with a verifier; the record of the verified person may fall to zero at most.

(3) Restitution is not a penalty and is not subject to an upper limit.

(4) Restitution is not a debt. The Foundation holds no claim against the user on the basis of restitution, may not collect, assign or enforce it, and does not present it as an asset. The effect of restitution exists solely within the system.

(5) Restitution does not prevent a user from exchanging goods and services. POEN that reach the user first fill the restitution; the user disposes of their POEN record only once the restitution has been settled.

(6) Restitution is not exclusion. Suspension and exclusion are separate measures imposed under the procedure established by the Terms of Use; restitution neither replaces, implies nor precludes them.

(7) Restitution persists upon termination of the user's status; a negative record is not annulled upon termination of status and does not pass to the Protocol (Article 33 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). A negative record under this Article is an exception under Article 17 paragraph 2 point 1 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

### Article 37 — Position of a user whose verification is annulled through no fault of their own

(1) For a user whose verification has been annulled, and for whom it has not been established that they are non-existent or non-unique, the verification terminates and the POEN records under Article 19 arising from that verification are annulled, at most down to zero. The uncovered part passes to the verifier as restitution under Article 36 and does not burden the user.

(2) Such a user may be verified again under the general rules of this Rulebook, and the POEN under Article 19 are then recorded for them again. They may also be verified by the verifier who introduced them into the chain through the annulled verification (Article 28 paragraph 3).

### Article 38 — False verifier

(1) A false verifier is subject to measures in accordance with the rules on termination and suspension of status established by the Terms of Use. An established false verification is, as a rule, a ground for excluding the verifier; the Foundation decides on exclusion under the procedure under the Terms of Use, subject to the right to lodge a complaint.

(2) Restitution under Article 36 arises by the annulment itself and is not a measure under paragraph 1. The imposition or non-imposition of suspension or exclusion does not affect restitution, nor does restitution replace those measures.

## X — Final Provisions

### Article 39 — Aligned existing verifications (transitional provision)

(1) The alignment of the contribution recorded under verifications performed before the introduction of the condition of the first confirmed contribution (Article 19 paragraph 2) has been carried out once. The alignment is not a measure against the user: it does not presuppose an irregularity and does not initiate the procedure under Chapter IX, and the verification record and the verification remain unchanged.

(2) The write-off is executed even where the record thereby becomes negative (Article 17 paragraph 2 point 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System); the restitution under Article 36 does not apply to that record.

(3) The noted contribution does not lapse by the alignment: it is entered when the condition under Article 19 paragraph 2 is met.

### Article 40 — Entry into force

This Rulebook enters into force on the date of its adoption.
