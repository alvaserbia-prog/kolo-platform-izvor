# Terms of Use of the KOLO Platform

*Date of entry into force: 16.06.2026.*

## Chapter I — Introductory Provisions

### Article 1 — Subject Matter

These Terms of Use (hereinafter: the Terms) govern the rights and obligations between the KOLO Foundation (hereinafter: the Foundation) and every person who accesses or uses the KOLO Platform (hereinafter: the Platform).

The Terms of Use are a platform act within the meaning of the Rulebook on the Hierarchy of Acts of the KOLO System. The Terms must be consistent with the Rulebook on the KOLO System (hereinafter: the Rulebook) and may not narrow or deprive users of rights recognized by the Rulebook. In the event of inconsistency between these Terms and the Rulebook, the provision more favorable to the user shall apply.

Mandatory provisions on consumer protection and protection of the weaker contracting party apply directly and remain unaffected by the provisions of these Terms.

The Platform is intended for persons living in the Republic of Serbia. The place is selected from the code list of settlements in Serbia, donations are received in dinars, and the Foundation is registered in Serbia. Translations of the Platform and of the acts into other languages exist for members of national minorities and foreigners living in Serbia, and not for offering services to persons in other states.

### Article 2 — Definitions

Terms defined in the Rulebook have the same meaning in these Terms. The terms user, participant, member without confirmation, Kolo member, Kolo keeper, Kolo, confirmation and introduction into the Kolo have the meaning established by Article 2 of the Rulebook: a user is any person with an account on the Platform, regardless of status, and that is a user of the service within the meaning of the regulations; a user is a child, a participant or, where neither, simply a user, and a participant is a person who is in the Kolo. The Kolo comprises members without confirmation, Kolo members and Kolo keepers. The Kolo is not a legal entity, and belonging to the Kolo, including the position of a member without confirmation, is not membership in the Foundation or in any other legal entity. In addition, the following terms are used in these Terms:

**Platform** — the information system through which users access the KOLO system; the technical infrastructure for recording, exchange, and governance.

**Account** — a user's personal account on the Platform, created by registration, which enables access to system functions according to the user's status.

**Exchange space** — the part of the Platform intended for advertising and arranging the exchange of goods and services between users.

**Listing** — a publication in the exchange space by which a user offers or seeks a good or a service. By a listing, a user may present themselves to Kolo members; even then, their reality is confirmed only by verification in the chain of confirmations — by a confirmation, on the basis of personal acquaintance, or by introduction into the Kolo, after a conversation (Article 16 and Article 16b).

**Session** — the period of active use of the Platform between the user's login and logout.

### Article 3 — The Foundation

The KOLO Foundation is a non-profit private legal entity registered in the Register of Endowments and Foundations in the Republic of Serbia, registration number 28836627, tax identification number (PIB) 115840443, with its registered address at Šetalište 16, 25000 Sombor.

Contact address for all questions relating to these Terms: kontakt@ekolo.rs.

### Article 4 — Legal Nature of the Platform

The Platform is a contribution recording system and governance infrastructure of the KOLO system. The Platform is not a payment system, payment institution, bank, exchange, or investment platform.

The units recorded on the Platform — POEN and ZRNO — are not money, electronic money, cryptocurrency, digital assets, or securities within the meaning of the applicable regulations of the Republic of Serbia. A POEN record is not a claim either against the Foundation or against anyone else, it is not issued in exchange for a payment, in a ledger update the Foundation does not receive, hold or transfer monetary funds and does not charge for the update, and there is no payout or redemption from the record. The legal nature of POEN and ZRNO is established by the Rulebook (Article 13).

### Article 5 — System Phase

The KOLO system is in Phase 1 within the meaning of the Rulebook. In this phase, system governance is exercised by the founder of the Foundation and the Foundation (Article 42 of the Rulebook). The founders are the initial Kolo keepers designated under Article 14 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality (Arts. 2 and 82 of the Rulebook); the capacity of founder does not confer the powers of the Management Board. The identity of the founders is publicly published on the platform, and the founders' shares within the meaning of the Rulebook on Founding Contribution are publicly available within the founding-contribution system.

Certain functionalities provided for by the Rulebook are activated as modules according to their own prerequisites. Circles and internationalization are not operational. Social programmes, the **Children Module**, and the **Cooperatives Module** are activated; the participation of minors is governed by the Rulebook on the Participation of Children, and the participation of cooperatives by the Rulebook on Cooperatives.

The Upper Kolo and the voting right of Kolo keepers are activated upon the system's transition to Phase 2, in accordance with the conditions established by the Rulebook.

These Terms will be updated in accordance with the development of the system. Users will be notified of amendments in accordance with Article 40 of these Terms.

### Article 6 — Acceptance of the Terms

By using the Platform, the user confirms that they have read, understood, and accepted these Terms in their entirety, together with the Platform's Privacy Policy. The Terms and the Privacy Policy are accepted by checking the confirmation box during registration, separately for each, because they relate to different matters.

The acceptance is recorded. Alongside it, the moment of acceptance, the designation of the version of the act accepted, and the text displayed to the user are recorded. Without such a recorded acceptance, the account is not created. The manner in which that record is kept, and the data that are not recorded alongside it, are set out in Article 4a of the Privacy Policy.

The user confirms that they are familiar with the Rulebook on the KOLO System, which is publicly available on the Platform.

A person who does not accept these Terms may not use the Platform.

## Chapter II — Registration and Account

### Article 7 — Registration Conditions

The right to register is held by any natural person who has reached 18 years of age, holds a valid email address, accepts these Terms and the Platform's Privacy Policy, and has not previously been excluded from the Platform without a special Foundation decision on re-admission.

A minor who has reached seven years of age participates on the Platform under a special regime governed by the Rulebook on the Participation of Children. A minor's account may be opened by a parent or legal guardian, or by the minor themselves — in which case, until it is taken over by a parent, the account has only a profile and friendships made by scanning a code in person, without other Platform functions. A minor does not enter an email address for their own account but the address of a parent, and solely for the purpose of obtaining consent.

Registration is free of charge.

### Article 8 — One Account per Person

Each natural person may hold exclusively one account on the Platform. Registration of multiple accounts by the same natural person is prohibited regardless of the identifiers used. This rule is the operationalization of the principle of one person, one account from the Rulebook. That two accounts are accounts of the same person is established by the Foundation, with a statement of reasons and the right to a complaint, under the conditions and with the consequences set out in Article 4b of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality; the verifier does not guarantee this.

### Article 9 — Registration Process

Upon registration, the user selects a pseudonym, enters an email address, and sets a password.

A message with a confirmation link is sent to the email address entered. Confirmation is not a condition for using the account — the account operates in full even before it. Confirmation establishes that the address belongs to the user who created the account, thereby ensuring that notices under Article 40 of these Terms actually reach them and that their access is recoverable if they lose their password.

The pseudonym is the only identifier of the user visible to other users, to the extent that depends on the status of the viewer. The user is responsible for ensuring that the pseudonym does not contain personal data that could lead to their identification.

### Article 10 — Accuracy of Data

The user is responsible for the accuracy of all data entered during registration and throughout the use of the Platform. The user must update data without delay in the event of a change.

### Article 11 — Protection of Access Credentials

The user is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their access credentials. Any suspicion of unauthorized access must be reported by the user to the Foundation at the address in Article 3 of these Terms without delay.

The Foundation is not responsible for damage arising from unauthorized access to the account due to the user's failure to maintain the confidentiality of access credentials.

### Article 12 — Prohibition on Transfer of Account

An account is personal and may not be transferred, assigned, or shared with third parties.

## Chapter III — Platform Access and User Statuses

### Article 13 — User Statuses

An adult user may hold the status of a user, a member without confirmation, a Kolo member, or a Kolo keeper. Statuses, conditions for acquisition, and transitions between statuses are established by the Rulebook. A minor user does not hold a status under this Article; their position is governed by the Rulebook on the Participation of Children. Articles 14 and 15 of these Terms govern the scope of access to Platform functions according to the user's status.

### Article 14 — User Who Is Not in the Kolo

The status of user is held by an adult user registered on the Platform who is not in the Kolo.

A user may view general system indicators, familiarize themselves with the rules, and view the amounts and timestamps of POEN transactions without the parties' pseudonyms and without access to account balances.

A user may view listings in the exchange space, exchange goods and services, and post a listing offering or seeking a good or a service, under the conditions set out in Article 16 of these Terms. They may write in the common conversation space (Chat Room), respond in a conversation in which they participate, and themselves initiate a conversation with an advertiser regarding the advertiser's listing, at most three new conversations per day. They may not access advertisers' contact details or, outside a listing, contact users who have not contacted them. They see another user's profile to the extent set out in Article 17 of these Terms.

In POEN ledger updates a user participates as a receiver and as a user who initiates an update in favor of another user (Art. 28 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). A ledger update moves an existing record and does not change the total number of POEN.

A user may not verify other users or give recommendations (Article 17a). A contribution is recorded for them through the channel of contribution to platform content (Art. 40a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System) and through the channels of financial contribution and sponsorship; through the other channels it cannot be.

A user who does not know any Kolo member may get onto the call list (Article 16b). Neither getting onto the list, nor a donation, sponsorship or farm registration confirms their reality; it is established only by verification in the chain of confirmations (Art. 32 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System and Art. 4a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality). By verification they become a Kolo member or, where they have been introduced into the Kolo, a member without confirmation (Article 15).

### Article 15 — Kolo Member, Member without Confirmation and Kolo Keeper

A Kolo member is a user whose reality has been confirmed in the chain of confirmations — by the confirmation of a Kolo member, or of a founder, who knows them personally, or by a confirmation received as a member without confirmation — in accordance with Article 29 of the Rulebook and the Rulebook on Proof of Reality.

A Kolo member has full access to the Platform: they may exchange goods and services in the exchange space, have contributions recorded in POENs, confirm users and members without confirmation whom they know personally and introduce into the Kolo users from the call list in accordance with the proof-of-reality rules, donate to the Foundation, and participate in a cooperative as a cooperative member, in accordance with the Rulebook on Cooperatives. A Kolo member has access to the pseudonyms of all users, POEN account balances, and all transactions with the pseudonyms of parties. A member without confirmation is an adult user whom someone has introduced into the Kolo and who has not yet been confirmed by a Kolo member who knows them personally (Article 28a of the Rulebook and Article 5a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality). They are a participant and have the rights that the acts attach to a participant: they exchange goods and services, have contributions recorded in POENs, donate, participate in a cooperative, in social programmes and in collective procurement, have the same access to the ledger as a Kolo member, inscribe ZRNO and may write off free ZRNO. They do not have the rights that the acts attach to a Kolo member, unless an act expressly provides otherwise: they do not see the call list and do not introduce users from it into the Kolo, they do not confirm anyone — neither the existence of a child, nor other members without confirmation, nor the fulfilment of the conditions for a support programme — they do not give recommendations (Article 17a), do not carry out supervision and do not activate ZRNO; they register a farm under the conditions of the Rulebook on Farms. They may introduce into the Kolo an acquaintance whom they know personally, and the person introduced is likewise a member without confirmation. A member without confirmation is confirmed by a Kolo member, or a founder, who knows them personally, or by the Foundation on its own decision. A member without confirmation who has been in the Kolo for at least 30 days and meets the other conditions of Article 5c of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality may request a conversation for confirmation, after which a Kolo keeper may confirm them even when no one knows them personally. An exchange of goods and services may be the way in which they became acquainted, but it is not a condition of confirmation. A confirmation is subject to supervision in the same way as a verification. When a user becomes a Kolo member, they receive a list of linked users: those whom they introduced into the Kolo while they were a member without confirmation and the one who introduced them, if these are still members without confirmation; from that list they personally confirm those whom they know (Article 5d of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality). Each such confirmation is a personal confirmation for which the one who gives it is responsible, brings no POENs and is subject to supervision. A confirmation never occurs automatically.

A Kolo keeper is a Kolo member for whom ZRNO is inscribed in the Protocol. A member without confirmation for whom ZRNO is inscribed is not a Kolo keeper and does not activate ZRNO until they are confirmed. A Kolo keeper has all the rights of a Kolo member. In addition, they have a position in the accounting system and, by activating ZRNO, acquire the right to participate in the governance of the system through the Upper Kolo, once it is established.

A Kolo keeper whose verification terminates becomes a user. Their ZRNO remains inscribed and frozen: until they are confirmed again, a user for whom ZRNO is inscribed does not activate it, does not write it off, does not delegate a vote on its basis and is not part of the Upper Kolo (Art. 30 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). A Kolo keeper whose confirmation alone is annulled, while their introduction into the Kolo remains valid, becomes a member without confirmation: their ZRNO remains inscribed, active ZRNO transitions to the free state, and they may activate it again once they are confirmed.

### Article 16 — Listing by a User Who Is Not in the Kolo

A user may post a listing offering or seeking a good or a service. By posting a listing the user may present themselves to Kolo members (Art. 32 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). A listing is not in itself a path to verification: the advertiser is verified by a participant who knows them personally or, after a conversation, by a Kolo member who called them from the call list (Article 16b). The existence of that path does not change the nature, procedure, or conditions of verification established by the Rulebook on the KOLO System and the Rulebook on Proof of Reality.

A listing posted by a user must meet the content minimum: a title, a description, at least one photograph, a category, and a place, whereby the length of the title and the description is not a condition. A listing that does not meet the content minimum is not published. The same condition applies when an already published listing is edited.

A listing posted by a user carries a visible mark indicating that the advertiser is not in the Kolo. The mark is available to everyone to whom the listing itself is available, including non-registered persons.

A user may, starting from a published listing, initiate a direct conversation (messages) with the advertiser; a user at most three new conversations per day. In the conversation, the user may respond even before being in the Kolo. The processing of data in the conversation is governed by the Platform's Privacy Policy.

A participant, or a founder, may verify the advertiser if they know them personally, using a one-time code that the advertiser generates — a Kolo member by a confirmation, and a member without confirmation by introduction into the Kolo. Verification is carried out under the same conditions and checks established by the Rulebook on Proof of Reality (including the rule of one confirmation, the daily pace of confirmation, and supervision).

For the first listing in which they offer a good or a service and which meets the content minimum, a contribution to platform content is recorded for the user under the conditions set out in Art. 40a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. The listing is published immediately, while the contribution is recorded when the Foundation approves the listing; this applies to every user, regardless of whether they are in the Kolo. If the contribution is not approved, the user receives a notification with a statement of reasons, and the listing remains published.

The Foundation may remove a listing that breaches these Terms, the Rulebook, or the law, with notice to the user stating the reason (Article 21 and Article 25). If the listing is removed before the contribution has been recorded, the noted contribution is annulled.

### Article 16a — Farm and the DOMAĆE Mark

An adult user may register a farm — a household that produces food or agricultural products — and invite other adult users whom they know personally to join it, under the conditions set out in Article 40c of the Rulebook on the KOLO System and the Rulebook on Farms, which also determines who may register a farm that is not entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings and when a farm may have members. All members of the farm who are Kolo members, as well as an applicant who is a user or a member without confirmation, manage the farm on the Platform equally; for each person invited to join the farm, the one who invited them is responsible. Registration is free of charge.

By registering, the applicant declares that the data on the farm are accurate; responsibility for that declaration lies with the holder of a farm entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings, or with the applicant in the case of a farm not entered in that register, in accordance with the Rulebook on Farms. By accepting an invitation, the user declares that they work on the farm. The Foundation does not check those data in state registers and relies on the declaration; no extract from the Register of Agricultural Holdings is attached to the application. A farm may be registered by any adult user, as the holder, also when the farm is not entered in that register. An application for a farm submitted by a user places them on the call list (Article 16b), but does not confirm their reality; the conversation after which a Kolo member introduces them into the Kolo is at the same time the conversation for the purpose of approving the farm (Article 6a of the Rulebook on Farms).

An approved farm is displayed on the public map, at its exact location, with the address, on the basis of the applicant's statement given in the application, with its name, description, listings on behalf of the farm, and the pseudonyms of its members. Display on the map is a condition of the farm contribution.

A listing that a member posts on behalf of an approved farm automatically carries the DOMAĆE mark and leads to the farm's profile. The mark indicates only that the listing has been posted on behalf of a farm that has joined the system. The Foundation does not check or guarantee the origin, quality, or safety of the good offered; the user who posted the listing is responsible for them (Article 16 of these Terms and Article 16 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

### Article 16b — Conversation Request and Call List

A user who does not know any Kolo member may get onto the list of users waiting for a call (call list). The list is kept on the Confirmations page. A user gets onto the call list:

1) by a conversation request ("Contact us"), in which they state their place, briefly what they do, and when they are available for a conversation;

2) when the Foundation establishes the receipt of their public donation in the amount of at least 5,000 dinars, made in a single payment;

3) when the Foundation establishes the receipt of a sponsorship in money under an application they submitted;

4) when, as the holder, they submit an application for a farm, entered or not entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings,

under the conditions established by Article 4a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality.

The Foundation is obliged to ensure that a user on the call list is called by a Kolo member at the latest within seven days of the day on which they came onto the list. It ensures the call by displaying the request to Kolo members together with the deadline; the Kolo member conducts the conversation of their own free will and uses the name and number only for that conversation (Article 4a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality).

A member without confirmation who meets the conditions of Article 5c of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality also comes onto the call list when they request a conversation for confirmation ("I'd like someone to call me for confirmation"). A member without confirmation who has inscribed ZRNO comes first on the list, and the Foundation is obliged to ensure that a Kolo keeper calls them within three days; other members without confirmation on the list are called by a Kolo keeper within seven days. The request of a member without confirmation is seen and opened only by Kolo keepers. Paragraphs 2 to 7 of this Article apply to it, provided that after the conversation a Kolo keeper may confirm the member without confirmation, under the conditions of Article 5c of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality.

Before getting onto the list, the user, on each ground under paragraph 1, enters their name and surname, place and telephone number and is informed that a Kolo member will call them and that this Kolo member will see their name and surname from the identity register (Art. 31a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System) and their telephone number, as well as whether the same number, or the same name and surname, is kept alongside another account, together with the place of that account. The user provides the data themselves, with explicit consent for them and the other data on the list to be available to Kolo members in the manner set out in this Article.

The call list is available only to Kolo members; members without confirmation do not see it (Article 5a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality). The name and surname and the telephone number are not visible until a Kolo member opens them with a click. By opening the data, the Kolo member opens a call: the data are available only to them and only while the call is open. A call is open at any one time to only one Kolo member, for at most ten minutes, and one Kolo member may have only one call open at any one time. Next to the name and number, the Kolo member sees an indication where the same telephone number, or the same name and surname, is kept alongside another account, and the place of that account, but not its pseudonym; a match is not a prohibition, and only the Foundation decides on a multiple account (Article 4b of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality). The Foundation records which Kolo member opened the data; that record is available only to the Foundation's administrative team, is kept for as long as the account of the called user exists and is not exported through the Platform, and the called user obtains it upon a request sent by email to privatnost@ekolo.rs.

A Kolo member who calls a user and, after the conversation, assesses that they know them well enough to be responsible for the verification may introduce them into the Kolo, under the conditions and checks established by the Rulebook on Proof of Reality. A conversation is not in itself a verification and does not oblige the Kolo member to introduce the user into the Kolo. A user introduced into the Kolo becomes a member without confirmation (Article 5a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality).

Getting onto the call list does not confirm reality. Neither the donation or sponsorship, nor the contribution recorded on their basis, depends on it.

The request and the data on the list are deleted when the user enters the Kolo, or when the member without confirmation is confirmed, when they withdraw the request, or when their user status terminates; the record of which Kolo member opened the data is kept under paragraph 3 of this Article. The name and surname, place and telephone number remain alongside the account as long as it exists and are compared with the data kept alongside other accounts in order to prevent multiple accounts; the user may change the place and telephone number, but may not delete them, and they are deleted upon closure of the account. The telephone number from the list is not used for any other purpose and is not displayed in any other part of the Platform, except where the user themselves enables its display on their profile.

Misuse of a name and surname or a telephone number obtained from the call list, including calls unrelated to getting acquainted and verification, constitutes a breach of these Terms (Article 24).

### Article 17 — Transparency and Visibility of Data

The Platform is a transparent recording system. By accepting these Terms, the user accepts the visibility model described in this Article and in Article 6 of the Privacy Policy.

Non-registered persons see only general system indicators.

Users who are not in the Kolo see the amounts and timestamps of POEN transactions, but do not see the pseudonyms of the parties to them, POEN account balances, or users' ZRNO status. They see another user's profile to a restricted extent: pseudonym, picture, place if the user has made it visible, status, date of registration, active listings, and the number of recommendations received. They see the list of donations in the same way as Kolo members: with the name and surname of the donor of a public donation, the amount and the date, without the pseudonym.

Participants — members without confirmation, Kolo members and Kolo keepers — have full access to the pseudonyms of all users, account balances, and all transactions with the pseudonyms of parties. Excluded from that access are transactions in which either party is a minor user; they are not displayed, because the children's space is not public. A record of a contribution recorded under a social programme is displayed with the pseudonym of the support recipient, the name of the programme and the amount, while the ground on which the entitlement was obtained is not displayed to any user; to users and non-registered persons, a daily total per programme is displayed instead of individual records (section 4.6 of the Privacy Policy).

The user may, on their own initiative and at any time, choose to make their name and surname and telephone number visible to all participants. This disclosure is voluntary and may be withdrawn at any time.

A user who chooses a public donation (within the meaning of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations) consents to their name and surname, together with the amount and date of the donation, being published in the Foundation's list of donations. The list of donations is available to adult users; the donor's pseudonym and a link to their profile are not displayed in it, and the pseudonym is visible only in the overview of POEN transactions, which is available to participants. A user sees their own donations regardless of status. The name and surname of the donor are published exclusively in that list and are not entered into the record of recorded POEN. A donation made anonymously enters the list solely by its amount and date, without a name, without a pseudonym and without a link to a profile. The amounts of confirmed sponsorships are publicly available. The name and surname are recorded alongside the donation at the time it is recorded. The user may at any time withdraw consent to their publication, separately for each public donation, and upon the termination of user status the name and surname are removed from the list; the donation then remains in the list with its amount and date (Art. 5a of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations). The publication serves the transparency of the Foundation's disposal of funds and the verification of the ground on which POEN was recorded; the Foundation does not set it as a condition on which it agrees to record a contribution. For an anonymous donation the name is not published and no POEN is recorded, because a record that cannot be attributed to any person is not verifiable (Art. 5a of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations); the absence of recording is not a consequence of consent being withheld.

The visibility of the pseudonymous contribution ledger is a structural characteristic of the system that the user may not disable. A user who does not accept ledger transparency may not use the Platform.

The Foundation is not responsible for the consequences arising if the user themselves reveals their identity through their choice of pseudonym or voluntary disclosure of data.

### Article 17a — Member Recommendation

An adult Kolo member, as well as a founder, may recommend another adult user. A member without confirmation does not give recommendations (Article 5a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality). A recommendation is given on the user's profile, where that profile is accessible to the one who recommends. A minor user does not give recommendations. No one may recommend themselves.

One user may recommend another once. A recommendation may be withdrawn at any time.

The user's profile displays exclusively the number of recommendations they have received. Who recommended the user is not displayed to anyone — neither to the user who received the recommendation nor to other users.

A recommendation is the personal opinion of the one who gives it. A recommendation is not a verification and does not replace it. It is not recorded as a contribution, produces no POEN record and does not affect the user's status, access to the functions of the Platform, verification or the display of listings. The Foundation does not check recommendations and does not vouch for a user whom someone has recommended.

Upon termination of a user's status, the recommendations they have given and the recommendations they have received are deleted.

## Chapter IV — Exchange Space

### Article 18 — Purpose of the Exchange Space

The exchange space is the part of the Platform intended for advertising offers and requests for goods and services and arranging exchanges between users.

Browsing listings in the exchange space — the content of an offer or request, the amount in POEN, location, and the advertiser's pseudonym — is public and accessible to all visitors.

Posting a listing is available to all registered users; to a user who is not in the Kolo under the conditions of Article 16. Initiating communication on account of a listing is available to all registered users; to a user who is not in the Kolo at most three new conversations per day. Access to advertisers' contact details is available exclusively to participants.

For non-registered persons, the advertiser's pseudonym in the exchange space is not linked to the contribution ledger or the user's profile, and for users who are not in the Kolo it is linked only to the restricted profile under Article 17, without the contribution ledger.

The Foundation is not a party to the exchange and does not mediate in it. The legal relationship arises exclusively between the users who exchange goods and services.

### Article 19 — Determining the Amount in a Listing

The amount in a listing is expressed in POENs and is set by the user who posts the listing. The Foundation publishes no ratio of POEN to the dinar or to any other currency and does not recommend the ratio by which a user should set the amount.

If, in setting the amount, a user starts from a value expressed in money, that is their own estimate. It applies only to that listing, does not bind the other party, and is not a ratio that the Foundation establishes, publishes, or applies.

The Foundation applies no ratio of POEN to money in any of its own procedures: it does not record contributions by it, does not establish any amount prescribed by the rulebooks, and does not determine the number of POENs in projects and collective procurements. The amounts prescribed by the rulebooks are expressed in POENs and are not derived from dinars.

The estimate under paragraph 2 of this Article is not a guarantee of convertibility, does not constitute an exchange rate within the meaning of payment services regulations, and does not change the legal nature of POEN established by the Rulebook. The Foundation guarantees and maintains no value of POEN.

The Foundation does not buy back POEN or ZRNO and does not provide for their conversion into dinars or any other means of payment. There is no contract for the exchange of dinars for POEN.

Users freely determine the amount for the goods and services they offer. The Platform does not establish, limit, or control amounts.

### Article 20 — Posting Rules

A user who posts an offer or request in the exchange space must provide an accurate and clear description of the good or service, a realistic amount in POENs, and all terms relevant to the exchange.

The user may not post content that is false, misleading, or that misrepresents the nature, quality, or quantity of the good or service.

The content minimum for a listing — a title, a description, at least one photograph, a category, and a place, without any condition as to the length of the title and the description — is a condition for posting a listing by a user who is not in the Kolo (Article 16) and a condition for recording a contribution to platform content for any user (Art. 40a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). For a participant, a listing that does not meet the content minimum is published, but no contribution is recorded for it.

A user who offers a good or a service is responsible for complying with the rules that apply to that good or service — including rules on business registration, on production and trade, on food safety, and tax rules. Those rules apply also where the exchange is arranged through the Platform. Recording POEN on account of an exchange neither determines nor changes the tax, accounting or other public-law qualification of the transaction between users. A user who offers a good or a service within an activity on account of which they are obliged to issue an invoice, a fiscal receipt or another document is obliged to issue it regardless of whether the exchange has been recorded in POEN. The Foundation does not verify or confirm compliance with those requirements and bears no responsibility for it.

### Article 21 — Prohibited Goods and Services

The offering and exchange of goods and services whose trade is prohibited by law is forbidden, including narcotic drugs and precursors, weapons and explosives whose trade is not permitted, stolen goods or goods for which there is a suspicion that they originate from a criminal act, personal documents and official papers, personal data of third parties, content that incites hatred, violence, or discrimination, and financial instruments or schemes designed to circumvent regulations.

Listings whose subject is POEN or ZRNO are likewise prohibited — offering, seeking, buying, or selling them for money or for any other value outside the system (Article 24 of these Terms and Article 16 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

A minor user must not offer or acquire goods whose provision to minors is prohibited or restricted by law — in particular alcoholic beverages, tobacco and related products, including electronic cigarettes and liquids for them, energy drinks, medicines and food supplements, pyrotechnic articles, and sharp objects. The list of prohibited categories is not exhaustive. The Foundation may remove a listing it determines to be in violation of the law, these Terms, or the Rulebook, and notifies the user of the removal, stating the reason.

### Article 22 — Execution of Exchange

The exchange is arranged directly by the users. Users are free to establish the terms of the exchange, including the manner and deadline for delivery of the good or provision of the service.

Upon the users' agreement, the Protocol records the exchange by updating the POEN ledger in accordance with Art. 16 of the Rulebook: the record of the user providing the good or service is increased, and the record of the receiving user is decreased by the same amount.

Exchange redistributes existing POENs and does not increase their total number in the system. Exchange is not a payment transaction and the POEN ledger update does not constitute a payment within the meaning of payment services regulations.

The users who take part in the exchange are responsible for its performance, quality, and risk, pursuant to the general rules of the law of obligations.

Where one party to the exchange is a minor user, the regulations on the contractual capacity of minors also apply. A minor user undertakes legal transactions within the limits set by those regulations, and the parent's consent given under the Children's Module does not replace consent to a particular legal transaction where the regulations require it. The conditions of a minor user's exchange are governed in more detail by the Rulebook on the Participation of Children.

The rule under paragraph 4 of this Article applies in particular to an exchange based on a listing by a user: the advertiser is not in the Kolo, which the listing itself indicates by a visible mark (Article 16). A user entering such an exchange does so at their own risk. In that case too, the Foundation is not a party to the exchange, does not mediate in it, and is not liable for the performance of users' obligations.

The Platform does not require users to separately mark, confirm, or rate an exchange. The only record of an exchange is the update of the POEN ledger which the users carry out themselves.

A user who has re-registered POEN on account of an exchange that has not been fulfilled lodges a complaint with the Foundation in accordance with Article 37a of these Terms, from their own profile. Deciding on the complaint concerns the record in the Protocol's ledger; it does not make the Foundation a party to the exchange and does not constitute mediation in the exchange within the meaning of paragraph 6 of this Article.

The provisions of this Article relate to exchange between users. They do not apply to collective procurement, in which the Foundation is the buyer and the contracting party in relation to the supplier.

### Article 22a — Collective Procurement

The Foundation may use dinar funds to acquire goods and make them available to participants in equal shares, with the annulment of POEN records under Article 14a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

Collective procurement is open to participants — Kolo members and members without confirmation; minor users do not take part in it. Participation is voluntary. A participant proposes an object of procurement by stating the name of a good, applies for a published procurement and, if placed in the order up to the number of shares, confirms participation by entering the day of collection.

A share is collected directly from the supplier, using a collection code. Upon collection, the number of POEN published in the calculation is annulled. Until collection, the POEN is reserved and cannot be disposed of.

In relation to the supplier, the Foundation is the buyer and the contracting party; the user does not enter into a contractual relationship with the supplier. Collection of a share is not a purchase and does not establish a claim against the Foundation.

The procedure, the deadlines, the manner of establishing the order, and the manner of calculating the number of POEN are governed by the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement.

In a similar manner, a participant may take a voucher from the allocation of goods and services from sponsorship. The good is handed over, or the service provided, by the giver directly to the voucher holder, and the giver is liable for them to the voucher holder; the Foundation is not a party to that relationship. The voucher is issued to the holder, is kept under their pseudonym and cannot be transferred; when checking it, the giver sees the holder's pseudonym. Taking the voucher annuls the number of POEN displayed with the voucher, and the record of this, with the description of the good or service and the name of the giver, is displayed in the ledger accessible to other participants, to which the holder gives consent when taking the voucher. A voucher that the holder does not redeem before the expiry of the period lapses, and the annulment is not corrected; if the giver does not hand over the good or does not provide the service, the annulment is corrected upon a complaint.

### Article 22b — Relation to Consumer Protection Rules

The Foundation does not act on the market towards users: it does not offer them goods or services for consideration, does not charge for use of the Platform, does not mediate in exchange, and receives no value on account of exchange between users.

Where a good or a service is offered by a person doing so within a business activity, consumer protection rules apply to the relationship with a user acquiring them for their own needs, regardless of the fact that the exchange was arranged through the Platform.

Rights under those rules are exercised against that person. The Foundation is not a party to the exchange, bears no responsibility for their fulfilment, and does not establish in what capacity a user is acting.

### Article 28 — Exclusion

The Foundation may exclude a user from the system due to a serious breach of system rules. Serious breaches include in particular: false registration — false identity, use of third-party data, or registration of multiple accounts; manipulation of the ledger — fictitious transactions, artificial generation of POENs, or arranged transactions for the purpose of manipulation; unlawful conduct — money laundering, fraud, or use of the Platform for unlawful activities; false verification within the meaning of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality; a false declaration when registering a farm or when joining a farm, in which case the person who made the false declaration or knew of it is excluded, while for the other members of the farm only the farm contribution is written off (Article 12 of the Rulebook on Farms); a false declaration of parenthood when opening or taking over a minor user's account, which entails exclusion (Article 4e of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children); and repeated breach of the rules of conduct in Chapter V of these Terms after a suspension has been imposed. Exclusion is a Foundation decision. The Foundation notifies the user of the exclusion in writing to their email address, with a statement of reasons. The user may lodge a complaint with the Foundation within 15 days of receipt of the notification. The Foundation considers the complaint and responds within 30 days.

An excluded user may not re-register an account without a special Foundation decision.

### Article 29 — Consequences of Status Termination

Upon termination of status — whether by withdrawal or exclusion — the consequences established in Art. 34 of the Rulebook ensue.

The user's POEN records are annulled, and the Protocol's corresponding counter-entry is reduced by the same amount. The user's active ZRNO transitions to the free state, after which all free ZRNO is written off and returned to the available ZRNOs in the Protocol. The write-off of ZRNO upon status termination does not trigger the recording of POENs pursuant to the accounting coefficient.

No monetary compensation is paid to the user on any basis.

Upon status termination, the pseudonymization of user data is initiated in accordance with the Rulebook and the Privacy Policy: the email address and voluntarily submitted data are deleted, the user's connections in the verification graph are anonymized, and the numerical history is retained under a pseudonym that does not point to the name and still constitutes personal data.

POEN and ZRNO records are not inherited as property rights.

### Article 30 — Handling in the Event of a User's Death

In the event of a user's death, the Foundation initiates the termination of status upon becoming aware of the user's death. This awareness may arise from family members, a legal representative, or publicly available data.

The Foundation may request appropriate documentation confirming the death of the user before initiating the procedure.

Upon initiation of the procedure, the consequences of status termination in Article 29 of these Terms apply. The Foundation acts within a reasonable period of becoming aware.

A user may have at most three open complaints per type.

Lodging a complaint does not exclude the user's right to judicial protection.
