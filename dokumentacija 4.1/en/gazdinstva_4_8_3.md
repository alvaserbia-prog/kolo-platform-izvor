# Rulebook on Farms

*This Rulebook governs the participation of agricultural farms in the KOLO system: registration, farm members, listings on behalf of a farm, the map of local producers, the procedure in the event of a false declaration, and the termination of the farm. It is adopted on the basis of Article 40c of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.*

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1

*Subject matter and relationship with the Rulebook on the KOLO System*

This Rulebook elaborates Article 40c of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. In the event of inconsistency, the provisions of the Rulebook on the KOLO System shall prevail.

Terms not defined by this Rulebook have the meaning established by the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

### Article 2

*Definitions*

**Farm** — a household that produces food or agricultural products and offers them in the platform's advertising space, whether or not it is entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings.

**Farm member** — an adult user who works on the farm permanently or occasionally and whose account is linked to the farm in accordance with this Rulebook.

**Applicant** — the farm member who submitted the registration.

**Holder** — the person in whose name the farm is entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings. For a farm that is not entered in that register, as well as for a registered farm that the holder has not joined, the powers and obligations of the holder under this Rulebook belong to the farm member who, under Article 9, is responsible for the declaration of accuracy of data.

**BPG** — the agricultural holding number from that register.

### Article 3

*A farm is not a user*

A farm has no account, has no record in the Protocol, and does not participate in exchange. The farm members participate in exchange, each on their own behalf, under the general rules of Article 16 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

Entry in the Register of Agricultural Holdings is not a condition for registration. A farm entered in that register and a farm not entered in it participate under the same conditions.

## II — Registration and Approval

### Article 4

*Registration*

The registration is submitted by an adult user, from their own account, under the conditions of Article 4a. The registration contains:

1) the BPG, if the farm is entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings, or an indication that it is not entered in it;

2) the name of the farm;

3) the settlement from the code list, the address and the exact location of the farm, with the applicant's statement that they consent to the address being displayed alongside the point on the map;

4) the role of the applicant — holder or member of the farm;

5) a description of the farm: what is produced and in what manner;

6) the applicant's declaration that the data are accurate and that the farm exists and produces what has been stated;

7) at least three listings offering food or an agricultural product, posted on behalf of the farm;

8) the first and last name of the applicant;

9) the telephone number of the holder, or of the applicant where the holder has no account on the platform, for the purpose of the conversation under Article 6a.

The first and last name of the applicant is entered in the identity register under Article 31a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, alongside the applicant's account, and not alongside the farm. If a first and last name has already been entered alongside the account, it is not entered again. The first and last name is required also where the farm is not entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings.

If, under Article 4a, the farm may have members, the applicant may, with the registration, invite other adult users whom they know personally to join the farm (Articles 7 and 7a).

Registration is free of charge.

### Article 4a

*Who may register a farm and when a farm may have members*

A farm may be registered by any adult user, regardless of whether the farm is entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings. Whether the farm may have members depends on the status of the applicant:

1) a Kolo member, or a founder, registers a farm as holder or as member, and the farm may have members;

2) a member without confirmation (Article 5a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality) registers a farm as holder, and only without members; the farm may have members only once they have been confirmed by a Kolo member, or a founder, who knows them personally, by a Kolo keeper after a telephone conversation, or by the Foundation;

3) a user registers a farm as holder, and only without members; by submitting the registration they are placed on the call list (Article 4a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality). Once they have been introduced into the Kolo, item 2 applies to them, and once they have been confirmed, item 1;

4) a minor user may not register a farm.

Being placed on the call list and approval of the farm do not confirm the applicant's reality; reality is confirmed only in the chain of confirmations, under the Rulebook on Proof of Reality.

### Article 4b

*Register of Agricultural Holdings*

No extract from the Register of Agricultural Holdings is attached to the registration. The Foundation does not check data in that register; the BPG, where stated, serves only as an identifier of the farm in the Foundation's records.

Entry in the Register is of significance only when the holder of a registered agricultural holding is entered as a sponsor, under the conditions of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations.

### Article 5

*Listings accompanying the registration*

The listings accompanying the registration must meet the content minimum established by the Terms of Use. A listing may be seasonal or for advance reservation.

The listings are published immediately, as listings of the applicant, and Article 40a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System applies in respect of them. The DOMAĆE mark is added to them when the farm is approved.

The amount in a listing is determined by the person who posts the listing.

The condition of three listings is met for the farm as a whole.

### Article 6

*Review and approval*

The Foundation reviews the registration together with the members who have joined by that time and with the listings, and checks that the registration is complete, that the declaration has been given, and that the listings represent a genuine offer of food or agricultural products.

If the registration is not complete, the Foundation returns it to the applicant, stating what needs to be supplemented.

When the conditions are met, the Foundation approves the farm. Upon approval, the farm receives a public profile and a point on the map of local producers, listings on behalf of the farm receive the DOMAĆE mark, and the contribution under Article 40c of the Rulebook on the KOLO System is noted for the members.

The Foundation does not check data in the Register of Agricultural Holdings and relies on the applicant's declaration (Article 4b).

The farm is not approved until every member who has joined by that time has a first and last name entered in the identity register and until the conversation under Article 6a has been held with the holder.

### Article 6a

*Conversation with the holder*

Before approving the farm, the Foundation speaks with the holder, by telephone or in person, for the purpose of approving the farm: in order to satisfy itself that the farm exists and produces what has been stated in the registration. The conversation does not serve to confirm the holder's reality and is not a check of documents; no documents are requested in it. The Foundation does not speak with the other farm members under this Article.

Where the holder is a user who came onto the call list by the registration, the conversation under this Article and the conversation after which a Kolo member introduces them into the Kolo (Article 5 paragraph 7 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality) are held as a single conversation. The Kolo member who calls them from the call list then also records that the conversation for the purpose of approving the farm has been held, and whether after it there are grounds for suspicion under paragraph 4 of this Article. That Kolo member is responsible for the introduction into the Kolo, under the Rulebook on Proof of Reality; the Foundation decides on the approval of the farm.

The telephone number from the registration (Article 4, item 9) is accessible only to the Foundation, is used exclusively for this conversation and is deleted when the Foundation decides on the registration. The Foundation records only that the conversation took place. A user leaves a telephone number for the call list separately, when they consent to the call (Article 4a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality).

If the conversation cannot be held or if after it there are grounds to suspect that the declaration in the registration is untrue, the Foundation returns the registration for supplementation, stating the reasons.

## III — Farm Members

### Article 7

*Joining*

Only an adult user may be a farm member. A minor user may not be invited to join or join a farm.

The farm admits members when, under Article 4a, it may have members. Every farm member who is a Kolo member may invite another adult user whom they know personally to join the farm, upon registration or later; the member who issued the invitation is responsible for each person they invite. The invited person joins by accepting the invitation, with a declaration that they work on the farm permanently or occasionally and with their own first and last name, which is entered in the identity register in the manner under Article 4, paragraph 2. Joining is not approved until the first and last name has been entered and until the condition under Article 7a has been met.

A person may be a member of only one farm. A person has the contribution under Article 40c of the Rulebook on the KOLO System for at most one farm. A person who withdraws from a farm that continues to exist retains the contribution noted, or recorded, for them in respect of that farm, and does not acquire it again by joining another farm. A person whose contribution was annulled, or written off, upon the termination of a farm may acquire it again, under the conditions of Article 12a.

### Article 7a

*Condition for joining*

The invited person joins the farm if one of the following conditions is met:

1) the invited person is a Kolo member, or a founder;

2) the invited person is a user, and the member who invited them confirms them in the chain of confirmations, because they know them personally;

3) the invited person is a member without confirmation, and the member who invited them confirms them, under Article 5a, paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality, because they know them personally; an exchange between them is not a condition for the confirmation.

The confirmation under items 2 and 3 may, instead of the member who issued the invitation, also be carried out by any other Kolo member, or founder, who knows the invited person personally (Article 5a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality). They may in the same manner also confirm any other farm member whom they know personally, including the holder under Article 4a, item 2.

The confirmation is carried out under the Rulebook on Proof of Reality, only if the person carrying it out knows the invited person personally, and that person is responsible for it as for any verification. Joining does not require that the invited person be placed on the call list.

### Article 8

*Contribution upon joining*

For a member who joined before approval, the contribution is noted at the moment of the farm's approval. For a member who joins after approval, the contribution is noted when the Foundation approves their joining; the Foundation reviews the joining in the same manner as the registration (Article 6), without the conversation under Article 6a and without the extract under Article 4b.

The noted contribution is recorded in the member's record, equally for all members, when both conditions are met:

1) the member has published a listing in which they offer a good or a service and which meets the content minimum, on behalf of the farm or a personal one; the condition is also met by a listing published before joining, and also when it is no longer published, and if a contribution for the first listing under Article 40a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System has already been recorded for the member, the contribution is recorded immediately;

2) they are in the Kolo, by verification in the chain of confirmations.

The order of joining is determined by the moment of accepting the invitation; the applicant is the first member. For the seventh member in order and any further members, no contribution is noted.

### Article 9

*Management of the farm, responsibility for the declaration and withdrawal*

All farm members who are Kolo members manage the farm on the platform equally: each of them posts listings on behalf of the farm, edits the farm's profile and, where under Article 4a the farm may have members, invites users whom they know personally to join the farm (Article 7). The member who issued the invitation is responsible for each person they invite. An applicant who is a user, or a member without confirmation, manages the farm in the same manner.

One person is responsible for the declaration of accuracy of the data from the registration (Article 4, item 6): for a farm entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings, the holder, and for a farm not entered in that register, the applicant. If a farm entered in the Register was registered by a member, the applicant is responsible for the declaration until the holder joins the farm; upon joining, the holder gives the declaration under Article 4, item 6, and from then on is responsible for it. The data under Article 4, items 1 to 3, are changed only by the person responsible for the declaration. The order of joining and a contribution already recorded are not changed thereby.

A member may withdraw from the farm at any time. The POEN in their record remains theirs, and the contribution that had been noted to them is recorded under the conditions of Article 8; the contribution of a member who has withdrawn is not annulled or written off even when the farm later terminates, except when the farm terminates because of a false declaration (Article 12). When the person responsible for the declaration of accuracy of data withdraws from the farm, requests the deletion of a farm that also has other members (Article 11, paragraph 6), or their status as a user terminates, the next member in the order of joining automatically receives a request to take over the declaration under Article 4, item 6. If they take it over, they are responsible for it from then on. If they refuse it or do not respond within thirty days of the request, the request is addressed to the next member in the order of joining, under the same conditions. The request contains a notice that the farm terminates if no member takes over the declaration, and that the farm contribution is then annulled, or written off, for the members (Article 12a). If no member takes over the declaration, or if the farm no longer has any members, the farm terminates.

### Article 9a

*Loss of confirmation of a farm member*

When the confirmation of the holder or of another farm member ceases (Article 17 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality), they lose the rights under this Rulebook that belong to a Kolo member: to manage the farm (Article 9, paragraph 1) and to invite users to join the farm (Article 7). They acquire the rights again once they have been confirmed.

The farm continues to operate. The loss of confirmation of one member does not change their status as a farm member, the order of joining, the responsibility for the declaration under Article 4, item 6, or the contribution noted, or recorded, for them in respect of the farm; the other farm members who are Kolo members retain all rights under Article 9, paragraph 1.

## IV — Listings on Behalf of a Farm and the DOMAĆE Mark

### Article 10

When posting a listing, a farm member chooses whether to post it on behalf of the farm or as a personal listing.

A listing posted on behalf of an approved farm automatically carries the DOMAĆE mark and leads to the farm's profile. The mark indicates that the listing has been posted on behalf of a farm that has joined the system. The Foundation does not check or guarantee the origin, quality, or safety of the good offered; the member who posted the listing is responsible for them, under Article 16 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

The update of the POEN ledger on account of an exchange under a listing on behalf of a farm is made in the record of the member who posted the listing.

## V — Map of Local Producers

### Article 11

An approved farm is displayed on the map, at its exact location, with its name, description, and listings on behalf of the farm. The address is written out alongside the point. The display of the address is part of the participation requested by the applicant and lasts as long as the farm exists on the platform. For a farm approved before this provision entered into force, the address is written out once the applicant gives the statement under Article 4, item 3; until then it is accessible only to the Foundation's administrator team. Sponsors under the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations are also displayed on the same map. Display on the map is a condition of the contribution under Article 40c of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

The BPG is not displayed to other users. The first and last name of members is not displayed to other users, nor alongside the farm.

The map is public. Farm members are displayed under their pseudonyms.

The point is removed from the map upon the termination of the farm, on any ground under Article 12a.

The person who, under Article 9, is responsible for the declaration of accuracy of data may delete the farm from the platform if the farm has no other members and has no agreement on sponsorship in goods and services with unredeemed vouchers. Upon deletion, the farm terminates: the point is removed from the map, listings on behalf of the farm cease to be displayed, the name, description, address, location, and BPG are deleted, and the farm contribution is annulled, or written off, under Article 12a. Before deletion, the person is informed of that consequence, including the number of POEN that will be written off from them.

The person under paragraph 5 may not delete a farm that also has other members. When such a person requests deletion, the request has the effect of their withdrawal from the farm: the other members, in the order of joining, receive a request to take over the declaration under Article 4, item 6, in the manner and within the time limit under Article 9, paragraph 3. The farm terminates only if no member takes over the declaration (Article 12a, paragraph 1, item 1).

## VI — False Declaration

### Article 12

A false declaration includes in particular the registration of a farm that does not exist or does not produce what has been stated, the registration of another person's farm without the knowledge of its members, and joining a farm by a person who does not work on it.

When the Foundation establishes that a declaration is false, it annuls the farm's approval. Upon annulment of the approval the farm terminates, and the contribution that had been noted, or recorded, under Article 40c of the Rulebook on the KOLO System for all persons who were farm members in the period to which the false declaration relates, including those who have since withdrawn from the farm, is annulled, or written off, under Article 12a.

All persons under paragraph 2 are excluded, under the procedure set out in Article 28 of the Terms of Use.

A complaint against a decision under this Article may be lodged under Article 37a of the Terms of Use.

## VIa — Termination of the Farm

### Article 12a

*Termination of the farm and write-off of the farm contribution*

An approved farm terminates:

1) when no member takes over the declaration of accuracy of data, or when the farm no longer has any members (Article 9, paragraph 3 and Article 11, paragraph 6);

2) upon deletion from the platform (Article 11, paragraph 5);

3) upon annulment of the approval because of a false declaration (Article 12).

Upon the termination of the farm, the point is removed from the map, and the name, description, address, location, and BPG are deleted.

Upon the termination of the farm, on any ground under paragraph 1, the contribution that had been noted for the farm members under Article 40c of the Rulebook on the KOLO System is annulled, and the contribution that had been recorded for them under that Article is written off for all farm members, on the ground under Article 14, paragraph 3, item 6 of that Rulebook. The write-off is executed even where the record thereby becomes negative; each person returns solely what was recorded to them, and the burden is not transferred to another person. In the case of termination under paragraph 1, items 1 and 2, a farm member within the meaning of this paragraph is the person who is a farm member at the moment of termination, and the contribution of a member who withdrew earlier is not affected (Article 9, paragraph 3). In the case of termination because of a false declaration (paragraph 1, item 3), a farm member within the meaning of this paragraph also includes a person who was a farm member in the period to which the false declaration relates, including where they have since withdrawn (Article 12).

A contribution recorded under Article 40a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System is not written off upon the termination of the farm.

Every affected person is notified of the termination of the farm, of the annulment of the noted contribution and of the number of POEN written off from them.

A person whose contribution was annulled, or written off, upon the termination of a farm may register a farm or join a farm and acquire again the contribution under Article 40c of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, under the conditions of Articles 4a, 7, 7a and 8 of this Rulebook. This does not apply where the farm terminated because of a false declaration, since in that case the persons under Article 12, paragraph 2 are excluded (Article 12, paragraph 3).

A complaint against the termination of the farm, and against the annulment and write-off under this Article, may be lodged under Article 37a of the Terms of Use.

## VII — Final Provisions

### Article 13

This Rulebook is adopted and amended by the Management Board (UO) of the KOLO Foundation, under the procedure established in the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

### Article 14

This Rulebook enters into force on the date of its adoption by the Management Board (UO) of the KOLO Foundation.
