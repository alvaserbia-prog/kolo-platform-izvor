# Rulebook on the Participation of Children

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1 — Subject matter

(1) This Rulebook governs, in accordance with Article 52 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, the participation of minors in the KOLO system: the states and opening of accounts, the relationship between the parent and the minor user, the scope of activities, the POEN ledger in the children's space and the transition to an adult account.

(2) Terms from the Rulebook on the KOLO System have the same meaning in this Rulebook.

(3) Every rule of this Rulebook is interpreted and applied in accordance with the best interests of the child (Article 52 paragraph 1 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

### Article 2 — Minor user and parent

(1) A minor user is a person who has reached seven and has not reached eighteen years of age.

(2) A minor user is a user, but has no status from the division into users who are not in the Kolo and participants (Article 28 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System); their position is determined by the state of the account (Article 5).

(3) A parent is a parent or legal guardian of the minor user.

(4) A minor user may have at most two parents; both have the same powers under this Rulebook. The number of children per parent is not limited.

### Article 3 — Parental consent

(1) A minor user participates in the system with parental consent. Consent is a condition of participation, not a condition for opening the account (Article 8).

(2) The parent gives consent once, upon the opening or take-over of the account. The parent does not approve individual actions of the minor user, except the re-registration under Article 30 paragraph 3.

(3) The legal basis for processing the data of a minor user under fifteen years of age is parental consent, in accordance with Article 16 of the Law on Personal Data Protection ("Official Gazette of the RS", No. 87/2018; hereinafter: LPDP). For a minor user who has reached fifteen years of age, the legal basis for processing is the performance of a contract to which they are a party, and parental consent is a condition of their participation.

### Article 4 — Age

(1) The system does not verify age. The declaration of adulthood is given upon opening the account and recorded together with the consent to the Terms of Use; the person who gives it is responsible for its accuracy.

(2) The age of a minor user is established from the date of birth stated by the parent upon the opening or take-over of the account (Article 12), or entered by the decision on conversion (Article 15). The entered date of birth is not changed.

## II — States and Opening of Accounts

### Article 5 — States of the account

(1) A minor user's account is in one of three states:

1) **waiting child**: a parent has not yet taken over the account. The minor user has a profile, makes friendships by scanning a code in person (Article 32) and sees listings as a visitor who is not signed in. They have no access to the Chat Room, do not participate in communication or in exchange, do not state a school, do not publish listings and do not participate in updates of the POEN ledger;

2) **child with a parent**: a parent has opened or taken over the account, but the conditions under point 3 are not met. The minor user sees listings without the advertiser's pseudonym, except listings of their friends; uses the Chat Room and messages only with their friends; states a school (Article 26). They do not publish listings, do not participate in exchange and do not participate in updates of the POEN ledger, not even by re-registration with the parent, in either direction; no POEN is entered to them, and the contribution under Article 33 is only noted for them;

3) **Kolo child**: at least one parent is a Kolo member, or a founder, and the existence of the child has been confirmed to that parent under Article 13. The minor user has full access within the limits of this Rulebook.

(2) A founder meets the condition under paragraph 1 point 3 regarding confirmed status even where no one has confirmed them. A member without confirmation does not meet that condition, so their child does not become a Kolo child.

(3) A parent who is neither a Kolo member nor a founder has all the powers and responsibilities of a parent under this Rulebook; only the state of the child's account depends on their position.

(4) The minor user's profile states what the account is waiting for: for a parent to take over the account, for a parent to become a Kolo member, or for the existence of the child to be confirmed.

### Article 6 — Transition between states

(1) The account moves from state to state by itself, according to the parent's position and the confirmation of the existence of the child.

(2) When no parent any longer meets the conditions under Article 5 paragraph 1 point 3, because a parent's confirmed status has ceased or because the confirmation of the existence of the child has been annulled, the account moves to the "child with a parent" state. No data are deleted, and the parent's account is not affected thereby.

(3) A return from the "Kolo child" state to the "child with a parent" state is not a measure and the time limits prescribed for suspension do not run for it.

### Article 7 — Opening of the account by a parent

(1) A minor user's account is opened by the parent from their own account. The account may be opened by any adult user who gives the declaration under Article 12; their confirmed status is not a condition.

(2) Before any data about the child are entered, the parent is shown a notice about the processing of data and about their powers and responsibilities under this Rulebook.

(3) An account opened in this way is immediately in the "child with a parent" state.

### Article 8 — Independent opening of the account

(1) A minor may open an account independently. Upon opening, they enter a pseudonym, a password and the parent's email address; they do not enter the date of birth.

(2) Such an account is in the "waiting child" state until a parent takes it over.

(3) A minor user whose account is awaiting take-over may delete the account themselves at any time.

### Article 9 — Processing of data before take-over of the account

(1) Until the account is taken over, the pseudonym, the data necessary for access to the account and the parent's email address are processed about the minor user. If the minor user makes a friendship during that period, the record of that friendship with its date is processed as well.

(2) Those data are processed on the basis of legitimate interest, for the purpose of establishing contact with the parent in order to obtain the consent required by law. Processing on that basis ceases upon take-over of the account, when the basis becomes the parental consent under Article 3, or upon deletion of the account.

(3) The parent's email address is data about a third party entered by the minor. It is processed solely in order to send the message under Article 10 and does not become the address of the minor user's account.

### Article 10 — Invitation to the parent

(1) A message with three actions is sent to the address under Article 8: take-over of the account, a declaration that the person is not that child's parent, and deletion of the account.

(2) The message states only the minor user's pseudonym.

(3) The message contains a one-time authorisation valid for 7 days.

(4) A declaration that the person is not that child's parent extinguishes the invitation; the account is not deleted thereby. Deletion of the account under this Article is executed without signing in, on the basis of the authorisation under paragraph 3.

(5) An account that nobody takes over within 14 days of opening is deleted. The time limit relates exclusively to the take-over of the account.

### Article 11 — Take-over of the account

(1) The account is taken over by an adult user who gives the declaration under Article 12; their confirmed status is not a condition. The account is taken over via the email address stated by the minor or by entering the minor user's pseudonym and the six-digit code visible in their profile. The second parent takes over the account by the same path.

(2) The minor user's consent to the establishment of parenthood is not required. Upon take-over, the account moves to the "child with a parent" state.

(3) The minor user receives a notification each time someone takes over their account, stating the pseudonym of the person who took it over. If that person is not their parent, the minor user may report this under Article 17.

### Article 12 — Parent's declaration

(1) A parent who opens or takes over the account gives a declaration under full responsibility that they have a child of the stated age and states the child's date of birth. Where an account has been converted into a minor's account by a decision of the Foundation, the parent gives the declaration upon receipt of the decision on conversion.

(2) The text of the declaration is kept alongside the parent–child link and is not altered after it is given.

### Article 13 — Confirmation of the existence of a child

(1) A parent who is a Kolo member, or a founder, requests confirmation of the existence of a child, for each child separately. The same applies to a parent who becomes one later, including a member without confirmation who is confirmed.

(2) The request is seen by Kolo members and founders. The notification of the request contains only the parent's pseudonym and a statement that the parent requests confirmation that they have a child; the child's pseudonym, age, sex and school are not stated.

(3) The existence of the child is confirmed by a Kolo member, or a founder, who knows the parent personally. The other parent of the same child does not give the confirmation. A member without confirmation does not confirm the existence of a child and does not see the request (Article 6 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality).

(4) The confirmation of the existence of a child brings POEN neither to the person who gives it nor to the parent.

(5) Whoever confirms the existence of a child who does not exist, or a parenthood that does not exist, is responsible as for a false verification, and Chapter IX of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality applies to that confirmation. The confirmation is subject to supervision under the conditions of Article 22 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality. Where it is established that the confirmation is false, it is annulled, and the account moves to the "child with a parent" state if it does not meet the conditions under Article 5 paragraph 1 point 3 through the other parent.

(6) The request for confirmation has no time limit. If no confirmation arrives, nothing is taken away from the parent: their confirmed status and records are not affected, and the child's account remains in the "child with a parent" state.

### Article 14 — The minor user's email address

(1) A minor user may state their own email address. Stating it is voluntary and is not a condition for using the account.

(2) The address is processed exclusively for the purpose of regaining access to the account, that is, setting a new password when the minor user has forgotten it. No notifications or other mail are sent to that address.

(3) The address is entered on the account only after the minor user has confirmed it by opening a link sent to that very address; until confirmation it has no effect. The confirmation link is valid for 24 hours.

(4) The minor user removes the stated address at any time. Upon transition to an adult account the address is retained, and it is deleted upon termination of the status of user.

## III — Conversion of the Account and Report of False Parenthood

### Article 15 — Conversion of an adult account into a minor's account

(1) Where it is established that a minor stands behind an account opened as an adult account, the Foundation converts that account into a minor user's account. The decision on conversion designates the parent and enters the date of birth. The converted account is in the "child with a parent" state.

(2) The conversion is a correction of an incorrectly stated age. It is not a measure against the user, does not presuppose a breach of obligation and does not initiate the procedure under Chapter IX of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality.

(3) Listings, conversations and the history of records are not deleted. Article 19 applies to existing conversations with adult persons from the conversion onwards.

(4) The converted account passes into an adult account under Article 35, on the day of majority calculated from the date of birth entered by the decision on conversion.

### Article 16 — Consequences of conversion

(1) Upon conversion the account leaves the chain of confirmations: all verifications the account touches are annulled, in both directions, as well as the confirmations of a member without confirmation that the account gave or received. The annulment does not mean that a verification was untrue. The account's ZRNO is written off, enrolments in support programmes and in the operational contribution cease, and the noted contribution for a first listing (Article 38 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System) is deleted.

(2) The contribution recorded to the account through the channels under Article 19 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System is annulled, in the number entered through those channels reduced by what has already been returned to the Protocol. POEN re-registered to the account by another user is not affected: a re-registration is not a recording of contribution but a change in the holder of the record, and a minor user may hold POEN.

(3) The write-off also affects persons to whom POEN was recorded on the occasion of the verifications that fall with the conversion. Each of them returns solely what they received on the occasion of that verification; the burden is not transferred to another person and the restitution under Article 36 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality does not apply.

(4) Upon conversion the account ceases to be a farm member, and the farm contribution that had been noted for it is annulled; the farm contribution that had been recorded to it is written off under paragraph 2.

(5) The write-off under paragraphs 2 to 4 is executed even where the record thereby passes into a negative value, in accordance with Article 17 paragraph 2 point 4 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. Every affected person is notified of the write-off and of the number of POEN written off from them, and may lodge a complaint against the decision under Article 40 of the Terms of Use.

### Article 17 — Report of false parenthood

(1) A minor user, the other parent or any other user may report to the Foundation that the person who opened or took over the minor user's account is not that child's parent.

(2) Upon receipt of the report, the Foundation without delay temporarily suspends that person's link with the minor user's account. While the link is suspended, the person has neither the insight under Article 19 nor the powers of a parent under this Rulebook, and the condition under Article 5 paragraph 1 point 3 is not met in respect of them. The suspension lasts until the Foundation decides on the report; it is not a measure against the person and does not mean that the declaration under Article 12 is untrue. Nothing is deleted by the suspension, and the minor user's data are processed on the same basis as before the report.

(3) The Foundation examines the case and decides on the report. Where it establishes that the person is not the minor user's parent, it terminates the link (Article 18); otherwise the suspension ends and the link again takes effect. The Foundation does not disclose to the person to whom the report relates who filed the report, either during the procedure or after the decision.

(4) Where the Foundation has already decided, for the same link, that the person is the parent, a new report does not suspend the link; the Foundation examines it and decides on it in the manner under paragraph 3.

(5) The person who filed the report and the person to whom the report relates may lodge a complaint against the decision on the report under Article 40 of the Terms of Use.

### Article 18 — Termination of the link

(1) Upon termination of the link, the consent, insight and powers the person had as a parent cease. A confirmation of the existence of a child given to that person ceases to have effect; whether it was false is assessed under Article 13 paragraph 5.

(2) The parent's place the person occupied is freed, so the account may be taken over by another parent under Article 11 even where both places were occupied before the termination.

(3) If after termination of the link the minor user has no parent, the account is not deleted but moves to the "waiting child" state, and the 14-day time limit for take-over of the account under Article 10 paragraph 5 runs anew, from the day of termination of the link.

(4) A false declaration of parenthood is a serious breach of the rules of the system. Where the Foundation establishes that the person is not the minor user's parent, it mandatorily imposes exclusion on the person who gave the declaration, under the procedure under Article 32 of the Terms of Use.

## IV — Relationship Between the Parent and the Minor User

### Article 19 — Parental insight

(1) The parent opens their child's profile from their own account. The profile contains the records of POEN ledger updates and published listings.

(2) The parent does not read the conversations that the minor user has with other minor users. The parent has access to the list of friendships with dates and the list of conversations, without messages.

(3) The parent reads a minor user's conversation with an adult user, but does not write in it. The adult user is shown a notice in the conversation that the conversation is read by a parent. The parent is notified of the conversation upon the first message in it.

(4) The minor user is informed of the scope of the insight under this Article.

### Article 20 — Powers and responsibility of the parent

(1) On their child's profile, the parent may remove any listing the child has published.

(2) At the same place, the parent approves, by means of a switch, the minor user's communication and exchange with adult users. Consent may be withdrawn at any time. The switch takes effect once the minor user has reached the age of fifteen (Article 22).

(3) The parent does not end their child's friendships, has no account in the children's space, does not publish content in it and does not address any minor user.

(4) The parent is responsible for content published by the minor user up to the moment of removal and for the actions of the minor user on the Platform.

### Article 21 — The minor user's password

(1) On their child's profile, the parent sets a new password for the minor user. The previous password is not required for this. The minor user is notified of the change.

(2) The power under paragraph 1 ceases once the minor user enters and confirms their own email address under Article 14. From then on the password is set exclusively by the minor user, through the forgotten-password procedure. Where access to the account has genuinely been lost, the parent files a complaint, which is decided by the Foundation.

## V — Scope of Activities

### Article 22 — Age groups

(1) Minor users are classified into two age groups: from the age of seven to the age of fifteen, and from the age of fifteen until majority.

(2) A minor user communicates and exchanges with other minor users, regardless of age group.

(3) A minor user who has reached the age of fifteen communicates and exchanges with adult users with the parental consent under Article 20 paragraph 2.

(4) A minor user who has not reached the age of fifteen neither communicates nor exchanges with adult users. Parental consent does not remove this and is not displayed for them.

(5) The classification under paragraph 1 concerns exclusively the relationship with adult users. Friendships, the Chat Room and exchange between minor users are not classified by age.

(6) Paragraphs 2 to 4 apply to an account in the "Kolo child" state; an account in the other states communicates only within the limits under Article 5.

### Article 23 — Exchange and legal capacity

(1) A minor user's exchange is an exchange of goods and services of small value, such as takes place among children in any case. It is not work that the minor user performs for another and it establishes neither an employment relationship nor work outside an employment relationship.

(2) A minor user undertakes legal transactions within the limits of their legal capacity, under the regulations governing family relations. The parental consent under Article 20 paragraph 2 does not replace consent to a particular legal transaction where the regulations require it; consent to a particular re-registration is governed by Article 30.

(3) The Foundation is not a party to the exchange and does not mediate in it. The relationship between the parties is governed by the general rules of the law of obligations and by the regulations on legal capacity. In the amicable resolution of a dispute (Article 39 of the Terms of Use), the parent takes part on behalf of the minor user. The annulment of a legal transaction under those regulations is effected between the parties; the Platform does not carry out the annulment of an exchange and does not overturn the update of the POEN ledger on that ground.

(4) The exchange under paragraph 1 relates exclusively to goods which a minor is permitted by the regulations to acquire. The prohibition under Article 23 of the Terms of Use applies also to exchange between minor users, regardless of whether it was arranged through a listing on the Platform or in another way.

### Article 24 — Listings

(1) A listing is published by a minor user whose account is in the "Kolo child" state. The same conditions apply to publication as to the listing of any other user; only visibility differs.

(2) A minor user's listing is visible to minor users and to their parents. The listing is not publicly available and is not displayed in search engines.

(3) The listing of a minor user who has reached the age of fifteen is also visible to adult users, with the parental consent under Article 20 paragraph 2. The listing of a minor user who has not reached the age of fifteen is not visible to adult users, not even with parental consent.

### Article 25 — Profile of a minor user

(1) The profile of a minor user is not available to adult users. Instead of the profile, a notice is displayed containing the pseudonym of the minor user and the pseudonym of their parent.

(2) The parental consent under Article 20 paragraph 2 does not open access to the profile.

(3) Contact between an adult user and a minor is established in connection with a listing published by the minor user, in the manner under Article 22 and subject to the parental insight under Article 19.

(4) A minor user may access the profile of a minor user with whom they have made a friendship.

(5) Information on who the parents of a minor user are, and on who the children of an adult user are, is available to participants.

(6) This Article does not restrict the Foundation in acting upon a report or in removing content.

### Article 26 — School and overview by school

(1) A minor user whose account is in the "child with a parent" or "Kolo child" state may themselves state the school they attend, by choosing from the list of schools; free-text entry is not possible. Stating a school is not a condition for using the account. The stated school is displayed on the minor user's profile.

(2) The Platform publishes an overview by school: the number of minor users whose account is in the "Kolo child" state and their share in the number of pupils enrolled at the school. A minor user whose account is in the "child with a parent" state is not included in that number; when the account moves to the "Kolo child" state, it is included in the number with the school already stated. The overview contains no personal data and is available to everyone.

(3) An overview of the minor users of a single school, with their pseudonym and the state of their POEN record, is available exclusively to a minor user of that same school whose account is in the "Kolo child" state, and covers only minor users whose account is in that state. That overview is not shown to an adult user, to a person who is not signed in, to a minor user whose account is not in the "Kolo child" state or to a minor user of another school. That overview does not give access to a minor user's profile.

(4) The stated school may be changed at most once every 30 days. The school datum is deleted upon transition to an adult account and upon termination of the status of user; no history of earlier choices is kept.

(5) A position in the overview is not a ground for recording a contribution and carries no POEN.

### Article 27 — Chat Room

(1) Minor users have a single Chat Room, separate from the Chat Room of adult users.

(2) The Chat Room is not available to an account in the "waiting child" state.

(3) In the Chat Room, a minor user sees the messages of their friends. A reply quoting another's message is not supported.

### Article 28 — Wish Box

(1) The Wish Box is a part of the children's space in which a minor user proposes a new function, game or rule of the children's space and supports the proposals of other children (Article 52 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). A minor user whose account is in the "child with a parent" or "Kolo child" state takes part in it.

(2) Proposals and support are given within the limits displayed by the Platform. A minor user supports the same proposal once and may withdraw their support; they do not support their own proposal.

(3) The Foundation reviews a proposal before other children see it and rejects a proposal that contains personal data or inappropriate content. A proposal is not edited. The reason for rejection is communicated to the minor user who submitted the proposal.

(4) A proposal is displayed only in the children's space, without the name or pseudonym of its author. The author of a proposal is not shown to anyone, not even to the Foundation's administrator team. The Wish Box has no comments and is not a means of communication among children or with adult users.

(5) The Foundation gives a reasoned answer to the proposals with the most support, after which support is closed. A proposal brings no POEN and does not bind the Foundation.

(6) The parent sees only the text of their child's proposals (Article 19).

(7) Upon deletion of the account and upon transition to an adult account, the minor user's proposals, with their support and answers, are deleted, as is the support they gave.

### Article 29 — Limitations

(1) A minor user does not enter ZRNO and does not participate in decision-making in the Upper Kolo.

(2) A minor user does not enter the chain of confirmations, does not confirm anyone or introduce anyone into the Kolo, does not confirm the existence of a child and does not give recommendations.

(3) A minor user may not be a farm member or be invited to join a farm.

(4) A minor user has no right to support programmes and does not participate in the operational contribution.

(5) A minor user does not donate to the Foundation (Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations).

(6) The limitations under this Article are not removed by parental consent.

## VI — POEN Ledger

### Article 30 — Re-registration of POEN

(1) A parent and their child re-register POEN to one another without any limit on the amount, in both directions. Minor users re-register POEN among themselves. Re-registration between a minor and an adult user is permitted under the conditions of Article 22.

(2) The correction of the ledger upon a complaint because of an unfulfilled exchange (Article 20 paragraph 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System) does not apply to a re-registration to which a minor user is a party.

(3) A re-registration that reduces a minor user's record is carried out upon the parent's approval once it reaches the threshold of 5,000 POEN for a minor user who has not reached the age of fifteen, or 20,000 POEN for a minor user who has reached the age of fifteen.

(4) Until approval, the minor user's record is not reduced and no POEN moves. The parent responds within 7 days; if they do not respond within that time limit, the re-registration is not carried out.

(5) Approval is not required for a re-registration between a minor user and their parent, nor for a re-registration that increases the minor user's record.

(6) This Article applies to an account in the "Kolo child" state; an account in the other states does not participate in updates of the POEN ledger, so not even re-registration between parent and child is possible, in either direction.

### Article 31 — Negative record

(1) A minor user's record may be negative exclusively under Article 16 paragraph 5, Article 34 paragraph 2 and Article 35 paragraph 2. These are the exceptions under Article 17 paragraph 2 points 3 and 4 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System: the write-off of the contribution from the children's space and the write-off upon conversion of an adult account into a minor's account.

(2) The regime under Article 17 paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System applies to a negative record.

(3) The minor user is notified of every write-off of the contribution from the children's space, together with the ground and the amount, even where they did not initiate the write-off themselves (Article 52 paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

### Article 32 — Friendships

(1) Minor users make a friendship exclusively by scanning a one-time code, directly, one in the presence of the other. The code is valid for 5 minutes and does not contain a pronounceable number.

(2) The number of friendships per minor user is not limited.

(3) The parent does not approve a friendship. The parent is notified of a friendship that has been made and sees the list of friendships with dates.

(4) When a minor user whose account is in the "Kolo child" state makes a friendship with a minor user whose account is not in that state, the parent of the Kolo child is notified that such a friendship has arisen. The notification does not open insight into conversations.

### Article 33 — Contribution from the children's space

(1) For a friendship that has been made, the Protocol enters 500 POEN to each minor user. This is the channel under Article 19 paragraph 1 point 9 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System and the only channel by which new POEN records arise in the children's space; the channels under points 8 and 10 of the same paragraph (contribution for a first listing and farm contribution) do not apply to minor users.

(2) The contribution is recorded when both parties are in the "Kolo child" state. While one party is waiting, the contribution is noted and visible to both parties, but it is not a POEN record and does not enter the state of the record, the total number of recorded POEN or public aggregates.

(3) A friendship between minor users who have a parent in common is made, but carries no contribution.

(4) By the recording under this Article the minor user receives neither money nor a thing. The Foundation makes no payment on that basis, nothing passes from its assets into the minor user's assets, and the record so created cannot be turned into money, assigned as a claim or collected (Article 16 paragraph 8 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). The recording is not remuneration: it pays for no work or other performance, nor is anything required of the minor user in return towards the Foundation.

(5) The POEN recorded under this Article enters the total number of recorded POEN.

### Article 34 — Ending a friendship

(1) A friendship is ended by a minor user, either of the two parties. The parent does not end a friendship.

(2) Upon the ending, 500 POEN is written off from each of the two parties, provided the contribution under Article 33 had been recorded. The write-off is executed even where the record thereby becomes negative (Article 31), and both parties are notified of the write-off.

(3) Before the ending, the minor user is shown a notice about the write-off.

(4) An ended friendship may be made again; the contribution is then recorded again.

## VII — Majority and Deletion of the Account

### Article 35 — Transition to an adult account

(1) Upon reaching eighteen years of age, the account ceases to be a minor user's account.

(2) On that day the POEN recorded under Article 33 is annulled: for that user, in the amount of the product of the number of valid recorded friendships and 500 POEN, and for each of those friends, 500 POEN. The annulment is executed even where the record thereby becomes negative (Article 31). Contributions relating to ended friendships, to friendships under Article 33 paragraph 3 and to friendships that had not been recorded are not annulled.

(3) The friendships cease. Other POEN records are not affected.

(4) The verification is given to the adult user by a parent under Article 7 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality; the other parent takes no action.

(5) The minor user and their friends are notified of the consequences under paragraph 2 at the latest 30 days before majority.

### Article 36 — Deletion of the child's account

(1) A parent may delete their child's account.

(2) Deletion of the account annuls all of the minor user's POEN records, with a counter-entry of the Protocol, and removes all of their listings. Friendships cease without a write-off on the side of other minor users.

## VIII — Final Provisions

### Article 37 — Existing confirmations of the existence of a child (transitional provision)

Confirmations of the existence of a child entered before 22.10.2026 remain valid. The existence of a founder's child whose account was linked with the founder's account before that day is deemed confirmed.

### Article 38 — Entry into force

This Rulebook enters into force on the date of its adoption.
