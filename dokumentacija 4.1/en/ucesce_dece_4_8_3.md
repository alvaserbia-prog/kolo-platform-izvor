# Rulebook on the Participation of Children

*This Rulebook governs the participation of minors in the KOLO system — accession, the scope of activities, the relationship with the parent, the contribution from the children's space, and the transition to an adult account. It is adopted on the basis of Article 58 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System (v4.8.3), which expressly refers to this act. In the event of inconsistency, the provisions of the Rulebook on the KOLO System shall prevail.*

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1

This Rulebook governs the participation of minors in the KOLO system, in accordance with Article 58 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

### Article 2

A minor user is a person who has reached seven and has not reached eighteen years of age.

### Article 3

A minor user participates with the consent of a parent or legal guardian (hereinafter: the parent).

The legal basis for processing the data of a minor user under fifteen years of age is parental consent, pursuant to Article 16 of the Law on Personal Data Protection. For a minor user who has reached fifteen years of age, parental consent is the legal basis for their participation as a contracting party.

The system does not verify age. Verification is not necessary because a minor user has no reason to declare their age untruthfully: an adult user participates in exchange, but acquires support programmes and the POEN record upon verification only once they enter the Kolo, and participation in governance only once they become a Kolo keeper, whereas a declaration that the person is a child leads to friendships, to the children's space and to the recording of the contribution under Article 14b.

## II — Accession

### Article 4 — Entry via a Parental Account

A minor user's account is opened by the parent from their own account. A child's account may be opened by any adult user who declares that they are that child's parent (Article 6, paragraph 1). An account opened in this way is immediately in the "child with a parent" state under Article 4c, and moves to the "Kolo child" state once the conditions under Article 4c, paragraph 1, item 3 are met.

Before any data about the child is entered, the parent is shown a notice about the processing of data and about their powers and responsibilities under this Rulebook.

The number of children per parent is not limited.

### Article 4a — Independent Accession by a Minor

A minor may open an account independently. Such an account is in the "waiting child" state under Article 4c until a parent takes it over. Upon opening, the minor enters a pseudonym, a password, and the parent's email address.

The date of birth is not entered upon opening. It is entered by the parent upon taking over the account (Article 7).

Until the account is taken over, the pseudonym, the data necessary for access to the account, and the parent's email address are processed about the minor user. If the minor user forms a friendship under Article 14a during that period, the record of that friendship with its date is processed as well.

Until the account is taken over, the minor user's data are processed on the basis of legitimate interest, for the purpose of establishing contact with the parent in order to obtain the consent required by law. Processing on that basis ceases upon takeover of the account, when the basis becomes the parent's consent under Article 3, or upon deletion of the account under Article 4b.

A minor user whose account is awaiting takeover may delete the account themselves at any time.

The parent's email address is data about a third party entered by the minor. It is processed on the basis of legitimate interest, for the purpose of obtaining the consent required by law, and solely in order to send the message under Article 4b.

### Article 4b — Invitation to the Parent and Takeover of the Account

A message with three actions is sent to the address under Article 4a: takeover of the account, a declaration that the person is not that child's parent, and deletion of the account.

The message states only the minor user's pseudonym. The name and other data are not stated, because it is not known whether the address is correct.

The message contains a one-time authorization with a period of seven days.

A declaration that the person is not that child's parent extinguishes the invitation; the account is not deleted thereby. Deletion of the account under this provision is executed without login, on the basis of the one-time authorization under paragraph 3.

An account that nobody takes over within fourteen days of opening is deleted. The period relates exclusively to the takeover of the account.

The account is taken over by an adult user who declares that they are that child's parent (Article 6, paragraph 1); their confirmation is not a condition (Article 5). The parent takes over the account in two ways: via the email address stated by the minor, or by entering the minor user's pseudonym and the six-digit code visible in their profile. The second parent takes over the account by the same path. A minor user may have at most two parents; both have identical powers under this Rulebook. The minor user's consent to the establishment of parenthood is not required. Upon takeover the account moves to the "child with a parent" state under Article 4c.

The minor user receives a notification each time someone takes over their account, stating the pseudonym of the person who took it over. If that person is not their parent, the minor user may report this under Article 4e.

### Article 4c — Account States

A minor user's account is in one of three states:

1) **waiting child** — a parent has not yet taken over the account. The minor user has a profile, makes friendships under Article 14a by scanning a code in person, and sees listings as a visitor who is not signed in. There are no other functions: they have no access to the Chat Room, do not participate in the communication under Article 12, do not state a school, do not publish listings, and do not participate in the updating of the POEN record;

2) **child with a parent** — a parent has opened or taken over the account, but no parent meets the conditions under item 3: none is a Kolo member or a founder (a parent who is a member without confirmation does not meet that condition, Article 5a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality), or the existence of the child has not been confirmed under Article 6 to any parent who is a Kolo member or a founder. The minor user sees listings without the advertiser's pseudonym, except listings of their friends under Article 14a; uses the Chat Room and messages only with their friends under Article 14a; states a school (Article 7). They do not publish listings and do not participate in the updating of the POEN record, not even by re-registration with the parent, in either direction; no POEN is recorded to them, and the contribution under Article 14b is only noted to them (Article 14b, paragraph 2);

3) **Kolo child** — at least one parent is a Kolo member or a founder, and the existence of the child has been confirmed to that parent under Article 6. Full access within the limits of this Rulebook.

The boundary between the "waiting child" and "child with a parent" states is the parent's consent (Article 3): until it is given, only what serves to establish contact with the parent is processed (Article 4a). The limitations of the "waiting child" state are also of a security nature and are explained in Article 18, paragraph 2.

The limitations of the "child with a parent" state exist because behind such an account there is no parent whose reality has been confirmed by someone who knows them personally and whose parenthood has been confirmed by a Kolo member who knows the parent personally.

The account moves between states by itself, according to the parent's position and the confirmation of the child's existence. A return from the "Kolo child" state to the "child with a parent" state is not a measure and the periods prescribed for suspension do not run.

The minor user's profile states what the account is waiting for: for a parent to take over the account, for a parent to become a Kolo member, or for the existence of the child to be confirmed.

### Article 4d — Conversion of an Adult Account into a Minor's Account

Where it is established that a minor stands behind an account opened as an adult account, the Foundation converts that account into a minor user's account. The decision is taken by an authorised person of the Foundation; it designates the parent and enters the date of birth under Article 7. The converted account is in the "child with a parent" state and moves to the "Kolo child" state under Article 4c.

The conversion is a correction of an incorrectly stated age. It is not a measure against the user, does not presuppose a breach of obligation, and does not initiate the procedure under Chapter VIII of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality.

Upon conversion the account leaves the chain of confirmations: all verifications the account touches are annulled, in both directions, as well as the confirmations of a member without confirmation that the account gave or received. The annulment does not mean that a verification was untrue — a minor user does not enter the chain of confirmations (Article 15), so those verifications are left without a basis. The account's ZRNO is written off, enrolments in support programmes and in the operational contribution cease, and the contribution noted under Article 40a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System is deleted.

The contribution recorded to the account through the channels under Article 15 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System is annulled, in the number recorded through those channels reduced by what has already been returned to the Protocol. POEN re-registered to the account by another user is not affected: a re-registration is not a recording of contribution but a change in the holder of the record, and a minor user may hold POEN.

The write-off also affects persons to whom POEN was recorded on the occasion of the verifications that fall with the conversion. Each of them returns solely what they received on the occasion of that verification; the burden is not transferred to another person and the compensation under Article 20b of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality does not apply.

The write-off under paragraphs 4 and 5 is executed even where the record thereby passes into a negative value, in accordance with Article 14, paragraph 3, item 4 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. Every affected person is notified of the write-off and of the number of POEN written off from them, and may lodge a complaint against the decision under Article 37a of the Terms of Use.

Upon conversion the account ceases to be a member of a farm, and the farm contribution that had been noted to it is annulled; the farm contribution that had been recorded to it is written off under paragraph 4.

Listings, conversations and the history of records are not deleted. Existing conversations with adult persons fall under Article 9 from the conversion onwards.

A converted account is thereafter subject to this Rulebook. It passes into an adult account under Article 19, on the day of majority calculated from the date of birth entered by the decision on conversion.

### Article 4e — Report of False Parenthood

A minor user, the other parent or any other user may report to the Foundation that the person who opened the minor user's account under Article 4, or took it over under Article 4b, is not that child's parent.

Upon receipt of the report, the Foundation without delay temporarily suspends that person's link with the minor user's account. While the link is suspended, the person has neither the insight under Article 9 nor the powers of a parent under this Rulebook, and the condition under Article 4c, paragraph 1, item 3 is not met in respect of them. The suspension lasts until the Foundation decides on the report; it is not a measure against the person and does not mean that the statement under Article 6, paragraph 1 is untrue.

The Foundation examines the case. The report is decided upon by an authorised person of the Foundation. Where it establishes that the person is not the minor user's parent, the Foundation terminates the link; otherwise the suspension ends and the link again takes effect. The Foundation does not disclose to the person to whom the report relates who filed the report, either during the procedure or after the decision.

Upon termination of the link, the consent, insight and powers the person had as a parent cease. A confirmation of the child's existence given to that person ceases to have effect; whether it was false is assessed under Article 6, paragraph 6. The parent's place the person occupied is freed, so the account may be taken over by another parent under Article 4b even where both places were occupied before the termination. If after termination of the link the minor user has no parent, the account is not deleted but moves to the "waiting child" state, and the fourteen-day period for takeover of the account under Article 4b, paragraph 5 runs anew, from the day of termination of the link.

A false statement of parenthood under Article 6, paragraph 1 is a serious breach of the rules of the system. Where the Foundation establishes that the person is not the minor user's parent, it mandatorily imposes exclusion on the person who gave the statement, under the procedure of Article 28 of the Terms of Use.

The person who filed the report and the person to whom the report relates may lodge a complaint against the decision on the report under Article 37a of the Terms of Use.

### Article 5

For the opening of an account under Article 4 and for the takeover of an account under Article 4b, the parent's confirmation is not a condition. Upon opening or takeover, the account moves to the "child with a parent" state.

The account moves to the "Kolo child" state when at least one parent is a Kolo member or a founder and the existence of the child has been confirmed to them under Article 6. A founder meets the condition regarding confirmation even where no person has confirmed them, because they are the origin of the chain of confirmations.

A parent who is neither a Kolo member nor a founder has all the powers and responsibilities of a parent under this Rulebook; only the state of the child's account depends on their position.

With regard to the "Kolo child" state and the confirmation of the child's existence, a member without confirmation does not have the position of a Kolo member: their child does not become a Kolo child, they do not see the request for confirmation of the child's existence, and they do not confirm the existence of a child (Article 5a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality).

### Article 6 — Parent's Statement and Confirmation of the Child's Existence

A parent who opens an account under Article 4, or who takes over an account under Article 4b, gives a statement under full responsibility that they have a child of the stated age. The statement is given at the moment of opening or taking over the account; its text is kept alongside the parent–child link and is not altered after it is given. Where an account has been converted into a minor's account by a decision of the Foundation, the parent gives the statement upon receipt of the decision on conversion.

A parent who is a Kolo member or a founder requests confirmation of the child's existence. Confirmation is requested for each child separately.

The request is seen by Kolo members and founders, on the Confirmations page. Members without confirmation do not see the request (Article 5a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality). The notification of the request contains only the parent's pseudonym and a statement that the parent requests confirmation that they have a child; the child's pseudonym, age, sex and school are not stated.

The existence of the child is confirmed by a Kolo member or a founder who knows the parent personally. The other parent of the same child does not give the confirmation. A member without confirmation does not confirm the existence of a child (Article 5a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality).

The confirmation of the child's existence brings POEN neither to the person who gives it nor to the parent.

Whoever confirms the existence of a child who does not exist, or a parenthood that does not exist, is liable as for a false verification; Chapter VIII of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality applies to a false confirmation of a child's existence. The confirmation of a child's existence is subject to supervision under the conditions of Article 10 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality. Where it is established that the confirmation is false, it is annulled, and the child's account moves to the "child with a parent" state if it does not meet the conditions under Article 4c, paragraph 1, item 3 through the other parent.

The request for confirmation has no deadline. If no confirmation arrives, nothing is taken away from the parent: their confirmation and records are not affected, and the child's account remains in the "child with a parent" state.

A parent who becomes a Kolo member, including a member without confirmation who becomes confirmed (Article 5a of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality), requests confirmation of the child's existence under this Article.

### Article 7

The age of a minor user is established from the date of birth stated by the parent — upon the opening of the account (Article 4) or upon its takeover (Article 4b). The stated date is not changed.

A minor user whose account is in the "child with a parent" or "Kolo child" state may themselves state the school they attend, by choosing from the list of schools. Stating a school is not a condition for using the account. An account in the "waiting child" state does not state a school.

### Article 7a — The Minor User's E-mail Address

A minor user may state their own e-mail address. Stating an address is voluntary and is not a condition for using the account.

The address referred to in paragraph 1 is processed exclusively for the purpose of regaining access to the account — setting a new password when the minor user has forgotten it. No notifications or other mail are sent to that address.

The address is recorded on the account only after the minor user has confirmed it by opening a link sent to that very address. Until confirmation the address has no effect and is not recorded on the account. The confirmation link is valid for twenty-four hours.

The minor user removes the stated address at any time.

Upon transition to an adult account the stated address is retained, since an adult account has an e-mail address in any event. The data is erased upon cessation of the capacity of user.

The parent's e-mail address referred to in Article 4a does not become the address of the minor user's account and is not affected by this Article.

## III — The Relationship Between the Parent and the Minor User

### Article 8

The parent gives consent once, upon the opening or takeover of the account. The parent does not approve individual actions of the minor user.

### Article 9 — Parental Insight

The parent opens their child's profile from their own account. The profile contains the records of POEN record updates and published listings.

The parent does not read the conversations that the minor user has with other minor users. Instead of content, the parent has access to the list of friendships with dates and the list of conversations, without messages. The reason is that insight into a conversation also touches the other child, whose parent has not granted that insight.

A minor user's conversation with an adult user is an exception: the parent reads it. The parent does not write in that conversation. The adult user is shown a notice in the conversation that the conversation is read by a parent.

The parent is notified of a conversation under paragraph 3 upon the first message in that conversation.

The minor user is informed of the scope of the insight under this Article.

### Article 10

On their child's profile, the parent may remove any listing the child has published.

At the same place, the parent approves, by means of a switch, the minor user's communication and exchange with adult users. Consent may be withdrawn at any time. The switch takes effect once the minor user has reached the age of fifteen (Article 12, paragraphs 3 and 4).

On their child's profile the parent sets a new password for the minor user. The previous password is not required for this, since the parent does not know it. The minor user is notified of the change. This power exists because a minor user's account as a rule has no e-mail address, so the forgotten-password procedure is not available to them.

The power under paragraph 3 ceases once the minor user enters and confirms their own e-mail address under Article 7a. From then on the password is set exclusively by the minor user, through the forgotten-password procedure. Where access to the account has genuinely been lost, the parent files a complaint, which is decided by the Foundation.

The parent is liable for content published by the minor user up to the moment of removal, and for the actions of the minor user on the Platform.

The parent does not end their child's friendships (Article 14c, paragraph 1).

### Article 11

The parent has no account in the children's space, does not publish content in it, and does not address any minor user.

## IV — Scope of Activities

### Article 12

Minor users are classified into two age groups: from the age of seven to the age of fifteen, and from the age of fifteen until majority.

A minor user communicates and exchanges with other minor users, regardless of age group.

A minor user who has reached the age of fifteen communicates and exchanges with adult users with the parent's consent under Article 10.

A minor user who has not reached the age of fifteen neither communicates nor exchanges with adult users. The parent's consent under Article 10 does not remove this and is not displayed for them.

The classification under paragraph 1 concerns exclusively the relationship with adult users. Friendships under Article 14a, the Chat Room under Article 18 and exchange between minor users are not classified by age; existing friendships are not ended by this and the contribution under Article 14b is not written off on that ground.

Paragraphs 2 to 4 of this Article apply to an account in the "Kolo child" state. An account in the "waiting child" state participates neither in the communication nor in the exchange under this Article. An account in the "child with a parent" state communicates exclusively with its friends under Article 14a and does not participate in exchange.

### Article 12a — The Nature of the Exchange and Contractual Capacity

A minor user's exchange is an exchange of goods and services of small value, such as takes place among children in any case. It is not work that the minor user performs for another and it establishes neither an employment relationship nor work outside an employment relationship.

A minor user undertakes legal transactions within the limits of their contractual capacity, under the regulations governing family relations. The parent's consent under Article 10 does not replace consent to a particular legal transaction where the regulations require it; consent to a particular re-registration is governed by Article 14.

The Foundation is not a party to the exchange and does not mediate in it (Article 22 of the Terms of Use). The relationship between the parties is governed by the general rules of the law of obligations and, where one party is a minor user, also by the regulations on contractual capacity.

The annulment of a legal transaction under those regulations is effected between the parties. The Platform does not carry out the annulment of an exchange and does not overturn the update of the POEN record on that ground (Article 14, paragraph 8).

The exchange referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article relates exclusively to goods which a minor is permitted by law to acquire. The prohibition laid down in Article 21 of the Terms of Use applies also to exchange between minor users, regardless of whether it was arranged through a listing on the Platform or in another way.

### Article 13

The same conditions apply to the publication of a minor user's listing as to the listing of any other user. Only visibility differs.

A minor user's listing is visible to minor users and to their parents. The listing is not publicly available and is not displayed in search engines.

The listing of a minor user who has reached the age of fifteen is also visible to adult users, with the parent's consent under Article 10. The listing of a minor user who has not reached the age of fifteen is not visible to adult users, not even with the parent's consent.

A listing is published by a minor user whose account is in the "Kolo child" state. An account in the "child with a parent" state sees listings without the advertiser's pseudonym, except listings of its friends under Article 14a; an account in the "waiting child" state sees listings as a visitor who is not signed in (Article 4c).

### Article 14 — Updating the POEN Record

The channels for recording the contribution to platform content and the farm contribution under Article 15, items 8 and 10 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System do not apply to minor users.

A parent and their child re-register POEN to one another without any limit on the amount, in both directions.

Minor users re-register POEN among themselves.

The re-registration of POEN between a minor and an adult user is permitted under the conditions of Article 12.

A re-registration that reduces a minor user's record is carried out upon the parent's approval once it reaches the threshold of 5,000 POEN for a minor user who has not reached the age of fifteen, or 20,000 POEN for a minor user who has reached the age of fifteen.

Until approval, the minor user's record is not reduced and no POEN moves. The parent responds within seven days; if they do not respond within that period, the re-registration is not carried out.

Approval is not required for a re-registration between a minor user and their parent, nor for a re-registration that increases the minor user's record.

For a minor user there is no monthly limit, no prohibition period, and no annulment of a re-registration; the history is visible as it is for adult accounts.

A minor user's record may be negative exclusively under Article 4d, paragraph 6, Article 14c, paragraph 3 and Article 19, paragraph 2. These are the exceptions to the prohibition of a negative record under Article 14, paragraph 3, items 3 and 4 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System — the write-off of the contribution from the children's space and the write-off upon conversion of an adult account into a minor's account. The restitution regime applies to such a record: it is not a debt, it is not collected, POEN received first replenishes it, re-registration to another is possible only once it passes zero, and the exchange of goods and services is not restricted.

Paragraphs 2 to 8 of this Article apply to an account in the "Kolo child" state; an account in the other states does not participate in the updating of the POEN record, so not even the re-registration between parent and child under paragraph 2 is possible, in either direction (Article 4c).

### Article 14a — Friendships

Minor users make a friendship exclusively by scanning a one-time code, directly, one in the presence of the other. The code is valid for five minutes and does not contain a pronounceable number.

The number of friendships per minor user is not limited.

The parent does not approve a friendship. The parent is notified of a friendship that has been made and sees the list of friendships with dates.

When a minor user whose account is in the "Kolo child" state makes a friendship with a minor user whose account is not in the "Kolo child" state, the parent of the Kolo child is notified that such a friendship has arisen. The notification does not open insight into conversations (Article 9, paragraph 2).

### Article 14b — The Minor User's Contribution

For a friendship that has been made, the Protocol records 500 POEN to each minor user.

The contribution is recorded when both parties are in the "Kolo child" state under Article 4c. While one party is waiting, the contribution is noted and visible to both parties, but it is not a POEN record and does not enter the balance, the circulation, or public aggregates.

The channel is an automatic act of the Protocol and does not count towards the daily limit of Protocol Programs. It is the channel under item 9 of Article 15 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

A friendship between minor users who have a parent in common is made, but carries no contribution.

By the recording under this Article the minor user receives neither money nor goods. The Foundation makes no payment on that basis, nothing passes from its assets into the minor user's assets, and the record so created cannot be turned into money, assigned or collected (Article 13 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). The recording is not remuneration: it pays for no work or other performance, nor is anything required of the minor user in return towards the Foundation.

Recording under this Article is the only channel by which new POEN records arise in the children's space. The POEN thereby recorded enters the total number of recorded POEN and thus the accounting framework of the system — it shifts the threshold of the founding step and the accounting coefficient of ZRNO, in both directions, because it is written off under Articles 14c and 19.

### Article 14c — Ending a Friendship

A friendship is ended by a minor user, either of the two parties. The parent does not end a friendship.

Upon the ending, 500 POEN is written off from both parties, provided the contribution under Article 14b had been recorded.

The write-off is executed even where the record thereby becomes negative (Article 14, paragraph 9). Without this, a minor user who immediately re-registers the POEN would avoid the write-off, and the cycle of making and ending the same friendship would be a source of unlimited recording.

Before the ending, the minor user is shown a notice about the write-off.

An ended friendship may be made again; the contribution is then recorded anew.

### Article 15

A minor user does not inscribe ZRNO and does not participate in decision-making in the Upper Kolo. A minor user does not enter the chain of confirmations, verifies no one, does not confirm the existence of a child and gives no recommendations. A minor user may not be a member of a farm or be invited to join one (Rulebook on Farms). These limitations are not removed by parental consent.

### Article 15a — The School and the Overview by School

The school referred to in Article 7 is chosen by the minor user themselves, whose account is in the "child with a parent" or "Kolo child" state, from the list of schools; free-text entry is not possible. A minor user whose account is in the "waiting child" state does not state a school. The stated school is displayed on the minor user's profile.

The Platform publishes an overview by school: the number of minor users whose account is in the "Kolo child" state, and their share in the number of pupils enrolled at that school. A minor user whose account is in the "child with a parent" state is not included in that number; when the account moves to the "Kolo child" state, it is included in the number with the school already stated, without stating it again. This overview contains no personal data and is available to everyone.

An overview of the minor users of a single school, with their pseudonym and the balance of their POEN record, is available exclusively to a minor user of that same school whose account is in the "Kolo child" state, and covers only minor users whose account is in that state. It is not shown to adult users, to persons who are not signed in, to a minor user whose account is not in the "Kolo child" state, or to a minor user of another school.

The two conditions in the preceding paragraph are independent of one another. The "Kolo child" state is required because no adult person stands behind a minor user whose account is awaiting takeover — the account is opened independently and the minor-user attribute is recorded immediately — so without that condition an adult could present themselves as a minor user and obtain an overview containing pseudonyms, pictures and amounts; behind an account in the "child with a parent" state stands a parent whose reality or parenthood has not been confirmed (Article 4c, paragraph 3), whereas behind an account in the "Kolo child" state stands a parent whose reality has been confirmed by someone who knows them personally and whose parenthood has been confirmed by a Kolo member. The restriction to one's own school is required because the overview serves a community in which the minor users already know one another; without it, a single account would obtain an overview of the minor users of every school.

That overview does not give access to a minor user's profile (Article 15b).

The stated school may be changed at most once every thirty days. The school datum is deleted upon transition to an adult account and upon termination of the capacity of user; no history of earlier choices is kept.

A position in the overview referred to in this Article is not a ground for recording a contribution and carries no POEN.

### Article 15b — Access to the Profile of a Minor User

The profile of a minor user is not available to adult users. Instead of the profile, a notice is displayed containing the pseudonym of the minor user and the pseudonym of their parent.

The parental consent referred to in Article 10 does not open access to the profile; it governs communication and exchange.

Contact between an adult user and a minor is established in connection with a listing published by the minor user, in the manner set out in Article 12 and subject to the parental insight under Article 9.

A minor user may access the profile of a minor user with whom they have formed a friendship.

Information on who the parents of a minor user are, and on who the children of an adult user are, is available to signed-in users.

This Article does not restrict the Foundation in acting upon a report or in removing content.

## V — The Chat Room

### Article 18

Minor users have a single Chat Room, separate from the Chat Room of adult users.

The Chat Room is not available to an account in the "waiting child" state. The reason is a security one: behind an account opened or taken over by a parent stands an adult user who has given the statement under Article 6 under full responsibility and who is liable for the child's actions (Article 10), whereas behind a waiting account stands no one. Until the account is taken over, that check does not exist even indirectly, because the parent does not read conversations between children (Article 9, paragraph 2).

In the Chat Room, a minor user sees the messages of their friends under Article 14a.

A reply quoting another's message is not supported, because it would circumvent the limitation under paragraph 3.

## VI — Majority, Dormancy, and Termination

### Article 19 — Transition to an Adult Account

Upon reaching eighteen years of age, the account ceases to be a minor user's account.

On that day the POEN recorded under Article 14b is annulled: for that user, in the amount of the product of the number of valid recorded friendships and 500 POEN, and for each of those friends, 500 POEN. The annulment is executed even where the record thereby becomes negative, under the restitution regime of Article 14, paragraph 9. Contributions relating to ended friendships, to friendships under Article 14b, paragraph 4, and to friendships that had not been recorded are not annulled.

The verification is given to them automatically by the first parent, in the order of linking with the child's account, who is a Kolo member or a founder; that verification is a confirmation, so the adult user becomes a Kolo member. If no parent is a Kolo member or a founder, but a parent is a member without confirmation, the verification is given automatically by the first such parent in the order of linking; that verification is an introduction into the Kolo, so the adult user becomes a member without confirmation (Article 5b of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality). The other parent gives no verification and takes no action. If no parent is in the Kolo, the adult user has no status other than that of user.

The friendships under Article 14a cease.

Other POEN records are not touched.

The minor user and their friends are notified of the consequences under paragraph 2 one month in advance.

Rationale: a friendship under Article 14b arises from a direct meeting and carries 500 POEN, whereas a verification carries 1,000 POEN but requires a statement about another person's identity and one's own liability for its truthfulness. Without the annulment, a minor user who accedes immediately before majority would retain a stock from the cheaper channel and acquire the more expensive one alongside it, thereby being permanently in a more favourable position than a peer who accedes one month later.

### Article 20 — Return to the "Child with a Parent" State

Where no parent any longer meets the conditions under Article 4c, paragraph 1, item 3 — because a parent's confirmation has ceased, or because the confirmation of the child's existence has been annulled — the minor user's account moves to the "child with a parent" state under Article 4c. No data are deleted.

The parent's own account is not affected thereby.

### Article 21

A parent may delete their child's account.

Deletion of the account annuls all of the minor user's POEN records, with a corresponding counter-entry of the Protocol, and removes all of their listings. Friendships cease without a write-off on the side of other minor users, because the POEN has already been annulled in full.

## VII — Final Provisions

### Article 22

The module is activated upon the adoption of this Rulebook, the alignment of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, the Terms of Use, and the Privacy Policy, and upon the update of the data protection impact assessment.

### Article 22a — Existing Confirmations of a Child's Existence

Confirmations of a child's existence given before the entry into force of this version of the Rulebook remain valid.

The existence of a founder's child whose account was linked with the founder's account before the entry into force of this version of the Rulebook is deemed confirmed.

### Article 23

This Rulebook enters into force on the day of its adoption.
