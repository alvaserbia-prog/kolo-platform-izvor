# Rulebook on Support Programmes

## I — General provisions

### Article 1 — Subject matter

(1) This Rulebook governs, in accordance with Article 51 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, the support programmes: the groups to which they relate, the application and the confirmation that the conditions are met, consent, the amounts and duration of the recording of POEN under each programme, and the protection of personal data in support programmes.

(2) Terms from the Rulebook on the KOLO System have the same meaning in this Rulebook.

### Article 2 — Programmes and support recipient

(1) The support programmes are:

1) Mothers Support: for mothers and primary caregivers of children;

2) Elderly Support: for participants who have reached the age of 50;

3) Special Support: for participants with reduced capacity and participants who have lost their home;

4) Student Support: for students.

(2) A **support recipient** is a participant whose application to a programme has been approved, while the application is valid.

(3) A minor user is not entitled to support programmes.

(4) New programmes are introduced by the Foundation in Phase 1, or by the Upper Kolo in Phase 2, by amending this Rulebook (Article 51 paragraph 4 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

### Article 3 — Nature of the recording

(1) By the recording of POEN under a support programme the support recipient receives neither money nor goods. The Foundation makes no payment on that basis, nothing passes from its assets into the support recipient's assets, and the record so created cannot be turned into money, assigned as a claim, or collected (Article 16 paragraph 8 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(2) The recording is not remuneration: it pays for no work, service, or other performance, nor is anything required of the support recipient in return towards the Foundation. A support recipient is not employed by the Foundation and enters into no contractual relationship with it on the basis of the programme. The recording contains no promise of a future service or other performance, so the record is not a claim either against the Foundation or against another user (Article 51 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(3) A support programme does not replace rights under the social protection system and is not conditional on waiving them.

## II — Application and confirmation that the conditions are met

### Article 4 — Application

(1) The right to a programme is exercised by a participant who enters in the application the data establishing membership of a group under Article 2 and gives the consent under Article 6.

(2) No documents are attached to the application. Only the data that this Rulebook specifies for a particular programme are entered.

(3) The Foundation decides on the application. It may approve the application only once it has been confirmed under Article 5 that the conditions are met. Automatic recording of POEN begins upon approval of the application.

(4) For the same programme a participant may have only one valid application.

### Article 5 — Confirmation that the conditions are met

(1) Before the Foundation decides, the fact that the conditions are met is confirmed by a Kolo member, or a founder, who knows the applicant personally. That is the Kolo member, or founder, who confirmed the applicant, as well as the Kolo member who, while they were themselves a member without confirmation, introduced the applicant into the Kolo as a user whom they know personally.

(2) The fact that the conditions are met is not confirmed by a Kolo member who introduced the applicant into the Kolo, or confirmed them, after a telephone conversation (Articles 4 and 16 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality), nor by a member without confirmation (Article 6 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality).

(3) Where the applicant has more than one Kolo member under paragraph 1, confirmation is requested from all of them, and confirmation by one is sufficient. Where all of them refuse confirmation, the application is refused.

(4) The Kolo member confirms, under full responsibility and on the basis of their own knowledge, that the applicant satisfies the condition of the programme, or refuses confirmation with a statement of reasons. They do not see the data entered by the applicant.

(5) Where the applicant has no Kolo member under paragraph 1, the Foundation establishes whether the conditions are met, with a statement of reasons.

(6) The Kolo member from whom confirmation is requested is notified of the request within the Platform. The message the Platform sends by electronic mail or to the user's device states neither the name of the programme nor the applicant's pseudonym.

### Article 6 — Consent

(1) The application and the request for confirmation that the conditions are met are made exclusively with the explicit consent of the applicant. Consent is given before the application is submitted and before confirmation is requested from anyone.

(2) The consent expressly states:

1) how many persons will be asked to confirm, or that the Foundation establishes whether the conditions are met;

2) that those persons learn which programme is concerned and that this information may reveal to them the applicant's membership of a special category of data, and that they do not see the data entered;

3) that the recording of POEN under the programme is displayed to participants, together with the applicant's pseudonym, the name of the programme, and the amount (Article 20);

4) what may be derived from the amount: in the case of the Elderly Support programme the applicant's year of birth, and in the case of the Mothers Support programme the number of children and their ages;

5) that the ground on which the entitlement was established is not displayed to any user.

(3) The text of the consent given by the applicant is kept with the application.

### Article 7 — Withdrawal of consent

(1) Consent may be withdrawn at any time, without stating reasons. The withdrawal action is available to the applicant within the Platform, alongside the programme itself.

(2) Withdrawal terminates the procedure, ends the automatic recording of POEN, and deletes the data entered in the application. POEN recorded up to that moment is not affected.

### Article 8 — Suspension and new application

(1) Automatic recording is suspended, and the application ceases to be valid:

1) when the support recipient ceases to be in the Kolo;

2) when the period for which the entitlement lasts, or the review period established by this Rulebook, expires;

3) upon termination of user status.

(2) The support recipient is notified of the suspension and its reason.

(3) After the application ceases to be valid, or after it has been refused, the participant may submit a new application. A new application requires re-entry of the data, fresh consent, and fresh confirmation that the conditions are met.

## III — Recording

### Article 9 — Moment of recording

(1) The recording of POEN under support programmes is an automatic act of the Protocol and is performed once daily, at the end of the accounting period, while the application is valid.

(2) Amounts are recorded as whole numbers; rounding is performed downwards.

### Article 10 — Daily limit and proportional distribution

(1) Support programmes and operational contribution have a common daily limit. The daily limit and the proportional distribution where the sum of the amounts requested exceeds the limit are established by Articles 20 and 21 of the Rulebook on Operational Contribution; the unrecorded surplus is not carried over to the next accounting period.

(2) Apart from the daily limit, the number of POEN recorded under support programmes for one support recipient is not limited.

## IV — Mothers Support

### Article 11 — Right, data and duration

(1) The date of birth of each child is entered in the application; no other data on the child are entered.

(2) Support for a particular child continues until the child reaches the age of 20; upon reaching the age of 20, that child is no longer counted.

### Article 12 — Amount

(1) The daily base amount is 2,000 POEN per child and is reduced by 100 POEN for each completed year of the child's age. The reduction is applied to the base amount, not to the coefficient, and the base amount may not fall below zero.

(2) The amount per child is multiplied by a coefficient according to the child's birth order:

| Birth order | Coefficient |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | 1.0 |
| 2nd | 1.2 |
| 3rd | 1.5 |
| 4th | 2.0 |
| 5th | 3.0 |
| 6th | 4.5 |
| 7th | 6.0 |
| 8th | 8.0 |
| 9th | 10.0 |

(3) For the tenth child and each subsequent child, the coefficient increases by 2.0 per child.

(4) The daily amount for the child in birth order i equals ⌊(2,000 − 100 × age) × coefficient(i)⌋. The total daily amount is the sum of the amounts for all children.

## V — Elderly Support

### Article 13 — Right and amount

(1) The right is held by a participant who has reached the age of 50. The date of birth is entered in the application.

(2) The daily amount equals 1,000 + 100 × (number of completed years − 50) POEN.

## VI — Special Support

### Article 14 — Grounds

(1) The right under the Special Support programme is exercised on one of two grounds:

1) reduced capacity (Article 15);

2) loss of home (Article 16).

(2) The right under this programme is also exercised alongside the right under the Mothers Support programme, including where both are exercised in respect of the same child.

### Article 15 — Reduced capacity

(1) The right on this ground exists where a decision of the competent authority has established a degree of disability, including a decision of the work-capacity assessment commission, as well as where a person, on account of an acute or chronic illness, is unable or substantially limited in participating in exchange and in other activities in the system.

(2) What is taken into account is the existence of the decision, not its content. The date of the decision and, where the decision contains one, the date of expiry are entered in the application; the degree of disability, the assessment of work capacity, a diagnosis, and medical documentation are neither entered nor retained. Where the right is exercised on account of illness, the proof is a declaration of the applicant made under full responsibility.

(3) The right is exercised by the person with reduced capacity where that person is an adult participant. Where that person is not a user, the right is exercised by the participant who directly cares for them, and where that person is a minor, by the parent or legal representative who directly cares for them. One right arises per person with reduced capacity.

(4) Concerning the person for whom the applicant cares, only the date on which they come of age is entered in the application, where they are a minor. Nothing is requested of that person.

### Article 16 — Loss of home

(1) The right on this ground is held by a participant who has lost their home or the property necessary for living as a result of a natural disaster, fire, flood, or forced displacement.

(2) The proof is a declaration of the applicant made under full responsibility. The date of the event is entered in the application.

### Article 17 — Amount

The daily amount is 2,000 POEN, the same on both grounds. A support recipient who meets both grounds, or who cares for more than one person, is entitled to one daily amount.

### Article 18 — Duration and review

(1) The right on the ground of reduced capacity is subject to review every 12 months from the approval of the application. Where it is exercised on account of an acute illness, it lasts 6 months from approval.

(2) The right on the ground of reduced capacity does not last beyond the expiry of the decision and, where it is exercised for a minor, not beyond that person coming of age.

(3) The right on the ground of loss of home lasts 12 months from the event and ceases upon the expiry of that period, without review. An application submitted after the expiry of that period is refused.

(4) Upon the expiry of the period under paragraph 1 the application ceases to be valid, and the support recipient may submit a new application if the condition persists (Article 8).

## VII — Student Support

### Article 19 — Right and statement

(1) The right is held by a participant who is duly enrolled at a higher education institution.

(2) Proof of status is a statement made under full responsibility, stating the institution and the year of study. Enrolment confirmations, certificates, and other documents are not requested.

(3) An untrue statement entails the measures set out in the Terms of Use, including suspension and exclusion (Articles 31 and 32 of the Terms of Use), and the cessation of recording under this programme.

(4) The daily amount is 2,000 POEN.

(5) The right is subject to review every 6 months from the approval of the application. Upon the expiry of that period the application ceases to be valid, and the support recipient may submit a new application with a new statement.

## VIII — Display of recording and data protection

### Article 20 — Display of recording

(1) The recording of POEN under a support programme is displayed to participants together with the support recipient's pseudonym, the name of the programme, and the amount.

(2) Persons who are not signed in and users who are not in the Kolo are shown exclusively a daily total per programme: the number of support recipients and the total number of recorded POEN. A day on which, within one programme, a contribution was recorded for only one support recipient is not published.

(3) The ground on which the entitlement was established is not displayed to any user. A breakdown by ground is available only to the support recipient, alongside the programme, and to the person processing the application within the Foundation.

(4) The name of the programme is not entered in the description of the POEN record.

### Article 21 — Data processing

(1) Support programmes require the processing of special categories of personal data within the meaning of Article 17 of the Law on Personal Data Protection ("Official Gazette of the RS", No. 87/2018). The legal basis for processing is the explicit consent of the applicant under Article 6.

(2) Only the data necessary to establish status and amount, as specified by this Rulebook, are collected.

(3) The entered data are not public and are accessible exclusively to the person processing the application within the Foundation, and not to the Kolo members who confirm that the conditions are met. The Foundation does not disclose data on participation in a programme to anyone, except where it is obliged to do so by law.

(4) The entered data are deleted when the application ceases to be valid: by refusal, by suspension under Article 8, by withdrawal of consent, or by termination of user status.

(5) An amendment to this Rulebook that introduces a new category of special data is adopted following a prior update of the data protection impact assessment.

## IX — Final provision

### Article 22 — Entry into force

This Rulebook enters into force on the date of its adoption.
