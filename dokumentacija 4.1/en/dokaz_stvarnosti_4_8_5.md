# Rulebook on Proof of Reality

*This Rulebook governs the operational mechanics of proof of reality — the user verification model of the KOLO system based on personal acquaintance. It is adopted on the basis of Art. 32 paragraph 6 and Art. 15 point 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.*

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1

*Subject matter*

This Rulebook governs: belonging to the Kolo and confirmation status of users, the call list, the chain of confirmations, confirmation and introduction into the Kolo, confirmation after a telephone conversation, the list of linked users, the position of a member without confirmation, verification upon coming of age, the verification record, the POEN ledger entry for verification, the daily pace of confirming, supervision and the supervision outcome, the supervision case, the chain without a loop, the initial mechanism, the consequences of loss of status on verifications, the procedure for establishing a false verification, and restitution for POEN records withdrawn from the network.

Terms not defined in this Rulebook shall have the meaning established in the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

### Article 2

*Relationship with the Rulebook on the KOLO System*

This Rulebook elaborates the provisions of Chapter V of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. In the event of any inconsistency, the provisions of the Rulebook on the KOLO System shall prevail.

As of this amendment, the term "participant" in the acts of the Foundation denotes only a person who is in the Kolo and replaces the earlier term "participant in the Kolo", or "verified participant"; where an act adopted before this amendment refers to a verified participant, a participant is meant, where it refers to a participant regardless of status, a user is meant, and where it refers to a user as a status, a user who is not in the Kolo is meant.

## II — Belonging to the Kolo and Confirmation

### Article 3

*One confirmation*

A user is in the Kolo when one valid verification has been recorded for them in the chain of confirmations. A verification is a confirmation or an introduction into the Kolo (Article 5a). A user for whom a confirmation has been recorded is a Kolo member; a user introduced into the Kolo is a member without confirmation until they are confirmed (Article 5a paragraph 3). A user's reality in the chain of confirmations is established by one verifier. The position of founders is governed by Article 14 of this Rulebook.

A participant is a Kolo member, including a Kolo keeper and a founder, and a member without confirmation. Only a Kolo member is confirmed: a user for whom a confirmation has been recorded, a member without confirmation once they have been confirmed (Article 5a paragraph 3), and a founder (Article 14). Where this Rulebook and other acts of the Foundation refer to confirmation status, or to a confirmed user or a user whose reality has been confirmed, this means only a Kolo member. A member without confirmation has the rights and obligations that the acts of the Foundation attach to a participant; they do not have the rights attached to a Kolo member or to a confirmed user, unless an act expressly provides otherwise.

A participant cannot be verified again while their verification remains valid. A second verification of the same user is not recorded; the confirmation of a member without confirmation is not a new verification (Article 5a).

Belonging to the Kolo has no degrees. The system distinguishes only between a user who is in the Kolo and a user who is not in the Kolo; no number, percentage or other indicator of a degree of belonging is kept. The difference between a confirmation and an introduction into the Kolo is not a degree of belonging: the reality of a member without confirmation is established by verification in the same way as the reality of a Kolo member, and the difference determines only the actions under Article 5a paragraphs 8 and 9 of this Rulebook and other actions that the acts of the Foundation attach to a Kolo member or to a confirmed user.

### Article 4

*Functional effect of verification and confirmation status*

Verification conditions access to the system functions that belong to a participant. Confirmation status conditions confirming the reality of other users and other actions that the acts of the Foundation attach to a Kolo member.

A Kolo member has full access to all platform functions — exchange, contribution recording, participation in cooperatives and social programmes, and confirming the reality of other users. A member without confirmation has the same access, within the limits of Article 5a of this Rulebook.

An adult user who is not in the Kolo has the status of user. The only exception are the founders, who are the origin of the chain of confirmations (Article 14). The position of a minor user is governed by the Rulebook on the Participation of Children.

A Kolo keeper whose verification ceases becomes a user. Their ZRNO remains entered and frozen: they do not activate it, do not write it off, do not delegate a vote and do not take part in the Upper Kolo until they are confirmed again (Article 30 paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

### Article 4a

*Call list*

A user who does not know any Kolo member may be placed on the list of users awaiting a call. A member without confirmation who seeks confirmation after a telephone conversation (Article 5c) also comes onto the call list. The list is kept on the Confirmations page.

A user is placed on the call list:

1) when they themselves ask for a conversation ("Contact us"), under the conditions of Article 16b of the Terms of Use;

2) when the Foundation establishes the receipt of their public donation in an amount of at least 5,000 dinars, made in one payment, where the name of the payer from the bank statement matches the user's name and the payer is not attached to another account;

3) when the Foundation establishes the receipt of a sponsorship in money under an application the user submitted (Article 8 of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations);

4) when, as the holder, they submit an application for a farm, regardless of whether the farm is entered in the Register of Agricultural Holdings.

An anonymous donation, the sum of several smaller payments, a payment for which it has been established that the payer is attached to another account, and sponsorship in goods and services do not place a user on the call list.

Before being placed on the list, the user, or the member without confirmation, on each ground under paragraph 2 and under Article 5c, enters their first and last name, place and telephone number and is informed that a Kolo member, or a Kolo keeper, will call them and that they will see their first and last name and telephone number, as well as the indication under paragraph 12. The first and last name is entered in the identity register (Article 31a paragraph 2 item 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), and the place and telephone number alongside the account. Those data remain alongside the account as long as it exists and are deleted upon its closure; the user may change the place and telephone number, but may not delete them.

The first and last name of the user, or of the member without confirmation, from the identity register (Article 31a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System) and their telephone number are revealed by a click, only to the Kolo member who opened the call and only while the call is open. The Foundation records which Kolo member revealed the data. The request of a member without confirmation for confirmation is seen and opened only by Kolo keepers, because only they confirm after a telephone conversation (Article 5c).

The call list is not visible to members without confirmation (Article 5a).

Being placed on the call list does not confirm reality. Neither the donation or sponsorship, nor the contribution recorded on account of them, depends on it; that contribution is recorded under the general rules of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations.

A user from the call list is introduced into the Kolo by a Kolo member after a conversation (Article 5 paragraph 7 and Article 5a). A member without confirmation from the call list may be confirmed by a Kolo keeper after a conversation under the conditions of Article 5c.

The Foundation is obliged to ensure that a user or a member without confirmation who has come onto the call list is called by a Kolo member, or a Kolo keeper respectively, at the latest within seven days of the day on which they came onto the list. The platform shows them by which day they will be called, and shows that deadline to Kolo members next to each row of the list. The Foundation ensures the call by displaying the request to Kolo members, or to Kolo keepers respectively, together with the deadline, and by separately marking a request whose deadline has passed.

The conversation is conducted by the Kolo member of their own free will: no one obliges them to conduct it, and by the conversation they do not perform any work for the Foundation. Before the name and number are opened for the first time, the Kolo member declares that they will use them only for that conversation, will not record them and will not pass them on to anyone; only the time of the declaration is recorded. They process the name and number under the authority of the Foundation and solely for that purpose.

A member without confirmation who has entered ZRNO (Article 5a paragraph 7) comes onto the call list in first place, as soon as they meet the conditions of Article 5c paragraph 2. The Foundation is obliged to ensure that a Kolo keeper calls them as soon as possible, and at the latest within three days of the day on which they came onto the list. If the conditions of Article 5c paragraph 2 are not met, the platform shows them which condition is not met and from which day it will be met.

A minor user is not placed on the call list. An account for which the Foundation has established under Article 4b that it is another account of the same person is not placed on the list; until that has been established, a match of the data under paragraph 12 does not exclude the account from the list.

The first and last name and the telephone number are compared with the names and numbers kept alongside other accounts, in order to prevent multiple accounts. Where the same telephone number, or the same first and last name, is kept alongside another account, the Kolo member who opened the call sees, next to the name and number, an indication that a match exists and the place of the other account, but not its pseudonym; both accounts are seen only by the Foundation's administrator team. A match is not a prohibition and prevents neither entry onto the list nor introduction into the Kolo: the same number may be shared by members of a household, and the same name by namesakes. Only the Foundation decides on a multiple account, under Article 4b.

### Article 4b

*Establishing a multiple account*

Each natural person may have one account (Article 8 of the Terms of Use). The verifier does not guarantee that the verified person has no other account, because they know the person, not their accounts; that two accounts are accounts of the same person is established by the Foundation under this Article.

The procedure is initiated on the basis of the indication under Article 4a paragraph 12, on a note by a Kolo member after a conversation, on a report, or on knowledge obtained in another way. Only the Foundation establishes this, never the platform by itself.

Before establishing this, the Foundation compares the data kept alongside both accounts — telephone number, first and last name, the payer from the bank statement, place, and the chain of confirmations — and contacts the person on both accounts. The decision is taken with a statement of reasons communicated to the person. A complaint against the decision is permitted within fifteen days (Article 37a of the Terms of Use).

Where it establishes that two accounts are accounts of the same person, the Foundation determines the account the person retains, and the other account ceases. The contribution recorded to the ceasing account is annulled (Article 34 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), and the verifications that account received and gave fall under the rules of Chapter VIII of this Rulebook on accounts that are not unique. The account the person retains has written off the net amount of the record updates made from the ceasing account in favour of the retained account, reduced by the record updates in the opposite direction; the write-off is executed even where the record thereby becomes negative (Article 14 paragraph 3 item 8 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), and the person is notified of the write-off.

Where the ceasing account was used to acquire POENs or a vote, the Foundation may also exclude the person (Article 28 of the Terms of Use).

The records of other persons in whose favour the ledger was updated from the ceasing account are not affected. Together with the decision under paragraph 4, the Foundation opens a single supervision case concerning the recipients of those record updates, with the sole question of whether the recipient is the same person or whether the record updates were fictitious (Article 24 of the Terms of Use). While the case is pending, the recipients' accounts are not restricted, and the recipient is not notified until a measure is imposed on them; a measure is imposed only under the procedure under this Article, or under the Terms of Use. The case is opened only upon establishing a multiple account and is not monitoring of ledger updates.

## III — Chain of Confirmations

### Article 5

*Verification mechanism*

Verification is carried out within the chain of confirmations: a verifier — a Kolo member, a founder or a member without confirmation — establishes the reality of a user on the basis of direct acquaintance, by a confirmation or by an introduction into the Kolo (Article 5a). The verifier confirms two things: reality (the verified person exists as a natural person) and continuity (the same person who accesses the system), and declares that they are not aware that the verified person has another account in the system. The verifier does not guarantee uniqueness; a multiple account is established by the Foundation (Article 4b).

Verification is an act of personal acquaintance, not of document verification. The verifier does not collect or submit the personal documents of the person being verified.

Verification is based on direct personal acquaintance sufficient for the verifier to confirm, on their own responsibility, the reality and continuity of the verified person and to answer for that confirmation. This Rulebook does not prescribe the manner of acquiring such acquaintance nor require physical presence at the moment of verification; the verifier alone assesses whether they know the person being verified well enough to confirm their reality.

The platform provides a technical mechanism for consent and account binding: the user seeking verification generates a one-time code by which they consent to the verification and bind their account to that act, and a verifier who knows them carries out the verification using that code. This mechanism does not collect the personal data of the person being verified and does not constitute proof of presence, but rather confirmation of the verified person's consent and account identity.

The verified person is notified of the completed verification and may report it if they do not know the verifier.

The verifier is responsible for the truthfulness of the verification. A verification by which the reality of a person who does not exist as a natural person, or whose continuity is not ensured, has been confirmed, as well as a verification by which the verifier confirmed a person whom they knew to have another account in the system, is a false verification and entails the consequences set out in Chapter VIII of this Rulebook.

A user who does not know any Kolo member may be placed on the call list (Article 4a). A Kolo member who calls them may introduce them into the Kolo only if, after the conversation, they assess that they know them well enough to answer for the verification (paragraph 3); the conversation is not in itself a verification. A verification carried out after such a conversation is an introduction into the Kolo (Article 5a).

### Article 5a

*Confirmation and introduction into the Kolo*

A verification is a confirmation or an introduction into the Kolo. A confirmation is a verification by which a Kolo member or a founder confirms the reality of a user whom they know personally; the confirmed person becomes a Kolo member. An introduction into the Kolo is a verification by which a Kolo member, after a telephone conversation, introduces a user from the call list (Article 4a), or by which a member without confirmation introduces a user whom they know personally; the introduced person becomes a member without confirmation. The type of verification is entered in the verification record.

A member without confirmation is an adult user whom someone has introduced into the Kolo and who has not yet been confirmed by a Kolo member who knows them personally. A verification carried out by a member without confirmation is always an introduction into the Kolo, so the person they introduce is also a member without confirmation.

A member without confirmation is confirmed by a Kolo member or a founder who knows them personally; the confirmed person thereby becomes a Kolo member. An exchange of goods and services may be the way in which they met, but is not a condition for confirmation. A member without confirmation may also be confirmed by a Kolo keeper after a telephone conversation, under the conditions of Article 5c. The Foundation may also confirm a member without confirmation by its own decision, according to criteria it sets by a separate decision. A member without confirmation never becomes a Kolo member automatically.

The confirmation of a member without confirmation is not a new verification: no new verification record is recorded and no POEN is entered on account of it. Who confirmed the member without confirmation, and when, is noted alongside the verification record. Whoever confirms them is responsible for it as for a confirmation, and Chapter VIII of this Rulebook applies to that confirmation; the prohibition under Article 12 applies to it as well, except in the case under Article 5d paragraph 3. The confirmation of a member without confirmation is subject to supervision in the same way as a verification (Articles 10 and 11); a confirmation given by a founder or a Kolo keeper is not subject to supervision (Article 9). If such a confirmation is established to be false, it is annulled, and the user remains a member without confirmation for as long as their introduction into the Kolo remains valid.

When a user becomes a Kolo member, they receive a list of linked users, from which they personally confirm those whom they know (Article 5d). A confirmation from the list of linked users is a confirmation of a member without confirmation under paragraph 4 of this Article; there is no automatic confirmation.

A founder has the position of a Kolo member in the chain of confirmations.

A member without confirmation enters ZRNO under the conditions of Article 19 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, but does not activate it: they have no vote, are not part of the Upper Kolo and do not delegate a vote. They may write off free ZRNO. By entering ZRNO they do not become a Kolo keeper; they become a Kolo keeper when they are confirmed (paragraph 3 of this Article). By entering ZRNO, a member without confirmation comes onto the call list for confirmation, in first place (Article 4a).

A member without confirmation may not:

1) see the call list (Article 4a) or introduce users from that list into the Kolo;

2) confirm anyone — neither another member without confirmation, nor the existence of a child, nor the fulfilment of the conditions for a support programme (Article 4 of the Rulebook on Support Programmes) — nor see a request to confirm the existence of a child;

3) give a member recommendation (Article 17a of the Terms of Use);

4) activate ZRNO (paragraph 7 of this Article);

5) perform supervision (Article 10).

A member without confirmation may register a farm as holder, without members, under the conditions of the Rulebook on Farms. Their child does not become a Kolo child; the position of the child is governed by the Rulebook on the Participation of Children.

An introduction into the Kolo is an ordinary verification: POEN are recorded on account of it under Article 7, and the daily pace, supervision and responsibility for a false verification apply as they do to a confirmation. The verifier is responsible only for the truthfulness of the verification, not for monitoring the verified person after it.

### Article 5b

*Verification upon coming of age*

When a minor user comes of age, the verification is given to them automatically by the first parent, in the order of linking to the child's account, who is a Kolo member or a founder; that verification is a confirmation (Article 5a).

If no parent is a Kolo member or a founder, but a parent is a member without confirmation, the verification is given automatically by the first such parent in the order of linking; that verification is an introduction into the Kolo (Article 5a).

If no parent is in the Kolo, the adult user has no status other than that of user.

The transition of a minor user to an adult account is further governed by the Rulebook on the Participation of Children (Article 19).

### Article 5c

*Confirmation after a telephone conversation*

A member without confirmation whom no Kolo member knows personally may be confirmed by a Kolo keeper after a telephone conversation. Such a confirmation is a confirmation of a member without confirmation under Article 5a paragraph 4.

A Kolo keeper may confirm a member without confirmation after a telephone conversation only if all of the following conditions are met:

1) the member without confirmation has been in the Kolo for at least 30 days from the introduction;

2) no exchange report or listing report against them has been upheld;

3) no supervision case (Article 11a) against them has been concluded with a finding of false verification.

A member without confirmation who meets the conditions under paragraph 2 may request a conversation for confirmation and then comes onto the call list (Article 4a); a member without confirmation who has entered ZRNO comes onto the list in first place. The platform notifies them of this as soon as the conditions are met.

A Kolo keeper confirms only if, after the conversation, they assess that they can answer for the reality and continuity of the member without confirmation (Article 5 paragraph 1); the conversation in itself is not a confirmation. They are responsible for the confirmation under Chapter VIII of this Rulebook. A confirmation after a telephone conversation is always subject to supervision, even when given by a founder (Article 10), and is counted in the daily pace of confirming (Article 8). No POEN is entered on account of it.

### Article 5d

*List of linked participants*

When a user becomes a Kolo member, whether by being confirmed or by having a confirmation recorded as their verification, the platform shows them the list of linked users: those whom they introduced into the Kolo while they were a member without confirmation and who are still members without confirmation (one level down), and the one who introduced them into the Kolo, if that person is also still a member without confirmation (one level up).

A Kolo member confirms from the list of linked participants those whom they know personally, by marking them, without a new conversation. Each marked person is a separate confirmation of a member without confirmation (Article 5a paragraph 4): the one who marked them is responsible for it, under Chapter VIII of this Rulebook, it is subject to supervision under Article 10 and is counted in the daily pace of confirming (Article 8). No POEN is entered on account of a confirmation from the list of linked participants.

The confirmation of the person who introduced the Kolo member (one level up) is an exception to the prohibition under Article 12: a Kolo member may confirm the member without confirmation who introduced them into the Kolo, because that person introduced them on the basis of personal acquaintance. Such a confirmation is always subject to supervision, even when given by a Kolo keeper or a founder.

The list of linked participants covers only one level down and one level up. Everyone who is confirmed from the list of linked participants becomes a Kolo member and receives their own list of linked participants. Confirmation thus spreads through the chain step by step, and each step is a personal confirmation by the one who knows the linked person.

### Article 6

*Verification record*

Every verification is recorded in a verification record containing six data items:

— the identifier of the verifier (pseudonym);

— the ordinal number of the verifier's verification — which verification in sequence this is for that verifier;

— the identifier of the verified person (pseudonym);

— the timestamp of the verification;

— the type of verification (confirmation or introduction into the Kolo, Article 5a), and, alongside an introduction into the Kolo, the information on who confirmed the introduced person and when, and in what manner (personally, from the list of linked participants under Article 5d, or after a telephone conversation under Article 5c), where this occurs, and the identifier of the supervisor of that confirmation or an empty field if it is not subject to supervision;

— the identifier of the supervisor (pseudonym), or an empty field if the verification is not subject to supervision.

A verification subject to supervision is supplemented, once a supervision outcome is recorded, with supervision data: the supervision outcome and, alongside the outcomes "needs review" and "disputed", the subject of suspicion and the reason code under Article 11 of this Rulebook. Supervision data are kept for each supervisor who recorded an outcome. The same applies to the confirmation of a member without confirmation that is subject to supervision (Article 5a paragraph 4).

Supervision data are not public. They form part of the verification graph and the visibility rules of Article 67 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System apply to them; they are shown neither to the verifier nor to the verified person.

The verification record forms part of the record of the common good. Verification records constitute the verification graph within the meaning of Article 32 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

### Article 7

*POEN ledger entry for verification*

Upon the recording of a verification record, the Protocol notes new POEN records: 1,000 POEN to the verifier and 1,000 POEN to the verified person.

A noted contribution is entered in the Protocol once the verified person makes their first confirmed contribution to the common good, namely:

1) the first listing by which they offer a good or a service, approved within the meaning of Article 40a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System;

2) a donation to the Foundation with publication of the donor's details, upon receipt of which the contribution is recorded within the meaning of Article 5a of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations;

3) a sponsorship under which the contribution has been recorded in the record of the user who submitted the application;

4) an operational contribution whose performance has been verified within the meaning of the Rulebook on Operational Contribution.

What all the forms in the preceding paragraph have in common is that the contribution has been confirmed by the Foundation or by a Kolo keeper — none of them can be brought about by the user's own decision. Updating the POEN ledger between users (Article 16 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System) is not a contribution within the meaning of this Article.

Where the condition under paragraph 2 is met before the verification record is recorded, the entry occurs at the moment that record is recorded.

Until entered, a noted contribution does not constitute a POEN record: it forms no part of any user's record, nor of the total number of POEN recorded in the system.

The verification record, the verification and the rights arising from them come into being upon the recording of the verification record and do not depend on the POEN entry under this Article.

The condition under paragraph 2 does not apply to verification upon coming of age (Article 5b of this Rulebook and Article 19 of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children); in that case the entry occurs at the moment the verification record is recorded.

If the verification record ceases to be valid before the entry, the noted contribution lapses. The annulment of POEN records and the restitution under Article 20b do not apply, since no entry was ever made.

The Foundation may enter a noted contribution even where the condition under paragraph 2 is not met, subject to a statement of reasons that is recorded. That possibility exists for users who contribute to the common good in a manner not achieved through any of the forms under paragraph 2.

Supervision of a verification (Article 10) is not recorded as a contribution and no POEN is entered to the supervisor for it. The total entry per verification amounts to 2,000 POEN, regardless of whether the verification is subject to supervision.

POEN records a contribution to the common good. A verification record establishes that the verified person is a real person, but in itself contains no contribution — which is why a POEN record comes into being once the first confirmed trace of that user's participation appears in the system. This also prevents the total number of recorded POEN from growing on account of accounts that have made no contribution whatsoever in the system.

The recording of POEN for verification is an automatic act of the Protocol within the meaning of Article 15 point 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

## IV — Daily Pace of Confirming and Supervision

### Article 8

*Daily pace of confirming*

The number of verifications a verifier may perform is not limited. What is limited is the pace: the verifications the verifier has performed in the course of one calendar day according to Serbian time are counted, including confirmations of members without confirmation (Article 5a paragraph 4), confirmations from the list of linked participants (Article 5d) and confirmations after a telephone conversation (Article 5c).

Before the third and before the fourth verification on the same day, the verifier is warned that rapidly confirming a larger number of persons is regarded as a sign of possible abuse of the chain of confirmations and that, if they continue, their confirming will be stopped.

The fifth verification on the same day is not recorded. The verifier's ability to verify is stopped until the end of that day, and the Foundation is notified thereof. The stop applies exclusively to verifying other users; other platform functions remain available.

Stopping verifying is neither a suspension nor a measure against the verifier. On the occasion of the notification, the Foundation may initiate the procedure under Chapter VIII of this Rulebook or the suspension procedure under the Terms of Use.

The rule in this Article applies to all verifiers, including founders, Kolo keepers and members without confirmation. It does not apply to verification upon coming of age (Article 5b of this Rulebook and Article 19 of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children).

### Article 9

*Verifications by founders and Kolo keepers*

Verifications performed by founders and Kolo keepers, as well as confirmations of members without confirmation given by them, are not subject to supervision (Article 10), except in the case under paragraph 2 of this Article. The daily pace of confirming under Article 8 of this Rulebook applies to the verifications.

An introduction into the Kolo after a telephone conversation with a user from the call list (Article 4a and Article 5 paragraph 7) is always subject to supervision, even when carried out by a Kolo keeper. Only a founder is exempt. A confirmation after a telephone conversation (Article 5c) and an upward confirmation from the list of linked users (Article 5d paragraph 3) are always subject to supervision, without exception.

### Article 10

*Supervision*

Verifications performed by members without confirmation and by Kolo members who are neither founders nor Kolo keepers are subject to supervision. The confirmation of a member without confirmation given by a Kolo member who is neither a founder nor a Kolo keeper (Article 5a paragraph 4), including a confirmation from the list of linked users (Article 5d), is also subject to supervision, as is every confirmation after a telephone conversation (Article 5c) and every upward confirmation from the list of linked users (Article 5d paragraph 3), regardless of who gives it. The confirmation of the existence of a child (Article 6 of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children) given by a Kolo member who is neither a founder nor a Kolo keeper is also subject to supervision. An introduction into the Kolo after a telephone conversation with a user from the call list is always subject to supervision, even when carried out by a Kolo keeper; only a founder is exempt (Article 9 paragraph 2). Other verifications and confirmations performed by founders and Kolo keepers are not subject to supervision.

A supervisor is every Kolo keeper. The supervisory function follows automatically from status, without appointment, and does not depend on the phase of the system.

The supervisor examines the legitimacy of the verification performed and records the supervision outcome in accordance with Article 11 of this Rulebook. No POEN is entered for supervision performed (Article 7).

Supervision may not be performed by a Kolo keeper who took part in the supervised verification or confirmation — neither as verifier nor as the verified person. The same supervisor may not record an outcome twice on the same verification record.

The provisions of this Article and of Articles 11 and 11a on the verification, the verification record, the verifier and the verified person apply also to the confirmation of a member without confirmation, to the person giving the confirmation and to the confirmed person.

### Article 11

*Supervision procedure and supervision outcome*

Supervision is performed after verification. A verification takes effect upon the recording of the verification record. The supervisor subsequently examines the verification and records the supervision outcome in the verification record.

The supervision outcome is one of three:

— **sound** — the supervisor finds nothing objectionable;

— **needs review** — something is unclear to the supervisor and they ask that another supervisor look at the record; the record remains available to the other supervisors. This outcome is not an assertion that the verification is false, but an invitation for someone else to look;

— **disputed** — the supervisor considers that the verification is untrue.

Alongside the outcomes "needs review" and "disputed", the supervisor must enter the subject of suspicion — the verifier, the verified person, both users, or a part of the network — and a reason code from the list in paragraph 4 of this Article. The outcome "sound" requires no statement of reasons.

The reason codes are:

— *they do not know each other* — there are grounds to suspect that the verifier and the verified person are not directly acquainted;

— *account shows no signs of reality* — the verified person's account shows no signs that a real person stands behind it;

— *duplicate account* — there are grounds to suspect that the verified person already has an account in the system;

— *pattern of verifications* — the distribution or frequency of verifications indicates coordinated conduct;

— *report by the verified person* — the verified person has reported that they do not know the verifier (Article 5 paragraph 5);

— *other* — a reason not covered by the preceding items, with a brief description.

No deadline is prescribed for recording a supervision outcome. The supervision outcome does not affect the verifier's ability to perform further verifications.

Recording a supervision outcome does not alter the effect of the verification. A verification takes effect upon the recording of the verification record and is annulled solely under the procedure in Chapter VIII of this Rulebook.

Verifications performed by a member without confirmation (Article 5a) are shown to supervisors first in the supervision queue, regardless of which verification in sequence it is for that verifier. The order does not alter the effect of the verification.

With each verification record awaiting supervision, the Platform shows the supervisor signals: that the verification is the second or a later one performed by the verifier on the same day; that the verifier was a member without confirmation at the time of the verification (Article 5a); that it is an introduction into the Kolo from the call list (Article 4a); that it is a confirmation after a telephone conversation (Article 5c) or an upward confirmation from the list of linked participants (Article 5d paragraph 3); that the verified person has not carried out any exchange seven, fourteen or thirty days after the verification; and that the verifier and the verified person are not from the same place and have not exchanged with each other. Signals are derived from data already kept in the system and are not stored as a separate record. A signal is not a supervision outcome or a claim that the verification is disputed, does not alter the effect of the verification, and does not release a verification without signals from supervision.

From data already kept in the system — the verification graph and the POEN ledger — the Foundation also carries out a closed-group check: it identifies groups of members without confirmation who have introduced one another into the Kolo and who exchange only within the group, without exchange with a Kolo member. The check only shows the group to the Foundation, which may invite the members of the group to a conversation. It produces no effect whatsoever towards a user: it does not change their status, verification or records, is not a supervision outcome or a claim that the verification is false, and does not in itself initiate the procedure under Chapter VIII of this Rulebook.

### Article 11a

*Supervision case*

Recording the outcome "needs review" or "disputed" automatically forms a supervision case. The case contains the designation of the verification record, the recorded outcomes, the subject of suspicion and the reason code.

The supervision case is available to the Management Board of the Foundation. It is not available to other supervisors, to the verifier, to the verified person, or to the public.

The supervision case is a record, not a body. Forming a case does not in itself produce legal effect towards a user and does not mean that the verification is false. A false verification is established solely by the body referred to in Article 18, under the procedure in Chapter VIII.

A case is closed by the establishment of a false verification or by a finding that there are no grounds for the suspicion. A case closed by a finding of no grounds is deleted upon the expiry of 90 days from closure. A suspicion that was not confirmed does not remain as a permanent record about a user.

If no other supervisor responds following the outcome "needs review", the case remains open. This produces no effect towards a user.

## V — Chain Without a Loop

### Article 12

*Chain without a loop*

A verifier may not verify anyone from their own ascending line — the person who verified them, nor the person who verified that verifier, and so on upward, to a founder.

The only exception to the prohibition under paragraph 1 is the upward confirmation from the list of linked participants (Article 5d paragraph 3): a Kolo member may confirm the member without confirmation who directly introduced them into the Kolo. The exception does not extend further upward, nor to verification.

The prohibition under paragraph 1 ensures that the chain of confirmations contains no loop: no one can be verified by a user whom they themselves, directly or indirectly, introduced into the chain. It is determined from the verifications in force; when a verification is annulled, the prohibition that arose from it ceases as well.

### Article 13

*Purpose*

Since every participant who is not a founder has one verifier, the chain of confirmations is a tree whose roots are the founders. The prohibition under Article 12, with the sole exception of the upward confirmation from the list of linked users, which is always under supervision, is the only safeguard of the graph that is needed: a second verification is not recorded (Article 3), so a user cannot collect confirmations within the same part of the network, and a loop would arise only if someone confirmed the person who had introduced them into the chain.

The defence against fake accounts does not rest on the number of confirmations, but on the verifier's responsibility for every confirmation (Chapter VIII), on the daily pace of confirming (Article 8), on supervision (Articles 10 and 11) and on the fact that the POEN record for a verification is entered only once the verified person makes a first contribution confirmed by the Foundation or a Kolo keeper (Article 7).

## VI — Initial Mechanism

### Article 14

*Founders*

Founders are the initial Kolo keepers — the persons forming the founding core of the Foundation: persons entered in the register of the Serbian Business Registers Agency as the founder or as members of the Foundation's bodies, and persons designated by a decision of the Management Board at the establishment of the system, with their identity publicly disclosed on the platform.

Founders are confirmed from the establishment of the account, and that confirmation status does not derive from the chain of confirmations. The reality of persons from the SBRA register derives from the public record; the reality of persons designated by a decision of the Management Board is confirmed directly by the Management Board, with their identity publicly disclosed.

Founders cannot be verified in the chain of confirmations.

### Article 15

*Rights of founders*

With regard to verification, founders have the rights of Kolo keepers: their verifications are not subject to supervision, not even in the case of an introduction into the Kolo after a telephone conversation with a user from the call list (Article 9 paragraph 2), and the daily pace of confirming under Article 8 applies to them as well.

## VII — Consequences of Loss of Status on Verifications

### Article 16

*Loss of verifier status*

The termination of the status of a user who was the verifier of other users (withdrawal, exclusion, death) does not affect the verifications they performed. The users whom they verified retain their verification, status and the POEN records arising from those verifications.

A verification is annulled solely under the procedure in Chapter VIII of this Rulebook. The termination of a verifier's status is not a ground for annulling a verification.

A verification record whose verifier has ceased to be a user is processed in accordance with Article 34 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, but remains valid and continues to form part of the verification graph.

A noted contribution under Article 7 of this Rulebook that has not been entered by the time the verifier's status terminates is entered, once the conditions are met, only to the verified person. No POEN record is entered for the person whose status has terminated.

### Article 17

*Termination of verification and confirmation status*

A verification terminates solely by annulment under the procedure in Chapter VIII of this Rulebook.

A user whose verification has terminated becomes a user. They lose access to the functions that belong to a participant, but retain their account and may be verified again through the chain of confirmations. For a Kolo keeper whose verification has terminated, ZRNO remains entered and frozen (Article 4 paragraph 4).

When only the confirmation of a member without confirmation is annulled (Article 5a paragraph 4), confirmation status terminates, and the user remains a member without confirmation for as long as their introduction into the Kolo remains valid. If they were a Kolo keeper, their ZRNO remains entered, and active ZRNO passes into the free state; they may activate it again once they are confirmed (Article 30 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

This Article does not concern a founder, because a founder's confirmation status does not derive from verification (Article 14).

## VIII — False Verification and Restitution

### Article 18

*Establishing a false verification*

A false verification is a verification by which the verifier confirmed the reality of a person who does not exist as a natural person or whose continuity is not ensured, as well as a verification of a person whom the verifier knew to have another account in the system. A verification of a person who is subsequently established to have another account (Article 4b), where the verifier did not know this, is not a false verification; the ceasing account falls under Article 4b.

A false verification is established by the Management Board of the Foundation in Phase 1, or by the Upper Kolo in Phase 2.

Proceedings are initiated on a supervision case under Article 11a, on a report by the verified person under Article 5 paragraph 5, or on knowledge obtained in another way. Establishment is made separately for each verification.

The procedure under this Chapter applies to every verification, regardless of whether it is a confirmation or an introduction into the Kolo, as well as to the confirmation of a member without confirmation (Article 5a) and to the confirmation of the existence of a child (Article 6 of the Rulebook on the Participation of Children).

### Article 19

*Consequences of an established false verification*

Upon the establishment of a false verification, that verification is annulled, and it terminates for the verified person (Article 17). Upon the establishment of a false confirmation of a member without confirmation, that confirmation is annulled (Article 5a paragraph 4).

The establishment of one false verification triggers a review of the verifier's other verifications, but does not in itself annul them. Each of them is annulled solely if it is itself established as false under Article 18.

The verification of a user who exists as a natural person, who is unique and whose continuity is ensured remains in force even where the same verifier performed a false verification in another case. Truthfulness is assessed by reference to the user to whom the verification relates, not by reference to the verifier.

Annulling all of one verifier's verifications would strip real people of their status because of an act they did not commit and could not influence. A measure that strikes the innocent is not protection of the network but damage to it.

### Article 20

*Cascade of annulment*

The annulment of a verification is carried further solely through accounts for which it has been established that no real person stands behind them, or that they are not unique.

An account for which this has been established can neither directly know anyone nor be known to anyone as a real person. The establishment therefore annuls all verifications that the account touches — both those it performed and those it received — without separate establishment under Article 18 for each of them.

The cascade stops at the first user for whom it has not been established that they are non-existent or non-unique: for that user, only the verification received from such an account is annulled, while the verifications they themselves performed remain in force.

A network of fabricated accounts is brought down by an establishment being made for each of its accounts. With each establishment all the ties of that account fall, so the order of establishment does not affect the outcome.

The termination of a verification does not in itself trigger a cascade. A user whose verification has terminated has the position under Article 17 of this Rulebook, and the verifications they performed remain in force.

The cascade follows non-existence, not the verifier. A network of fabricated accounts falls in its entirety, because none of them knows anyone; a real person who was honestly introduced does not lose their status because the one who introduced them lied elsewhere.

### Article 20a

*Scope of the annulment of POEN records*

The annulment of a verification annuls solely the POEN records that arose from that verification through the channel in Article 7 of this Rulebook: 1,000 POEN to the verifier and 1,000 POEN to the verified person.

POEN records arising through the other recording channels under Article 15 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System — through exchange, operational contribution, financial contribution, sponsorship, growth of collective forms, founding contribution, or contribution to platform content — are not annulled.

Article 34 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System does not apply to the annulment of POEN records under this Article. What is annulled is what arose from the verification, not the user's entire ledger; a financial contribution is non-returnable under Article 73 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System and cannot be annulled indirectly.

Every annulment of a POEN record is accompanied by a counter-entry of the Protocol in the same amount. The zero-sum invariant under Article 14 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System remains preserved.

### Article 20b

*Restitution*

If the user whose POEN records are being annulled does not hold enough recorded POEN to cover the annulment, their record passes into a negative value for the uncovered part. That negative value is restitution.

The uncovered part of an annulment affecting the verified person is transferred to the verifier from that verification, and their record is reduced by the same amount. The uncovered part of an annulment affecting the verifier themselves remains on their own record.

A negative POEN record may arise solely with a verifier. The record of the verified person may fall to zero at most.

The sum of all restitutions corresponds to the value of goods and services withdrawn from the network on the basis of false verifications. Restitution is therefore not a penalty but a settlement of that value, and is not subject to an upper limit.

Restitution is not a debt. The Foundation holds no claim against the user on the basis of restitution, may not collect, assign or enforce it, and does not present it as an asset. The effect of restitution exists solely within the system.

Restitution does not prevent a user from exchanging goods and services. POEN that reach the user first fill the restitution; the user disposes of their POEN record only once the restitution has been settled. Restitution is worked off by giving to the network.

Restitution does not mean exclusion. Suspension and exclusion are separate measures imposed under a separate procedure established by the Terms of Use; restitution neither replaces, implies nor precludes them.

Restitution persists upon termination of the user's status. By way of exception to Article 34 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, a negative record is not annulled upon termination of status and is not transferred to the Protocol; otherwise, leaving the system would erase the restitution and the burden would fall on the other users.

A negative POEN record under this Article is one of the exceptions to the prohibition in Article 14 paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System (point 1).

### Article 20c

*Position of a user whose verification is annulled through no fault of their own*

For a user whose verification has been annulled, and for whom it has not been established that they are non-existent or non-unique, the verification terminates and the POEN records under Article 7 arising from that verification are annulled.

The annulment of such a user's POEN records is limited by their balance — the record may fall to zero at most. The uncovered part passes to the verifier as restitution under Article 20b and does not burden the user. Whoever is at no fault does not remain in a negative value.

Such a user may be verified again under the general rules of this Rulebook. Upon re-verification they become a participant again and the POEN under Article 7 are recorded for them, so that on this ground they suffer no lasting loss.

The prohibition under Article 12 is determined from the verifications in force. Whatever arose from the annulled verification ceases, so that user may also be verified by the verifier who introduced them into the chain through the annulled verification.

### Article 21

*Status of a false verifier*

A false verifier is subject to measures in accordance with the rules on termination and suspension of status established by the Terms of Use. An established false verification is, as a rule, a ground for excluding the verifier; the Foundation decides on exclusion under the procedure laid down in the Terms of Use, subject to the right to lodge a complaint.

Restitution under Article 20b arises by the annulment itself and is not a measure under paragraph 1 of this Article. The imposition or non-imposition of suspension or exclusion does not affect restitution, nor does restitution replace those measures.

## IX — Transitional and Final Provisions

### Article 22

*Existing verifications*

Verifications recorded before the rule of one confirmation (Article 3) was introduced remain valid. A user for whom more than one verification was recorded up to that point retains all of them; they are in the Kolo as long as at least one of them remains valid, and no new verification is recorded for them.

The provisions of this Rulebook that refer to the verifier of the verified person apply, for such a user, to each of their verifiers.

### Article 22a

*Alignment of existing verifications*

A contribution recorded under verifications performed before the introduction of the condition under Article 7 paragraph 2 of this Rulebook is returned to a noted contribution where the verified person has, up to the implementation of this provision, not achieved any of the forms of contribution under that paragraph.

The alignment is carried out once. Each affected person has written off solely what was recorded to them on the occasion of that verification — 1,000 POEN to the verifier, 1,000 POEN to the verified person and 500 POEN to the supervisor. The write-off is executed by a counter-entry in favour of the Protocol; the sum of all records remains zero, and the total number of recorded POEN decreases.

The write-off is executed even where the record thereby becomes negative, under Article 14 paragraph 3 point 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. The burden is not transferred to any other person; the restitution under Article 20b of this Rulebook does not apply on this ground.

A write-off limited to the balance of the record is not applied because it would produce an outcome contrary to the purpose of the alignment: a user who has already used the recorded contribution would keep it, while a user who has not used it would return it in full.

The alignment does not extinguish the noted contribution. It is entered when the condition under Article 7 paragraph 2 is met, under the same rules that apply to verifications performed after the introduction of that condition.

The alignment is not a measure against the user. It does not presuppose an irregularity and does not initiate the procedure under Chapter VIII of this Rulebook; the verification record and the verification remain unchanged.

Each affected person is notified of the executed write-off, stating the number of POEN and the balance of the record. A complaint against the write-off may be lodged under Article 37a of the Terms of Use.

### Article 22b

*Annulment of the existing contribution for supervision*

A contribution recorded to a supervisor for supervision performed before supervision ceased to be recorded as a contribution (Article 7) is annulled. Only a contribution that is still recorded to the supervisor is annulled; a contribution that has already been annulled earlier on another ground — by the annulment of a false verification, by termination of status or by the conversion of the account into a minor's account — is not annulled again.

The annulment is carried out once, by a counter-entry in favour of the Protocol, under Article 14 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. The sum of all records remains zero, and the total number of recorded POEN decreases. The entry and the counter-entry remain in the history of the ledger.

The annulment cannot make a supervisor's record negative; this ground does not introduce an exception to the prohibition of a negative record under Article 14 paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. If the record of any supervisor would become negative, the annulment is not carried out.

A noted contribution for supervision that has not been entered lapses and is not entered.

The annulment is not a measure against the supervisor and does not presuppose an irregularity. Each affected person is notified of the annulment, stating the number of POEN. A complaint against the annulment may be lodged under Article 37a of the Terms of Use.

### Article 23

*Amendments to the Rulebook*

This Rulebook is adopted and amended by the Management Board (UO) of the KOLO Foundation, under the procedure established in the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

### Article 24

*Entry into force*

This Rulebook enters into force on the date of its adoption by the Management Board (UO) of the KOLO Foundation.
