# Records of Processing Activities of Personal Data

This Register is the record of processing activities under Article 47 of the Law on Personal Data Protection ("Official Gazette of the RS", no. 87/2018; hereinafter: LPDP), which the KOLO Foundation keeps as controller, in accordance with Article 54 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System and Article 9 of the Rulebook on the Hierarchy of Acts of the KOLO System. Terms from the Rulebook on the KOLO System have the same meaning in this Register. The Register is applied together with the Privacy Policy and the Data Protection Impact Assessment.

The right to erasure then does not apply to the extent that processing is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of a legal claim (Article 30 paragraph 3 of the LPDP). A statutory retention obligation exists only for dinar records of donations and sponsorship (activities no. 5 and 6). |

| **Transfer to a third country** | Only under the common provisions. |
| **Retention period** | While the account exists. | **Protection measures** | Pseudonymisation; integrity of the ledger (zero-sum rule with automatic verification, atomic recording, timestamping of records); the name of a support programme and the name of a person are not written into the record description; an aggregate indicator is not shown when it covers fewer than five persons, contains no pseudonym and does not relate to re-registrations between users, and its daily snapshot contains no personal data. |

**Processing activity no. 12 — Integrity monitoring**

| **Purpose of processing** | Detection of patterns indicating abuse (false or multiplied verifications, multiple accounts, funnelling of POEN), in order to preserve the credibility of verifications, the ledger and voting. |
| --- | --- |
| **Categories of data subjects** | Users, through the record of the chain of confirmations and the contribution ledger. |
| **Categories of data** | No new data are collected. Processed are the record of the chain of confirmations (type, supervision, timestamps), the time the account was created, the metadata of the POEN ledger (type, amount, time), the existence of messages, listings and exchanges as yes/no, without content, place, status and confirmed status. Derived record: finding (pseudonym or group of pseudonyms, rule markings, score, status). |
| **Legal basis** | The Foundation's legitimate interest (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 6 of the LPDP): protection of the system from abuse and preservation of the integrity of the ledger and voting. |
| **Recipients / processors** | Infrastructure. Findings are seen only by superadministrators. The alert channel (Telegram Messenger Inc., Resend, Inc.) receives only aggregate numbers. |
| **Transfer to a third country** | Alert channel, only aggregate numbers. |
| **Retention period** | Open finding: until resolved by human review; resolved or dismissed: at most 12 months. Upon termination of status, findings linked to the user are deleted. |
| **Protection measures** | The system does not take automated decisions (Article 38 of the LPDP): it only flags, and a measure is taken by an authorised person; the rules give weight to the absence of actual activity, not to the density of connections; a finding may be dismissed; right to object (Article 37 of the LPDP). The closed group check works on the same data, produces no effect towards the user and only shows the group to the Foundation. |
| **Note** | Proportionality test: the processing introduces no new data, works on pseudonyms, does not decide and is subject to human review and objection, so the interest of the Foundation and of the other users prevails over the interference with the rights of persons. |

An error report is deleted upon termination of status. A correction of a POEN record remains in the ledger (activity no. 4). |
| **Protection measures** | Every decision is taken by a human, with a statement of reasons; the submitter of a listing report is not disclosed; the content of a removed message is not copied into the audit log; reasons from a closed list; actions are recorded in the audit log. |

**Processing activity no. 28 — Wish Box (children's space)**

| **Purpose of processing** | Participation of minor users in shaping the children's space through proposals and support (Rulebook on the Participation of Children, Article 28). |
| --- | --- |
| **Categories of data subjects** | Minor users in the state of child with a parent and Kolo child; their parents (only as recipients of the text of their child's proposal). |
| **Categories of data** | Text of the proposal, support (which proposal the minor user supported), the state of the proposal, the reason for refusal and the Foundation's response. |
| **Legal basis** | As for activity no. 11: for a minor user under the age of 15, parental consent (Article 16 of the LPDP); for a minor user who has reached the age of 15, execution of a contract (Article 12 paragraph 1 point 2 of the LPDP). |
| **Recipients / processors** | Infrastructure. The administrator team reviews the proposal before display, without data on the author. Other children see the text of the proposal without the author; the parent only the text of their own child's proposal. The author is not displayed to anyone. |
| **Transfer to a third country** | Only under the common provisions; notifications do not contain the text of the proposal. |
| **Retention period** | While the minor user's account exists. Upon deletion of the account and upon coming of age, the proposals, with the supports and the response, and the supports the minor user gave are deleted. |
| **Protection measures** | Review before display and refusal without alteration; the author is not displayed even to the administrator team; no comments and no ranking list of children; notifications without the text; Google Analytics is not loaded. The data export covers proposals and supports. |

## Final provisions

The Register is updated upon every change of a processing activity or of the technical and organisational protection measures, and a new activity is entered together with the amendment of the Data Protection Impact Assessment. The Register is available to the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection upon request.

This Register enters into force on the date of its adoption.
