# Rulebook on Founding Contribution

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1 — Subject matter

(1) This Rulebook governs, in accordance with Article 35 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, the concept of a founder and of founding work, the upper limit of founding contribution, the amount and the condition for recording a step, the distribution among founders, the closure of the channel and the public availability of data on the channel.

(2) Terms from the Rulebook on the KOLO System have the same meaning in this Rulebook.

### Article 2 — Founder

(1) A **founder** within the meaning of this Rulebook is a natural person who performed work on the design, development and preparation of the KOLO system before the platform was opened. Where this Rulebook refers to a founder, it means a founder within the meaning of this Article.

(2) A founder within the meaning of this Rulebook is distinct from a founder within the meaning of Article 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System (an initial Kolo keeper, Article 29 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality) and from the founder of the Foundation. The same person may hold more than one of these capacities, but they are not equated: the capacity of a founder within the meaning of this Rulebook gives only a share in the founding contribution and gives neither a position in the chain of confirmations nor powers of governance.

(3) The circle of founders is closed. The founders are established by the decision on distribution referred to in Article 8. No decision adopted after the publication of the decision on distribution may expand the circle of founders.

### Article 3 — Founding work

(1) Founding work encompasses work performed before the platform was opened, in particular: the design of the system architecture, the drafting of rules and protocols, legal preparation, organisational preparation and the preparation of system documentation.

(2) Founding work is not work commissioned by the Foundation, nor work outside an employment relationship, in particular not a contract for work. The founders undertook it on their own initiative, before the platform was opened; the Foundation did not commission it, concluded no contract with the founders regarding it and acquires no exclusive right on the basis of its result.

(3) Founding work is operational contribution performed before the system existed. Article 24 of the Rulebook on Operational Contribution and Articles 16 and 35 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System apply mutatis mutandis to the recording of founding contribution.

(4) The result of founding work (the software, the protocol and the documentation of the system) enters the common good under the licences referred to in Article 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System: software under the AGPL-3.0 licence, content under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. Founders acquire no exclusive right to the result on the basis of that work; the result is available to all persons on the same terms, and the licences cannot be replaced with more restrictive ones.

## II — Upper Limit and Recording Steps

### Article 4 — Upper limit

(1) The upper limit of founding contribution is 2,400,000 POEN. This is the total maximum amount that the Protocol may record through the founding contribution channel.

(2) The limit is a measure of the contribution that preceded the system. The founders spent their own time and their own money on the design, construction, and legal and organisational preparation of the system, in a period in which the system did not exist and in which no recording channel was in operation. It is the work that is recorded, not the money spent.

(3) That contribution cannot be recorded through the operational contribution channel. The daily limit of that channel, common with all active support programmes, amounts to 10% of the total number of POEN recorded in the system (Article 20 of the Rulebook on Operational Contribution); while that number is zero, the limit is likewise zero and the channel cannot record any contribution. The same constraint affects work performed in the initial phase, while the total number of recorded POEN is still negligible.

(4) The upper limit is established in advance and cannot be increased.

### Article 5 — Recording step

(1) Founding contribution is recorded in steps of a fixed amount of 24,000 POEN. Each step records the same amount, without exception.

(2) The total number of steps is 100.

### Article 6 — Condition and moment of recording a step

(1) The Protocol records one step for each threshold of 100,000 POEN that the total number of POEN recorded in the system first reaches or exceeds. The thresholds are fixed: 100,000, 200,000, 300,000 POEN and so on, up to and including the threshold of 10,000,000 POEN. After the step recorded at that threshold, founding contribution is no longer recorded.

(2) The Protocol checks whether a threshold has been reached at the end of each accounting period (Article 26 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). If the total number of POEN has reached several thresholds in that period, the Protocol records, in the same check, one step for each of them.

(3) The recording of a step is an automatic act of the Protocol, without a discretionary decision by any actor.

(4) POEN recorded through founding contribution are included in the total number of POEN recorded in the system and count towards the cumulative growth that activates the next step, from the next check onwards; steps recorded in one check are not counted in the total number against which the reaching of a threshold is established in that same check.

### Article 7 — Share of founding contribution in the total number of POEN

(1) The step is of a fixed amount, so the impact of a single step on the accounting coefficient decreases as the total number of POEN grows.

(2) The aggregate recorded founding contribution does not exceed approximately 24% of the total number of recorded POEN, except when the total number decreases through the annulment of records; in the first steps the share is smaller, and after the closure of the channel it decreases as the total number grows. The Protocol states that share as an aggregate indicator (Article 56 paragraph 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(3) The channel follows the growth of the total number of POEN whatever its pace may be. If the total number grows abruptly, the steps are recorded at the same speed at which the growth occurred; this Rulebook does not promise that recording will be spread over a longer period.

(4) A step is recorded also when the growth of the total number of POEN originates from a single large individual contribution; the share of that contribution in the total number of POEN is thereby smaller than it would be if the channel did not exist. That effect is bounded by the upper limit and ceases when the channel closes.

## III — Distribution and ZRNO of Founders

### Article 8 — Decision on distribution

(1) The founders and the share of each of them in the upper limit are established by the Management Board by a decision on the distribution of founding contribution (hereinafter: the decision on distribution). The sum of all shares equals the upper limit.

(2) Each step is distributed among the founders in proportion to the shares from the decision on distribution and recorded in their records. The amounts per founder are expressed in whole numbers, so that the sum per step is exactly 24,000 POEN, except in the case referred to in paragraph 3.

(3) When a founder's user status terminates before the closure of the channel, their share is from then on not recorded and is not distributed to other founders; the step is reduced by that share.

(4) The decision on distribution is published on the platform, to the extent referred to in Article 11.

### Article 9 — ZRNO of founders

A founder inscribes ZRNO from Phase 2, under the same threshold and the same limit per accounting period as any other participant (Article 22 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). The capacity of an initial Kolo keeper (Article 58 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System) gives no exemption from the conditions for ZRNO inscription.

## IV — Closure of the Channel and Public Availability

### Article 10 — Closure of the channel

(1) The founding contribution channel closes when the Protocol records the 100th step, that is, when the total recorded amount of founding contribution reaches the upper limit of 2,400,000 POEN.

(2) The closure of the channel is permanent and irrevocable. The channel may not be reopened by a decision of the founders within the meaning of this Rulebook, the founder of the Foundation, the Foundation, the Management Board or the Upper Kolo.

### Article 11 — Public availability of data on founding contribution

(1) The following are publicly available: the total recorded amount of founding contribution, the number of recorded steps, the remaining amount up to the upper limit and the percentage of channel utilisation.

(2) The founders' shares from the decision on distribution are available to all users; together with the founder's pseudonym they are available to participants (Article 56 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

## V — Final Provisions

### Article 12 — Entry into force

This Rulebook enters into force on the date of its adoption.
