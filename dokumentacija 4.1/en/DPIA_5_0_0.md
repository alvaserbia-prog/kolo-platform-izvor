## 2. Description of processing

## 2.1. The system and the controller

The KOLO system is a platform for the ledger of contributions to the common good. The Protocol, as the technical means of processing, records contributions in POEN. The controller is the Foundation, because it determines the purposes and means of processing (Article 4 point 8 of the LPDP); the processors from the Register act on the basis of a processing agreement (Article 45 of the LPDP). Personal data are stored on infrastructure provided by the Foundation, with processors with a processing agreement (Article 15 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

## 2.2. Design decisions for data protection

Data protection is based on data protection by design and by default (Article 42 of the LPDP) and on three decisions:

1) pseudonymity of the ledger: records are linked to pseudonyms; the name and surname are kept only in the identity register, separately from the contribution ledger, are entered only in five cases (Article 31 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System) and are not displayed to other users, except in the cases in section 5.16. Pseudonymity is not anonymity: pseudonymised data remain personal data;

2) separation of data: identification data are kept separately from accounting data, and the banking documentation of donations separately from Platform data;

3) minimisation: only data necessary for the functioning of the system are collected; this is a limit of the common good that cannot be abolished by any governance decision (Articles 10 and 15 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

## 2.4. Processors and transfer

The application is executed and the database is located in the European Union (Frankfurt region), which provides an adequate level of protection, so for the data in the database there is no transfer within the meaning of Chapter V of the LPDP; there remains the possibility of administrative access from the United States of America, because Vercel Inc. and Neon Inc. are companies from that country. Transfer to the United States of America actually takes place for the database backup copy (GitHub Inc., Google LLC), images and attachments (Cloudflare, Inc.), email sent by the Platform (Resend, Inc.), alerts to the Foundation (Telegram Messenger Inc.) and audience measurement upon consent (Google). Card payment is processed by the Foundation's commercial bank and its intermediary in the Republic of Serbia.

## 3. Necessity and proportionality

## 3.1. Processing based on contract (no. 1, 2, 4, 14, 16, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23, 25, 26, 27)

Registration (pseudonym, address, password) is the smallest set for the existence of an account: without a pseudonym there is no ledger, without an address there is no recovery of access. Proof of reality (the record of the chain of confirmations, the type of verification, confirmed status) is necessary for the principle of one person, one account; the datum on the type of verification and on who confirmed a member without confirmation is needed in order to apply the restrictions on a member without confirmation and so that a confirmation can be subject to supervision and responsibility like a verification. The contribution ledger is the essence of the Protocol; anonymisation would make the system impossible, so pseudonymisation is the mildest measure that achieves the purpose.

A farm (no. 20) is displayed on the map because the visibility of the supply of local food by place is the purpose for which the farm joins the system; only what the display and the conditions of contribution require is processed, the address is shown on the basis of a statement that is part of the application, and the BPG is not public.

The identity register (no. 21) serves the principle of one person, one account, which the chain of confirmations cannot verify by itself: the verifier does not know how many accounts a person has. The name is therefore entered only where the system obtains it by the nature of things or where it is verifiable: for a public donation (the bank has identified the payer), a cooperative member (the cooperative knows them), a farm (the name is entered by the member themselves, with the declaration of accuracy), sponsorship (the name of the representative is compared with the register of business entities) and the call list (the name is entered by the user themselves). A milder alternative (not keeping the name with the account) does not achieve the purpose, because the same person with two accounts then cannot be recognised.

Vouchers (no. 22) process only what distribution requires; the display of the record of annulment alongside the pseudonym rests on consent given upon taking, and a user who does not want the display does not take a voucher. Recommendations (no. 23) process only the pair of who recommended whom and when, which is the least that makes possible the rule of one recommendation per pair and withdrawal.

Collective procurement (no. 16) processes only the name of the good in the proposal and the data on the order and collection; the number of POEN is read from the ledger, and not collected anew. Apart from verification, no participation criteria are set, so that data on the activity and circumstances of users are not collected.

Voting in the Upper Kolo (no. 25) requires that the voter's voting power in that period is known alongside the vote and that one person is not allowed two votes; the individual vote is therefore kept, but not published, and only the totals and the outcome are public. Operational contribution (no. 26) requires a plan and proof of execution, because without them the confirming person cannot establish that the work has been done; the proof relates to the work done, not to the person. Complaints and reports (no. 27) require data on the subject matter and on the person to whom they relate, because without them the matter cannot be decided; the submitter of a listing report is not disclosed to the advertiser.

Conclusion: the processing operations are necessary for the purpose and proportionate with the measures in section 5.

## 3.2. Processing based on consent (no. 3, 9, 13, 19, 24)

Voluntarily entered data are optional and do not condition any function. Publishing a listing is consent given with a warning that the listing is public and is withdrawn by removing the listing. The name alongside a public donation is published only at the donor's choice, separately for each donation, and may be withdrawn at any time; an anonymous donation is a real alternative, and POEN is not recorded for it because an entry that cannot be attributed to any person cannot be verified, and not because publication is a condition. The data of cooperative members are delivered only with consent and are reduced to the name, pseudonym and name of the cooperative; without the name, the number of cooperative members cannot be reliably established, because a pseudonym does not prove membership in the cooperative.

The call list (no. 24) processes the telephone number only with the explicit consent of the user, who leaves it themselves. A milder form than the one the purpose requires does not exist: a person who does not know anyone in the network can be introduced only if someone contacts them, and a telephone conversation is the smallest step by which this is achieved. The caller needs the name to verify that they are talking with the person who came onto the list, and not with someone impersonating them. The name and number are not displayed with the list, but only upon opening, with one Kolo member at a time.

## 3.3. Processing based on a statutory obligation (no. 5, 18)

Dinar records of donations and sponsorship are kept for 10 years under the Law on Accounting and tax regulations; the identity of the payer is ensured by the bank, so a milder means does not exist. The consent record fulfils the burden of proof under Article 15 paragraph 1 of the LPDP, without any additional identifier.

## 3.4. Processing based on legitimate interest (no. 6, 7, 12 and parts of no. 4, 11, 21, 24, 25, 26, 27)

The data on the sponsor's representative are limited to what a valid agreement requires; only the name is published on the ranking list, because the registered address of an entrepreneur would often be their home address. Technical data and logs are necessary for security and are kept for 12 months.

Integrity monitoring (no. 12) does not collect new data, works on pseudonyms, does not decide and is subject to human review and objection; the interest of the Foundation and of honest users in a credible ledger and voting prevails over the minimal interference.

Retention of the numerical history after termination of status (no. 4) serves the defence of legal claims and the integrity of the ledger, because each record is at the same time part of the other party's record and of the Protocol's counter-entry; deletion would change someone else's history. Only numbers are retained, alongside a pseudonym that does not point to the name, and free text is deleted. A statutory obligation exists only for dinar records of donations and sponsorship.

The other parts (no. 11, 21, 24, 25, 26, 27) are assessed in sections 5.10, 5.16, 5.19, 5.20, 5.21 and 5.22, and the Wish Box (no. 28) in section 5.23.

## 3.5. Automated decision-making (no. 8)

The recording of POEN, the accounting coefficient and distribution within the daily limit are automated processing operations that may significantly affect a person within the meaning of Article 38 of the LPDP. A person's status is never changed automatically: there is no automatic confirmation of the chain, and if the confirmation of the existence of a child does not arrive, nothing is taken away from the parent. The formula is public and deterministic; the person has the right to an explanation of the logic, human review and objection. Conclusion: the processing is necessary, with the safeguards in section 5.8.

## 5. Risk mitigation measures

## 5.1. Technical measures

Pseudonymisation of the ledger; the name and surname only in the identity register (section 5.16); the unique personal identification number is not processed. Separation of identification data from accounting data. TLS encryption of at least version 1.2. Encryption at rest at the infrastructure level. Integrity of the ledger: zero-sum rule with automatic verification, atomic recording and timestamping; deviations are visible and are checked. Password hashing. Audit log of administrative actions. Aggregate indicators of the state of the system and of concentration are not shown when they cover fewer than five persons, and the daily snapshot of the indicators contains no personal data.

## 5.2. Organisational measures

Access control based on the principle of minimum necessity (Article 50 of the LPDP). Multi-factor authentication for access to the infrastructure. Confidentiality obligation for all persons with access to data, and for the Kolo member who opens a call and for the cooperative administrator also a statement of confidentiality. Training of persons with access. Security checks and penetration testing. Procedure for a data breach, with notification of the Commissioner within 72 hours (Article 52 of the LPDP). Annual review of retention periods.

## 5.3. Backup copy and recovery

A copy of the entire database is made every hour and kept with GitHub Inc. for 14 days and on the Foundation's Google Drive account for 30 days. It covers the ledger and identification data; only persons with administrative access to the infrastructure have access. Encryption of the copy and regular recovery testing are introduced before the full launch of the system.

## 5.4. Measures for cross-border transfer

Processing in the European Union: the application is executed and the database is located in the Frankfurt region, so the Protocol's ledger, data from applications for support programmes, messages and the data of minor users remain in the area with an adequate level of protection. Narrow scope of what leaves: the backup copy, images and attachments, email, alerts and audience data. Alert channel reduced to the minimum: pseudonym and type of event, without data from an application for a programme and without the content of messages. Agreement as a condition of transfer: only to a processor with standard contractual clauses or other measures under Article 65 of the LPDP. The Foundation keeps a copy of each agreement and at least once a year checks whether it is in force and whether the list of sub-processors has changed; collection of the copies is in progress. Google Analytics is not loaded before consent, and cookieless measurement does not track the visitor across sites.

## 5.5. Measures for integrity monitoring

No new data: monitoring works on data that are already being processed, in pseudonymous form. No automated decision-making: the system only flags accounts or groups for review; a measure is taken by an authorised person. Weight on the absence of activity, not on the density of connections, so that a close-knit real community is not treated as abuse. A finding may be dismissed, after which the account is not flagged again for a certain time. Right to object (Article 37 of the LPDP). Findings are available only to superadministrators; the alert channel carries only aggregate numbers. Resolved findings are deleted at the latest after 12 months. The closed group check produces no effect towards the user; the Foundation may invite the members of the group to a conversation, and the conversation is not a measure.

## 5.6. Measures for the supervision case

A case arises from the supervisor's assessment of a record that is already being processed. Closed code list: free text only with the reason "other", which prevents the entry of arbitrary observations about a person. No conflict of interest: supervision is not carried out by anyone who took part in the verification, nor by the same supervisor twice over the same record. No decision-making: the case is a record; a false verification is established by the Foundation by decision, so there is no automated decision-making. The case is seen only by the superadministrator; the verifier and the verified person do not see it. An unfounded suspicion is deleted 90 days after the closure of the case, without a request by the person. Objection under Article 37 of the LPDP and complaint against a decision under the Terms of Use.

## 5.7. Measures for listings

Publication requires a content minimum about the good or service, not about the person; a photograph of the person and the name are not required. The telephone number is available only to participants. A visible marking that the advertiser is not in the Kolo protects the other party. Removal of the listing at any time also withdraws consent. A listing of a minor user is seen only by those to whom it is available under the Rulebook on the Participation of Children.

## 5.8. Measures for automated decision-making

A public deterministic formula that every user can verify; right to an explanation of the logic; right to have the decision reviewed by an authorised person; objection (Article 38 of the LPDP). A person's status is not changed automatically.

## 5.9. Measures for support programmes

Explicit consent: the application and the request for confirmation are initiated only with explicit consent, given before confirmation is requested from anyone. The consent states how many persons will be asked; that those persons will learn which programme is concerned and that this may disclose to them membership of a special category; that the recording is displayed to participants alongside the pseudonym, the name of the programme and the amount, and to persons who are not signed in and users who are not in the Kolo only as a daily total per programme; what can be derived from the amount for Elderly Support and Mothers Support; and that the ground is not displayed to any user. The text of the consent is stored, because the number of persons asked differs for each applicant.

Minimisation towards those who confirm: they do not see the content of the application; they confirm on the basis of personal knowledge. Narrow circle: the request is received only by a Kolo member or founder who personally knows the applicant; a member without confirmation and a Kolo member who introduced the applicant into the Kolo, or confirmed them, after a telephone conversation do not confirm. Special categories of data are seen only by the superadministrator, who also decides on the application. No external channels: notification of the request only within the Platform; email and device messages do not carry the name of the programme or the pseudonym. Withdrawal of consent next to the programme itself, without reasons, with termination of the procedure and deletion of the entered data. Deletion of the entered data also upon refusal, suspension and termination of status; a new application requires new entry and new confirmation. Closure of the procedure: upon conclusion, requests and notifications are removed from the accounts of those from whom confirmation was requested.

Minimisation of special categories of data: from the disability decision only its existence and date are taken; the degree, the assessment of working capacity, the diagnosis and medical documentation are not collected, because that would be a disproportionate interference with a special category of data for a purpose for which the existence of the decision suffices. For illness, loss of home and studying, the proof is a statement, not a document.

Graded display and omission of the ground: the individual record is displayed to participants alongside the pseudonym, the name of the programme and the amount; to persons who are not signed in and users who are not in the Kolo only the daily total, and a day with a single support recipient in a programme is not published, because the total would equal the individual amount. The load-bearing measure is the omission of the ground: the ground is not displayed to any user, so the name Special Support, which covers both reduced capacity and loss of home, does not tell which ground is concerned: display of the ground would be display of data concerning health, or concerning forced displacement, which is close to data concerning national origin. For Elderly Support and Mothers Support the amount is derived from the data on the basis of which the entitlement was established and reveals them (the year of birth, or the number and age of children); the amount is nevertheless displayed, because the verifiability of the ledger towards the community is the prevailing interest, and the applicant explicitly consents to it. For Special Support and Student Support the amount is fixed and reveals nothing. Verifiability remains complete, because the total of the daily amounts with the other channels gives the change in the total number of POEN. The record description does not name the programme: the name is derived from the application, not from the record description. The breakdown by ground is seen only by the support recipient and the superadministrator.

Parental consent for data on a child: for Mothers Support, only the date of birth of a child who is not a user is processed, without a name, with parental consent (Article 16 of the LPDP) given by the applicant; it is deleted when the application ceases.

Accepted consequence: membership of a programme remains known to those from whom confirmation was requested even after the application ceases. This cannot be removed without abolishing confirmation, which protects the programme from untruthful applications. The mitigation lies in the fact that consent is given after that consequence has been explicitly communicated, with the only alternative: that the user does not apply.

## 5.10. Measures for minor users

Age is not verified: verification would require inspection of a document or the date of birth of every adult user, which is disproportionate to the purpose and contrary to the principle of minimisation (Article 5 of the LPDP). A minor has no reason to make an untruthful statement about their age, because an adult account gives nothing that would attract them, and the statement that they are a child leads to friendships, the children's space and the recording of contribution. The residual risk is R25.

Minimisation before consent: until the take-over of the account, the pseudonym, access data, the parent's address and friendships formed in that period are processed; the date of birth is entered by the parent and does not change after entry. Closed functions: the Chat Room, school, listings, communication with adults and recording of POEN are not available until the account has been taken over, because behind a waiting account there is no adult who is responsible for the child's actions, and the parent does not read conversations between children; a friendship is formed only by scanning a code in person. The minor user's own exit: an account waiting for take-over can be deleted by the child alone, without the parent and without contacting the Foundation. Period as a measure: an account that nobody takes over within 14 days is deleted without a request by the person. No identifying data in a message to a third party: the message to the parent contains only the pseudonym, because the accuracy of the address has not been verified. Two exits without signing in: the recipient of the message may declare that they are not the parent and may delete the account.

Proportionality assessment for processing based on legitimate interest: the processing of the parent's address and of the child's data until the take-over of the account serves to obtain the consent required by law: without contact with the parent, consent cannot even be requested. There is no milder means: the parent's address is the only datum the child has, and without a record of the account the invitation could not be linked to anyone. The child's rights prevail wherever they can: the scope is reduced to what is necessary, the period to 14 days, functions that would expose the child to adults are closed, the message contains only the pseudonym, the recipient has two exits, and the child its own. The processing is initiated only by the child, by entering their parent's address, and the only outcome is a message to that parent. The address is not entered as the address of the child's account and does not open a way to sign in. Accepted consequence: the account can be deleted with a single-use authorisation from the message, so it can also be deleted by someone who is not the parent; the alternative is that the account continues to use the address of a person who has nothing to do with the child, and the only one the system can then ask is the one who holds the message.

Three states of the child and indirect verification of parenthood: full access and recording of contribution (Kolo child) only when the parent is a Kolo member or founder and when the existence of the child is confirmed by a Kolo member or founder who personally knows the parent; at that point both the parent and the existence of the child have been confirmed by third parties in the real world. Until then the child does not publish listings, does not re-register POEN, does not write to those who are not their friends and is not included in the overview by school. The confirmation of the existence of a child brings no POEN, and responsibility for a false confirmation is the same as for a false verification; the request is displayed to Kolo members only with the parent's pseudonym, without data on the child: that the pseudonym belongs to a parent is already visible to adults from the notice that stands in place of the child's profile. If the confirmation does not arrive, nothing is taken away from the parent.

Restricted parental insight: the parent does not read conversations between children, because the insight would also touch the other child, whose parent has not given it; they see friendships and the list of conversations without content. The parent reads a conversation with an adult, and the adult interlocutor is shown this. The parent of a Kolo child receives a notification when the child becomes friends with a child who is not a Kolo child, without content. Filtering of the children's Chat Room by friendships, without replies with a quotation, so that the filter cannot be circumvented. Non-publicity of the graph: friendships do not enter public displays or aggregates. Closed profile: instead of the child's profile, adults are shown a notice with the parent's pseudonym. A record in which a minor user is a party is not displayed to any observer.

School: only numbers and shares by school are published, without personal data. The overview of the children of one school covers only Kolo children and is available only to a Kolo child of the same school: the state "Kolo child" is required because otherwise an adult could present themselves as a child and obtain the overview with pseudonyms and amounts, and the restriction to one's own school because the overview serves a community in which the children know each other anyway. The profile cannot be accessed from the overview. Only the current school is kept, with at most one change in 30 days.

The child's own address: it is entered only upon confirmation by opening a link, so a typing error does not hand the account over to a third party; notifications are not sent to it; when there is no address, a new password is set by the parent, with a notification to the child.

Report of false parenthood: the child is notified of every take-over of the account, with the pseudonym of the person who took it over; the child, the other parent or any user may report that a person is not the parent; the link is immediately temporarily suspended, so until the decision the person has no insight or powers of a parent; the decision is taken by an authorised person of the Foundation, and the person to whom the report relates is not told who submitted it; if the child is left without a parent, the account passes into the state of waiting child and the 14-day period runs anew. The decision is taken by a human.

Recording of contribution for friendships is an application of published rules and does not affect status, so there is no automated decision-making.

## 5.11. Measures for collective procurement

A proposal contains only the name of the good. The register of proposals is published without pseudonyms. Apart from verification, no participation criteria are set. The number of POEN for the order is read from the ledger. The supplier receives only the list of codes. With the list of those who collected, the pseudonym and the place in the order are published, without the number of POEN. The order is an application of published rules, with a complaint decided by a human. A proposal is deleted upon selection of another name, upon removal, upon carrying out the procurement and at the latest after 12 months.

## 5.12. Measures for termination of status and the right to erasure

An accurately named procedure: upon termination of status the account is pseudonymised, not anonymised; the retained records remain personal data, and the restriction of erasure rests on Article 30 paragraph 3 of the LPDP (legal claim, and for dinar records of donations and sponsorship also the statutory retention obligation). Deleted are the email address, voluntarily entered data, the name and surname from the identity register, the content and photographs of listings (listings are removed from the advertising space, photographs from storage), the plan, description and proof of execution of operational contribution and free text alongside re-registrations, complaints and reports. Messages in a conversation are deleted when at least one party closes their account. What remains are numerical records alongside a pseudonym that does not point to the name and the ground of entry; connections in the record of the chain of confirmations are pseudonymised, and the verifications the person gave remain. The name alongside a public donation is removed from the list and deleted from the donation record. Correction of an inaccurate datum is requested in the Platform, by a complaint. At least once a year the Foundation checks whether the retention period has expired for any category. Upon a report of death (Terms of Use), the data of the person who reported the death and the proof of death, when requested, are processed only for the purpose of that procedure and are deleted upon its conclusion.

## 5.13. Measures for proof of consent

For every processing operation based on consent and for the acceptance of the Terms of Use, the moment, the version of the act and the displayed text are recorded, in the language of display. An account is not opened without a record, and the verification is on the server; the record is created in the same atomic operation as the account. Acceptance of the Terms and of the Privacy Policy is recorded as two statements (Article 15 paragraph 2 of the LPDP). The published version of an act does not change, so the record permanently points to the text that was in force. Parental consent to the processing of a child's data is recorded separately from the request for confirmation of the existence of the child, so that withdrawal of consent does not defeat the assertion that the child exists. No IP address, device data or other identifiers are recorded alongside the record; for a visitor who is not signed in, the decision on cookies is recorded only in the cookie, together with the version of the notice, and a decision under an earlier version is not carried over to a new one. Consent is withdrawn as easily as it was given; the record is not deleted in that case, but receives the moment of withdrawal. Confirmation of the address by a link establishes that the address belongs to the person who opened the account, and is not a condition for using the account.

## 5.14. Measures for cooperatives

Of the cooperative member, only the name and surname, pseudonym, name of the cooperative and the moments of linking and termination are processed; the cooperative administrator additionally sees the telephone number that the cooperative member entered on the profile. Documents, address and registration number are not requested. The datum is delivered only for persons who have consented; upon withdrawal the capacity of cooperative member terminates, without consequences for the POEN in the record. The name is not displayed to other users; the cooperative's records are displayed under the name of the cooperative, and cooperative members under a pseudonym. The cooperative administrator has a separate role linked to one cooperative, works only in its record and with its cooperative members, does not see the rest of the panel or special categories of data and signs a statement of confidentiality before authorisation; their actions are recorded in the audit log. Minor accounts are not covered. The cooperative does not confirm reality, so the record of the chain of confirmations does not change. When the cooperative assists in a dispute between users, the data are given to it by the parties themselves and the cooperative is the controller for them; the Foundation does not deliver data on users to it, so no new processing activity arises.

## 5.15. Measures for farms and the map of local producers

The name, settlement, location, description of production, the applicant's role and the pseudonyms of members are processed; the BPG is stated when the farm is entered in the register and is not checked; an extract from the Register of Agricultural Holdings is not attached. The telephone number is taken only from the holder, for the conversation before approval, is seen only by the administrator team and is deleted by the decision on the application; the Foundation does not talk with the other members on that basis. The name and surname of members are kept in the identity register, not with the farm. Adult accounts only. The Foundation reviews the application and every joining before display. The address is shown alongside the point on the map on the basis of the applicant's statement, which is part of the application, and the display ceases upon deletion of the farm; upon termination of the farm the point is removed, and the name, description, location and BPG are deleted. The address search goes from the applicant's browser. Aggregate assessment: a holder who is a user receives an offer for the call list, so the Kolo member who opens the call sees their name, place and number, and the map publicly shows the farm with the pseudonyms of members; the list carries neither the address nor a link to the farm, and combining is possible only with that one Kolo member, with recording and on the basis of a pseudonym that is in any case public on the Pijaca, so the aggregate risk does not exceed the assessment of R20.

## 5.16. Measures for the identity register

A closed circle of cases of entry (Article 31 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System); an extension requires an amendment of the Rulebook and of this assessment. Only the name and surname, pseudonym, case and moment of entry are processed; the comparison with the register of business entities is recorded only as a fact. The name is not written into the contribution ledger, the description of a POEN record, notifications or the audit log. The name does not appear in displays to other users, with two exceptions: the Kolo member who calls a user from the call list, to whom the name is opened by a click while the call is open and with recording (section 5.19), and the cooperative administrator, for the cooperative members of their cooperative (section 5.14); this is verified by an automated test of the source code. The entry does not confirm reality. One name per account; correction only upon a complaint. Access for the administrator team, with multi-factor authentication for the infrastructure. No register is kept for minors. Existing names are not transferred retroactively. It is deleted upon termination of status.

## 5.17. Measures for vouchers

Consent before taking, with an explicit warning that the display may disclose data about the holder, including about health; without consent a voucher is not taken, and taking is voluntary. The display is alongside the pseudonym, never alongside the name. The record is seen only by those to whom the ledger is available. Vouchers are confirmed only by persons designated by the giver. A voucher is personal and non-transferable. Upon withdrawal of consent, the description of the good or service and the name of the giver are deleted from the description of the record of annulment, and the record remains. The photograph of the price list is seen only by the administrator team.

## 5.18. Measures for recommendations

Only the number is displayed on the profile; who recommended whom is seen only by the person who gave the recommendation. A recommendation is not given in negative form, so the system keeps no unfavourable judgement about anyone. It produces no POEN record and does not affect status, access, verification or listings. One per pair, with withdrawal. A minor neither gives nor receives it; a member without confirmation does not give it.

## 5.19. Measures for the call list, multiple accounts and the adults' space

Name and number hidden until opening: they are opened by a click, only to the Kolo member who opened the call and while the call is open; a call is open with one Kolo member, and one Kolo member holds one open call. Recording of who opened: the record contains only pseudonyms and time, is seen only by the administrator team, and is kept while the account of the person called exists, because it serves to establish misuse of their data while they take part in the system; it is not exported through the Platform, and the user obtains it on request. The Kolo member who calls a user and introduces them into the Kolo is at the same time their verifier, so is known to the user anyway. Narrower circle of recipients: the list is seen only by Kolo members, whose reality has been confirmed and who are responsible for misuse under the Terms of Use; a request for the purpose of confirmation is seen only by Kolo keepers. Prior notice and consent: the user knows that a Kolo member will call them and that this member will see the name and number; they leave the number themselves and withdraw consent without consequences for the donation, sponsorship, farm and contribution under them. Statement of confidentiality: before the first opening the Kolo member declares once that they will use the data only for that conversation and will not record or forward them; without the statement nothing is opened. The Kolo member processes the data under the Foundation's authorisation, not as an independent recipient. No transfer: the name and number do not enter notifications, email or the audit log. Short period: the user is removed from the list upon verification or confirmation, upon withdrawal and upon termination of status.

Multiple accounts: the name, place and number are compared with the data linked to other accounts; the comparison blocks nothing; the caller sees only the match marking and the place of the other account, never its name or pseudonym; both accounts are seen only by the administrator team. A multiple account is established by the Foundation, after contacting the person, with a statement of reasons and a complaint. A supervision case over the recipients of re-registrations is opened only upon establishment, with the sole question of whether the recipient is the same person or the re-registrations are fictitious, has no effect on their accounts, and the recipient is not notified until a measure is imposed on them; this is not monitoring of re-registrations.

Adults' space: the children's regime remains available by a statement of age and offers the child more than an adult's account; a conversation of an adult with a minor still requires parental consent; the Foundation removes content in the common space when it learns that it breaches the rules, and it learns of it through the Foundation's email address and complaints. Risk R25 has been accepted by decision of the controller as a consequence of opening exchange to users.

## 5.21. Measures for operational contribution

The contributor and the confirming person are displayed only under a pseudonym, and applications, plans and objections only to participants. Proof is requested of the work done, not of the person; next to the proof field the contributor is warned that the photograph should not show faces or data of third parties (the measure is introduced together with the proof screen). Upon termination of status, the plan, description and proof are deleted, and the stored statement and numbers remain under a pseudonym.

## 5.22. Measures for complaints, reports and moderation

Every complaint and report is decided by a human, with a statement of reasons. The advertiser is not told who reported the listing; in an exchange report, the other party to the re-registration is invited to respond before the decision, because they are a party to the re-registration anyway. The reasons for a report are chosen from a closed list. The content of a removed message is not copied into the audit log. A complaint under the Terms of Use is permitted against every decision. Upon termination of status, free text is deleted.

## 5.23. Measures for the Wish Box

A proposal is reviewed before display and refused without alteration when it contains personal data or inappropriate content. The author is not displayed to anyone, not even to the administrator team; there are no comments and no ranking list of children, and the number of supports relates to the proposal, not to the child. Notifications do not carry the text of the proposal, and Google Analytics is not loaded. The parent sees only the text of their own child's proposal. Upon deletion of the account and upon coming of age, everything the child submitted and supported in the Wish Box is deleted.

## 5.24. Residual risk

After the application of the measures, the residual risk is acceptable. The highest are the risks at the medium level: R1, R2, R11, R13, R16, R20, R21, R22, R24 and R25; R11 is at the upper limit of that level. The risks are further mitigated by monitoring, testing and updating of the measures.
