# Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement

## I — General provisions

### Article 1 — Subject matter

(1) This Rulebook governs, in accordance with Article 47 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, the funds available for projects, collective procurement (the proposal and selection of the object of procurement, the parameters, offers and calculation, the application, the order, collection, and the annulment of POEN records upon use), and the allocation of vouchers from sponsorship in goods and services.

(2) Terms from the Rulebook on the KOLO System have the same meaning in this Rulebook. Voucher and giver have the meaning established by the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations.

### Article 2 — Definitions

(1) The following terms are also used in this Rulebook:

1) **project**: the dedicated spending of the Foundation's dinar funds for the benefit of the community, outside the Foundation's operational costs; the allocation of vouchers from sponsorship in goods and services, by which no dinar funds of the Foundation are spent, is also a project;

2) **collective procurement**: a project by which the Foundation acquires goods from a third party using dinar funds, and participants collect those goods in equal shares with the annulment of POEN records upon use;

3) **object of procurement**: a good that users cannot obtain within the system, selected under Article 12;

4) **share**: an equal quantity of the good belonging to one user within a single procurement;

5) **supplier**: a third party from which the Foundation acquires goods; the supplier is not a party to the relationship between the Foundation and users.

(2) A cooperative is neither a supplier nor a bidder in a collective procurement.

(3) A period set in days expires upon the expiry of the last day of the period, according to the time in Serbia.

### Article 3 — Nature of collective procurement

(1) Collective procurement pursues the purposes of the Foundation under Article 6 of the Statute, in particular social protection and solidarity support and support for the voluntary association of citizens for mutual aid and self-organisation. Collective procurement is a way of pursuing the purposes under Article 7 point c) of the Statute (organising collective procurement of goods and services for the benefit of programme users) and is carried out as a programme within the meaning of Article 9 paragraph 3 of the Statute. The goods are distributed to programme users within the meaning of Article 9 paragraph 4 of the Statute.

(2) Collective procurement is not an economic activity. The Foundation derives no revenue and no profit from the procurement, does not offer the goods on the market to an indeterminate circle of persons, and acquires no value whatsoever on the basis of ceding them. The funds used to acquire the goods originate from donations and sponsorship contributions.

(3) The Foundation may not receive consideration for a ceded good, whether in money or in any other form, either from users or from a third party.

### Article 4 — Participants

(1) Collective procurement is participated in by participants (Kolo members and members without confirmation) with an active account.

(2) The same circle of persons submits procurement proposals and takes vouchers (Article 30).

(3) Minor users do not submit procurement proposals, do not apply for a procurement, do not collect goods and do not take vouchers.

## II — Funds

### Article 5 — Funds available for projects

(1) The funds available for projects are established as the difference between the balance of the Foundation's dinar funds and the operating reserve.

(2) The balance of the Foundation's dinar funds consists of donations and sponsorship contributions received, reduced by the recorded operational costs and by funds already spent on projects. Earmarked funds from public sources and from calls for proposals do not form part of the balance and are spent for the purpose for which they were given (Article 36 paragraph 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(3) The operating reserve amounts to three operational costs of the Foundation for the preceding month and is not spent on projects.

(4) If the difference under paragraph 1 is equal to or less than zero, no procurement is carried out.

### Article 6 — Relationship to operational costs

(1) Funds spent on projects do not form part of the Foundation's operational costs and are not taken into account in establishing the operating reserve (Article 5 paragraph 3).

(2) Records of project funds are kept separately from records of operational costs.

### Article 7 — Upper spending limit

(1) For a single procurement, no more dinar funds may be spent than the funds available for projects multiplied by the spending coefficient.

(2) The spending coefficient is 1.00. A change to the spending coefficient constitutes an amendment to this Rulebook.

(3) The upper limit does not determine the quantity of the good to be acquired (Article 13).

### Article 8 — Other projects

A project that is not a collective procurement, an allocation of vouchers or support to a cooperative under Article 20 of the Rulebook on Cooperatives is carried out only once it has been governed by an amendment to this Rulebook; until then, dinar funds are not allocated to that form of project.

## III — Proposal and selection of the object of procurement

### Article 9 — Procurement proposal

(1) A procurement proposal contains solely the name of a good. The proposal does not contain quantity, price, reasoning or any other data.

(2) The name of the good is chosen from a dictionary of names maintained by the system. If no suitable name exists in the dictionary, the user enters a new name and thereby adds it to the dictionary.

(3) Each user may have one active procurement proposal. A new proposal replaces the previous one.

(4) Submitting a proposal is voluntary and does not affect the user's status or the right to apply for a procurement.

### Article 10 — Register of proposals

(1) The Protocol maintains a register of procurement proposals. The register displays, in aggregate form and without user pseudonyms, the names of goods and the number of distinct users who proposed them.

(2) The number of users who proposed the same name is the sole measure of standing in the register. The number of POEN of a proposer does not affect the position of a name in the register.

(3) Upon completion of a procurement, the proposals for the selected name of the good are deleted; proposals for other names remain in the register. A proposal is also deleted upon the expiry of 12 months from its submission, and upon termination of the user's status.

### Article 11 — Goods that are not procured

(1) Collective procurement acquires only a good that users cannot obtain within the system (Article 47 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). A good offered by a user on the Pijaca is not procured.

(2) Before initiating a procurement, the Foundation verifies whether the selected good is offered by a user. If it is, no procurement is carried out and the need is directed to that listing.

### Article 12 — Selection of the object of procurement

(1) The object of procurement is selected by the Upper Kolo through a selective vote under the Rulebook on the Upper Kolo, from the list of names in the register of proposals. Until the Upper Kolo is constituted, the object is selected by the Management Board, from the same list and by the same criteria (Article 47 paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(2) If two or more names have an equal sum of voting power, the name proposed by the greater number of distinct users is selected; if that number is also equal, the name entered into the register earlier is selected.

(3) Article 47 paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System applies to the decision on the object and parameters of a procurement. The type of decision is derived from the subject matter of the decision and does not depend on the proposer's choice.

## IV — Parameters, offers and calculation

### Article 13 — Parameters of the procurement

(1) The decision initiating the procurement establishes the total quantity of the good to be acquired, the size of a single share and the number of POEN annulled per share.

(2) The decision states the need of the community that the procurement meets and the purpose of the Foundation under Article 3 paragraph 1 thereby pursued. The parameters under paragraph 1 are established according to that need.

(3) The parameters under paragraph 1 are whole numbers greater than zero. The number of shares equals the quotient of the total quantity and the size of a share; the total quantity must be divisible by the size of a share without remainder.

(4) The parameters under paragraph 1 are established before offers are collected and are not derived from the dinar price of the good, which is not known at that time. They are published with the calculation, together with a statement of reasons for how they were established, and are not modified after publication.

### Article 14 — Collection of offers

(1) The Foundation collects at least three offers for the selected good and chooses the most favourable one.

(2) An offer is accepted exclusively from a registered legal entity or entrepreneur.

(3) All collected offers, with amounts and the name of the bidder, including offers that were not selected, are published as part of the dinar side of the procurement (Article 37 paragraph 2).

### Article 15 — Conflict of interest

(1) A person connected to a bidder by kinship, a business relationship or a common interest does not take part in the selection of the offer.

(2) An offer from a user of the system is accepted only if it is the most favourable, with a published comparison against the other offers.

### Article 16 — Dinar cost and the upper limit

(1) The total dinar cost of the procurement equals the product of the total quantity and the purchase price per unit from the selected offer.

(2) If the total dinar cost exceeds the upper limit under Article 7, no procurement is carried out. The funds remain available for the next procurement, and a new decision may establish a smaller quantity.

(3) Funds remaining above the total dinar cost stay with the Foundation.

### Article 17 — Nature of the number of POEN per share

(1) The number of POEN per share is not a price of the good. It is derived neither from the purchase price nor from the retail value of the good, and need not stand in proportion to it. The number of POEN per share expresses the measure of participation in the common good that is annulled by the collection of a share, and is the criterion by which it is determined who takes part in the distribution.

(2) The user gives nothing when collecting a share. There is no performance rendered by the user towards the Foundation or towards the supplier, nor is anything required of them in return; what changes is solely the record of their earlier contribution to the community, which is reduced by the number of POEN per share.

(3) The record that is annulled is not a unit that the user obtained in order to collect a share, but the record of a contribution they had already made to the common good. A recorded contribution does not constitute a claim against the Foundation and confers no right to a good (Article 12 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). The right to take part arises from the decision initiating the procurement, while the recorded contribution is the criterion by which precedence in that participation is determined. The Foundation plans the procurement so that the shares are small and sufficient in number for everyone who applies; precedence according to the recorded contribution decides only when there are not enough shares.

(4) POEN is not exchanged for goods and does not reach the supplier. The annulled record does not pass to the Foundation: it is extinguished, the Foundation does not acquire it and receives no value whatsoever on the basis of the annulment of the record.

(5) The goods are provided to users free of charge, from the Foundation's funds, in the pursuit of its purposes. The annulment of the record is neither consideration for the good provided nor a condition for it, but a consequence of participation having been used.

### Article 18 — Calculation

(1) Before applications are opened, the Foundation publishes the calculation, which contains:

1) the selected good;

2) the parameters under Article 13 (the total quantity, the size of a share, the number of shares and the number of POEN per share), with a statement of reasons for how they were established;

3) the total number of POEN to be annulled;

4) the place and period of collection.

(2) The dinar side of the procurement is not published alongside the calculation (Article 37 paragraph 2).

(3) The calculation is not modified after publication. Modifying the calculation requires suspending and re-initiating the procurement. Reasons are given for the suspension, and the suspension releases the reserved POEN.

## V — Application, order and collection

### Article 19 — Application

(1) Every participant under Article 4 applies for a published procurement, regardless of which name they proposed and whether they submitted a proposal at all.

(2) Application does not depend on the number of POEN in the user's record. The number of POEN determines only the place in the order (Article 20). The origin of the POEN is not examined; all contribution recording channels count equally.

(3) The application period is 3 days from the publication of the calculation.

(4) An application is a condition of participation. A user who does not apply does not enter the order.

### Article 20 — Order

(1) The order is established according to the number of POEN in the user's record, from higher to lower.

(2) The number of POEN is established at the moment the application period expires and does not change for that procurement thereafter. Subsequent changes to the record do not shift the order.

(3) If two or more users have an equal number of POEN, the user who applied earlier is placed ahead; if that too is equal, the older account is placed ahead.

(4) A place in the order does not constitute a user status and produces no effect outside the individual procurement.

### Article 21 — Invitation and confirmation

(1) An invitation to confirm is sent to users according to the order, up to the number of shares.

(2) A user confirms participation by entering the day of collection, which must fall within the published collection period. A user who does not have a sufficient number of POEN at the moment of confirmation cannot confirm participation.

(3) Upon entry of the day of collection, the number of POEN per share is reserved in the user's record. The user cannot dispose of the reserved POEN until collection, or until the place is released.

(4) The period for responding to an invitation is 3 days from the sending of the invitation.

### Article 22 — Released place

(1) A place is released if the user withdraws, if the period for responding to the invitation expires without confirmation, or if the user does not collect the share on the day entered. When a place is released, the reserved POEN is also released.

(2) A share that becomes free before the expiry of the collection period is offered to the next user in the order, under Article 21. Invitations are repeated as long as there are users in the order and the collection period lasts. No separate waiting list is maintained; the order under Article 20 continues beyond the number of shares.

### Article 23 — Number of shares acquired

(1) As many shares are acquired as have been confirmed by the expiry of the confirmation period.

(2) If the number of confirmed shares is lower than the number of shares in the calculation, fewer units are acquired and a proportionally lower amount is paid. The size of a share does not change.

### Article 24 — Collection

(1) A user collects their share directly from the supplier, on the day they entered, using a collection code. Only the user sees the collection code.

(2) The collection period is 3 days and is published with the calculation.

(3) The Foundation provides the supplier solely with a list of collection codes. Pseudonyms and other user data are not provided to the supplier.

(4) The Foundation does not store, transport or issue the goods.

### Article 25 — Annulment of POEN records upon use

(1) Upon collection of a share, the number of POEN per share is annulled by a counter-entry in favour of the Protocol. This is annulment upon use under Article 18 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

(2) Annulment occurs at the moment of collection. A reservation is not an annulment.

(3) A user's record cannot pass into a negative value on the basis of a collective procurement or an allocation of vouchers. Only the number of POEN present in the record is reserved and annulled.

### Article 26 — Uncollected shares

(1) Shares not collected by the expiry of the collection period enter the next procurement as additional units.

(2) Uncollected shares are not sold. The Foundation cedes them solely without consideration, in accordance with Article 3.

## VI — Relationship with the supplier and defects in the good

### Article 27 — The Foundation as buyer

(1) In relation to the supplier, the Foundation is the buyer of the goods and the contracting party.

(2) The user does not enter into a contractual relationship with the supplier. Collection of a share is not a purchase and does not establish a contractual relationship between the user and the Foundation with respect to POEN.

(3) The procurement is executed on the basis of an invoice which the Foundation retains. The value added tax calculated in the price is borne by the Foundation as a cost of procurement and is not deducted as input tax.

### Article 28 — Defect in the good

(1) In relation to the supplier, the Foundation contracts for and exercises the rights arising from a defect in the good for the benefit of the users who collected the good.

(2) A user may submit a complaint under Article 40 of the Terms of Use that a share was not collected although it was marked as collected, or that the collected good is not usable for the purpose for which it was procured. The notification of collection contains information about that right and about the deadline for the complaint.

(3) Upon a complaint, the Foundation acts without delay towards the supplier and informs the user of the outcome with a statement of reasons. A good replaced by the supplier is provided by the Foundation to the same user, without a new annulment of POEN records, and the record is not corrected. If the supplier neither replaces the good nor remedies the defect, the annulment is corrected under Article 35.

(4) The obligation under this Article is an obligation of the Foundation towards its own programme. It does not establish a contractual relationship between the user and the supplier, nor between the user and the Foundation, and the user acquires no claim against the Foundation on that ground.

## VII — Allocation of vouchers from sponsorship in goods and services

### Article 29 — Nature of the allocation

(1) The allocation of vouchers is a project of the Foundation in which participants take vouchers issued under Chapter IV of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations, with the annulment of POEN records upon use (Article 18 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(2) In the allocation, the Foundation procures, pays for and possesses nothing. The goods are handed over, or the services provided, by the giver directly to the voucher holder. Article 3 applies mutatis mutandis to the allocation.

### Article 30 — Who takes a voucher

(1) Vouchers are taken by the participants under Article 4, except the persons under Article 36 of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations.

(2) A participant may at any one time have at most two unused vouchers.

### Article 31 — Number of POEN per voucher

(1) The number of POEN annulled per voucher equals the number of POEN that would have been recorded under the agreement without the increase by the recording coefficient, divided by the number of vouchers and rounded to the nearest whole number.

(2) The number of POEN per voucher is derived from the entry recorded under the agreement, and not from a ratio of POEN to money. It is not the price of the good or service and does not express their value. No dinar value is published alongside it; the value of the agreement is published in the report under Article 37 paragraph 2.

### Article 32 — Availability of vouchers

(1) Vouchers are available from the moment of issuance. For each agreement, the Platform displays: a description of the good or service; the name of the giver and the place and address of handover or provision; the number of remaining vouchers; the number of POEN per voucher; the validity period.

(2) No application period and no order are set; a voucher is taken by the participant who requests it first.

### Article 33 — Taking a voucher

(1) A participant takes a voucher on the Platform. When taking it, the participant declares that they are not a person under Article 36 of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations and gives express consent for the record of the annulment, with the description of the good or service and the name of the giver, to be displayed alongside their pseudonym in the contribution ledger (Article 56 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), with a statement that they are aware that such display may reveal data about them, including data concerning health where the voucher relates to such a service. Without the statement and consent, the voucher cannot be taken.

(2) The participant may withdraw consent to the display at any time. Upon withdrawal of consent, the description of the good or service and the name of the giver are deleted from the description of the annulment record and are no longer displayed to other users; the annulment record remains (Article 55 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(3) The voucher is issued to the holder, is kept under their pseudonym and cannot be transferred to another person.

(4) By taking the voucher, the participant has used their participation in the allocation, so the number of POEN under Article 31 is then annulled in their record, by a counter-entry in favour of the Protocol (Article 18 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). A participant who does not have a sufficient number of POEN cannot take a voucher.

### Article 34 — Use and expiry of a voucher

(1) The giver, or the participant whom the giver has designated (Article 32 paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations), sees, when checking a voucher, its description, its validity period and the holder's pseudonym, and confirms the handover of the good or the provision of the service.

(2) Upon confirmation, the voucher is used and is noted with the giver, together with the holder's pseudonym.

(3) The Platform issues to the giver a handover confirmation for the used vouchers. The confirmation contains the description of the good or service, the number of used vouchers and the holder's pseudonym, and does not contain POEN.

(4) A voucher that the holder does not use before the expiry of its validity period lapses, and the annulment effected upon taking is not corrected.

(5) A voucher holder to whom the giver refuses the handover or provision, or whose voucher was marked as used although the good was not handed over or the service was not provided, submits a complaint under Article 40 of the Terms of Use.

## VIII — Correction of annulment and complaint

### Article 35 — Correction of annulment

(1) An annulment upon use is corrected by a counter-entry in favour of the user, in the number of POEN that had been annulled, in accordance with Article 18 paragraph 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System:

1) where the complaint under Article 28 is well-founded and the supplier neither replaces the good nor remedies the defect;

2) where the complaint under Article 34 paragraph 5 is well-founded, for a voucher that has not been used;

3) where the agreement has been terminated under Article 35 of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations, for each taken voucher that has not been used.

(2) The correction of the ledger is not a payment. On account of the correction, the user receives no value other than the record that had been annulled, and the Foundation neither pays out nor receives anything on that ground.

### Article 36 — Complaint against the order and the outcome of a procurement

(1) Against the established order and the outcome of a procurement, a user may submit a complaint under Article 40 of the Terms of Use.

(2) The establishment of the order is the application of rules published by this Rulebook and by the calculation, and does not constitute decision-making producing legal consequences for the user's status.

## IX — Transparency

### Article 37 — Publicity of data

(1) The following are publicly available: the register of proposals in aggregate form, the outcome of the selective vote, the calculation in full, the number of confirmed shares and the total number of annulled POEN.

(2) The dinar side of the procurement (all collected offers with amounts and the name of the bidder, the selected supplier, the purchase price per unit, the total dinar cost and its relation to the upper limit under Article 7) is published in full in the report on the disposal of the Foundation's funds (Article 7 paragraph 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), separately from the calculation, because the number of POEN per share is not the price of the good (Article 17) and must not be read as an expression of it. Nothing is omitted from the dinar side. The value of agreements on sponsorship in goods and services is also published in the same report.

(3) Participants have access to the list of users who collected a share, with the pseudonym and the place in the order. The number of POEN in a user's record is not published alongside that list.

(4) Data on the participation of an individual user is not published beyond the scope under paragraphs 1 and 3.

(5) For the current calendar year, the Foundation publicly publishes the total amount of dinar funds spent on projects, the number of procurements carried out, the number and value of agreements on sponsorship in goods and services and the number of used vouchers. The publication does not limit the number or frequency of procurements.

## X — Final provisions

### Article 38 — Entry into force

This Rulebook enters into force on the date of its adoption.
