# Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement

*This Rulebook governs the allocation of the Foundation's dinar funds to community projects and, as the first regulated form of such a project, collective procurement — the joint acquisition of goods that users cannot obtain within the system. It is adopted on the basis of Articles 14a and 51a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, and in accordance with Article 7 point c) and Article 9 paragraph 3 of the Foundation's Statute.*

## I — General provisions

### Article 1

*Subject matter*

This Rulebook governs: the funds available for projects, the procurement proposal and the register of proposals, the selection of the object of procurement, the collection of offers and the calculation, the application and the determination of the order, the collection of goods, and the annulment of POEN records upon use, as well as the allocation of goods and services from sponsorship in goods and services.

Terms not defined by this Rulebook have the meaning established by the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

### Article 2

*Relationship with other acts*

This Rulebook elaborates the provisions of Articles 14a and 51a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. In the event of any inconsistency, the provisions of the Rulebook on the KOLO System prevail.

Collective procurement is one of the ways of pursuing the Foundation's purposes under Article 7 point c) of the Statute (organising collective procurement of goods and services for the benefit of programme users) and is carried out as a programme within the meaning of Article 9 paragraph 3 of the Statute.

The decision-making procedure of the Upper Kolo is governed by the Rulebook on the Upper Kolo and applies to decisions under this Rulebook as well.

### Article 3

*Definitions*

A **project** is the dedicated spending of the Foundation's dinar funds for the benefit of the community, outside the Foundation's operating costs. The allocation of goods and services from sponsorship in goods and services, by which no dinar funds of the Foundation are spent, is also a project.

**Collective procurement** is a programme of the Foundation within the meaning of Article 7 of the Statute, carried out as a project by which the Foundation acquires goods from a third party using dinar funds, and participants collect those goods in equal shares with the annulment of a corresponding number of POEN.

The **object of procurement** is the good selected in the manner established by Articles 11 and 12 of this Rulebook.

A **share** is an equal quantity of the good belonging to one user within a single procurement.

A **supplier** is a third party from which the Foundation acquires goods. The supplier is not a party to the relationship between the Foundation and users. A cooperative participating in the system is not a supplier, because the subject of a collective procurement are goods that users cannot obtain within the system.

### Article 3a

*Nature of the procurement*

Collective procurement pursues the purposes of the Foundation established by Article 6 of the Statute, in particular social protection and solidarity support and support for the voluntary association of citizens for mutual aid and self-organisation. The goods are distributed to programme users within the meaning of Article 9 paragraph 4 of the Statute.

Collective procurement is not an economic activity. The Foundation derives no revenue and no profit from the procurement, does not offer the goods on the market to an indeterminate circle of persons, and acquires no value whatsoever on the basis of ceding them. The funds used to acquire the goods originate from donations and patronage contributions.

The Foundation may not receive consideration for a ceded good, whether in money or in any other form, either from users or from a third party.

Introducing consideration for a ceded good would constitute an economic activity and is not permitted without amending the Statute and registering that activity in accordance with the law.

### Article 4

*Circle of users*

Collective procurement is participated in by participants — Kolo members and members without confirmation — with an active account.

The condition of verification follows from the purpose of procurement: goods are distributed to the beneficiaries of programs within the meaning of Article 9 paragraph 4 of the Foundation's Statute, and that can only be a person for whom someone in the network has stood up and established, on their own responsibility, that they exist. Without that condition, the place in the order under Article 22 would be attainable by updating the ledger in favour of a newly opened account, so goods would be distributed to a person whose existence no one has established.

Minor users do not submit procurement proposals, do not apply for procurement, and do not collect goods.

## II — Funds

### Article 5

*Funds available for projects*

The funds available for projects are established as the difference between the balance of the Foundation's dinar funds and the operating reserve.

The balance of the Foundation's dinar funds consists of donations and patronage contributions received, reduced by the recorded operating costs and by funds already spent on projects.

The operating reserve amounts to three operating costs of the Foundation for the preceding month. The operating reserve is not spent on projects.

If the difference referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article is equal to or less than zero, no procurement is carried out.

### Article 6

*Relationship to operating costs*

Funds spent on projects do not form part of the Foundation's operating costs and are not taken into account in establishing the threshold of financial autonomy under Article 49 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

Records of project funds are kept separately from records of operating costs.

### Article 7

*Protective veto*

While the Foundation's protective veto is in force within the meaning of Article 48 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, no procurement is carried out.

### Article 8

*Upper spending limit*

For a single procurement, no more dinar funds may be spent than the funds available for projects multiplied by the spending coefficient.

The spending coefficient is 1.00. A change to the spending coefficient constitutes an amendment to this Rulebook.

This limit does not determine the quantity of the good to be acquired. The quantity, the size of a share, and the number of POEN per share are established by the decision initiating the procurement (Article 17), while the dinar cost becomes known only through the collection of offers (Article 15).

## III — Procurement proposal and register of proposals

### Article 9

*Procurement proposal*

A procurement proposal contains solely the name of a good. The proposal does not contain quantity, price, reasoning, or any other data.

The name of the good is chosen from a dictionary of names maintained by the system. If no suitable name exists in the dictionary, the user enters a new name and thereby adds it to the dictionary.

Each user may have **one active procurement proposal**. A new proposal replaces the previous one.

Submitting a proposal is voluntary. The absence of a proposal has no consequences for the user's status and does not affect the right to apply for a procurement.

### Article 10

*Register of proposals*

The Protocol maintains a register of procurement proposals. The register displays the names of goods and the number of distinct users who proposed them.

The register is displayed in aggregate form, without user pseudonyms.

The number of users who proposed the same name is the sole measure of standing in the register. The number of POEN held by a proposer does not affect the position of a name in the register.

### Article 11

*Goods that are not procured*

A good offered by a user in the platform's advertising space is not procured.

Before initiating a procurement, the Foundation verifies whether the selected good is offered by a user. If it is, no procurement is carried out and the need is directed to that listing.

This provision protects exchange among users from competition with the community's funds.

## IV — Selection of the object of procurement

### Article 12

*Decision-making*

The object of procurement is selected by the Upper Kolo through a selective vote, from the list of names in the register of proposals.

Until the Upper Kolo is constituted in Phase 2, the same decision is made, following the same procedure and with the same publication, by the Management Board of the Foundation.

### Article 13

*Selective vote*

A Kolo keeper who has activated ZRNO casts a vote for one name from the list. The vote is weighted by the voting power established in accordance with Articles 5 and 6 of the Rulebook on the Upper Kolo.

The name with the highest sum of voting power is selected. If two or more names have an equal sum, the name proposed by the greater number of distinct users is selected; if that number is also equal, the name entered into the register earlier is selected.

### Article 14

*Nature of the decision*

A decision on collective procurement concerns the allocation of dinar funds and has the nature of a recommendation to the Management Board of the Foundation within the meaning of Article 51 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System and Article 20 of the Rulebook on the Upper Kolo.

The type of decision is derived from the subject matter of the decision and does not depend on the proposer's choice.

## V — Offers and calculation

### Article 15

*Collection of offers*

The Foundation collects at least three offers for the selected good and chooses the most favourable one.

All collected offers, with amounts and the name of the offeror, are published together with the calculation, including offers that were not selected.

An offer is accepted exclusively from a registered legal entity or entrepreneur.

### Article 16

*Conflict of interest*

A person connected to an offeror by kinship, a business relationship, or a common interest does not take part in the selection of the offer.

An offer from a user in the system is accepted only if it is the most favourable, with a published comparison against the other offers.

### Article 17

*Parameters established by the procurement decision*

The decision initiating the procurement establishes the total quantity of the good to be acquired, the size of a single share, and the number of POEN annulled per share, in accordance with Article 12 of this Rulebook.

The decision states the need of the community that the procurement meets and the purpose of the Foundation under Article 3a paragraph 1 thereby pursued. The parameters under paragraph 1 are established according to that need.

The number of shares equals the quotient of the total quantity and the size of a share.

The parameters under paragraph 1 are established before offers are collected and are not derived from the dinar price of the good, which is not known at that time. They are published with the calculation, together with a statement of reasons for how they were established, and are not modified after publication.

### Article 18

*Dinar cost and the upper limit*

The total dinar cost of the procurement equals the product of the total quantity and the purchase price per unit from the selected offer.

If the total dinar cost exceeds the upper limit under Article 8, no procurement is carried out. The funds remain available for the next procurement, and a new decision may establish a smaller quantity.

Funds remaining above the total dinar cost stay with the Foundation.

### Article 19

*Nature of the number of POEN per share*

The number of POEN per share is not a price of the good. It is derived neither from the purchase price nor from the retail value of the good, and need not stand in proportion to it.

The number of POEN per share expresses the measure of participation in the common good that is annulled by the collection of a share.

POEN is not exchanged for goods and does not reach the supplier; the goods are acquired with dinar funds, while POEN is annulled as a record of participation that has been used.

The Foundation receives no value whatsoever on the basis of the annulment of the record. The goods are provided to users free of charge, in the pursuit of the Foundation's purposes; the annulment of the record is not consideration for the good provided but a consequence of participation having been used.

The number of POEN per share is the measure by which it is determined who takes part in the distribution, and not a price at which the good is provided.

The annulled record does not pass to the Foundation. It is extinguished, the Foundation does not acquire it and receives nothing on that basis; the annulment is neither a condition nor consideration for the good provided, but a consequence of participation having been used.

The record that is annulled is not a unit that the user acquired in order to collect a share, but the record of a contribution they had already made to the common good (Article 13 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). A recorded contribution does not constitute a claim against the Foundation and confers no right to a good; the right to take part arises from the decision on the procurement.

The good is provided from the Foundation's funds, free of charge, in pursuit of its objectives laid down in Article 6 of the Statute. The user renders no performance whatsoever towards the Foundation or towards the supplier, nor is anything required of them in return.

The user gives nothing when collecting a share. There is no performance rendered by the user towards the Foundation or towards the supplier; what changes is solely the record of their earlier contribution to the community, which is reduced by the number of POEN under Article 17.

An earlier recorded contribution confers no right to a good and does not constitute a claim against the Foundation (Article 13 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). The right to take part does not arise from the earlier giving but from the decision initiating the procurement; the recorded contribution is the measure by which precedence in that participation is determined.

### Article 20

*Calculation*

Before applications are opened, the Foundation publishes the calculation, which contains: the selected good, the parameters under Article 17 — the total quantity, the size of a share, the number of shares and the number of POEN per share — together with an explanation of how they were determined, the total number of POEN to be annulled, the place of collection and the collection period.

The dinar side of the procurement — all collected offers with amounts, the selected supplier, the purchase price per unit, the total dinar cost and its relation to the upper limit under Article 8 — is published within the report on the disposal of the Foundation's funds under Article 31, and not alongside the calculation. Both sets of data are public; they are kept apart because the number of POEN per share is not the price of the good (Article 19) and must not be read as an expression of it.

The calculation is not modified after publication. Modifying the calculation requires suspending and re-initiating the procurement.

## VI — Application and order

### Article 21

*Application*

Every user referred to in Article 4 of this Rulebook applies for a published procurement, regardless of which name they proposed and regardless of whether they submitted a proposal at all.

Application does not depend on the number of POEN in the user's record. The number of POEN determines only the place in the order (Article 22); a user who at the moment of confirmation does not have a sufficient number of POEN cannot confirm participation (Article 23). The origin of the POEN is not examined; all contribution recording channels count equally.

The application period is three days from the publication of the calculation.

An application is a condition of participation. A user who does not apply does not enter the order.

### Article 22

*Order*

The order is established according to the number of POEN in the user's record, from higher to lower.

The number of POEN is established at the moment the application period expires and does not change for that procurement thereafter. Subsequent changes to the record do not shift the order.

If two or more users hold an equal number of POEN, the user who applied earlier is placed ahead; if that too is equal, the older account is placed ahead.

A position in the order does not constitute a user status and produces no effect outside the individual procurement.

### Article 23

*Invitation and confirmation*

An invitation to confirm is sent to users according to the order, up to the number of shares.

A user confirms participation by entering the day of collection, which must fall within the published collection period. Upon entry of the day of collection, the corresponding number of POEN is reserved in the user's record.

The period for responding to an invitation is three days from the sending of the invitation.

A user who does not hold a sufficient number of POEN at the moment of confirmation cannot confirm participation.

### Article 24

*Withdrawal, expiry, and failure to collect*

A place is released if the user withdraws, if the period for responding to the invitation expires without confirmation, or if the user does not collect the share on the day entered.

When a place is released, the reserved POEN is released and an invitation to confirm is sent to the next user in the order.

No separate waiting list is maintained; the order established by Article 22 continues beyond the number of shares.

### Article 25

*Number of shares acquired*

As many shares are acquired as have been confirmed by the expiry of the confirmation period.

If the number of confirmed shares is lower than the number of shares stated in the calculation, fewer units are acquired and a proportionally lower amount is paid. The size of a share does not change.

## VII — Collection and annulment of records

### Article 26

*Collection*

A user collects their share directly from the supplier, on the day they entered, using a collection code.

The collection period is three days and is published with the calculation.

The Foundation provides the supplier solely with a list of collection codes. Pseudonyms and other user data are not provided to the supplier.

The Foundation does not store, transport, or issue the goods.

### Article 27

*Annulment of POEN records upon use*

Upon collection of a share, the number of POEN established by Article 17 of this Rulebook is annulled by a counter-record in favour of the Protocol.

Annulment upon use is a distinct ground for annulling records within the meaning of Article 14a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. It is not a measure against the user, does not presuppose any irregularity, and is not associated with annulment under Article 34 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System or with Chapter VIII of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality.

Annulment occurs at the moment of collection. Until collection, POEN is reserved but not annulled.

Annulment reduces the total number of recorded POEN. The sum of all records remains unchanged.

### Article 28

*Prohibition of a negative record*

A user's record cannot pass into a negative value on the basis of collective procurement.

Only the number of POEN present in the record is reserved and annulled.

### Article 29

*Uncollected shares*

A share that becomes free before the expiry of the collection period — through withdrawal, expiry of an invitation, or failure to collect — is offered to the next user in the order, under Article 23 of this Rulebook. Invitations are repeated as long as there are users in the order and the collection period lasts.

Shares not collected by the expiry of the collection period enter the next procurement as additional units.

Uncollected shares are not sold. The Foundation cedes them solely without consideration, in accordance with Article 3a of this Rulebook.

## VIII — Relationship with the supplier

### Article 30

*The Foundation as buyer*

In relation to the supplier, the Foundation is the buyer of the goods and the contracting party.

The provision of Article 22 of the Terms of Use, according to which the Foundation is not a party to an exchange, relates to exchange between users and does not apply to the relationship between the Foundation and the supplier.

The user does not enter into a contractual relationship with the supplier. Collection of a share is not a purchase and does not establish a contractual relationship between the user and the Foundation with respect to POEN.

The procurement is executed with a registered legal entity or entrepreneur, on the basis of an invoice which the Foundation retains. The value added tax calculated in the price is borne by the Foundation as a cost of procurement and is not deducted as input tax.

In relation to the supplier, the Foundation contracts for and exercises the rights arising from a defect in the goods for the benefit of the users who collected the good. Where a user reports a defect, the Foundation acts without delay towards the supplier and informs the user of the outcome. Goods replaced by the supplier are made available by the Foundation to the same user, without a new annulment of POEN records.

The obligation under paragraph 5 of this Article is an obligation of the Foundation towards its own programme. It does not establish a contractual relationship between the user and the supplier, nor between the user and the Foundation, and the user acquires no claim against the Foundation on that ground.

### Article 30a

*Procedure upon a reported defect*

A user may report that a share was not collected although it was marked as collected, or that the collected good was not usable for the purpose for which it was procured.

The report is submitted as a complaint to the Foundation, from the user's profile, within seven days of the notification of collection. Article 37a of the Terms of Use applies to the complaint procedure.

Upon receipt of the complaint, the Foundation acts without delay towards the supplier, in accordance with Article 30 of this Rulebook, and informs the user of the outcome with a statement of reasons.

If the supplier replaces the good, the POEN record is not corrected — the participation has been used.

If the supplier neither replaces the good nor remedies the defect, the Foundation corrects the ledger: the annulment of the POEN record effected under Article 27 of this Rulebook is removed by a counter-entry in favour of the user, in the number of POEN that had been annulled, in accordance with Article 14a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

The correction of the ledger is neither a refund of consideration nor any form of payment. On account of the correction the user receives no value other than the record that had been annulled; the Foundation neither pays out nor receives anything on that ground, whereby the provisions of Articles 3a and 19 of this Rulebook remain unaffected.

The notification of collection contains information about the right under this Article and about the deadline.

## VIIIa — Allocation of goods and services from sponsorship

### Article 30b

*Nature of the allocation*

The allocation is a project of the Foundation in which users take vouchers issued under Chapter IVa of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations, with the annulment of POEN records upon use within the meaning of Article 14a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

In the allocation, the Foundation procures, pays for and possesses nothing. The goods are handed over, or the services provided, by the giver directly to the voucher holder. Article 3a of this Rulebook applies mutatis mutandis to the allocation.

### Article 30c

*Circle of users*

A voucher may be taken by the users referred to in Article 4 of this Rulebook, except the persons referred to in Article 13k of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations.

A user may at any one time hold at most two unredeemed vouchers.

### Article 30d

*Number of POEN per voucher*

The number of POEN annulled per voucher equals the number of POEN that would have been recorded under the agreement without the increase by the recording coefficient, divided by the number of vouchers.

The allocation annuls that part of the recording which corresponds to the donated item, so that sponsorship in goods and services, once the vouchers are redeemed, does not increase the total number of POEN beyond the increase by the coefficient. The increase by the coefficient remains as recognition of the contribution, as with a monetary contribution.

The number of POEN per voucher is derived from the recording made under the agreement, and not from a ratio of POEN to money. It is not the price of the good or service and does not express their value. No dinar value is published alongside it; the value of the agreement is published in the report under Article 31 of this Rulebook.

### Article 30e

*Availability of vouchers*

Vouchers are available to users from the moment of issuance. For each agreement, the platform displays: a description of the good or service; the name of the giver and the place and address of handover or provision; the number of remaining vouchers; the number of POEN per voucher; the validity period.

No application deadline and no order are set; a voucher is taken by the user who requests it first.

### Article 30f

*Taking a voucher and annulment*

A user takes a voucher on the platform. When taking it, the user declares that they are not a person referred to in Article 13k of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations and gives consent for the record of the annulment, with the description of the good or service and the name of the giver, to be displayed alongside their pseudonym in the ledger accessible to other users, with express awareness that such display may reveal data about them, including data concerning health where the voucher relates to such a service. Without the declaration and consent, the voucher cannot be taken.

The voucher is issued to the user, is kept under their pseudonym and cannot be transferred to another person.

By taking the voucher, the user has used their participation in the allocation within the meaning of Article 14a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, so the number of POEN under Article 30d of this Rulebook is then annulled in their record, by a counter-entry in favour of the Protocol. A user who does not have a sufficient number of POEN cannot take the voucher.

### Article 30g

*Performance, expiry and correction*

The giver, or a participant whom the giver has designated on the platform to confirm vouchers on the giver's behalf, sees, when checking a voucher, its description, its validity period and the holder's pseudonym, and confirms the handover of the good or the provision of the service. The giver is responsible for the confirmation of such a user, and such a user acquires no other authority in connection with the agreement. Upon confirmation, the voucher is redeemed and is recorded with the giver, together with the holder's pseudonym. Article 28 of this Rulebook applies mutatis mutandis.

A voucher that the holder does not redeem before the expiry of its validity period lapses, and the annulment effected upon taking is not corrected.

A voucher holder to whom the giver refuses the handover or provision, or whose voucher was marked as redeemed although the good was not handed over or the service was not provided, lodges a complaint under Article 37a of the Terms of Use. Where the complaint is well-founded, as well as where the agreement has been terminated under Article 13j of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations, the annulment effected upon taking a voucher that has not been redeemed is corrected by a counter-entry in favour of the holder, in accordance with Article 14a of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. The correction is neither a refund of consideration nor any form of payment.

## IX — Transparency

### Article 31

*Publicity of data*

The following are publicly available: the register of proposals in aggregate form, the outcome of the selective vote, the calculation in full, the number of confirmed shares, and the total number of annulled POEN.

The dinar side of the procurement under Article 20, paragraph 2 is published within the report on the disposal of the Foundation's funds, separately from the calculation. It is published in full and nothing is omitted from it.

Participants additionally have access to the list of users who collected a share, showing the pseudonym and position in the order. The number of POEN in a user's record is not published alongside that list.

Data on the participation of an individual user is not published beyond the scope set out in paragraphs 1 and 3 of this Article.

The Foundation also publishes aggregate data on projects for the current calendar year: the total amount of dinar funds spent on projects and the number of procurements carried out, as well as the number and value of agreements on sponsorship in goods and services and the number of vouchers redeemed. The publication is a record of scale, not a limit — the frequency of procurements is not restricted by this Rulebook.

## X — Final provisions

### Article 32

*Deletion of proposals*

Upon completion of a procurement, proposals for the selected name of the good are deleted. Proposals for other names remain in the register.

A proposal is also deleted upon termination of user status.

### Article 33

*Right of complaint*

Against the established order and the outcome of a procurement, a user may lodge a complaint in accordance with Article 37a of the Terms of Use. The complaint is decided upon by an authorised person of the Foundation.

The establishment of the order is the application of rules published by this Rulebook and by the calculation, and does not constitute decision-making producing legal consequences for the user's status.

### Article 34

*Other projects*

Projects other than collective procurement are governed by an amendment to this Rulebook, prior to their implementation.

Until a particular form of project is governed by this Rulebook, dinar funds are not allocated to that form.

### Article 35

*Amendments to the Rulebook*

This Rulebook is adopted and amended by the Management Board of the KOLO Foundation in Phase 1, and by the Upper Kolo in Phase 2, following the procedure established by the Rulebook on the KOLO System and the Rulebook on the Hierarchy of Acts of the KOLO System.

### Article 36

*Entry into force*

This Rulebook enters into force on the day of its adoption by the Management Board of the KOLO Foundation.
