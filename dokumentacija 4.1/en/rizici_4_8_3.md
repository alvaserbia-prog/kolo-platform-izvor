# Risk Acceptance Statement

### Article 1 — Subject matter and legal nature

This Risk Acceptance Statement (hereinafter: the Statement) is a platform act of the KOLO system within the meaning of Article 10 of the Rulebook on the Hierarchy of Acts of the KOLO System. It describes the risks of participation in the KOLO system that the user should understand before joining the system.

The Statement applies together with the Terms of Use. In the event of any inconsistency between this Statement and the Rulebook on the KOLO System or any other rulebook, the solution more favourable to the user shall apply.

By joining the KOLO system, the user confirms that they have read, understood, and accepted the content of this Statement.

### Article 2 — Nature of the KOLO system

The KOLO system is a participatory common-good system based on the principles of social and solidarity economy and mutuality.

The KOLO system is not a financial service, payment service, investment scheme, collective investment scheme, payment institution, or issuer of digital assets. Participation in the KOLO system is not investing. Joining the system does not create an expectation of financial return.

The relationship between the user and the KOLO Foundation is a contractual relationship governed by the terms of use. The user is not a client purchasing a service, but a person who voluntarily accepts the rules of a shared system.

### Article 3 — Nature of the accounting units

POEN is an internal accounting unit of the system by which contribution and other forms of participation in the common good are recorded in the KOLO Protocol's ledger. POEN is not money, currency, token, means of payment, electronic money, or digital asset.

ZRNO is an internal accounting unit of the system by which the user's position in the common good is recorded in the Protocol's ledger. ZRNO does not represent a share, equity, security, collective investment unit, investment contract, or digital asset. ZRNO carries no dividend, interest, return, or right to a liquidation surplus.

POEN and ZRNO have no value outside the system and cannot be converted into money, currency, or any instrument with value outside the system, directly or indirectly, including exchange for vouchers, gift cards, or other instruments with external value. The Foundation does not redeem POEN or ZRNO.

Users have no property right over POEN and ZRNO records in the system's ledger. A POEN record is data in the common-good ledger, not an asset owned by the user. ZRNO is a record of position in the common good, not a form of property right. Records may not be transferred to another person.

Recorded contribution does not constitute a claim against the Foundation.

### Article 4 — Risks of the accounting framework

The accounting coefficient is the numerical ratio of the total number of POEN recorded to the number of ZRNO available for registration in the Protocol. The accounting coefficient is an administrative figure — it is not a price, exchange rate, or performance index.

The accounting coefficient changes with activity in the system. No user, no institution, and no actor in the system controls the coefficient. Its rise or fall is a consequence of the activity of the entire community, not of any individual's decision.

A change in the position of a user to whom ZRNO is registered within the accounting system is not a return and is not guaranteed. The difference between the accounting coefficient at the time of ZRNO registration and at the time of deregistration is an arithmetic consequence of changes in the state of the entire system's ledger. No actor pays or guarantees that difference. Any benefit arising from a change in position is realised solely in POEN — records in the ledger with no external property value.

Registered ZRNO confers no right to the Foundation's dinar funds, whether directly or indirectly, including when it is frozen. Dinar funds enter the Foundation and do not enter the accounting framework of the system. Decisions on the allocation of dinar funds, including projects and collective procurements, create no property right for any user to whom ZRNO is registered and confer no share in the Foundation's assets. Neither the cessation of the capacity of Kolo keeper nor the deregistration of ZRNO gives rise to any claim against the Foundation.

Activated ZRNO confers a vote in the Upper Kolo. The Upper Kolo is a body established by a rulebook of the Foundation and is not among the organs of the Foundation established by the Statute; its decision is implemented by an act of the Management Board, which is obliged to adopt it save on the grounds expressly enumerated in the acts. A vote in the Upper Kolo does not confer a right that the Foundation adopt a particular act, does not confer a share in its property, and does not create a claim if the act is not adopted on the grounds provided for by the acts.

Founding contribution is recorded in the records of a closed circle of persons, up to the upper limit established by the Rulebook on Founding Contribution, in steps tied to the growth of the total number of POEN. The aggregate recorded founding contribution constitutes between approximately one fifth and approximately one quarter of the total number of recorded POEN, and at the moment the channel closes approximately 24%. That share carries no property right, constitutes no claim against the Foundation, and confers no right to its dinar funds; the circle of founders is closed and cannot be expanded, and upon reaching the upper limit the channel closes permanently and irrevocably.

### Article 5 — Irrevocability of donations

Dinar funds that the user gives to the Foundation are donations within the meaning of the applicable regulations. A donation is irrevocable, regardless of the further development of the system.

A donor does not acquire a right to a refund, does not acquire a governance right in the Foundation by virtue of the donation, and does not acquire a share in the system by virtue of the donation.

The Protocol records the fact of the donor's contribution in POEN, but that record is not consideration for the donation. The donor may not make the donation conditional on the recording, nor does the recording create an obligation of the Foundation towards the donor.

The number of POEN recorded on the basis of a donation depends on the donation tier established by the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations. The difference in the recording coefficient represents the regard in which a contribution to the common good is held, and not consideration for the donation.

POEN is not acquired from other users for money or for any other value outside the system. Such an arrangement is prohibited by the Terms of Use, is concluded entirely outside the Platform and without any protection, and a user who enters into it acquires no donation tier, does not move the recording coefficient, and receives neither a donation confirmation nor public recognition of a contribution — and is therefore in a worse position than a user who gave the same amount to the Foundation.

### Article 6 — Public nature of the pseudonymous ledger

The contribution ledger is public in pseudonymous form. Pseudonymity is not anonymity — pseudonymised data remain personal data within the meaning of the Law on Personal Data Protection because they can, with additional information, be connected to an identified person.

The public nature of the pseudonymous contribution ledger is a structural characteristic of the system, not an option the user can disable. A user who does not accept this condition may not use the platform.

Even with pseudonymity, the combination of amounts, timing, and frequency of transactions may in certain cases enable indirect identification of the user. The user is aware of this limitation and accepts it by joining the system.

### Article 7 — Risks to system operation

The Foundation does not guarantee that the system will operate without interruption, that the infrastructure will not be affected by technical problems, or that the ledger will be protected against every security incident. The Foundation is obligated to take reasonable technical and organisational protective measures.

The system may be affected by technical problems, security incidents, or regulatory changes that affect its operation.

### Article 8 — Risk of Foundation cessation

The Foundation may cease to exist. In that case, the obligation to safeguard the common good and responsibility for the infrastructure pass to the legal successor in accordance with the Statute, but the continuity of the system's operation is not guaranteed.

### Article 9 — User responsibility in exchange

For the performance, quality, and risk in the exchange of goods and services, the users participating in such exchange are responsible in accordance with the general rules of the law of obligations. The Foundation does not mediate in exchange and is not a party to the legal relationship between users who exchange goods and services.

The user is responsible for the accuracy of the data they provide to the system. A verifier who confirms the reality of another user or introduces them into the Kolo is responsible for the truthfulness of the verification in accordance with the rules on proof of reality.

### Article 10 — Tax risk

The system's position is that POEN and ZRNO are records without property value and that the exchange of goods and services within the system redistributes existing records without creating a monetary flow.

Tax authorities may take a different position. The exchange of goods and services within the system may be qualified as barter or another taxable event within the meaning of tax regulations, including regulations on personal income tax and value added tax. Recording an exchange in POEN does not determine the tax qualification of the transaction between users, and a user who is obliged to issue an invoice or a fiscal receipt issues it regardless of that recording (Article 16 of the Terms of Use).

The same applies to POEN recorded on account of operational contribution and other channels for recording contribution. The system's position is that such an entry pays out no remuneration and gives no value whatsoever, but records a contribution made to the common good, and that on that basis the Foundation is neither the party commissioning work nor the beneficiary of a performance. Tax authorities may take a different position and qualify such an entry as income of the user, including income realised in non-monetary form.

The Foundation pays no money to a user on any basis. By the recording of POEN the user receives neither money nor goods, nothing passes from the Foundation's assets into their assets, and the record so created cannot be turned into money, assigned or collected (Article 13 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

The exception is the collection of a share from a collective procurement and the redemption of a voucher from the allocation of goods and services, in which the user receives a good or a service. The Foundation provides a good from a procurement free of charge and without consideration, in pursuit of its objectives; the user receives a good or service under a voucher directly from the giver, without any performance towards the giver or the Foundation (Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement, Chapter VIIIa). Tax authorities may also qualify such receipt as income of the user.

The Foundation does not provide tax advice and cannot guarantee the tax treatment of users' activities in the system. The user is responsible for their own tax obligations that may arise in connection with participation in the system and is advised to consult a tax adviser.

### Article 11 — Regulatory risk

The KOLO system combines legal concepts from several existing categories. Serbian law has no ready-made legal category for systems of this type. The legal qualification of the system derives from its structural characteristics, in particular from the four principles established by the Rulebook on the KOLO System: non-convertibility, absence of property rights over records, irrevocability of donations, and data minimisation.

A change in the regulatory framework or in the interpretation of competent authorities may affect the operation of the system or the legal position of users.

### Article 11a — Prohibition of abuse of the system

Use of the system for money laundering, the financing of terrorism or the concealment of the origin of funds is prohibited by Article 24 of the Terms of Use and entails the measures under Articles 27 and 28 of the Terms of Use, including exclusion, as well as notification of the competent authority where the regulations so provide.

The user is aware that the Foundation pays no money to a user on any basis whatsoever, that a donation is non-refundable and that POEN is neither redeemed nor converted into dinars, so that funds given to the Foundation cannot be returned in money in any form.

The Foundation applies the measures under Chapter IV of the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations. The Foundation lays those measures down voluntarily and they do not constitute an acknowledgement of the status of an obliged entity within the meaning of the regulations on the prevention of money laundering and the financing of terrorism.

### Article 12 — User declaration

By joining the KOLO system, the user declares:

— that they have read and understood this Statement, the Terms of Use, the Privacy Policy, and the system rules;

— that they understand the nature of POEN and ZRNO as internal accounting units with no value outside the system;

— that they understand that participation in the system is not an investment and that there is no expectation of financial return;

— that they understand that donations to the Foundation are irrevocable;

— that they understand the risks to system operation, including the possibility of technical problems, security incidents, and regulatory changes;

— that they understand that the Foundation does not guarantee the continuity of the system's operation;

— that they understand that the exchange of goods and services within the system may have tax implications and that they are responsible for their own tax obligations;

— that they accept the public nature of the pseudonymous ledger as a structural characteristic of the system;

— that they will not use the system for money laundering, the financing of terrorism or the concealment of the origin of funds;

— that they join the system voluntarily, on the basis of their own assessment and at their own responsibility.

### Article 13 — Amendments to the Statement

This Statement may be amended by a decision of the Management Board (UO) of the KOLO Foundation. Each amendment is communicated to users before it takes effect, within a period that allows them to become acquainted with the changes.

Continued use of the system after the amended Statement takes effect shall be deemed acceptance of the amendments.
