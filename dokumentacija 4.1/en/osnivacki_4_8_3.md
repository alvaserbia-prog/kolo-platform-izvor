# Rulebook on Founding Contribution

*This Rulebook governs the operational mechanics of founding contribution — the retrospective recording of work performed before the platform was opened. It is adopted on the basis of Article 37 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.*

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1

*Subject matter*

This Rulebook governs: the concept of a founder within the meaning of this Rulebook and founding work, the upper limit of founding contribution, the amount and schedule of recording steps, the condition for activating a step, the point at which the channel closes permanently, and the distribution of founding contribution among founders.

Terms not defined in this Rulebook shall have the meaning established in the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

### Article 2

*Relationship with the Rulebook on the KOLO System*

This Rulebook elaborates the provisions of Article 37 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. In the event of any inconsistency, the provisions of the Rulebook on the KOLO System shall prevail.

## II — Founders and Founding Work

### Article 3

*Concept of a founder within the meaning of this Rulebook*

A founder within the meaning of this Rulebook is a natural person who performed work on the design, development, and preparation of the KOLO system before the platform was opened. Where this Rulebook refers to a founder, it means a founder within the meaning of this Article.

A founder within the meaning of this Rulebook is distinct from a founder within the meaning of Article 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System — an initial Kolo keeper determined under Article 14 of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality — and from the founder of the Foundation. The same person may hold more than one of these capacities, but they are not equated: the capacity of a founder within the meaning of this Rulebook gives only a share in the founding contribution and gives neither a position in the chain of confirmations nor powers of governance.

The circle of founders is closed. The persons acquiring founder status are established by an internal act of the Foundation published in accordance with Article 13 of this Rulebook. No decision adopted after the publication of that act may expand the circle of founders.

### Article 4

*Subject matter of founding work*

Founding work encompasses work performed before the platform was opened, in particular: the design of the system architecture, the drafting of rules and protocols, legal preparation, organisational preparation, and the preparation of system documentation.

Founding work is by its nature operational contribution performed before the system existed and therefore could not be recorded at the time it was carried out.

Founding work is not work commissioned by the Foundation, nor work outside an employment relationship — in particular not a contract for work. The founders undertook it on their own initiative, before the platform was opened; the Foundation did not commission it, concluded no contract with the founders regarding it, and acquires no exclusive right on the basis of its result. Article 27 of the Rulebook on Operational Contribution and Article 13 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System apply mutatis mutandis to the recording of the founding contribution.

The result of founding work — the software, the protocol, and the documentation of the system — enters the common good under the licences set out in Chapter II of the Rulebook on the KOLO System: software under the AGPL-3.0 licence, content under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. Founders acquire no exclusive right to that result on the basis of that work; the result is available to all persons on the same terms, and the licences cannot be replaced with more restrictive ones (Article 50 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

## III — Upper Limit and Recording Steps

### Article 5

*Upper limit and its basis*

The upper limit of founding contribution is 2,400,000 POEN. This is the total maximum amount that the Protocol may record through the founding contribution channel.

The limit is a measure of the contribution that preceded the system. The founders invested their own monetary resources and their own time in the design, construction, and legal and organisational preparation of the system, in a period in which the system did not exist and in which no recording channel was in operation.

That contribution cannot be recorded through the operational contribution channel. The daily limit of that channel amounts to 10% of the total number of POEN recorded in the system (Article 23 of the Rulebook on Operational Contribution); while that number is zero, the limit is likewise zero and the channel can record no contribution at all. The same constraint affects work performed in the initial phase, while the total number of recorded POEN is still negligible.

The limit is established in advance and cannot be increased. The circle of persons among whom it is distributed is closed by Article 3 of this Rulebook, and the channel closes permanently and irrevocably in accordance with Articles 14 and 15.

### Article 6

*Recording step*

Founding contribution is recorded in steps of a fixed amount of 24,000 POEN. Each step records the same amount, without exception.

The total number of steps is 100.

### Article 7

*Condition for activating a step*

The Protocol records one step each time the total number of POEN recorded in the system reaches a new threshold of 100,000 POEN.

The thresholds are fixed: 100,000, 200,000, 300,000, and so on, up to and including the final threshold of 10,000,000 POEN. The Protocol activates a step at the moment the total number of POEN in the system first reaches or exceeds the next threshold. After the step activated at the threshold of 10,000,000 POEN, founding contribution is no longer recorded.

POEN recorded through founding contribution are included in the total number of POEN recorded in the system and count towards the cumulative growth that activates the next step.

### Article 8

*Effect on the accounting coefficient and share of the total number of POEN*

The step is of a fixed amount, so the impact of a single step on the accounting coefficient decreases as the system grows. In the early phase of the system, when the total number of POEN is small, a step of 24,000 POEN constitutes a larger share of the total; as the system grows, the same absolute amount represents an ever-smaller share.

The share of the aggregate recorded founding contribution in the total number of POEN is a different quantity, and it does not decrease. Since both the thresholds and the step are expressed in fixed amounts, and POEN recorded through this channel enters the total number (Article 7 paragraph 3), the aggregate founding contribution constitutes between approximately one fifth and approximately one quarter of the total number of recorded POEN throughout the entire life of the channel. At the moment the channel closes, that share amounts to approximately 24%.

The channel follows the growth of the total number of POEN whatever its pace may be. If the total number grows abruptly, the steps are recorded at the same speed at which the growth occurred; this Rulebook does not promise that recording will be spread over a longer period.

Tying recording to the growth of the total number of POEN also means that founding contribution is recorded precisely at the moments when the total number of records grows, including growth originating from a single large individual contribution. In such a case the share of a single person in the total number of records is smaller than it would be if the channel did not exist. This effect is bounded by the upper limit set out in Article 5 and ceases when the channel closes.

## IV — Recording Mechanics

### Article 9

*Method of recording*

The recording of founding contribution is an automatic act of the Protocol. When the total number of POEN in the system reaches the next threshold, the Protocol records the step without any discretionary decision by any actor.

### Article 10

*Founders' records*

POEN recorded through founding contribution are entered in the founders' records and have the same status as all other POEN — records in the ledger without a property right of the user, non-convertible and without external value.

### Article 11

*Relationship with other recording channels*

Founding contribution is a separate recording channel and does not affect the operational contribution limit.

A founder within the meaning of this Rulebook who registers ZRNO is subject to the same threshold and the same limit per accounting period as any other participant who registers ZRNO. The exemption from the conditions for registering ZRNO under Article 82 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System applies only to the establishment of the founders as initial Kolo keepers.

## V — Distribution Among Founders

### Article 12

*Internal distribution*

The distribution of the upper limit of founding contribution among founders is established by an internal act of the Foundation.

The internal act establishes the share of each founder in the total upper limit. The sum of all shares equals the upper limit set out in Article 5 of this Rulebook.

Each step of 24,000 POEN is distributed among founders in proportion to their shares established by the internal act.

### Article 13

*Publication of the distribution*

The internal act on the distribution of founding contribution is published to the users of the system.

## VI — Closure of the Channel

### Article 14

*Point of closure*

The founding contribution channel closes permanently at the moment the Protocol records the 100th step — that is, when the total amount recorded through founding contribution reaches the upper limit of 2,400,000 POEN.

### Article 15

*Permanence of closure*

The closure of the founding contribution channel is permanent and irrevocable. The channel may not be reopened by a decision of the founders within the meaning of this Rulebook, the founder of the Foundation, the Foundation, the Management Board (UO), or the Upper Kolo.

## VII — Transparency

### Article 16

*Public availability of data on founding contribution*

The following data are publicly available to all users of the system: the total amount recorded through founding contribution, the number of steps completed, the remaining amount up to the upper limit, the percentage of channel utilisation, and the founders' distribution shares.

## VIII — Final Provisions

### Article 17

*Amendments to the Rulebook*

This Rulebook is adopted and amended by the Management Board (UO) of the KOLO Foundation, under the procedure established in the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

### Article 18

*Entry into force*

This Rulebook enters into force on the date of its adoption by the Management Board (UO) of the KOLO Foundation.
