# Risk Acceptance Statement

## I — General provisions

### Article 1 — Subject matter

(1) This Statement (hereinafter: the Statement) describes the risks of participation in the KOLO system that the user should understand before registering on the Platform. The Statement is the only act of the KOLO system that describes those risks.

(2) Terms from the Rulebook on the KOLO System have the same meaning in this Statement.

(3) The Statement is a platform act within the meaning of the Rulebook on the Hierarchy of Acts of the KOLO System and applies together with the Terms of Use. In the event of any inconsistency between this Statement and the Rulebook on the KOLO System or any other rulebook, the solution more favourable to the user applies.

### Article 2 — Acceptance of the Statement

(1) The user accepts this Statement by registering on the Platform, together with the Terms of Use (Article 6 of the Terms of Use). The Statement is publicly available on the Platform.

(2) The Statement does not exclude or limit the Foundation's liability for damage it causes intentionally or through gross negligence (Article 37 paragraph 3 of the Terms of Use), nor the rights that belong to the user under the mandatory regulations on consumer protection and on personal data protection.

## II — Nature of the system and of the accounting units

### Article 3 — Nature of the KOLO system

(1) The KOLO system is a participatory common-good system (Article 9 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). It is not a financial service, payment service, investment scheme, collective investment scheme, payment institution, or issuer of digital assets. Participation in the KOLO system is not investing. Joining the system does not create an expectation of financial return.

(2) The relationship between the user and the Foundation is a contractual relationship governed by the Terms of Use. The Foundation does not charge the user for the use of the Platform and does not offer them goods or services for remuneration (Article 26 of the Terms of Use).

### Article 4 — POEN and ZRNO

(1) POEN is an internal accounting unit by which contribution and other forms of participation in the common good are recorded in the Protocol's ledger. POEN is not money, currency, token, electronic money, means of payment, digital asset, financial instrument, or security (Article 16 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(2) ZRNO is an internal accounting unit by which the participant's position in the common good is recorded in the Protocol's ledger. ZRNO does not represent a share, equity, security, collective investment unit, investment contract, or digital asset. ZRNO carries no dividend, interest, return, or right to a liquidation surplus (Article 21 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(3) POEN and ZRNO have no value outside the system and cannot be converted into money, currency, or any instrument with value outside the system, directly or indirectly, including exchange for vouchers, gift cards, or other instruments with external value. The distribution of vouchers from sponsorship in goods and services is not indirect conversion (Article 11 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). The Foundation does not redeem POEN or ZRNO.

(4) Users have no property right over POEN and ZRNO records in the system's ledger. A POEN record is data in the common-good ledger, not an asset owned by the user. ZRNO is a record of position in the common good, not a form of property right. Records are not transferred or inherited as property rights (Article 12 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(5) Recorded contribution does not constitute a claim against the Foundation. No monetary funds stand behind a record: the Foundation holds no funds whatsoever as cover for POEN and does not owe them to the holder of the record (Article 16 paragraph 4 point 7 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

## III — Risks of the accounting framework

### Article 5 — Accounting coefficient and the position of a Kolo keeper

(1) The accounting coefficient (Article 25 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System) is an administrative figure: it is not a price, not an exchange rate, and not a performance index.

(2) The accounting coefficient changes with the total number of recorded POEN and may rise and fall; it is not predetermined to rise. It is calculated by the Protocol from the ledger, automatically and without discretion; no person controls it or promises it. ZRNO inscription and write-off do not move the accounting coefficient.

(3) ZRNO is not acquired by investing funds, but is inscribed from contribution already recorded in the participant's record. A change in the position of a participant to whom ZRNO is inscribed is not a return and is not guaranteed: upon write-off they may receive fewer POEN than they used for the inscription. The difference between the accounting coefficient at the time of ZRNO inscription and at the time of write-off derives from a formula that the Protocol applies without discretion and may arise in either direction; no actor pays or guarantees it. A change in position is expressed solely in POEN, records in the ledger with no value outside the system.

### Article 6 — ZRNO and the Foundation's dinar funds

Inscribed ZRNO confers no right to the Foundation's dinar funds, whether directly or indirectly, including when it is frozen. Dinar funds enter the Foundation and do not enter the accounting framework of the system. Decisions on the allocation of dinar funds, including projects and collective procurements, create no property right for any user to whom ZRNO is inscribed and confer no share in the Foundation's assets. Neither the cessation of the capacity of Kolo keeper nor the write-off of ZRNO gives rise to any claim against the Foundation.

### Article 7 — Vote in the Upper Kolo

Activated ZRNO confers a vote in the Upper Kolo from Phase 2. The Upper Kolo is a body established by the Rulebook on the KOLO System and is not among the organs of the Foundation established by the Statute; its decision is implemented by the Management Board by its act, which it is obliged to adopt save on the grounds expressly enumerated in Article 46 paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. To the extent that it requires the spending of the Foundation's dinar funds, a decision of the Upper Kolo has the effect of a recommendation (Article 46 paragraph 6 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). A vote in the Upper Kolo does not confer a right that the Foundation adopt a particular act, does not confer a share in its property, and does not create a claim if the act is not adopted on the grounds provided for by the acts.

### Article 8 — Founding contribution

Founding contribution is recorded in the records of a closed circle of persons, up to the upper limit established by the Rulebook on Founding Contribution, in steps tied to the growth of the total number of POEN. The aggregate recorded founding contribution does not exceed approximately 24% of the total number of recorded POEN, except when the total number decreases through the annulment of records; in the first steps the share is smaller, and after the channel closes it declines as the total number grows. That share carries no property right, constitutes no claim against the Foundation, and confers no right to its dinar funds; the circle of founders is closed and cannot be expanded, and upon reaching the upper limit the channel closes permanently and irrevocably.

## IV — Donations and POEN obtained outside the system

### Article 9 — Non-refundability of donations

(1) Dinar funds that the user gives to the Foundation are donations within the meaning of the applicable regulations. A donation is non-refundable, regardless of the further development of the system. Sponsorship, in money and in goods and services, is also non-refundable (Article 13 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). A donation is not a deposit: it is not returned, it bears no interest, and the Foundation does not hold it for the donor, but spends it for the objectives set out in the Statute.

(2) A donor does not acquire a right to a refund, does not acquire a governance right in the Foundation by virtue of the donation, and does not acquire a share in the system by virtue of the donation.

(3) The Protocol records the fact of the donor's contribution in POEN, but that record is not consideration for the donation. The donor may not make the donation conditional on the recording, nor does the recording create an obligation of the Foundation towards the donor. The donation agreement does not contain the number of POEN, the recording coefficient, or the tier (Article 14 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(4) The number of POEN recorded on the basis of a donation depends on the donation tier established by the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations. A larger individual contribution has greater significance for the community; the difference in the recording coefficient represents the regard in which a contribution to the common good is held, and not consideration for the donation.

### Article 10 — POEN obtained outside the system

POEN is not obtained from other users for money or for any other value outside the system. Such an arrangement is prohibited by the Terms of Use (Article 28 paragraph 3), is concluded entirely outside the Platform and without any protection, and a user who enters into it acquires no donation tier, does not move the recording coefficient, and receives neither a donation confirmation nor public recognition of a contribution.

## V — Other risks

### Article 11 — Availability of the contribution ledger

(1) The contribution ledger is available to participants in pseudonymous form, and to users who are not in the Kolo to the extent established by Article 56 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. That availability is a characteristic of the system and the user cannot disable it.

(2) Pseudonymity is not anonymity: pseudonymised data remain personal data because they can, with additional information, be connected to an identified person. Even with pseudonymity, the combination of amounts, timing, and frequency of transactions may in certain cases enable indirect identification of the user.

### Article 12 — Risks to system operation

(1) The system may be affected by technical problems, security incidents, or regulatory changes that affect its operation.

(2) The Foundation does not guarantee that the system will operate without interruption, that the infrastructure will not be affected by technical problems, or that the ledger will be protected against every security incident. The Foundation is obliged to take reasonable technical and organisational protective measures, and its obligations under the regulations on personal data protection are not affected thereby.

### Article 13 — Dissolution of the Foundation

The Foundation may cease to exist. In that case the Management Board, in accordance with the Statute, transfers the infrastructure and the ledger to the person to whom the remaining assets of the Foundation are allocated, and only to a person who accepts the principles and limits of the common good and assumes the obligation to safeguard it; users are notified of the transfer before it is carried out (Article 48 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). The continuity of the system's operation is not guaranteed.

### Article 14 — Exchange and negative record

(1) For the performance, quality, and risk in the exchange of goods and services, the users participating in it are responsible, in accordance with the general rules of the law of obligations. The Foundation does not mediate in exchange and is not a party to the legal relationship between users who exchange goods and services (Articles 24 and 37 of the Terms of Use).

(2) As in any advertising space, the user may be the target of fraud. No one acting on behalf of the system asks for money, a password, or a sign-in code (Article 11 of the Terms of Use); whoever asks for it is not acting on behalf of the system.

(3) A POEN record may become negative only on the grounds set out in Article 17 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, among which are a correction following a complaint about an unfulfilled exchange and the restitution borne, upon an established false verification, by whoever confirmed the account or introduced it into the Kolo (Rulebook on Proof of Reality). A negative record is not a debt and is not collected, and the user is notified of it.

### Article 15 — Tax risk

(1) The system's position is that POEN and ZRNO are records without property value and that the exchange of goods and services within the system moves existing records without creating a monetary flow.

(2) Tax authorities may take a different position. The exchange of goods and services within the system may be qualified as barter or another taxable event within the meaning of tax regulations, including regulations on personal income tax and value added tax. Recording an exchange in POEN does not determine the tax qualification of the transaction between users, and a user who is obliged to issue an invoice or a fiscal receipt issues it regardless of that recording (Article 22 paragraph 3 of the Terms of Use).

(3) The same applies to POEN recorded on account of operational contribution and other contribution recording channels. The system's position is that such an entry pays out no remuneration and gives no value whatsoever, but notes a contribution made to the common good, and that on that basis the Foundation is neither the party commissioning work nor the beneficiary of a performance. Tax authorities may take a different position and qualify such an entry as income of the user, including income realised in non-monetary form.

(4) The Foundation pays no money to a user on any basis. By the entry of POEN the user receives neither money nor goods, nothing passes from the Foundation's assets into their assets, and the record so created cannot be turned into money, assigned as a claim, or collected; the re-registration of a record to another user in connection with an exchange is not an assignment (Article 16 paragraph 8 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(5) The exception is the collection of a share from a collective procurement and the use of a voucher from the distribution of goods and services from sponsorship, in which the user receives a good or a service. The Foundation provides a good from a procurement free of charge and without consideration, in pursuit of its objectives; the user receives a good or service under a voucher directly from the giver, without any performance towards the giver or the Foundation (Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement). The annulment of the POEN record in that connection is not remuneration for the good: the POEN returns to the Protocol, and the Foundation receives nothing (Article 18 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). Tax authorities may also qualify such receipt as income of the user.

(6) The Foundation does not provide tax advice and cannot guarantee the tax treatment of users' activities in the system. The user is responsible for their own tax obligations that may arise in connection with participation in the system and is advised to consult a tax adviser.

### Article 16 — Regulatory risk

(1) The KOLO system combines legal concepts from several existing categories. Serbian law has no ready-made legal category for systems of this type. The legal qualification of the system derives from its structural characteristics, in particular from the four principles (non-convertibility, absence of property rights over records, non-refundability of donations, and automatic recording) and the two limits of the common good (data minimisation and common-good licences), established by Article 10 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. The principles and limits cannot be amended by any governance decision.

(2) A change in the regulatory framework or in the interpretation of competent authorities may affect the operation of the system or the legal position of users.

### Article 17 — Prohibition of abuse of the system

(1) Use of the system for money laundering, the financing of terrorism, or the concealment of the origin of funds is prohibited by Article 28 of the Terms of Use and entails the measures under Articles 31 and 32 of the Terms of Use, including exclusion, as well as notification of the competent authority where the regulations so provide.

(2) The Foundation pays no money to a user on any basis, a donation is non-refundable, and POEN is neither redeemed nor converted into dinars, so funds given to the Foundation cannot be returned in money in any form.

(3) The Foundation applies the measures on the prevention of money laundering and the financing of terrorism set out in the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations. The Foundation lays those measures down voluntarily and they do not constitute an acknowledgement of the status of an obliged entity within the meaning of the regulations on the prevention of money laundering and the financing of terrorism.

## VI — User declaration and final provisions

### Article 18 — User declaration

By registering on the Platform, the user declares:

1) that they have read this Statement and that the Terms of Use, the Privacy Policy, and the other acts of the KOLO system are available to them on the Platform;

2) that they understand the nature of POEN and ZRNO as internal accounting units with no value outside the system;

3) that they understand that participation in the system is not an investment and that there is no expectation of financial return;

4) that they understand that the accounting coefficient may fall and that a change in position from inscribed ZRNO is not a return;

5) that they understand that donations to the Foundation are non-refundable;

6) that they understand the risks to system operation, including the possibility of technical problems, security incidents, and regulatory changes, and that the Foundation does not guarantee the continuity of the system's operation;

7) that they understand that the exchange of goods and services within the system may have tax implications and that they are responsible for their own tax obligations;

8) that they accept the availability of the contribution ledger in pseudonymous form as a characteristic of the system;

9) that they will not use the system for money laundering, the financing of terrorism, or the concealment of the origin of funds;

10) that they join the system voluntarily, on the basis of their own assessment.

### Article 19 — Amendments to the Statement

Amendments to this Statement are published to users and enter into force under the procedure for amending acts established by Article 43 of the Terms of Use.

### Article 20 — Entry into force

This Statement enters into force on the date of its adoption.
