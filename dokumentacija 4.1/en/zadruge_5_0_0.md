# Rulebook on Cooperatives

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1 — Subject matter

(1) This Rulebook governs, in accordance with Article 50 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, the participation of cooperatives in the KOLO system: the entry of a cooperative, the cooperative's record in the Protocol, the status of cooperative member, the recording of the growth of a cooperative's record (Article 19 paragraph 1 point 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), the grounds for entries, the distribution of goods, cooperative tasks and cooperative contribution, the cooperative's assistance to users in its place, support from the Foundation, and the termination of a cooperative's participation.

(2) Terms from the Rulebook on the KOLO System have the same meaning in this Rulebook.

### Article 2 — Definitions

The following terms are also used in this Rulebook:

1) **cooperative's record**: the record in the Protocol kept for a cooperative;

2) **cooperative member**: a person whom the cooperative has admitted to membership under its own rules and whose account is linked to the cooperative in accordance with Article 6;

3) **distribution**: the receipt of goods from users and their issuing to cooperative members, or to another cooperative, through the cooperative;

4) **cooperative task**: a task for the community in the place or for the cooperative, which the cooperative publishes with a number of POEN that it determines itself (Article 14), and which it re-registers to the contributor from its own record.

### Article 3 — Autonomy of the cooperative

(1) A cooperative is an autonomous legal entity. Within the system it operates in the place that it has designated itself (Article 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(2) A cooperative is managed by its cooperative members, through the bodies established by the Law on Cooperatives ("Official Gazette of the RS", No. 112/2015) and by the cooperative's rules. The Foundation has no right to vote, to propose, or to give consent in the bodies of the cooperative.

(3) The Foundation acquires no share, stake, or right to the property of the cooperative.

## II — Entry of a Cooperative

### Article 4 — Conditions for entry

(1) The Foundation enters a cooperative in the system when the following conditions are met:

1) the cooperative is entered in the register under the Law on Cooperatives;

2) a cooperation agreement has been concluded;

3) the cooperative has designated the place in which it operates within the system;

4) the condition under Article 5 is met in that place.

(2) The Foundation rejects a request for entry only if one of the conditions under paragraph 1 is not met, stating the reasons.

(3) The cooperative's record is opened when at least 5 cooperative members are linked to the cooperative, by the recording of the first threshold under Article 9. Until the record is opened, re-registrations under Article 11 are not carried out.

### Article 5 — Place

(1) More than one cooperative may operate in the same place. A participant belongs to the place stated in their account.

(2) The first cooperative in a place is entered when that place has at least 50 participants. The Foundation participates in the establishment of the first cooperative in a place through the support referred to in Article 20.

(3) The condition under paragraph 2 does not apply to any subsequent cooperative in the same place.

## III — Cooperative Members

### Article 6 — Linking of accounts

(1) Only the account of a participant is linked to a cooperative. While a cooperative member is not a participant, their status of cooperative member is dormant: they are not counted towards the growth thresholds and do not re-register POEN to the cooperative's record; the link to the cooperative and the thresholds already recorded remain.

(2) The cooperative submits to the Foundation the first and last name of the cooperative member and the pseudonym of their account, for persons who have consented to this. Upon receipt of the data, the account is linked to the cooperative, and the first and last name are entered in the identity register, under the conditions of Article 31 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

(3) A person may be a cooperative member within the system in only one cooperative.

### Article 7 — Rights of a cooperative member

(1) The status of cooperative member adds to the rights that a cooperative member has according to their status only rights within the cooperative: participation in distribution, in cooperative tasks and in cooperative contribution, and the use of the cooperative's equipment and land, under the cooperative's rules.

(2) The status of cooperative member confers no additional authority in the KOLO system.

### Article 8 — Termination of the status of cooperative member

(1) The status of cooperative member terminates:

1) upon withdrawal or exclusion from the cooperative, of which the cooperative notifies the Foundation;

2) upon withdrawal of the consent under Article 6 paragraph 2;

3) upon termination of the user's status;

4) upon termination of the cooperative's participation.

(2) Termination of the status of cooperative member does not affect the cooperative member's POEN record and does not delete the first and last name from the identity register (Article 31 paragraph 7 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

## IV — Growth of a Cooperative's Record

### Article 9 — Thresholds

(1) The Protocol records new POEN records in the cooperative's record when the number of cooperative members reaches the following thresholds:

| Number of cooperative members | POEN |
|---|---|
| 5 (opening of the record) | 50,000 |
| 10 | 100,000 |
| 20 | 200,000 |
| 50 | 500,000 |
| 100 | 1,000,000 |
| 200 | 2,000,000 |
| 500 | 5,000,000 |

(2) Every cooperative member whose account is linked to the cooperative is counted towards a threshold.

### Article 10 — Each threshold once

(1) Each threshold is recorded only once. When the number of cooperative members falls, the POEN recorded for a threshold reached is not written off, and reaching the same threshold again is not recorded again.

(2) Recording is an automatic act of the Protocol and does not count towards the common daily limit under Article 19 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. The POEN entered counts towards the total number of recorded POEN.

(3) Recording is neither a remuneration nor a grant from the Foundation and does not give rise to any obligation of the cooperative towards the Foundation.

## V — Cooperative's Record and Distribution

### Article 11 — Grounds for entries

(1) POEN is re-registered from or to a cooperative's record only on a ground entered in the Protocol:

1) receipt of a good for distribution;

2) issuing of a good from distribution;

3) a cooperative task (Article 16);

4) use of equipment, machinery or land at the cooperative's disposal;

5) a service that the cooperative provides or receives.

(2) A re-registration without a ground under paragraph 1 is not permitted, either towards a user or towards another cooperative.

(3) A re-registration from a cooperative member's record to the cooperative's record is initiated by the cooperative member themselves.

(4) The actions under this Rulebook are not carried out with the account of a minor user.

### Article 12 — Distribution

(1) The cooperative receives a good for distribution in the name and on behalf of its cooperative members. Upon receipt, the cooperative re-registers POEN from its own record to the user who brought the good.

(2) The cooperative issues a good to a cooperative member or to another cooperative; upon issuing, POEN is re-registered to the cooperative's record.

(3) A good that is not issued or that perishes gives rise to no obligation of the user who brought it.

### Article 13 — Exchange between cooperatives and logistics

(1) The linking of cooperatives in different places (exchange and issuing of goods between cooperatives, transport, and support for the establishment of a cooperative in a new place) is governed by the inter-cooperative logistics module (Article 49 paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System). Until the module is introduced, a good is issued to another cooperative under Article 12.

(2) Transport of goods between cooperatives is organised by the cooperatives themselves. The Foundation does not provide a transport service and does not bear its costs; support for transport may be provided only as a project under Article 47 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System.

### Article 14 — Amount

(1) The number of POEN for each re-registration under Article 11 is determined by the cooperative, through its bodies and under its own rules.

(2) The Foundation does not determine, recommend, approve or publish the amount.

### Article 15 — Who acts on behalf of the cooperative

(1) The cooperative proposes a person whom the Foundation authorises to act on behalf of the cooperative. That person enters re-registrations from the cooperative's record (Article 11), including the confirmation of completion of a cooperative task (Article 16), links accounts to the cooperative, and enters the termination of the status of cooperative member (Articles 6 and 8).

(2) The person under paragraph 1 acts only in the record and with the cooperative members of the cooperative that proposed them. Of the data on the cooperative members of that cooperative, they see the first and last name, the pseudonym, and the telephone number that the cooperative member has entered in their account. They have no access to other data kept by the Foundation. They do not enter a re-registration from the cooperative's record to their own record.

(3) The actions under paragraph 1 may also be carried out, on the instruction of the cooperative, by the Foundation's administrator team. The entry of a cooperative, the issuing of a good to another cooperative and the termination of a cooperative's participation are carried out only by the Foundation's administrator team.

(4) The person under paragraph 1 acts in accordance with the acts of the KOLO system and, before being authorised, signs a statement of confidentiality regarding the data they access. The authorisation terminates upon revocation by the Foundation, upon termination of the cooperative's participation, and upon termination of that person's status.

## VI — Cooperative Tasks and Cooperative Contribution

### Article 16 — Cooperative task

(1) The completion of a cooperative task is confirmed by the cooperative; the contributor does not confirm their own completion.

(2) Upon confirmation, POEN is re-registered from the cooperative's record to the contributor. No new POEN arises from a cooperative task. The number of POEN does not express the value of working time.

### Article 17 — Cooperative contribution

(1) **Cooperative contribution** is a form of operational contribution (Article 34 paragraph 3 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System): organisational units in the cooperative, for which the Protocol records new POEN to the contributor, rather than re-registering it from the cooperative's record.

(2) An organisational unit is work done for the cooperative and the community in the place, such as an organised distribution or a joint action that was led, and not time spent at work. The cooperative itself determines which organisational units exist and to whom it assigns them.

(3) The highest amount of POEN that the contributors of one cooperative may receive in one accounting period equals the number of base units from the following table, according to the number of cooperative members, multiplied by the base unit amount established under Article 23 of the Rulebook on Operational Contribution:

| Number of cooperative members | Number of base units |
|---|---|
| 5 | 1 |
| 10 | 1 |
| 20 | 2 |
| 50 | 4 |
| 100 | 7 |
| 200 | 12 |
| 500 | 25 |
| 1000 | 40 |

(4) Between two thresholds in the table, the lower threshold applies. An unused amount is not carried over to the next accounting period.

(5) The publication of units, the confirmation of completion, which is given by Kolo keepers, and recording within the common daily limit are governed by the Rulebook on Operational Contribution (Article 23). POEN is recorded only for a confirmed unit with a description of the work done. The cooperative receives no POEN on the basis of cooperative contribution.

(6) Cooperative contribution is not a remuneration for work and is not paid by time; Article 34 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System applies to it.

## VII — The Cooperative in the Community of the Place

### Article 18 — Assistance in the amicable resolution of disputes

(1) The cooperative, through the body it designates under its rules, assists in the amicable resolution of disputes between users from the place in which it operates, including where the user is not its cooperative member (Article 50 paragraph 5 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System and Article 39 of the Terms of Use). The cooperative assumes this obligation by the cooperation agreement.

(2) The cooperative does not adopt a decision binding on the ledger, does not become a party to the exchange, and does not assume responsibility for the performance of users' obligations. The right to judicial protection remains.

(3) The data needed for the dispute are provided to the cooperative by the parties themselves, and for a minor user by their parent. The Foundation does not provide the cooperative with data on users. The cooperative processes the data it receives from the parties only for the purpose of assisting in the dispute, as a controller, in accordance with the regulations on personal data protection.

(4) For a correction of a record following an exchange that was not performed, the user submits a complaint to the Foundation, under Article 40 of the Terms of Use.

### Article 19 — Assistance to users in using the platform

(1) The cooperative helps users from its place who have difficulty with the platform, in particular older persons, to use it (Article 50 paragraph 6 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System).

(2) Assistance is neither representation nor sharing of an account: actions on the account are taken by the user themselves, the account remains theirs, and no one acts on their behalf. The person providing assistance does not ask for and does not learn the password or the sign-in code (Article 11 of the Terms of Use).

## VIII — Support from the Foundation

### Article 20 — Support from the Foundation

(1) The Foundation provides the cooperative with organisational and expert support in its establishment and operation.

(2) The Foundation may, from the funds available for projects (Article 5 of the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement), finance the operational costs of the cooperative in dinars, for a specific purpose and by an annex to the cooperation agreement. The financing is without consideration and confers on the Foundation none of the rights under Article 3.

(3) The cooperative is solely responsible for meeting its tax, accounting and labour-law obligations, including those arising in connection with the cooperative's record and the tasks under Article 16. The Foundation does not determine or verify those obligations and is not liable for them; recording in POEN does not determine or alter the public-law qualification of the cooperative's business.

## IX — Transparency and Personal Data

### Article 21 — Visibility of records and data of cooperative members

(1) The records of a cooperative are visible to participants, under the name of the cooperative; cooperative members are shown in them under their pseudonyms.

(2) The first and last name and the telephone number of a cooperative member are not shown to other users, except to the person under Article 15 for the cooperative members of their cooperative.

(3) The Foundation processes the first and last name of a cooperative member for the purpose of linking accounts and counting cooperative members under Article 9 and keeps it in the identity register under Article 31 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System, in accordance with the Privacy Policy and the Records of Processing Activities.

## X — Termination of Participation

### Article 22 — Termination of a cooperative's participation

(1) The participation of a cooperative terminates when the termination of the cooperation agreement takes effect, or upon the deletion of the cooperative from the register.

(2) From the termination of the cooperation agreement, or from the initiation of the procedure for deleting the cooperative from the register, no re-registration is made from or to the cooperative's record, and cooperative contribution is not recorded.

(3) Upon termination of participation, the cooperative's record is closed, and the POEN recorded in it is annulled under Article 50 paragraph 4 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System. POEN is not the property of the cooperative and is not transferred to anyone. Users' records created earlier remain intact.

## XI — Final Provisions

### Article 23 — Entry into force

This Rulebook enters into force on the date of its adoption.
