# Terms of Use of the KOLO Platform

## I — Introductory provisions

### Article 1 — Subject matter

(1) These Terms of Use (hereinafter: the Terms) govern the rights and obligations between the KOLO Foundation (hereinafter: the Foundation) and every person who accesses or uses the KOLO Platform (hereinafter: the Platform).

(2) The Terms are a platform act within the meaning of the Rulebook on the Hierarchy of Acts of the KOLO System. The Terms may not narrow or deprive users of rights recognised to them by the Rulebook on the KOLO System (hereinafter: the Rulebook) and by other rulebooks; in the event of inconsistency, the solution more favourable to the user applies.

(3) Mandatory regulations on consumer protection and on the protection of the weaker contracting party apply directly and remain unaffected by the provisions of these Terms.

(4) The Platform is intended for persons living in the Republic of Serbia. The place is selected from the register of settlements in Serbia, donations are received in dinars, and the Foundation is registered in Serbia. Translations of the Platform and of the acts into other languages exist for members of national minorities and foreigners living in Serbia, and not for offering services to persons in other states.

### Article 2 — Definitions

(1) Terms from the Rulebook have the same meaning in these Terms. The Kolo is not a legal entity, and belonging to the Kolo, including the position of a member without confirmation, is not membership in the Foundation or in any other legal entity.

(2) In addition, the following terms are used in these Terms:

1) **Platform**: the information system through which users access the KOLO system; the technical infrastructure for recording, exchange, and governance;

2) **account**: a user's personal account on the Platform, created by registration, which enables access to system functions according to the user's status;

3) **Pijaca** (Marketplace): the part of the Platform intended for advertising and arranging the exchange of goods and services between users (exchange space);

4) **listing**: a publication on the Pijaca by which a user offers or seeks a good or a service;

5) **session**: the period of active use of the Platform between the user's sign-in and sign-out.

### Article 3 — The Foundation

(1) The KOLO Foundation is a non-profit private legal entity registered in the Register of Endowments and Foundations in the Republic of Serbia, registration number 28836627, tax identification number (PIB) 115840443, with its registered address at Šetalište 16, 25000 Sombor.

(2) Contact address for all questions relating to these Terms, for reports, and for complaints that are not submitted via the Platform: kontakt@ekolo.rs. Questions about the processing of personal data are addressed to the address set out in the Privacy Policy.

### Article 4 — Legal nature of the Platform

(1) The Platform is a contribution recording system and the governance infrastructure of the KOLO system. The Platform is not a payment system, payment institution, bank, exchange, or investment platform.

(2) POEN and ZRNO are not money, electronic money, cryptocurrency, digital assets, or securities. A POEN record is not a claim either against the Foundation or against anyone else, it is not issued in exchange for a payment, in a re-registration the Foundation does not receive, hold, or transfer monetary funds and does not charge for the re-registration, and there is no payout or redemption from the record. The legal nature of POEN and ZRNO, the four principles, and the two limits of the common good are established by the Rulebook (Articles 9 to 16 and 21).

### Article 5 — System phase

(1) The KOLO system is in Phase 1 within the meaning of the Rulebook. In this phase the rules of the system are established by the Management Board of the Foundation; an amendment may also be proposed by the founder of the Foundation (Article 42 of the Rulebook).

(2) The founders are the initial Kolo keepers, designated when the system was established under the Rulebook on Proof of Reality. The circle of founders is closed, and the capacity of founder does not confer the powers of the Management Board (Article 58 of the Rulebook). The founders' shares within the meaning of the Rulebook on Founding Contribution are publicly available within the founding contribution system.

(3) ZRNO inscription, the Upper Kolo, and the voting right of Kolo keepers begin upon the system's transition to Phase 2 (Article 41 of the Rulebook).

### Article 6 — Acceptance of the Terms

(1) By using the Platform, the user confirms that they have read, understood, and accepted these Terms in their entirety, together with the Platform's Privacy Policy. The Terms and the Privacy Policy are accepted by checking the confirmation box during registration, separately for each, because they relate to different matters.

(2) The acceptance is recorded. Alongside it, the moment of acceptance, the designation of the version of the act accepted, and the text displayed to the user are recorded. Without an acceptance so recorded, the account is not opened. The manner in which that record is kept, and the data that are not recorded alongside it, are established by the Privacy Policy.

(3) The user confirms that they are familiar with the Rulebook, which is publicly available on the Platform. The risks of participation in the system are described by the Risk Acceptance Statement, which the user accepts by registering.

(4) A person who does not accept these Terms may not use the Platform.

## II — Registration and account

### Article 7 — Registration conditions

(1) The right to register is held by any natural person who has reached 18 years of age, holds a valid email address, and accepts these Terms and the Privacy Policy. An excluded person registers again only under Article 32 paragraph 4.

(2) A minor who has reached seven years of age participates on the Platform under a special regime governed by the Rulebook on the Participation of Children.

(3) Registration is free of charge.

### Article 8 — One account per person

Each natural person may hold exclusively one account on the Platform. Registration of multiple accounts by the same natural person is prohibited regardless of the identifiers used. That two accounts are accounts of the same person is established by the Foundation, with a statement of reasons and the right to a complaint, under the conditions and with the consequences set out in the Rulebook on Proof of Reality; whoever confirmed the user or introduced them into the Kolo does not guarantee this.

### Article 9 — Registration process

(1) Upon registration, the user selects a pseudonym, enters an email address, and sets a password.

(2) A message with a link for confirming the address is sent to the email address entered. Confirmation is not a condition for using the account: the account operates in full even before it. Confirmation serves to ensure that the notices under Article 43 reach the user and that their access is recoverable if they lose their password.

(3) The pseudonym is the only identifier of the user visible to other users, to the extent that depends on the status of the viewer. The user is responsible for ensuring that the pseudonym does not contain data that could lead to their identification.

### Article 10 — Accuracy of data

The user is responsible for the accuracy of all data entered during registration and throughout the use of the Platform and must update them without delay in the event of a change.

### Article 11 — Protection of access credentials

(1) The user is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their access credentials. The user must report any suspicion of unauthorised access to the Foundation at the address under Article 3 without delay. No one asks the user for their password or sign-in code: neither the Foundation, nor a Kolo member, nor a person who helps them use the Platform.

(2) The Foundation is not liable for damage arising from unauthorised access to the account due to the user's failure to maintain the confidentiality of access credentials.

### Article 12 — Prohibition on transfer of account

(1) An account is personal and may not be transferred, assigned, or shared with third parties.

(2) Help in using the Platform, including help from a cooperative (Article 50 paragraph 6 of the Rulebook), is not sharing an account: actions on the account are taken by the user themselves, the account remains theirs, and no one acts on their behalf.

## III — Access to the Platform and user statuses

### Article 13 — User statuses

An adult user may hold the status of a user, a member without confirmation, a Kolo member, or a Kolo keeper. The statuses and the transition between them are established by the Rulebook and the Rulebook on Proof of Reality. A minor user does not hold a status under this Article; their position is governed by the Rulebook on the Participation of Children. Articles 14 and 15 govern the scope of access to Platform functions according to status.

### Article 14 — User who is not in the Kolo

(1) The status of user is held by an adult user registered on the Platform who is not in the Kolo.

(2) A user may view listings, exchange goods and services, and post a listing offering or seeking a good or a service (Article 18). They may write in the common conversation space (Chat Room), respond in a conversation in which they participate, and themselves initiate a conversation with an advertiser regarding the advertiser's listing, at most three new conversations per day. They may not access advertisers' contact details or, outside a listing, contact users who have not contacted them. They see the ledger and another user's profile to the extent set out in Article 16.

(3) A user re-registers POEN to another user and receives re-registrations.

(4) A user may not confirm other users, introduce them into the Kolo, or give recommendations. They do not take part in a cooperative, support programmes, collective procurement, or the distribution of vouchers, do not submit an application for sponsorship in goods and services, do not perform operational contribution, and do not inscribe ZRNO. Contribution is recorded for them only through the channel of contribution for a first listing and through the channels of financial contribution and sponsorship (Article 28 paragraph 2 of the Rulebook).

(5) A user who does not know any Kolo member may get onto the call list, under the conditions of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality. Neither getting onto the list, nor a donation, sponsorship, or farm application confirms their reality; it is established only in the chain of confirmations.

### Article 15 — Participants

(1) A Kolo member has full access to the Platform: they exchange goods and services, have contribution recorded in POEN, confirm users and members without confirmation whom they know personally, introduce into the Kolo users from the call list, give recommendations, and take part in a cooperative, support programmes, and collective procurement.

(2) A member without confirmation has the rights that the acts attach to a participant: they exchange goods and services, have contribution recorded in POEN, take part in a cooperative, support programmes, and collective procurement, have the same access to the ledger as a Kolo member, and introduce into the Kolo users whom they know personally. What they may not do and how they become a Kolo member is established by the Rulebook on Proof of Reality.

(3) A Kolo keeper has all the rights of a Kolo member and, by activating ZRNO, takes part in the Upper Kolo from Phase 2. The consequences of the annulment of a confirmation, or of an introduction into the Kolo, for inscribed ZRNO are established by the Rulebook (Article 29).

### Article 16 — Visibility of data

(1) The Platform is a transparent recording system. By accepting these Terms, the user accepts the visibility model described in this Article and in the Privacy Policy.

(2) Non-registered persons see only general system indicators.

(3) Users who are not in the Kolo see the amounts and timestamps of POEN transactions, but do not see the pseudonyms of the parties, account balances, or users' ZRNO status. They see another user's profile to a restricted extent: pseudonym, picture, place if the user has made it visible, status, date of registration, active listings, and the number of recommendations received.

(4) Participants see the pseudonyms of all users, account balances, and all transactions with the pseudonyms of the parties. Excluded from that access are transactions in which either party is a minor user; they are not displayed, because the children's space is not public. A record of contribution recorded under a support programme is displayed with the pseudonym of the support recipient, the name of the programme, and the amount, while the ground on which the entitlement was established is not displayed to any user; to users who are not in the Kolo and to non-registered persons, a daily total per programme is displayed instead of individual records.

(5) The list of donations is available to adult users: the donor of a public donation is stated by name and surname, together with the amount and the date, without a pseudonym and without a link to a profile; an anonymous donation only by its amount and date. A user sees their own donations regardless of status. The user may withdraw consent to the publication of their name at any time, separately for each public donation (Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations). The publication serves the transparency of the Foundation's disposal of funds and the verification of the ground on which POEN was entered. The Foundation does not set it as a condition on which it agrees to record a contribution. For an anonymous donation no POEN is recorded, because an entry that cannot be attributed to any person is not verifiable; the absence of recording is not a consequence of consent being withheld. The amounts of confirmed sponsorships are publicly available.

(6) The user may, on their own initiative and at any time, choose to make their name and surname and telephone number visible to all participants. This disclosure is voluntary and may be withdrawn at any time.

(7) The visibility of the pseudonymous contribution ledger to participants is a structural characteristic of the system that the user may not disable. A user who does not accept it may not use the Platform.

(8) The Foundation is not liable for the consequences arising if the user themselves reveals their identity through their choice of pseudonym or voluntary disclosure of data.

## IV — Pijaca

### Article 17 — Purpose and public nature of the Pijaca

(1) The Pijaca is the part of the Platform intended for advertising offers and requests for goods and services and arranging exchanges between users.

(2) Browsing listings (the content of an offer or request, the amount in POEN, the place, and the advertiser's pseudonym) is public and accessible to all visitors.

(3) Posting a listing and initiating a conversation regarding a listing are available to all registered users, and to a user who is not in the Kolo under the conditions of Article 14. Access to advertisers' contact details is available exclusively to participants.

(4) For persons who are not signed in, the advertiser's pseudonym is not linked to the contribution ledger or to the user's profile, and for users who are not in the Kolo it is linked only to the restricted profile under Article 16, without the contribution ledger.

(5) The Foundation is not a party to the exchange and does not mediate in it. The legal relationship arises exclusively between the users who exchange goods and services.

### Article 18 — Listing and content minimum

(1) The content minimum of a listing is a title, a description, at least one photograph, a category, and a place, whereby the length of the title and the description is not a condition. A listing of a user who is not in the Kolo that does not meet the content minimum is not published; the same condition applies when a published listing is edited. For a participant such a listing is published, but no contribution is recorded for them on account of it.

(2) A listing of a user who is not in the Kolo carries a visible mark indicating that the advertiser is not in the Kolo. The mark is available to everyone to whom the listing itself is available, including persons who are not signed in.

(3) By a listing a user may present themselves to Kolo members; even then, their reality is established only in the chain of confirmations (Rulebook on Proof of Reality).

(4) The Foundation may remove a listing that breaches these Terms, the Rulebook, or the law, with notice to the user stating the reason. The Foundation does not edit another person's listing.

### Article 19 — Contribution for a first listing and approval

(1) For the first listing in which they offer a good or a service and which meets the content minimum, a contribution is recorded for the user under the conditions set out in Article 38 of the Rulebook. The listing is published immediately, while the contribution is recorded when the Foundation approves the listing; this applies to every user, regardless of whether they are in the Kolo.

(2) The Foundation decides on approval within a reasonable period, by checking that the listing meets the content minimum, that it represents a genuine offer of a good or a service, and that it does not offer a prohibited good or service (Article 23). The Foundation does not approve a listing offering a prohibited good or service, but removes it, stating the reason.

(3) If the contribution is not approved, the user is notified with a statement of reasons; the listing is not thereby removed, and the contribution is considered again on account of a supplemented or new listing of the same user.

(4) If the listing is removed before the contribution has been recorded, the noted contribution is annulled. A contribution already recorded is not annulled by the removal of the listing.

### Article 20 — Farm and the DOMAĆE mark

(1) An adult user may register a farm, under the conditions of the Rulebook on Farms. Registration is free of charge.

(2) A listing that a member posts on behalf of an approved farm carries the DOMAĆE mark. The mark indicates only that the listing has been posted on behalf of a farm that has joined the system. The Foundation does not check or guarantee the origin, quality, or safety of the good offered; the user who posted the listing is responsible for them.

### Article 21 — Determining the amount in a listing

(1) The amount in a listing is expressed in POEN and is set by the user who posts the listing. The Foundation publishes no ratio of POEN to the dinar or to any other currency and does not recommend the ratio by which a user should set the amount.

(2) If, in setting the amount, a user starts from a value expressed in money, that is their own estimate. It applies only to that listing, does not bind the other party, and is not a ratio that the Foundation establishes, publishes, or applies.

(3) The Foundation applies no ratio of POEN to money in any of its procedures: it does not record contribution by it, does not establish any amount prescribed by the rulebooks, and does not determine the number of POEN in projects and collective procurements. The amounts prescribed by the rulebooks are expressed in POEN and are not derived from dinars.

(4) The estimate under paragraph 2 is not a guarantee of convertibility, does not constitute an exchange rate within the meaning of payment services regulations, and does not change the legal nature of POEN established by the Rulebook. The Foundation guarantees and maintains no value of POEN whatsoever.

(5) The Foundation does not redeem POEN or ZRNO and does not provide for their conversion into dinars or any other means of payment. There is no contract for the exchange of dinars for POEN.

(6) Users freely determine the amount for the goods and services they offer. The Platform does not establish, limit, or control amounts.

### Article 22 — Posting rules

(1) A user who posts an offer or request must provide an accurate and clear description of the good or service, a realistic amount in POEN, and all terms relevant to the exchange.

(2) The user may not post content that is false, misleading, or that misrepresents the nature, quality, or quantity of the good or service.

(3) A user who offers a good or a service is responsible for complying with the regulations that apply to that good or service, including regulations on business registration, on production and trade, on food safety, and tax regulations. Those regulations apply also where the exchange is arranged through the Platform. Recording POEN on account of an exchange neither determines nor changes the tax, accounting, or other public-law qualification of the transaction between users. A user who offers a good or a service within an activity on account of which they are obliged to issue an invoice, a fiscal receipt, or another document is obliged to issue it regardless of whether the exchange has been recorded in POEN. The Foundation does not check or confirm compliance with those requirements and bears no liability for it.

### Article 23 — Prohibited goods and services

(1) The offering and exchange of goods and services whose trade is prohibited by law is forbidden, including narcotic drugs and precursors, weapons and explosives whose trade is not permitted, stolen goods or goods for which there is a suspicion that they originate from a criminal offence, personal documents and official papers, personal data of third parties, content that incites hatred, violence, or discrimination, and financial instruments or schemes designed to circumvent regulations.

(2) Listings whose subject is POEN or ZRNO are likewise prohibited: offering, seeking, buying, or selling them for money or for any other value outside the system (Article 28).

(3) A minor user is prohibited from offering or acquiring goods whose provision to minors is prohibited or restricted by law, in particular alcoholic beverages, tobacco and related products, including electronic cigarettes and liquids for them, energy drinks, medicines and food supplements, pyrotechnic articles, and sharp objects. The prohibition applies regardless of parental consent and regardless of whether the exchange was arranged through a listing on the Platform or in another way.

(4) For goods whose trade is permitted but restricted by special regulations, a user offering them is obliged to observe those restrictions, including the prohibition of provision to minors and restrictions on advertising.

(5) The list of prohibited categories is not exhaustive. The Foundation may remove a publication that it determines to be in breach of the law, these Terms, or the Rulebook, and notifies the user of the removal, stating the reason.

### Article 24 — Execution of an exchange

(1) The exchange is arranged directly by the users. Users are free to establish the terms of the exchange, including the manner and deadline for delivery of the good or provision of the service.

(2) Upon the users' agreement, the exchange is recorded by a re-registration of POEN (Article 20 of the Rulebook): the record of the user providing the good or service is increased, and the record of the receiving user is decreased by the same amount. An exchange is not a payment transaction and the re-registration does not constitute a payment within the meaning of payment services regulations.

(3) The users who take part in the exchange are responsible for its performance, quality, and risk, in accordance with the general rules of the law of obligations.

(4) Where one party to the exchange is a minor user, the regulations on the legal capacity of minors also apply. A minor user undertakes legal transactions within the limits set by those regulations, and parental consent given under the Rulebook on the Participation of Children does not replace consent to a particular legal transaction where a regulation requires it.

(5) The Platform does not require users to separately mark, confirm, or rate an exchange. The only record of an exchange is the re-registration of POEN that the users carry out themselves.

(6) A user who has re-registered POEN on account of an exchange that has not been fulfilled lodges a complaint with the Foundation (Article 40). Deciding on the complaint concerns the record in the Protocol's ledger; it does not make the Foundation a party to the exchange and does not constitute mediation in the exchange.

(7) The provisions of this Article relate to exchange between users. They do not apply to collective procurement, in which the Foundation is the buyer and the contracting party in relation to the supplier.

### Article 25 — Collective procurement and vouchers

(1) Collective procurement and the distribution of vouchers from sponsorship in goods and services are governed by the Rulebook on Projects and Collective Procurement. Participants take part in them; minor users do not take part. Participation is voluntary.

(2) In relation to the supplier in a collective procurement, the Foundation is the buyer and the contracting party; the user does not enter into a contractual relationship with the supplier. Collection of a share is not a purchase and does not establish a claim against the Foundation.

(3) The good under a voucher is handed over, or the service provided, by the giver directly to the voucher holder, and the giver is liable for them to the holder; the Foundation is not a party to that relationship.

### Article 26 — Relation to consumer protection regulations

(1) The Foundation does not act on the market towards users: it does not offer them goods or services for remuneration, does not charge for use of the Platform, does not mediate in exchange, and receives no value whatsoever on account of exchange between users.

(2) Where a good or a service is offered by a person doing so within a business activity, consumer protection regulations apply to the relationship with a user acquiring them for their own needs, regardless of the fact that the exchange was arranged through the Platform.

(3) Rights under those regulations are exercised against that person. The Foundation is not a party to the exchange, is not liable for their fulfilment, and does not establish in what capacity a user is acting.

## V — Rules of conduct on the Platform

### Article 27 — General rules of conduct

(1) The user uses the Platform in accordance with its purpose, with respect for other users, and in accordance with the law.

(2) Harassment, including sexual harassment and stalking, threats, discrimination, or any form of conduct that endangers the dignity or safety of other users is prohibited.

### Article 28 — Prohibited use of the Platform

(1) The user is prohibited from registering multiple accounts or registering with a false identity or another person's data, conducting fictitious transactions for the purpose of artificially generating POEN, arranging transactions whose sole purpose is manipulation of the system or the ledger, using the Platform for money laundering, fraud, or any other unlawful activity, misusing the personal data of other users obtained through use of the Platform, falsely representing themselves as another user or as a representative of the Foundation, attempting to gain unauthorised access to other persons' accounts or to the Platform's infrastructure, and using automated tools, bots, or scripts to interact with the Platform without the Foundation's written authorisation.

(2) The name and surname and telephone number obtained from the call list are used only for a conversation for the purpose of getting acquainted, confirmation, or introduction into the Kolo. Any other use is misuse, in particular a repeated call or message outside that purpose, recording or forwarding the data, and asking for money, a password, or a code. A user who is approached in that way reports it to the Foundation; the Foundation acts under Articles 31 and 32 and under the regulations on personal data protection.

(3) The user is likewise prohibited from offering, buying, selling, or brokering trade in POEN or ZRNO for money or for any other value outside the system. Such trade is contrary to the principle of non-convertibility (Article 11 of the Rulebook on the KOLO System), according to which no accounting unit of the system may be converted into a means having value outside the system, whether directly or indirectly.

(4) An arrangement under paragraph 3 is concluded entirely outside the Platform. The Foundation does not mediate in it, does not record it, and does not provide protection to either party, and a user who obtains POEN in that manner acquires no donation tier and does not move the recording coefficient under the Rulebook on Sponsorship and Donations. A user acting contrary to paragraph 3 is subject to the measures under Articles 31 and 32.

(5) The user is likewise prohibited from assigning, renting out, or selling access to their account, and from using another person's account. An account is tied to the person who opened it and, in the case of a participant, to the person whose reality has been established in the chain of confirmations, and is not transferable. Assigning an account also transfers the records within it, including inscribed ZRNO, which under the Rulebook may not be transferred; the prohibition under this paragraph prevents that non-transferability from being circumvented. A user acting contrary to this paragraph is subject to the measures under Articles 31 and 32.

### Article 29 — User content

(1) The user is responsible for all content they publish on the Platform, including offers, requests, and all other communication via the Platform.

(2) The Foundation is not obliged to review user content in advance. The Foundation may remove content that breaches these Terms, the Rulebook, or the law, or that has been reported by other users as problematic, and notifies the user of the removal, stating the reason. The Foundation does not edit user content.

## VI — Termination of status and suspension

### Article 30 — Withdrawal

(1) The user may voluntarily withdraw from the system at any time. Withdrawal is effected by the user's electronic declaration via the Platform, without a notice period.

(2) Before the withdrawal declaration is submitted, the Platform shows the user a summary of the consequences of termination of status under Article 33 and requires explicit confirmation.

### Article 31 — Suspension

(1) The Foundation may temporarily restrict a user's access to some or all Platform functions in the event of a reasonable suspicion of a breach of these Terms or the Rulebook, while the fact-finding procedure is ongoing.

(2) During suspension, the user retains their status but may not use the functions to which the suspension applies.

(3) The Foundation notifies the user of the suspension and its grounds without delay via the email address registered on the account. The notification contains the reason for the suspension, the scope of restrictions, and the expected duration.

(4) Suspension lasts at most 30 days. If within that period the Foundation does not adopt a decision on exclusion, the suspension ceases and the user regains full access.

### Article 32 — Exclusion

(1) The Foundation may exclude a user from the system for a serious breach of the rules of the system. Serious breaches include in particular:

1) false registration: a false identity, use of another person's data, or registration of multiple accounts;

2) manipulation of the ledger: fictitious transactions, artificial generation of POEN, or arranged transactions for the purpose of manipulation;

3) unlawful conduct: money laundering, fraud, or use of the Platform for unlawful activities;

4) false verification within the meaning of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality;

5) a false declaration when registering a farm or when joining a farm, in which case the person who made the false declaration or knew of it is excluded, while for the other members of the farm only the farm contribution is written off (Rulebook on Farms);

6) a false declaration of parenthood when opening or taking over a minor user's account (Rulebook on the Participation of Children);

7) misuse of the name and surname or telephone number obtained from the call list (Article 28 paragraph 2);

8) repeated breach of the rules of conduct under Chapter V after a suspension has been imposed.

(2) Where the Foundation establishes that two accounts are accounts of the same person, the account that ceases is excluded, and the person is excluded where that account was used to obtain POEN or a vote (Rulebook on Proof of Reality).

(3) Exclusion is a decision of the Foundation. The Foundation notifies the user of the exclusion in writing to their email address, with a statement of reasons. The user may lodge a complaint against the exclusion (Article 40).

(4) An excluded user may not register an account again without a special decision of the Foundation.

### Article 33 — Consequences of termination of status

(1) Upon termination of status (by withdrawal, exclusion, or death), the consequences established by Article 33 of the Rulebook ensue: POEN records are annulled, ZRNO returns to the Protocol without the recording of POEN, and personal data are pseudonymised, whereby the numerical history remains under a pseudonym that does not point to the name.

(2) No monetary compensation is paid to the user on any basis. POEN and ZRNO records are not inherited as property rights.

### Article 34 — Death of a user

(1) The death of a user may be reported by a family member or another close person, by a message to the address under Article 3, stating the pseudonym or the email address of the account. Neither a probate decision nor a visit to the Foundation is required for the report.

(2) The Foundation may request proof of death only where it has grounds to doubt the accuracy of the report. Before the termination of status, the Foundation sends a notice to the email address of the account that a death has been reported; if no one responds from the account within 15 days, the status terminates.

(3) Upon termination of status, the consequences under Article 33 ensue. The family does not take over the account, does not access it, and does not inherit the records (Article 12 of the Rulebook), and on account of the account has no obligation whatsoever towards the Foundation or towards other users. The Foundation notifies the person who reported the death that the status has terminated.

(4) The death of a minor user is reported by a parent.

## IX — Dispute resolution and complaint

### Article 39 — Disputes between users

(1) Disputes between users in connection with an exchange are resolved in accordance with the general rules of the law of obligations. The right to judicial protection remains.

(2) A user may request help in the amicable resolution of a dispute from a cooperative operating in the place of the dispute, under the conditions of the Rulebook on Cooperatives. The place of the dispute is the place that the party to the dispute has stated in their account; where the parties have different places, help may be provided by a cooperative in the place of either of them. A cooperative also helps a user who is not its cooperative member.

(3) A cooperative helps through its organs. It does not adopt a decision binding on the ledger, does not become a party to the exchange, and does not assume liability for the performance of users' obligations. The data needed for the dispute are provided to the cooperative by the parties themselves; the Foundation does not provide the cooperative with data on users.

(4) Where there is no cooperative in the place of either party, help in amicable resolution is provided by the Foundation, upon a request sent to the address under Article 3, within 30 days of receipt. In doing so the Foundation does not adopt a binding decision, does not become a party to the exchange, and does not assume liability for the performance of users' obligations.

(5) Where a party to the dispute is a minor user, a parent takes part in the amicable resolution on their behalf and also provides the data needed for the dispute.

(6) For a correction of a record in the ledger following an exchange that has not been fulfilled, the user lodges a complaint with the Foundation (Article 40); help from a cooperative, or from the Foundation, in amicable resolution does not replace the complaint and does not suspend the deadline for lodging it.

### Article 40 — Complaint to the Foundation

(1) A user may lodge a complaint against a decision of the Foundation concerning them and against a record in the Protocol's ledger. The complaint is submitted from the user's profile, via the Platform; a user whose access to the account is restricted submits it to the address under Article 3.

(2) A complaint may be lodged in particular against:

1) a re-registration of POEN on account of an exchange that has not been fulfilled, as well as a correction of such a re-registration where the user's record has thereby been decreased;

2) a defect in a share collected in a collective procurement, including the case where the share was not collected although it was marked as collected;

3) non-performance of the giver's obligation under a voucher awarded in the distribution of goods and services from sponsorship, including the case where the voucher was marked as used although the good was not handed over or the service was not provided;

4) a decision on confirmation and on introduction into the Kolo, including a decision on false verification;

5) suspension and exclusion;

6) a decision on an application to a support programme;

7) a decision on the registration of a farm, on joining a farm, and on the annulment of a farm's approval, as well as the termination of the farm and the write-off of the farm contribution;

8) a decision on a report that the person who opened or took over a minor user's account is not their parent;

9) a decision establishing a multiple account;

10) the removal of a listing or a message;

11) an inaccurate personal data item;

12) other decisions of the Foundation concerning the user.

(3) A complaint states the subject matter to which it relates. A complaint under points 1 to 3 of paragraph 2 must designate exactly one re-registration, exactly one share, or exactly one voucher, respectively.

(4) A complaint against a decision on confirmation and on introduction into the Kolo, against a suspension, and concerning an inaccurate personal data item is not bound by a deadline. For other complaints the deadlines are:

1) for a defect in a share from a collective procurement: 7 days from the notification of collection;

2) for a complaint relating to an exchange: 30 days from the re-registration, or from its correction;

3) for exclusion and for the establishment of a multiple account: 15 days from receipt of the notification;

4) for non-performance of an obligation under a voucher: 30 days from the day on which the giver refused to hand over or to provide, or from the day on which the voucher was marked as used, and no later than 15 days from the expiry of the voucher's validity period;

5) for other decisions of the Foundation: 30 days from communication of the decision.

(5) Upon a complaint concerning an exchange that has not been fulfilled, the Foundation, before deciding, notifies the other party and gives them 7 days to state their position; if they do not state it within that period, the decision is made without it. The complaint is lodged exclusively by the user who initiated the re-registration, and at most one per re-registration. When it establishes that the exchange has not been fulfilled, the Protocol corrects the ledger in the amount of the entire re-registration (Article 20 paragraph 5 of the Rulebook).

(6) A user may have at most three open complaints per type.

(7) The Foundation responds to a complaint within 30 days, with a statement of reasons. Where the complaint relates to a record in the ledger, the statement of reasons is communicated to all persons whose record is affected by the decision.

(8) Lodging a complaint does not exclude the user's right to judicial protection.

### Article 41 — Disputes between users and the Foundation

Disputes between users and the Foundation in connection with the application of the rules of the system are resolved by agreement whenever possible. If agreement is not possible, the dispute is resolved before the competent court in Sombor, unless another territorial jurisdiction is determined by law.

### Article 42 — Disputes relating to data protection

Disputes relating to the protection of personal data are resolved in accordance with the law. The user has the right to lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection, Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, Belgrade, www.poverenik.rs.

## X — Amendments to acts

### Article 43 — Amendment procedure

(1) The procedure under this Article applies to amendments to these Terms, the Rulebook, and the other acts of the KOLO system.

(2) A change to the appearance, layout, technical solutions, or functions of the Platform that does not alter the text of these Terms is not deemed an amendment to the Terms and does not require prior notification of users.

(3) An amendment is published on the Platform before it enters into force. Of amendments that narrow the rights of users or expand the processing of personal data, the user is, in addition, notified without delay via the email address registered on the account.

(4) The Foundation may require express acceptance of the amended Terms upon the user's next sign-in to the account, and may restrict access to the account until acceptance. The restriction does not affect the user's right to download their data, to request deletion of the account, and to withdraw from the system (Article 30); those actions remain available before acceptance as well.

(5) If express acceptance is not required, continued use of the Platform after the amendments enter into force is deemed acceptance of the amended Terms. A user who does not accept the amendments may withdraw from the system (Article 30).

(6) Refusal to give consent for processing whose legal basis is consent may not be a ground for restricting access to the account.

(7) Amendments to these Terms or to other acts may not change the principles and limits of the common good (Article 10 of the Rulebook).

(8) The current version of the Terms is always available on the Platform.

## XI — Final provisions

### Article 44 — Governing law

These Terms are governed by the law of the Republic of Serbia.

### Article 45 — Severability clause

If any provision of these Terms is found to be void or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in force in full.

### Article 46 — Language

(1) These Terms are drawn up in the Serbian language, which is authoritative. Translations into English, Russian, Croatian, and Hungarian are available on the Platform for ease of understanding. In the event of any discrepancy between a translation and the Serbian text, the Serbian text prevails.

(2) The same applies to the other acts of the KOLO system: the Serbian text is the only authoritative one, and translations are informative in character.

### Article 47 — Entry into force

These Terms enter into force on the date of their adoption.
